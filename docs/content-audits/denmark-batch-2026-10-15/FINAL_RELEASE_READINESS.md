## Legal currency and editorial treatment

### Extension dates and BEK 828/2026

Retsinformation identifies [BEK no. 828 of 23 September 2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/828) as current. Its operative text provides that a permit may be extended to 17 March 2028 unless section 10 revocation grounds apply; the Immigration Service decides on its own initiative, and the extension case has suspensive effect while processed. It took effect on 1 October 2026 and repealed BEK 1095/2025. No later superseding regulation was found in this check.

The [Danish Immigration Service page for people displaced from Ukraine](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Ukraine/Displaced-from-Ukraine) and its [family-member page](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Ukraine/Family-to-displaced-from-ukraine) still state 17 March 2027. The entry about residence options and the application/extension entry attribute the dates to the respective sources, state that they have not been reconciled, and caution that neither date alone establishes an individual permit's validity. This is an accurately disclosed source disagreement, not an inferred resolution. The BEK page itself returned a JavaScript shell to the direct page reader; its official indexed entry exposed its current status and full relevant provisions. HTTP status was not established.

### Act 725/2026, Act 766/2026 and application rules

The official [Folketing record for L 14](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20252/lovforslag/L14/index.htm) records adoption as Act 725 on 1 September 2026. The current Immigration Service [military-age rules page](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/News-Front-Page/2026/09/New-rules-for-Ukrainian-men-of-military-age-%2823-59-years%29) says the rules apply to applications from 25 June 2026, describes the transition for permits granted before 3 September, and explains the under-23 validity limit. The application article preserves these conditions, the age range, the individual review and contact process, and the exception for applications submitted before 25 June. The direct promulgated Act 725 record was not readable in this audit tool; the official parliamentary record and current agency guidance support the claims used in the article.

[Act 766/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/766) is recorded by Retsinformation as current and amends several statutes, including the Ukraine Special Act. Its subject is serious-crime expulsion and electronic monitoring. The articles do not summarize those amendments; they retain cautious references to individual conditions and section 10 revocation grounds. The Retsinformation page was a JavaScript shell, so this audit does not claim direct full-text verification of Act 766.

### L 43

The official [Folketing L 43 record](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20261/lovforslag/L43/index.htm) reports status **Fremsat** (introduced), dated 8 October 2026, with first reading scheduled for 13 November 2026. Its proposal would add possible restrictions for applicants from less war-affected areas from 1 December 2026 and change provisions related to the arbejdspligt regime from 1 January 2027, with a proposed transition deadline of 1 May 2027. Those are proposals, not rules in force on this audit date.

No article describes those proposals as current law. The social-assistance entry explicitly dates its statement about the current arbejdspligt scope to 15 October 2026. These claims are time-sensitive: before deployment, confirm L 43's live status and, if enacted or amended, review the final text, effective dates and transitions against the affected articles. A pending bill alone does not block this snapshot.

### Other material legal dependencies

- The current [Integration Act, LBK no. 453/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/453/pdf), §12(1), says the municipality **must** designate temporary accommodation for a covered refugee; it **may instead** designate housing. Section 57(5) excludes a permit under Ukraine Special Act §2(1) from ordinary §12 placement, while permitting the Immigration Service to decide otherwise in very special personal circumstances. The Housing entry reflects that narrow scope and preserves the separate responsibility-transfer timing and no-guaranteed-dwelling qualifications.
- The official [Ukraine Special Act text](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/229946), §28, was directly readable in PDF form. It contains an ordinary eight-week appeal period and a seven-day condition for suspensive effect for covered decisions, alongside exceptions and discretion. BEK 828 §2(2) applies §28(3)–(5) to extension decisions. The application article keeps its non-numeric, decision-specific appeal wording, avoiding overgeneralization.
- The official [Active Social Policy Act record](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/accn/A20260079329) supports conditional, case-assessed cash assistance, including means and residence/work conditions. The social-assistance article does not promise eligibility or a fixed payment. Some Ukraine-specific details still rely partly on an undated SIRI FAQ identified in the historical addendum; the article keeps those claims conditional and directs readers to the authority for an individual assessment.
- Current official Immigration Service guidance supports the distinctions among section 1/section 2 applicants, current permit holders and applicants awaiting a decision. The employment articles make work rights conditional on permit/status and biometric requirements where applicable. Healthcare, education and benefit wording likewise avoids blanket eligibility claims.

## Technical release readiness

Repository evidence shows startup loads the JSON catalogs before the app is built and validates domain fields, uniqueness, country/category/source relationships, required translations and localization coverage. An invalid catalog or missing required translation fails startup. Country/category pages are data-driven; search and comparison use the same catalog, and all eight entries have Verified translations. Razor renders article text as encoded text and source links as URLs with `noopener noreferrer`; the categories route renders linked sources and localized status/date metadata.

Private Alpha authentication runs before page, static-asset and language endpoints in private mode; the middleware returns 401 without valid credentials, sets `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, and rejects direct non-HTTPS production requests. The root Docker image is built and smoke-tested in CI using synthetic private credentials. On the exact audited SHA, GitHub Actions run [38810321748](https://github.com/Alexandra-St/RefugeeNavigatorEurope/actions/runs/38810321748) completed successfully: Release build had zero warnings/errors, all 347 .NET tests and five deployment-script tests passed, Docker built, public/private smoke checks passed, and missing/invalid private configuration failed closed.

Repository documentation requires a real operator to confirm the Render Free plan and secret settings. This audit did not access Render, so the actual service plan, Auto-Deploy setting, environment values and live HTTPS/authentication behavior remain unverified. CI simulates configuration; it does not establish real Render edge behavior.
