**Research date:** 15 November 2026

**Scope:** Finland's EU temporary-protection end date, Migri's stated Finnish permit validity and extension instructions, and the domestic validity of physical residence-permit cards. Official sources only; no production records were changed.

## Research question

What date applies to the EU temporary-protection scheme, what validity does Migri currently state for a Finnish temporary-protection residence permit and card, whether the holder must apply or order a new card, and what can safely be said about the 2028 extension rollout?

## Established facts

1. **EU scheme:** Council Decision (EU) 2026/1912 extends the EU temporary-protection scheme until **4 March 2028**. This EU date is not an individual Finnish permit expiry date or a physical-card expiry date.
2. **Finnish permit:** Migri's central guidance and its 5 August 2026 customer bulletin state that current Finnish temporary-protection residence permits are valid until **4 March 2027**. The central guidance also lists separate expiry situations tied to another Finnish permit; an individual permit record and any separate expiry decision remain controlling for that person.
3. **Extension application:** Migri's Information Bank directly says the holder does not need to apply for the extension and that Migri will extend the permit automatically. The 5 August bulletin says further instructions will be provided when Migri starts extending permits for the 2028 period. The safe statement is therefore that **no separate extension application is stated to be required**, while holders should follow Migri's later rollout instructions and confirm their own permit record. The public general guidance does not show the date or completion status of every individual permit update.
4. **Physical card through 2027:** Migri says cards printed with 4 March 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 expiry dates remain valid **in Finland** through 4 March 2027 if the underlying permit is still valid. The printed date and domestic validity rule are different. The card's personal details must be current.
5. **New card:** Migri says a holder does not need to order a new card merely because Migri extends the temporary-protection permit, provided the existing card details are current. Changed personal details, a lost/stolen card, or another card-specific case may require a separate card application. This is a card rule, not proof that the residence permit itself has been extended.
6. **After 4 March 2027 / travel:** The sources reviewed do not establish the effective date of each holder's 2028 permit-record update, whether an existing physical card can be used in Finland after 4 March 2027, or how the domestic card rule applies to travel outside Finland. Do not infer those points from the EU scheme end date or from the older-card rule through 2027.

## Renewal / extension mechanics

- **Permit:** Migri's Information Bank says no holder application is needed for the extension and that Migri will extend permits automatically. The central page and 5 August customer bulletin give the present validity date as 4 March 2027 and say Migri will publish further instructions when the 2028 permit/card extension begins. These statements support a qualified “no separate application; monitor Migri's instructions and personal status” rule. They do not show whether a particular holder's permit record has already been updated.
- **Card:** No new card is needed solely because Migri extends the permit, if the current card details are correct. A different card application may be necessary when personal data changes, a card is lost/stolen, or another specific card problem applies.
- **Printed expiry vs domestic use:** For the listed 2023–2026 print dates, Migri recognizes the card in Finland through 4 March 2027 provided the permit itself remains valid. This limited Finnish use rule must not be generalized to travel or to a period after 4 March 2027.
- **2028 instructions:** The live Migri customer bulletin still directs holders to await separate instructions for the start of the 2028 permit/card extension. The exact date of the individual record update and the use of an existing physical card after 4 March 2027 remain unstated in the reviewed general guidance.

## Safe Compare assertion

**EN:** The EU temporary-protection scheme runs through 4 March 2028; Migri says Finnish permits are currently valid until 4 March 2027 and holders do not need to apply for the extension. Cards printed with a 2023–2026 expiry remain valid in Finland until 4 March 2027 if the underlying permit is valid and card details are current; Migri says a new card is not needed when it extends the permit and will provide further instructions for the 2028 rollout.

## Required qualification

Do not treat 4 March 2028 as an individual Finnish permit or card expiry. Do not promise that an individual's electronic permit record has already been extended, that a card with an earlier printed date can be used for travel or after 4 March 2027, or that no action of any kind could be needed. The direct Migri rule is that no separate permit-extension application is required; the holder should follow Migri's later instructions and check their own record. Card replacement is separate from permit extension and may be required for changed/missing or lost card details.
