# Finland — Finding a Job

Research checked: 2026-11-11  
Baseline: `4b347dbdf8cbdabcc6f649634201d9f91a4a69bc`  
Scope: exactly `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, `employment-assistance-conditions`.

This is an evidence record, not applicant-facing article text. It covers practical job search, public employment services and employment-related assistance for a person under Finnish temporary protection (TP). It does not cover pay, employment rights, contracts or residence transitions.

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS** for a practical article explaining official vacancy channels, the current local employment-service system, and conditional job-search support.

Job Market Finland (JMF) has public vacancy browsing; Work in Finland offers a narrower English-speaking vacancy collection; EURES provides EU/EEA mobility search and advice. None is shown to contain every Finnish opening. Since 1 January 2025, TE Offices have closed and municipal employment areas are responsible for local public employment services. JMF remains the national digital platform; KEHA Centre has national system, information and support functions, while the local employment authority handles individual job-search registration and service assessment [FI-JOB-S01–S09].

A TP holder with a granted Finnish TP residence permit and work right not restricted to a named employer can generally register as a jobseeker. A person whose TP application is pending may have a right to work from registration of the TP application, but that is not the same as eligibility for jobseeker registration: the current Act generally requires a granted residence permit for a third-country national. The Ukraine-specific JMF page says to register after the residence-permit decision and offers general advice before then [FI-JOB-S10–S14, S31]. No Finnish personal identity code or strong e-ID does not by itself close the route: contact the responsible local employment services/authority. The official national guide does not give a single exhaustive manual-registration document checklist [FI-JOB-S11–S13].

The evidence supports descriptions of assessment, employment planning, career guidance, coaching, labour-market training, integration services, work trial and employer pay subsidy, but these depend on service need, eligibility, local availability and authority decisions. There is no universal promise of a course place, Finnish/Swedish lessons, coaching or subsidy. Jobseeker registration is separate from a benefit application and payment decision [FI-JOB-S15–S22, S27].

## Current public employment-service structure

- **TE Offices:** all ceased operation on 31 December 2024; employment services transferred to municipal employment areas on 1 January 2025. “Go to the TE Office” is legacy wording for the 2026 system [FI-JOB-S01].
- **Local employment authority/employment area:** municipality or municipal consortium that has responsibility for public employment services in its region. It receives jobseeker registration, assesses service need, agrees employment plans and provides/localizes services [FI-JOB-S02, S10–S13].
- **Municipality and kotikunta:** the Act says the jobseeker's actual reported address is the primary presumed residence for applying the Act; the registered kotikunta is separately used for the kotikunta concept. Section 27 registration conditions require address/contact details but do not list having a kotikunta as a condition [FI-JOB-S02].
- **No kotikunta:** no reviewed national source establishes that absence of kotikunta automatically bars §27 jobseeker registration. Nor did research establish which employment area must serve every TP holder without kotikunta, or a special nationwide intake process. Give the person a safe direction: contact the local employment services for their actual address and explain that kotikunta is not yet registered; ask which authority will handle the case. Do not say “no kotikunta means no employment services” or promise a particular area [FI-JOB-S02, S06–S07, S11–S14, S39].
- **Job Market Finland:** national public portal for vacancies, information and digital dealings. Public vacancy search is available without logging in. E-services/login are channels for official matters; a profile, account, vacancy browsing or alert alone does not register a jobseeker [FI-JOB-S09, S23, S26].
- **KEHA Centre:** national digital/data, information, analysis, mobility and support functions. It is distinct from both local employment authority and Migri; individual local service assessment remains local [FI-JOB-S04–S05, S08].
- **Municipal integration information:** municipalities have a general duty to offer need-based guidance and information regardless of migration reason and duration. That does not amount to a guarantee of a kotikunta, integration plan, language course or jobseeker registration [FI-JOB-S07, S39].

A profile obligation does not guarantee matching or placement. |

## Benefits boundary

**Jobseeker registration does not itself establish unemployment-benefit entitlement or payment.** The employment authority/KEHA provides a labour-policy statement; the person must apply separately to Kela or the unemployment fund, which handles its own eligibility and payment decision [FI-JOB-S27, S35]. This research does not establish an individual TP holder’s eligibility, benefit amount, residence-based benefit rights or waiting period.

## Qualifications and regulated professions

Qualification recognition depends on the job and purpose. In unregulated occupations, employers or education institutions may assess foreign qualifications; regulated professions require the decision/eligibility of the responsible competent authority (which is not always EDUFI) [FI-JOB-S33–S34]. TP work authorization, a jobseeker registration, or JMF support does not itself recognize a qualification or grant a professional licence. No profession-specific requirements are researched here.

## Required distinctions

- TP work right ≠ jobseeker registration [FI-JOB-S03, S10, S14, S38].
- Jobseeker registration ≠ unemployment benefit award/payment [FI-JOB-S27, S35].
- JMF login/account, vacancy browsing, alerts or job applicant profile ≠ formal jobseeker registration [FI-JOB-S09, S13, S21, S23, S25–S26].
- Municipality/employment authority ≠ Migri; KEHA’s national role ≠ local authority’s individual service assessment [FI-JOB-S02, S04, S38].
- Kotikunta ≠ TP status, strong e-ID or Finnish personal identity code [FI-JOB-S02, S06, S11–S12].
- Training or integration plan ≠ guaranteed admission/place or universal language-course entitlement [FI-JOB-S06–S07, S15, S17].
- Work trial ≠ employment relationship; pay subsidy ≠ cash paid to a jobseeker [FI-JOB-S19–S20].
- Qualification recognition/licence ≠ work authorization [FI-JOB-S33–S34, S38].
- EURES search/advice ≠ Finnish registration or work/residence permission [FI-JOB-S28–S29].

## Open gaps

- **No kotikunta:** §27 does not list kotikunta among registration conditions, and §10 distinguishes actual reported address from registered kotikunta; however, national guidance does not identify the responsible employment area or precise service process for every TP holder without kotikunta. Confirm with local employment services [FI-JOB-S02, S06–S07, S39].
- **Manual registration documents:** national sources give the information required and the local office route, but not a complete TP-specific checklist for a person without Finnish ID/e-ID [FI-JOB-S02, S11–S13, S32].
- **Local authority:** national instructions point to the employment area pages; contact mode and local services vary [FI-JOB-S11–S13, S16–S20].
- **Ukraine-specific service:** the Ukraine page was updated 2025-07-18 and contains now-obsolete TE-office/telephone information. General pre-permit advice remains stated, but current capacity, response times and dedicated programme status are not established [FI-JOB-S14, S36–S37].
- **Language support/interpreters:** no nationwide Ukrainian interpreter or Ukrainian-speaking adviser availability was established. Russian national phone support ended 2026-09-30 [FI-JOB-S37].
- **Integration/language training:** individual assessment and potential services are clear, but TP-specific course eligibility, local supply, guaranteed free Finnish/Swedish lessons and guaranteed places are not [FI-JOB-S06–S07, S15, S17].
- **Work trial/pay subsidy:** general current conditions are documented, but whether a particular TP holder/job qualifies and local approval are individual authority decisions [FI-JOB-S19–S20].
- **Benefits:** no individual eligibility/amount research is included; Kela/fund makes separate benefit decisions [FI-JOB-S27, S35].
- **Vacancy completeness:** neither JMF nor Work in Finland claims every vacancy; not all jobs are publicly advertised [FI-JOB-S23–S24, S28, S31].
- **Languages and application mechanics:** no exhaustive proof that all JMF pages/listings are available in all three national/English languages; applications are governed by each vacancy’s instructions [FI-JOB-S23–S24, S28–S29].

## Editorial exclusions

Future article must not say, without new evidence:

- “Go to the TE Office” as a current route.
- Job Market Finland or Work in Finland contains every Finnish vacancy.
- A JMF account, profile, saved search or vacancy browsing registers the user as unemployed.
- Every TP holder is automatically a registered jobseeker.
- Pending TP work right alone ensures jobseeker registration.
- A municipality of residence is always required for jobseeker registration, or that its absence automatically bars registration.
- A person cannot register without Finnish online banking/e-ID.
- Registration guarantees unemployment benefit.
- Every registered TP holder receives free Finnish/Swedish classes, integration training, coaching, or a job.
- Work trial is an ordinary paid employment contract.
- Pay subsidy is paid to the jobseeker.
- A 2025 Ukraine page’s TE-office hours/Russian phone number remains current in 2026.
- EURES gives Finnish work authorization or jobseeker status.
- TP work permission replaces regulated-profession recognition/licensing.
