## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- Benefit-law reform remains pending in the source register and may change which authority handles health cover for some arrivals. This draft is incomplete for status-specific coverage until enactment/status is rechecked.
- Confirm current state/municipal medical-card procedures and whether the selected reader’s benefit authority is Jobcenter, Sozialamt or AsylbLG authority.
- Do not promise universal free care, a universal insurance card, interpretation or psychotherapy availability.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
