# Accessing Healthcare in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** The route to healthcare depends on residence status, insurance and local procedures.

## RULES

Healthcare coverage in Germany depends on your legal and insurance status and, where relevant, the social-benefit route. People with section 24 status may have different routes, including statutory health insurance or support through the Sozialamt; conditions and coverage are not the same for everyone. Do not assume all care is free.

## TASKS

Find out which authority or insurer handles your coverage, how to obtain a card or proof of entitlement and whether you may have costs to pay. For routine care, find a doctor and ask whether they accept your insurance. Prescriptions and co-payments depend on insurance and treatment.

## PLACES

For ordinary care, contact a doctor. For urgent but non-life-threatening problems outside normal office hours, 116117 can direct you to medical on-call care; call 112 for life-threatening emergencies. Psychosocial services and the languages available vary by region.

## NEXT STEPS

Check your situation with your Krankenkasse, Sozialamt or Jobcenter, depending on which institution handles your case. As of 15 October 2026, the Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz had not been published as a completed law. Its possible effects on benefits and healthcare coverage remain a publication blocker.
