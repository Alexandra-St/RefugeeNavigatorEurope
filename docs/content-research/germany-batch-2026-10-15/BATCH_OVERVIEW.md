## Final findings

- EU temporary protection extends through 4 March 2028. The currently consolidated UkraineAufenthFGV §§2–3, last amended 22 October 2025, provide through 4 March 2027 for specified §24 permits valid on 1 February 2026. These are different legal scopes. The inaccessible Germany4Ukraine Labour Law page and its indexed snippet are not used as evidence; do not infer individual validity or work permission.
- The corrected EU military carve-out is for continuous protection in the same Member State by/after 4 August 2026. Germany4Ukraine’s notice uses broader “already living in the EU” wording. UkraineAufenthÜV §2(3), effective for covered entries from 9 October 2026, is a separate German condition on the 90-day no-title exemption. The drafts keep these rules separate and do not invent eligibility decisions or proof lists.
- BMAS still showed the Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz as in process and not published on 16 October 2026; Bundestag DIP listed “Überwiesen”. It remains a proposal, not current law, so the bill alone does not block otherwise accurate current-law guidance.
- Education drafts now include the SGB VIII §24(1) parent-condition for under-one support and the §24(4) possibility of Land-authorized facility closures of up to four weeks during school holidays.
- All eight matrices and 24 UK/EN/RU drafts were reviewed for claim traceability and equivalent legal meaning. No article is approved or marked Verified.
