# Finding Accommodation in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Initial accommodation, private renting and help with costs depend on the Land and local circumstances.

## RULES

After arrival, authorities may arrange initial accommodation or allocation; the process depends on the responsible Land and municipality. This does not guarantee a particular home. Renting privately is a separate process that can involve affordability checks, a contract, a deposit and other costs.

## TASKS

Before signing a rental agreement, check the total cost, deposit, contract term and termination rules. If you need help with housing costs, ask the responsible authority in advance whether approval is required and what documents to submit. Do not rely on a payment before an official decision.

## PLACES

For initial accommodation, contact the local reception point or authority responsible for allocation. For housing-cost support, contact a Jobcenter or Sozialamt only if that institution handles your case. Germany4Ukraine gives a general overview but does not replace local rules.

## NEXT STEPS

After moving, check whether you must register your address with the Bürgeramt/Bürgerbüro. The general federal rule is registration within two weeks of moving in, subject to applicable exceptions. Requirements and housing availability vary locally. Older portal pages on social benefits may not reflect changes from 2025–2026.
