# Finding Accommodation in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** How to approach initial accommodation, private renting, housing-cost support and address registration without assuming a local entitlement.

## RULES

Initial reception or temporary accommodation for people seeking state support is organized through Land and municipal systems. The route depends on the place and the person’s situation; it does not guarantee a particular dwelling. Private renting is separate. Housing-cost support is also scheme-specific: SGB II §22 and SGB XII §35 refer to actual costs within applicable limits, while AsylbLG has its own rules. A residence permit alone does not promise payment of rent or a deposit.

## TASKS

If you need an initial place, use the reception or allocation instructions for the Land/municipality where you arrive. Keep identity and family documents and any registration or allocation paper. If you arrange a private room or apartment, keep the written offer, full rent and utility breakdown, deposit, start date and draft contract. Before signing, ask the office handling your benefits whether it must review the rent or give written approval; local limits and required documents differ. Never assume a verbal estimate is a payment decision.

## PLACES

Germany4Ukraine links to federal-state housing information. For a local shelter, accommodation allocation, housing-benefit or tenant-advice route, select the Land and municipality where you will live. If benefits are involved, use the authority named in your written decision—Jobcenter, Sozialamt or the AsylbLG authority—and ask which scheme covers your case. The pending Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz could change some routes; as checked 16 October 2026 it was still a bill under parliamentary consideration, not current law.

## NEXT STEPS

After moving into a dwelling, check the Bürgeramt/Bürgerbüro registration requirement. Federal law generally requires registration within two weeks, with exceptions in the statute; the local office explains appointment and document requirements. Keep your Meldebestätigung (registration confirmation), as later applications may ask for proof of address. Housing, deposit and support procedures vary by Land and municipality; the national portal is a starting point, not a promise of a local place or benefit.
