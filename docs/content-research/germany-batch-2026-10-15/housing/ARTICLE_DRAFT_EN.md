# Finding Accommodation in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** How to approach initial accommodation, private renting, housing-cost support and address registration without assuming a local entitlement.

## RULES

Initial reception for asylum applicants follows the Asylum Act; temporary protection under §24 AufenthG has a separate distribution route. For people seeking temporary protection, §24(3) allows distribution among Länder and §24(4) allows the Land to assign a place. Under §24(5), there is no entitlement to choose a Land or place, and a person must live at the assigned place. A §24 assignment decision ends when the residence permit is issued; follow any decision you receive and ask the immigration authority before moving while it applies. Neither route guarantees a particular dwelling, and live shelter capacity is local. Private renting is separate. Housing-cost support depends on the benefit scheme: SGB II §22 and SGB XII §35 recognize actual reasonable accommodation and heating costs under local rules; AsylbLG §3 has its own necessary-cost route and may use cash or in-kind support. Since 1 July 2026, SGB II and SGB XII cap accommodation costs during the one-year waiting period at 1.5 times the locally calculated abstract reasonableness limit, with hardship exceptions. Heating is assessed separately. A §24 permit alone does not promise rent or deposit payment.

## TASKS

If you need an initial place, follow the instructions for your route and the Land/municipality where you arrive. If an authority gives you an assignment decision under §24, follow it and ask the immigration authority before moving while it remains in force. Keep identity and family documents and any registration or allocation paper. If you arrange a private room or apartment, keep the written offer, rent and utilities breakdown, deposit, start date and draft contract. Before signing or moving, ask the office handling your benefits in writing which rent and heating costs it will recognize and what prior assurance is required. Under SGB II, assurance for the new housing costs should be requested from the authority responsible where you plan to live; moving and housing-search costs may require prior assurance from the current authority, while a deposit generally requires prior assurance from the new authority and is usually a loan. SGB XII also requires advance notice or consent for certain costs of a move, deposit or costs above the reasonable limit. AsylbLG procedures depend on the responsible local authority. A verbal estimate is not a payment decision.

## PLACES

Use official Land and municipal pages for reception, allocation, shelter capacity, housing benefit and tenant advice. The responsible local office can tell you where to report and whether a place is currently available. If benefits are involved, use the authority named in your written decision—Jobcenter, Sozialamt or AsylbLG authority—and ask which scheme applies. The Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz remains a bill as of 16 November 2026; its proposed §24 benefit-route changes are not current law. Separately, the SGB II housing-cost changes effective 1 July 2026 are already part of the current rules.

## NEXT STEPS

After moving into a dwelling, check the Bürgeramt/Bürgerbüro address-registration requirement. Federal law generally requires registration within two weeks, subject to statutory exceptions; the local office explains appointment and document requirements. Keep your Meldebestätigung (registration confirmation), as later applications may ask for proof of address. Housing availability, cost limits, deposit decisions and support procedures vary by Land and municipality; no national portal or residence permit guarantees a local place or payment.
