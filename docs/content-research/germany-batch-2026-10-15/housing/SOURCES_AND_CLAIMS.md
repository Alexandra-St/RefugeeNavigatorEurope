## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- Germany4Ukraine housing pages include post-June-2022 Bürgergeld descriptions and local examples. The current benefits bill may alter the route for some later arrivals; do not reuse those eligibility statements as current universal rules.
- Add locality-specific emergency shelter, rental support, deposit and tenant advice links only after choosing a Land/municipality scope. Do not generalize a city page nationally.
- State which housing costs require advance approval only after the current applicable benefit scheme is determined.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
