# Work Authorization and Employee Rights

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Check the permission stated in your document, employment terms and official routes for advice or complaints.

## RULES

Permission to work depends on the legal route and your individual document. Section 4a AufenthG provides that permission or restrictions are recorded on the residence title or an accompanying document. If an application is pending, a Fiktionsbescheinigung may contain a separate employment entry. Do not start work based only on a general website description.

## TASKS

Before starting, ask for a written contract or confirmation of key terms and keep payslips and working-time records. Check pay, hours and duties. The general statutory minimum wage from 1 January 2026 is €13.90 gross per hour; check the current rule and any job-specific exceptions before relying on this figure.

## PLACES

Contact the Ausländerbehörde about permission. Fair Integration provides free advice on labour and social law for people with a migration background; languages and availability vary by location. Zoll accepts reports concerning enforcement violations, while a claim for unpaid wages generally has to be pursued through civil channels.

## NEXT STEPS

Working-time and employee protections are separate from immigration permission. Germany’s Working Time Act generally sets an eight-hour workday; extension to ten hours is possible only if the statutory averaging conditions are met. The section 24 permit-duration source conflict remains unresolved. Recheck the minimum wage and exceptions before publication.
