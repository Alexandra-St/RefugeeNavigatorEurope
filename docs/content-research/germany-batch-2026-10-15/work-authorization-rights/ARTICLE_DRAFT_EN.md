# Work Authorization and Employee Rights

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Check the work entry in your own residence document, understand the basic pay and hours rules, and know where to seek advice.

## RULES

Work permission is linked to your legal status and document, not simply to Ukrainian nationality or having applied for protection. Section 4a AufenthG requires a title to show whether work is allowed and what limits apply. Read the residence card and any supplementary sheet. A Fiktionsbescheinigung can carry its own work entry; a pending application alone is not enough to assume permission.

EU temporary protection is extended to 4 March 2028, but German sources conflict about the continuation date of certain §24 permits. The exact work end date must therefore be checked against the person’s document and current authoritative rule; this guide does not give a universal expiry date.

## TASKS

Before the first shift, check whether your document permits the role, employer and hours. Keep a copy of the document, written contract or confirmation of essential terms, job description, schedules and payslips. Ask the employer how gross pay, hours, leave and probation are recorded. If your occupation is regulated, confirm the required professional licence separately from immigration permission.

As checked on 15 October 2026, the general statutory minimum wage was €13.90 gross per hour from 1 January 2026. Exceptions apply to some groups and a sector may set a higher floor; recheck the current amount before relying on it. Under the Working Time Act, the general daily limit is eight hours, with extension to ten only when the statutory average is met.

## PLACES

The Ausländerbehörde decides residence-document questions. Fair Integration offers free labour and social-law advice for people with migration experience; check the local centre for languages and appointment availability. German Customs’ FKS enforces certain undeclared-work and wage violations. Reporting to Customs does not itself recover unpaid wages; advice on a wage claim is a separate step.

## NEXT STEPS

If the document wording is unclear, obtain a written clarification before starting or changing work. If an application is pending, use the exact work entry in the Fiktionsbescheinigung and the conditions of §81(5a), rather than assuming all pending cases permit work. Recheck the §24 extension conflict, the current minimum wage and any sector exception immediately before publication. This article does not determine an individual right to work.
