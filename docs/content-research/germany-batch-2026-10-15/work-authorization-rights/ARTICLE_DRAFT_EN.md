# Work Authorization and Employee Rights

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Check the work entry in your own residence document, understand the basic pay and hours rules, and know where to seek advice.

## RULES

Work permission is linked to your legal status and document, not simply to Ukrainian nationality or having applied for protection. Section 4a AufenthG requires a title to show whether work is allowed and what limits apply. Read the residence card and any supplementary sheet. A Fiktionsbescheinigung can carry its own work entry; a pending application alone is not enough to assume permission.

The current EU temporary-protection period runs through 4 March 2027. A further extension through 4 March 2028 has been adopted and applies from 5 March 2027. Separately, current UkraineAufenthFGV §2 continues specified §24 permits valid on 1 February 2026 through 4 March 2027, subject to the regulation’s conditions. These dates concern different legal statuses; neither date alone establishes the validity or work authorization of an individual document. Check the work entry and dates on your own document and ask the competent Ausländerbehörde for written clarification if needed.

## TASKS

Before the first shift, check whether your document permits the role, employer and hours. Keep a copy of the document, written contract or confirmation of essential terms, job description, schedules and payslips. Ask the employer how gross pay, hours, leave and probation are recorded. If your occupation is regulated, confirm the required professional licence separately from immigration permission.

On 16 November 2026, the general statutory minimum wage is €13.90 gross per hour, effective from 1 January 2026. The legally adopted rate will rise to €14.60 from 1 January 2027. Statutory exceptions apply to some groups, and a sector may set a higher floor; check the rate and coverage for the work period. Under the Working Time Act, the general daily limit is eight hours, with extension to ten only when the statutory average is met.

## PLACES

The Ausländerbehörde decides residence-document questions. Since 1 January 2026, Fair Integration provides free, anonymous labour- and work-related social-law advice to refugees and migrants from outside the EU (third-country nationals), in multiple languages through centres across Germany. It does not advise on professional recognition; check local language and appointment availability. German Customs’ FKS enforces certain undeclared-work and wage violations. Reporting to Customs does not itself recover unpaid wages; advice on a wage claim is a separate step.

## NEXT STEPS

If the document wording is unclear, obtain written clarification before starting or changing work. If an application is pending, check the exact work entry in the Fiktionsbescheinigung and which part of §81 applies; a pending application does not always permit work. Section 81(5a) covers specified employment titles in Chapter 2, Sections 3 and 4; it is not a blanket work permission for a §24 application in Section 5. Because work permission depends on your exact status and document and wage rules can change, check current official requirements and any sector-specific exceptions before relying on them. This article does not determine an individual right to work.
