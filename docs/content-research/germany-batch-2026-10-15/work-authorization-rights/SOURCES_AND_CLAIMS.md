## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- The exact §24 permit-continuation date remains inconsistent across the current consolidated ordinance and Germany4Ukraine guidance. The article gives no universal date, separates EU temporary-protection duration from German title validity, and directs readers to check their document and obtain written authority confirmation. Preserve that qualification.
- Recheck the minimum wage, exceptions and applicable collective/sector rates immediately before public release.
- Check the exact residence document and provisional certificate; do not interpret a pending application as automatic work authorization.
