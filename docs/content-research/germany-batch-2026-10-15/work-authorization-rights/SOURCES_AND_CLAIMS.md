## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- **BLOCKED for blanket §24 duration.** Official sources disagree on whether permit validity runs to 4 March 2027 or 4 March 2028. This draft must not state a universal authorization end date.
- Confirm the exact wording of each reader's residence title/Fiktionsbescheinigung. Do not infer work permission from a submitted application alone.
- Recheck minimum-wage amount after 2026 and review exceptions/sectoral minima before any publication.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
