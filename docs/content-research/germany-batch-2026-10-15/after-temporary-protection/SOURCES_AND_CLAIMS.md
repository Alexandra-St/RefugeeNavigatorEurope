## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- This is a map of possible legal routes, not a full eligibility guide. A German immigration lawyer/editor should validate change-of-purpose admissibility, deadlines, livelihood conditions, and any asylum/family routes before publication.
- Do not describe any title as an automatic successor to §24 or advise readers which application to file.
- Coordinate the end-of-protection date with the separate national permit-extension conflict in article 1.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
