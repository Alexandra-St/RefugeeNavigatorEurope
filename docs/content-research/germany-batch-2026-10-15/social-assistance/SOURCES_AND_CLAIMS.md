## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- **BLOCKED: proposed benefits transition.** BMAS checked 15 October 2026 says the Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz is in process and the completed law has not been published. Before publication, inspect the current Bundestag/DIP stage, any enacted text, commencement and transition clauses, and the resulting Jobcenter/Sozialamt responsibilities.
- Recheck related health-insurance and medical-care amendments; the benefit track can affect health cover and must be harmonized with the Healthcare article.
- Do not insert benefit amounts from older portal content without a date-specific official source and confirmation the scheme applies.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
