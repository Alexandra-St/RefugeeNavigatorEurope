# Financial and Social Assistance

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** The support route depends on your documents, residence, income and circumstances; an official decision is needed for each claim.

## RULES

Current law has different routes. SGB II (§7) covers people who meet its conditions, including age, ability to work, need and residence rules. Section 74 provides a specific route for some people who applied for §24 protection and have a corresponding Fiktionsbescheinigung after identity registration; it is not a route for every pending applicant. AsylbLG §1 covers specified groups, including some people who have asked for §24 protection but do not yet hold the permit or qualifying provisional certificate. Other situations may fall under SGB XII. Nationality alone does not decide which scheme applies.

The Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz proposes changes to the routes between SGB II, SGB XII and AsylbLG for specified §24 groups. One criterion is first issuance after 31 March 2025 of the relevant §24 permit or a §81(3) Fiktionsbescheinigung; the bill includes exceptions for some earlier documents and transition rules. It would also require specified able-bodied, non-employed recipients to seek work promptly and could assign them to an Arbeitsgelegenheit under AsylbLG. The draft excludes people under 18, those still subject to full-time compulsory schooling, attending general school or in school-based training, and people who have reached the standard retirement age. A documented important reason would guard against assignment to an Arbeitsgelegenheit; it is separate from the proposed job-search duty. On 16 October 2026 BMAS described it as a government bill and Bundestag DIP listed it as “Überwiesen” (referred for further consideration); the records checked showed no enacted law or commencement date. These are proposals, not current duties.

If a transfer between schemes is enacted, the applicable healthcare and housing-cost rules may change because SGB II, SGB XII and AsylbLG have separate conditions. The bill expressly changes health provisions, but it does not by itself establish an individual entitlement to insurance or housing costs. Check the enacted text and your written decision; see also the Healthcare and Housing drafts.

## TASKS

Start by checking your residence document, registration papers and any Fiktionsbescheinigung. Apply to the authority responsible for the scheme that fits your situation: Jobcenter for an SGB II claim, Sozialamt or the local AsylbLG authority for their routes. Keep identity and residence documents, household details, income and bank information, rent or accommodation papers and health-insurance evidence ready; the office will tell you which items are required and whether translations are needed. Submit the requested application and keep the receipt, copies and written decision. Do not assume a particular amount or housing payment before a decision.

## PLACES

A special family payment is Kindergeld, administered by the Familienkasse. For a parent from Ukraine, the BA says the applicant must live in Germany, hold a §24 permit that allows work for at least six months, and have a child living in Germany, the EU, EEA or Switzerland. Actual employment is not required under the simplified rule described by the BA. The BA asks for the parent’s and child’s §24 documents and proof of where the child lives. Submit form KG1 and an Anlage Kind to the Familienkasse responsible for your address; the BA provides forms and a service finder. This is a conditional application route, not a guaranteed award. Other family payments are outside this review.

## NEXT STEPS

Read the written decision to see the scheme, period, conditions and appeal information. Report changes when your benefit rules require it. If the Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz advances, check the final text, document-issuance trigger and exceptions, commencement, transition and health-care effects before describing who moves between systems. Align any update with the Healthcare, Housing and Finding Employment articles. The date in a proposal is not a rule until the legislation is enacted and in force.
