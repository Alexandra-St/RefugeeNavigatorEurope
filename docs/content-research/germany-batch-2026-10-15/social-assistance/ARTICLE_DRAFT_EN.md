# Financial and Social Assistance

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** The applicable support depends on residence status, arrival date, income and the responsible authority.

## RULES

Different German support schemes may be administered by a Jobcenter, Sozialamt or authority responsible for asylum-seeker benefits. Eligibility does not depend on nationality alone: the residence title or document, other legal conditions, income, household and individual assessment matter. This article does not determine which benefit a particular person can receive.

## TASKS

Contact the authority responsible for your situation and ask which application and evidence are required. Keep decisions and report changes when your scheme requires it. Until you receive a written decision, do not assume a particular amount, housing-cost payment or family benefit is guaranteed.

## PLACES

Jobcenters, Sozialämter and AsylbLG authorities have different roles. Current SGB II provisions cover specified groups; section 74 sets separate conditions for certain section 24 applicants with a Fiktionsbescheinigung. The authority must check all statutory requirements, including status and identity evidence.

## NEXT STEPS

The Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz bill proposed changes for some people arriving from 1 April 2025, but on 15 October 2026 BMAS still listed it as in process. Do not treat the proposal as current law. Before publication, recheck any final law, commencement and transition provisions, and the effect on healthcare coverage. Family-benefit rules have not been researched in this draft and need a separate official-source review.
