# Applying for Temporary Protection and Extending Residence Permission

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** How to distinguish registration, an application under section 24 AufenthG and a residence document—and where to check conflicting extension dates.

## RULES

Section 24 of Germany’s Residence Act (AufenthG) is the national basis for a residence permit connected with EU temporary protection. Eligibility depends on the person’s category—for example, nationality and residence before 24 February 2022, a qualifying family relationship or a previous protection status in Ukraine. Displacement from Ukraine alone does not establish eligibility.

## TASKS

After arrival, ask the local authority about initial registration, any allocation process, address registration and applying to the Ausländerbehörde (immigration authority). These are distinct administrative steps, although a local process may combine them. Follow the instructions and deadlines given by the competent authority.

## PLACES

The competent Ausländerbehörde considers the application and issues the residence document. While a decision is pending, it may issue a Fiktionsbescheinigung, a temporary certificate of legal status. Read your own document: permission to work can depend on the legal route and the authority’s entry. Do not assume every applicant has the same rights.

## NEXT STEPS

Under Article 2 of the EU Decision, temporary protection granted from 5 August 2026 is limited to people who satisfy their military obligations in Ukraine and, where applicable, present proof. The rule does not apply to people who enjoyed temporary protection in a Member State on or before 4 August 2026 and continuously retain it after that date. German procedure and evidence requirements need confirmation. The EU extended temporary protection through 4 March 2028. However, the current text of Germany’s UkraineAufenthFGV gives 4 March 2027 for specified permits, while Germany4Ukraine pages show different dates. This conflict does not establish the end date printed on an individual document. Official reconciliation is needed before publication; check with your Ausländerbehörde for your case.
