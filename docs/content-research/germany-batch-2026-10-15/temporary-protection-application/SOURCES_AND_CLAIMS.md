## Claim review

| Claim ID | Material claim and source passage/section | Status | Conditions, exceptions and limits |

| TP01 | §24 permit is issued to a person granted EU temporary protection who declares willingness to be received in Germany; §24(2) exclusion also applies. S01 §24(1)–(2). | SUPPORTED | Does not make every person displaced from Ukraine eligible. |
| TP02 | Relevant protection categories depend on EU eligibility and case facts; Germany4Ukraine describes nationality, prior residence/protection and qualifying family categories. S02 eligibility; S03. | SUPPORTED | Do not reduce this to “all Ukrainians”; category-specific exclusions and evidence matter. |
| TP03 | Registration, address registration, allocation, §24 application and title issuance are distinct; qualifying UkraineAufenthÜV entrants may stay without a title for 90 days from first entry and obtain a title in Germany. S02–S03; S09 §§1–3. | SUPPORTED | §2 applies only to specified categories entering by 4 Dec 2027 and not already protected in another Member State; local steps vary. Not a §24 grant. |
| TP04 | A Fiktionsbescheinigung may document pending status; work permission depends on statutory route and the actual document entry. S03; S07; S08 §§81(3)–(5a). | PARTIALLY SUPPORTED | §81 effects differ by lawful status and timely filing; do not generalize work rights. |
| TP05 | EU TP runs through 4 Mar 2028, while UkraineAufenthFGV §2 and the registration page say 4 Mar 2027 for specified titles; Germany4Ukraine labour page says 2028. S04–S07. | CONFLICTED | Material unresolved conflict on individual German permit continuation; publication blocker. Do not infer a personal expiry date. |
| TP06 | EU Article 2 applies to new grants from 5 Aug 2026 and includes a continuity carve-out only for people who held protection in a given Member State by 4 Aug and continuously retain it there. S05 Art 2; S06 corrigendum. | SUPPORTED | Proof is required where applicable; decision’s exact text is narrower than a general “already living in the EU” description. |
| TP07 | Germany’s §2(3) UkraineAufenthÜV from 9 Oct 2026 removes §2(1)–(2) no-title exemption for entrants subject to Ukrainian military duties unless they prove compliance. S09 §2(3). | SUPPORTED | This is a German visa-waiver/title-exemption rule, not the EU TP grant test and not a statement that entry itself is denied. The ordinance specifies no universal evidence list. |
| TP08 | Germany4Ukraine says the EU military rule does not apply to people already living in the EU; the EU corrigendum says the person must have continuously retained TP in the same Member State by/after 4 Aug. S05–S06; S10. | CONFLICTED | Do not extend the EU carve-out to every person physically present in any Member State. Movement between states and German implementation need legal clarification. |

## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- **BLOCKED:** the 2027/2028 German permit-continuation conflict remains unresolved between current law text and portal statements.
- **BLOCKED:** clarify how the EU continuity carve-out applies when a person moves between Member States; Germany4Ukraine’s wording is broader than the corrigendum.
- Confirm German administrative evidence/process for new EU military-condition cases. The law and portal do not provide one comprehensive document checklist.
- Keep the §2(3) German no-title exemption condition distinct from EU eligibility for temporary protection.
