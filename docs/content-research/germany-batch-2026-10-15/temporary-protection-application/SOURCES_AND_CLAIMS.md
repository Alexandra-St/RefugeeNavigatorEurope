## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- **BLOCKED: extension conflict.** Council Decision (EU) 2026/1912 and its 5 August corrigendum set the EU temporary-protection period through 4 March 2028, but §2 UkraineAufenthFGV still says qualifying German permits valid on 1 February 2026 continue through 4 March 2027. Germany4Ukraine's registration page says 2027 while its labour-law page says 2028. These sources do not let this draft safely state the end date of every German permit.
- Recheck the national ordinance and current Interior Ministry / Germany4Ukraine wording immediately before publication. The military-obligation requirement’s corrected dates and application to new grants need German implementation guidance.
- Confirm current registration and local appointment workflow with up-to-date official federal/Land pages. Do not make an arrival certificate equivalent to a residence permit.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
