## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- **Individual permit validity:** UkraineAufenthFGV §2 provides through 4 Mar 2027 for the specified permit cohort; the EU protection period through 4 Mar 2028 is a separate legal scope. Neither statement determines the validity of every individual document. The Germany4Ukraine Labour Law page was inaccessible; an indexed snippet is not evidence and is excluded from the article. Reopen it directly before any future source-specific use. Readers should check their document with the competent Ausländerbehörde.
- **EU military condition:** the corrigendum’s exception is based on continuous protection in the same Member State, not physical presence anywhere in the EU. Germany4Ukraine’s notice is broader. State the binding text and the portal wording separately; for prior protection in another Member State or a move between states, direct readers to the competent authority. No universal proof list is claimed.
- Keep the German 90-day title exemption in UkraineAufenthÜV §2(3) distinct from the EU temporary-protection grant rule. Explain that §2(3) applies to covered entries from 9 October 2026 and requires proof of compliance where the person is subject to Ukrainian military duties.
- These unresolved official statements are safe to explain for integration only while the qualifications above remain prominent; they are not a personal status or eligibility decision.
