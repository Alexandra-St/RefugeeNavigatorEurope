# Finding a Job in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Where to look for vacancies, get advice and check qualification recognition and language support.

## RULES

Access to work depends on your permission and what your residence document says. Services from the Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit) and Jobcenters depend on your circumstances and the applicable support route. Not everyone is served by a Jobcenter, and individual programmes have their own admission conditions.

## TASKS

Start with vacancy search and advice from the Federal Employment Agency. Prepare a short CV and evidence of your experience and qualifications; check whether your occupation is regulated and requires formal recognition. Language and vocational courses may have admission and funding conditions.

## PLACES

The Federal Employment Agency offers job-search tools, advice and information about recognition of professional qualifications. A Jobcenter may be your contact point if it is responsible for your benefits and support. Germany4Ukraine also collects information for people from Ukraine. Services are not identical for everyone.

## NEXT STEPS

Ask which authority handles your case, which services are available and whether your document meets work requirements. As of 15 October 2026, the bill proposing changes to the benefits system had not been completed as law; any change to Jobcenter responsibilities must be checked before publication. Finding a vacancy does not itself confirm permission to work.
