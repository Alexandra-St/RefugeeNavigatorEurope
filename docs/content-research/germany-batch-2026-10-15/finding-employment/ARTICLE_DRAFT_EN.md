# Finding a Job in Germany

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Practical starting points for vacancy search, advice, recognition and language courses, with checks to make before starting work.

## RULES

A vacancy or job offer does not itself authorize work. Check the “Erwerbstätigkeit” entry on your residence title or provisional document and any limits on employer, hours or occupation. The Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, BA) offers job-search tools and guidance. A Jobcenter is a separate contact point and is responsible only where the applicable benefit and integration route assigns the case to it.

## TASKS

1. Prepare a short CV in German or English, contact details and a clear list of roles, dates and skills. Keep diplomas, training certificates and references; provide translations only when the employer or recognition authority asks for them.

2. Search the BA job portal and use its advice service to discuss realistic roles, application materials and training opportunities. You can ask what support is available without assuming that a funded measure or benefit is guaranteed.

3. Check whether your occupation is regulated. If it is, use the Recognition in Germany portal or BA recognition guidance to find the responsible federal or Land authority. Ask what certificates, translations, fees and compensatory steps are required before promising a start date.

## PLACES

The BA is a national starting point for vacancies and employment advice. If you receive a means-tested benefit, your decision letter identifies the responsible Jobcenter or Sozialamt; do not assume every newcomer should register with a Jobcenter. Germany4Ukraine links to Ukraine-specific job support. BAMF integration and vocational-language courses have admission, availability and sometimes fee conditions; the provider or BAMF confirms your route.

## NEXT STEPS

Before accepting a start date, confirm the work permission printed on your own document. Save the contract, job description and qualification correspondence. If the current benefit reform proposal becomes law, routes between SGB II/SGB XII and AsylbLG for specified §24 groups may change. The proposal uses document-issuance criteria and exceptions, not only a blanket arrival date; as checked on 15 October 2026 it remained in parliamentary process. That does not prevent using the general BA job-search service, but it means Jobcenter or benefit-linked support should be rechecked before publication.
