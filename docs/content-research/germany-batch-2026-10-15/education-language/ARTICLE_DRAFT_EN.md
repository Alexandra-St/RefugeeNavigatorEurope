# Education and German-Language Learning

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Use your Land’s school route, check age-based childcare rights, and verify separate admission conditions for courses, vocational training and university.

## RULES

School administration, compulsory-school rules and enrolment procedures are set by the 16 Länder. Use the current school or education authority for the place where the child lives; there is no single national application form.

Childcare is not only discretionary local policy. Under SGB VIII §6(2), non-German families generally need lawful habitual residence in Germany or a Duldung to claim these services; ask the local Jugendamt if it is unclear which proof applies. Under §24, a child under one may qualify where development or the parent’s work, job search, education or training creates the statutory need. From age one until three, the child has a statutory entitlement to early-childhood support; from age three until school entry, an entitlement to support in a day-care centre applies. From school year 2026/27, a child entering grade one in that or a later school year has a statutory entitlement under §24(4) to support in a day-care facility from school entry until the start of grade five, for eight hours on weekdays. Time spent in lessons and all-day primary-school programmes, including open all-day schools, counts toward that entitlement; during school holidays, it is treated as met if a public or recognised independent youth-welfare provider offers activities under §11. The statutory entitlement and local search/allocation are separate matters; neither guarantees a particular provider or an immediate vacancy.

## TASKS

For school, choose the Land where the child lives and follow its education ministry, Schulamt or school instructions. Keep identity papers, address evidence and any previous school records; the local authority will say what else and which translations it needs.

For a Kita or Kindertagespflege place, use the federal Family Portal to select the Land, then follow its registration system. In towns and districts, the Jugendamt usually helps with referral or placement. Ask about deadlines, waiting lists, age groups, opening hours and any local documents. If you cannot find a place, ask the Jugendamt about alternatives and how the statutory right is handled; the national law does not promise a specific provider or immediate vacancy.

## PLACES

Germany4Ukraine and the Standing Conference of Ministers of Education (KMK) link to Land-specific school information. For a childcare search, the federal Family Portal links to each Land’s own services. Universities decide admission and language requirements; DAAD’s National Academic Contact Point Ukraine provides orientation, not an admission decision. Professional recognition depends on the occupation and may be governed by federal or Land law.

## NEXT STEPS

For an integration course, check BAMF admission before enrolling: eligibility, fees, obligation and places depend on the person’s situation. Vocational training has its own contract, language and residence-title requirements; university enrolment is a separate process. If the goal is a regulated profession, start recognition with the responsible authority. Keep course, school or recognition decisions and ask the relevant institution about the next step. Do not treat a course place, childcare place, school enrolment or university admission as guaranteed by this overview.
