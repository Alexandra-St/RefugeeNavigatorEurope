# Education and Learning German

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** The 16 Länder organise schools; courses, vocational training and university admission have separate conditions.

## RULES

Responsibility for schools and many education processes lies with Germany’s 16 Länder. Compulsory-school ages, enrolment and language support vary by Land and local school. Universities set their own admission requirements. Do not assume one process applies throughout Germany.

## TASKS

For a child, contact a school, Schulamt or local education authority about enrolment and language support. For vocational training, check the requirements for the specific Ausbildung. For university study, contact the institution and verify prior-education recognition and language requirements.

## PLACES

Germany4Ukraine and the KMK provide official starting points for Land-level school information. Germany4Ukraine says refugees from Ukraine are generally not required to take an integration course, although an obligation may apply in some cases. BAMF sets admission conditions; participation, fees and availability depend on the person’s situation. Recognition of professional qualifications may be governed by federal or Land law.

## NEXT STEPS

Childcare registration and available places depend on local rules. Ask the municipality about the process for your address; this overview does not establish Land-level rights or enrolment timelines. For academic guidance, use the DAAD National Academic Contact Point Ukraine. Confirm requirements with the relevant authority or institution: this overview does not guarantee a place, admission or funding.
