## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- School enrollment, compulsory attendance, childcare place allocation and many recognition decisions are Land/local matters; the draft deliberately gives a referral path rather than a national application deadline.
- Before publication, link a current official school/childcare portal for each intended Land or clearly tell the reader to choose their current Land.
- Verify the end date and availability of any time-limited childcare-with-course programme before mentioning it; no such guarantee is included here.

## Draft limitations

Drafts deliberately avoid individual entitlement determinations, unsupported amounts and universal promises. This review is a point-in-time source check, not a legal opinion. Any law, administrative guidance, threshold or live service must be rechecked immediately before publication.
