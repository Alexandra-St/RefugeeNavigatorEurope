## Unresolved questions and publication gate

- The final drafts now state the relevant §24(1) conditions for under-one support and the §24(4) permission for Land law to allow closures of up to four weeks during school holidays.
- School entry rules, compulsory attendance, childcare allocation and local admission steps remain Land-specific; use current Land contacts or the KMK directory.
- Course, study, training and recognition routes remain subject to institution and current admission rules; no place or funding is promised.
