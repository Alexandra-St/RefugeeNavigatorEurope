# Denmark temporary protection: application, eligibility and extension

**Research checked: 15 October 2026. Editorial status: unpublished; not legal advice.** This document records the pages actually opened and the limitations encountered. No authority was contacted. The Danish Immigration Service’s New to Denmark pages and the Danish legal/parliamentary texts are in English only where available; the binding Danish texts should be checked by a qualified Danish reader before publication.

## Scope and relationship to existing content

This candidate concerns applying under Denmark’s national Ukraine Special Act, handling an application, post-decision steps, and extension. It deliberately does not repeat the existing Denmark article on residence options after protection (Labour Market Attachment Scheme, Educational Attachment Scheme, other permits, and permanent residence). It refers readers to the existing Denmark Healthcare, Housing, and Social Assistance articles for those subjects.

Denmark’s route is a national statutory scheme. Denmark does not participate in Council Directive 2001/55/EC (the Temporary Protection Directive) under its EU treaty opt-out. Eligibility under the Directive or a temporary-protection document issued by another EU country does not by itself establish eligibility under Denmark’s Special Act. This is a distinction between legal frameworks, not a claim about a person’s individual eligibility.

## Conflicting source statements: permit extension

- **BEK no. 828/2026:** the Ministry regulation was published 30 September, took effect 1 October 2026, and says a Special Act permit may be extended to **17 March 2028**, unless grounds for revocation under section 10 apply. It provides for the Immigration Service to decide on its own initiative and suspensive effect while cases are processed. This is a possible scheme-wide date, not a guarantee or the individual date shown on a permit.
- **Danish Immigration Service public guidance:** the main Ukraine application page (updated 3 September 2026), family page (updated 3 September 2026), the information page, and the October 2025 extension notice still show **17 March 2027**. ## Important limitations and unresolved questions

1. **Extension conflict remains a publication blocker.** Obtain a clear current authority statement reconciling 2027 agency text with BEK 828’s 2028 date. Recheck the underlying regulation directly; the research interface returned a JavaScript-only shell.
2. **Eligibility is individualized.** Date, residence, displacement, family relationship, nationality, foreign residence permission, military status and evidence can change the analysis. The page contains complex alternative branches that a short draft cannot adjudicate.

Sections 28(6)–(9) provide decision-type exceptions and routes. Act no. 1575/2023 §4 amended §28(3) and §28(7) for specified Home Return Agency cash-benefit decisions under §14. These provisions do not support treating deadlines or stay effects as universal across all decisions.
