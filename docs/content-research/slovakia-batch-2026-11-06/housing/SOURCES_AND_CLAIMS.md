## Core distinctions

- **Initial facility accommodation (§68):** accommodation in an asylum facility is a capacity-limited statutory option, not guaranteed housing, a guaranteed location or a promise of transfer/transport. The ordinary limit is 60 days from the person's first grant of temporary refuge in Slovakia. A separate vulnerable-person rule can permit longer residence, subject to written application and capacity.
- **Host contribution (§69):** this is paid by the municipality to a statutorily eligible host for qualifying free accommodation. It is **not rent money paid to the temporary-refuge holder** and is not an ordinary tenant subsidy.
- **Private rental:** a private lease is a separate contract between landlord and tenant. Eligibility for or expiry of a facility bed or host-side contribution does not itself create a private lease, guarantee a rental or provide cash to pay rent.
- **Two reporting tracks:** the contribution scheme's personal municipal notifications and host's monthly claims are separate from an individual's residence-address change through the municipal residence-reporting office and any statutory lodging-provider notification to Police.

## Findings and evidence boundaries

- **Facility process / availability:** §68 confirms the legal option and priority for international-protection applicants and TP applicants, but no current complete intake channel, place choice, transfer/transport policy, facility-by-facility capacity or guaranteed bed was established. MV SR registration guidance remains online but contains outdated pre-Act-69 rules; do not reuse its old numbers, 2022 locations, or instant-placement language.
- **§68 extensions:** the categories are intentionally narrow. The first facility stay is up to 60 days from first Slovak grant; a vulnerable person may request an up-to-six-month accommodation period and repeat requests, with capacity considered. Application is written to the Ministry; an extension request from someone currently accommodated is due at least 15 days before period end. No automatic extension or total maximum is stated in §68(5).
- **§69 support:** the vulnerable list is broader and purpose-specific. Eligibility and proof follow §§69(2)–(3); free accommodation, eligible host type and minimum requirements under §69(4)–(5), contribution limits, and exclusions apply. It is not cash for the beneficiary to pay rent.
- **Current rates:** effective interim schedule verified as €5/night for owner-provided residential housing with property-level monthly caps by room count; €6/night in qualifying short-stay non-residential property provided by a qualifying legal person or municipality/region. No child rate was stated. The current law expressly preserves Regulation 151/2024, amended by 36/2025, until a new implementing regulation takes effect; no newer regulation was found by the research date.
- **Reporting:** beneficiary's own municipal appearances, host's agreement and monthly claim, capacity declaration for relevant host classes, and provider/municipal payment timing are all in current §69. Keep these distinct from moving-address reporting. No current MV SR form link/checklist under the new §69 was verified; the law says templates are on the Ministry website and historic manual/FAQ predate the new Act.
- **Private lease:** ordinary Civil Code and special Act 98/2014 are separate regimes. Written ordinary contract is strongly advisable but not universally required by §686; ordinary flat termination generally uses §710, with statutory exceptions. There is no general deposit cap found for ordinary Civil Code flat rental; the separate short-term regime caps the security at three months including service payments. Exact lease, utilities and repairs are contractual/statutory matters and should not be reduced to a universal rule.
- **Address / landlord tasks:** TP-holder address change must be reported through the residence-reporting office with residence and accommodation evidence under Act 404/2011 §131k(5); that clause contains no numeric deadline. Any separate host duty depends on statutory “ubytovateľ” classification; where §§113 applies, it includes an official stay report to Police within five days. Do not rely on old MV SR registration copy for the current report mechanics. Lessor's tax registration is landlord-side.
- **Social housing:** no national TP-specific guarantee or standard placement was found. Local authority/landlord criteria, income limits and availability must be checked locally; “not established” is not a finding that an individual cannot apply.
