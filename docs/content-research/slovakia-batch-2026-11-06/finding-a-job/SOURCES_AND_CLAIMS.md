## Findings and evidence boundaries

**Official job-search channels.** The Employment Services portal supports public browsing/filtering without UoZ registration, but covers portal/office listings rather than every Slovak job. ÚPSVaR mediation is a separate, free service and does not guarantee a particular vacancy or hire. EURES provides European job search, mobility information and adviser support; it does not decide immigration, work authorization, benefits or Slovak UoZ status. Private fee-charging intermediaries and temporary-employment agencies are distinct; check current ÚPSVaR lists. Under §25(3), an employment placement fee is charged to the employer/client.

**Temporary-refuge registration boundary.** Section 23a(1)(k) permits employment without a work permit for a person granted temporary refuge. It does not answer eligibility for UoZ registration. Section 2(2) lists equal-position groups but does not expressly name TP holders, and the reviewed official UoZ instructions do not resolve the question. Do not make a categorical yes/no claim. A TP holder should contact ÚPSVaR to confirm formal UoZ eligibility, required documents and competent office. The same caution applies to formal ZoZ registration: the general ZoZ route is distinct and useful, including for employed people, but TP-specific acceptance is not explicit in the guidance. Regardless of register status, §42 expressly covers third-country nationals for free information/advisory services.

**Mediation and counselling.** Act §§32 and 42 support free mediation and information/advisory services. Selection is voluntary; no specific job is owed. Professional counselling under §43 is tied to defined registration/benefit categories, and individual action planning can be narrower. Do not promise all counselling to every TP holder.

**Training.** Sections 44–46 and current project guidance make training conditional on status, assessment, application/decision and project rules. Section 54(6) includes TP holders as a target group for integration programmes, not as automatic beneficiaries. Recheck current 2026 project intake, budget and criteria before publication. Do not promise a free course or payment.

**Qualification recognition.** Separate regulated and non-regulated professions. For a regulated profession, identify the profession-specific authority and follow its current recognition procedure. For ordinary non-regulated employment, do not state that formal recognition is universally required; employer requirements can vary. The Ministry has Ukraine-specific guidance for missing originals, but no outcome is guaranteed.

**Unemployment benefit.** Intentionally outside scope. UoZ/ZoZ status, TP status, work permission or service access must not be treated as proof of benefit entitlement.

### Unresolved evidence boundaries

1. Exact UoZ eligibility of a TP holder, TP-specific application documents and competent office.
2. Formal ZoZ registration eligibility for a TP-only case.
3. Availability of a particular local mediation, job fair or counselling product.
4. Individual training entitlement, current project budget/intake and TP-holder project eligibility.
5. Profession-specific recognition authority, dossier and current fees; employer requirements for non-regulated roles.
6. Details of any agency service other than employment placement, which require examining its separate legal and contractual basis.
7. Unemployment-benefit eligibility, which was not researched.

## Final topic verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING**, with all boundaries above retained. All three aspects are safely draftable if the content says TP-specific UoZ/ZoZ eligibility remains unresolved, directs the reader to confirm registration with ÚPSVaR, and identifies free §42 information/advisory services for third-country nationals. Never claim automatic inclusion or exclusion, job guarantee, training entitlement, or unemployment-benefit eligibility.
