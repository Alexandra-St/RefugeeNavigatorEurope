# Lithuania — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 2026-11-09
**Scope:** exactly `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, and `employment-assistance-conditions`. Official-source review only; this is not an individual eligibility determination.

## Scope

This block covers vacancy discovery, public employment institutions, registration with the Lithuanian Employment Service (`Užimtumo tarnyba`, UŽT), job-search assistance and training, unemployment insurance boundaries, and current Ukraine-specific employment support. It distinguishes public vacancy browsing from formal registration, and employment services from immigration decisions and insurance benefits.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** UŽT’s national vacancy search and EURES are the main official public search channels. UŽT says public vacancy browsing does not require registration; its vacancy portal has a quick-search route that can be opened in Ukrainian, while EURES’ public job search supports multiple EU languages. Neither source establishes a complete inventory of every job in Lithuania. Formal UŽT registration is separate: the registration page accepts a valid Lithuanian residence permit for third-country nationals and a travel or Migration Department registration document for Ukrainian war refugees. Registration can be made online or at the territorial customer-service office; assistance channels and status must be checked for the person’s exact documents. [LT-JOB-S01–S05]

UŽT supplies vacancy matching, market information, career counselling and, where eligible, active labour-market measures. Counselling is not synonymous with unemployed status; some information and career counselling is available to unregistered people. Training is conditional on eligibility, assessment, UŽT priorities, funding and an individual employment plan; it is not an automatic entitlement. UŽT handles employment support and certain specified work-permit/labour-market functions, while the Migration Department decides residence/temporary-protection status and Sodra decides and pays unemployment insurance benefits. [LT-JOB-S01, S04, S06–S11]

For claims arising on or after 2026-07-01, Sodra’s current guidance requires UŽT unemployed status, an application, and generally at least 12 months of unemployment insurance within the previous 24 calendar months; other statutory conditions and exceptions apply. TP status alone does not establish benefit entitlement. Sodra’s Ukrainian bilateral-agreement information describes pension coordination, not unemployment insurance; whether Ukrainian unemployment contributions count is **Not established** in the reviewed official evidence. [LT-JOB-S12–S14]

There is current Ukraine-specific UŽT project activity: JOINT-UA-EMPLOY planned Lithuanian B1–B2 classes for 40 TP holders through January 2027 and DUO DAY-UA job-shadowing activities through December 2026. However, the current project pages say participant registration has closed and selection is underway. No open participant intake, guaranteed place, current Ukraine-specific wage subsidy, or dedicated Ukraine hotline was established as of the research date. [LT-JOB-S15–S18]

## Public vacancy search boundaries

- **UŽT search:** public search of UŽT-registered vacancies, with keyword, field, location and wage filters. UŽT explicitly says no jobseeker registration is needed just to view vacancies. Online account features such as a CV or vacancy alerts are separate from browsing. [LT-JOB-S01–S05]
- **Ukrainian access:** UŽT’s vacancy portal exposes a Ukrainian locale route and the service publishes Ukrainian-language information. The evidence does not show that every listing, employer-provided description, or personalized function is translated. [LT-JOB-S05]
- **EURES:** official EU vacancy database and EURES Lithuania advisers serve European mobility/search needs. Public search is not limited to registered UŽT clients; adviser and mobility supports have their own conditions. A multilingual search interface does not guarantee that every employer’s original vacancy text is translated. [LT-JOB-S10–S11; EU EURES help page]
- **Other routes:** UŽT recommends company career pages, other portals, direct approaches, networks and career events. Many opportunities are not published as formal listings. [LT-JOB-S01, S11]
- **Completeness:** neither UŽT nor EURES is established as a complete census of all jobs. UŽT calls its database the most comprehensive for Lithuania in its EURES information, which supports “broad/major public source,” not “all vacancies.” [LT-JOB-S11]

## Assistance / counselling / training conditions

1. **Jobseeker status and identity:** UŽT’s registration page accepts (a) a valid Lithuanian residence permit for third-country nationals and (b) a travel document or Migration Department registration document for Ukrainian war refugees. It requests education/competence/qualification documents in Lithuanian. A TP holder’s digital residence document is a Lithuanian TRP; UŽT may still check status and supporting identity evidence. The page does not establish a universal personal-code or declared-address prerequisite. [LT-JOB-S01, S04]
2. **Where/how to register:** online through UŽT self-service with e-government identity confirmation, or in person at the territorial customer-service unit for actual residence. The online flow asks the applicant to choose a specialist consultation time. Registration by telephone is not provided. These instructions do not prove that every person can authenticate online. [LT-JOB-S03–S04]
3. **Unemployed is a distinct status:** the general UŽT FAQ says jobseeker registration is possible from age 14; the statutory unemployed status has separate requirements (including age 16 to pension age, capacity to be an employee, and not studying in ordinary general-education except specified adult education, among other criteria). Do not treat “jobseeker registered” as “unemployed,” or assume a person must be unemployed to browse vacancies or receive all information/counselling. [LT-JOB-S01, S07]
4. **Counselling:** UŽT publicly describes free information and consultation on vacancies, labour-market changes, CVs, applications, interviews, career choices, retraining and services. Its foreigner page says some career counselling may be available to unregistered people. Service form and appointment are subject to UŽT’s current delivery process. [LT-JOB-S01, S03, S06]
5. **Profiling, plans and training:** after the relevant assessment, UŽT can put training into an individual employment activity plan if a needed qualification/competence would materially improve employment prospects. Participation depends on law, funding-source criteria, UŽT priorities, planned measures and available providers. Priorities include unemployed people with limited employment prospects and other specified groups; selection is not automatic. Some training options also exist for employed clients under distinct conditions. [LT-JOB-S07–S08]
6. **Qualification recognition:** UŽT points to the competent academic/professional recognition bodies when required. Whether recognition is mandatory depends on the occupation and legal rules; a referral is not recognition or a promise of a job. [LT-JOB-S01]
7. **Subsidies and employer incentives:** UŽT operates general subsidised-employment measures for eligible referred candidates and employers. Eligibility, target groups, application windows, 2026 priorities and de minimis/employer rules apply. A TP holder is not automatically entitled to a subsidy solely because of nationality or TP; a Ukraine-specific open wage-subsidy intake was not established in this research. [LT-JOB-S07–S08, S18]

## Unemployment-benefit boundary

- **Administrator and application path:** UŽT first registers the person and grants unemployed status; the benefit application may be sent through UŽT or submitted to Sodra. Sodra assesses entitlement and pays. [LT-JOB-S12]
- **Current general contribution test:** for unemployed status granted from 2026-07-01, the current Sodra page generally requires at least **12 months of unemployment insurance within the prior 24 calendar months**, plus an application within 12 months of obtaining unemployed status and the other legal conditions. The page includes specific exceptions. Do not reuse the older 12-in-30-month rule for a new status granted after July 1. [LT-JOB-S12]
- **Involuntary loss / timing:** the claim is tied to having lost work and obtaining unemployed status; Sodra guidance delays commencement to three months after UŽT registration where dismissal was for the employee’s fault under Labour Code Art. 58. It also lists other overlapping payments and status conditions affecting start or amount. Avoid reducing the law to a single “involuntary termination” rule without the applicable facts. [LT-JOB-S12]
- **TP:** TP status gives neither insurance contributions nor an automatic unemployment benefit. A TP holder may qualify only if the insurance/status/application conditions are met; assess individually. [LT-JOB-S12]
- **Ukraine periods:** Sodra explains recognition of foreign insurance periods for coordinated countries/cases and identifies the Lithuania–Ukraine bilateral agreement in its country guidance as covering pensions. The reviewed sources do not establish that Ukrainian unemployment contribution periods aggregate toward the Lithuanian unemployment benefit. State **Not established**. Do not infer unemployment coordination from the pension agreement or TP. [LT-JOB-S12–S14]
- **EU/EEA/Swiss periods:** Sodra may aggregate or coordinate qualifying EU/EEA/Swiss insurance periods under the applicable rules and documents (U1/SED), often subject to the last-insurance-period and competent-state rules. This does not prove Ukrainian contributions are covered. [LT-JOB-S12–S13]

## 2026 changes / stale guidance

- **Benefit test changed on 2026-07-01:** new claimants whose unemployed status begins from that date generally need 12 months’ unemployment-insurance history in the previous 24 months, not the previous 30-month window. Use Sodra’s page specifically for statuses from 2026-07-01; its older page remains relevant only to earlier status cohorts. [LT-JOB-S12]
- **Benefit administration:** current Sodra instructions explain UŽT registration/status first, with the application routed to Sodra for decision/payment. Do not imply UŽT pays unemployment insurance. [LT-JOB-S12]

## Unresolved issues

- The reviewed public UŽT materials do not establish whether a Lithuanian personal code or a formally declared municipal address is an absolute prerequisite for every TP holder’s registration. They do state that the registration office follows actual residence and list the accepted document categories.
- The public rules do not provide one definitive rule for every TP-holder cohort/document edge case; UŽT instructs foreign citizens to verify eligibility against nationality, lawful-stay basis and documents. A pending application, expired permit or document outside the listed categories should be checked with UŽT before claiming registration eligibility.
- Whether Ukraine-accumulated unemployment-insurance periods count toward Lithuania’s benefit is not established by the reviewed sources. Sodra’s stated Ukraine agreement description covers pensions; obtain a case-specific Sodra determination rather than extrapolating.
- The JOINT-UA-EMPLOY activity pages show current project periods but participant registration is closed. Exact remaining places, candidate selection and delivery schedule are not public; contact the current named project coordinator or UŽT for a fresh update.
- No current official evidence reviewed confirms a dedicated Ukraine employment hotline, universally available Ukrainian-language counselling, open Ukraine-specific wage subsidy, or a national guaranteed Lithuanian-language course for every TP holder.
- Municipal integration/social assistance referrals may exist locally, but no separate municipal job-registration or unemployment-benefit decision role was established in the reviewed materials.
- Public portal availability can fail or be temporarily incomplete; a search result is not proof a vacancy is still open or that the employer has validated a job seeker’s work authorization.

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- UŽT and EURES are key official public vacancy-search channels; UŽT says registration is not needed merely to view public vacancy listings.
- Public vacancy search, formal UŽT registration, unemployed status, active-measure participation and unemployment insurance are distinct.
- UŽT registration materials expressly list a valid Lithuanian residence permit for third-country nationals and a travel or Migration Department registration document for Ukrainian war refugees; actual case eligibility/document checking remains with UŽT.
- Registration is available online or in person; online authentication and status assessment are separate steps, and telephone registration is not provided.
- UŽT provides free market information/career counselling and may provide matching, training or other measures under statutory eligibility, assessment, plan, priority and funding conditions.
- Sodra decides and pays unemployment insurance benefits after required UŽT status/application; for status from 2026-07-01, the general requirement is 12 insured months in the previous 24 months, subject to exceptions.
- Current Ukraine-specific UŽT projects include JOINT-UA-EMPLOY language training and DUO DAY-UA, but participant registration is closed on the reviewed pages and places are capped/selected.

### Require qualification

- Say a TP holder **can register** only with the supported documentation and subject to UŽT’s check of the person’s residence/work basis, identity and status.
- Describe Ukrainian-language support as a Ukrainian interface/guidance route where documented, not a guarantee that every vacancy, service or counselling appointment is translated.
- Describe training, subsidies, language courses, qualification referral and EURES mobility support as conditional, not guaranteed.
- For foreign unemployment insurance, identify country-specific coordination documents and last-work rules; treat Ukraine separately and mark countability not established.
- State that a Ukraine-specific project is active only within its published delivery period, while clearly noting closed intake and selection status.

### Do not assert

- That one vacancy portal lists all jobs in Lithuania, that all postings are current, or that vacancy browsing itself grants work rights.
- That a foreign national can register with UŽT on any visa/status or that a pending/expired document is enough.
- That every applicant needs a personal code, declared address or a bank account unless a current source specifically establishes it for that service route.
- That everyone registering is unemployed, that online submission alone grants unemployed status, or that benefit eligibility follows from registration/TP.
- That training, wage subsidies, Lithuanian-language classes, qualification recognition or job placement is guaranteed for TP holders.
- That Ukraine-specific 2022–2025 schemes or hotlines remain open in 2026 without current evidence.
- That the Lithuania–Ukraine pension agreement proves Ukrainian unemployment insurance periods can be counted.
- That UŽT decides temporary protection, residence permits or an individual’s general immigration rights.
