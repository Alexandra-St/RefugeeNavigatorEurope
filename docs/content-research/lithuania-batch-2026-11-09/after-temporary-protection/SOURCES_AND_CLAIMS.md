# Lithuania — Residence Options During and After Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-09
**Scope:** exactly `residence-routes`, `residence-conditions`, and `residence-filing`. Research only; this is a national-law evidence review, not an individual eligibility decision.

## Scope

This review asks which Lithuanian national residence routes may be available to a person who currently holds Lithuanian temporary protection (TP), what can be established about filing while that TP-based residence document remains valid, and what is known about the period after TP. It distinguishes temporary protection, the digital temporary residence document issued on that basis, ordinary temporary residence permits (TRPs), and the Lithuanian permit of a long-term resident to reside in the EU (commonly described by Lithuanian sources as a permanent residence permit).

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Lithuania has ordinary work, highly qualified employment / EU Blue Card, study, family, business, humanitarian and long-term residence grounds. A TP holder’s digital document is a Lithuanian temporary residence permit. Current Migration Department guidance permits an in-country employment or first-cycle bachelor-study application by a legally present person holding a valid Lithuanian TRP; it separately says highly qualified employment filings remain available in Lithuania under the ordinary procedure. Law and official guidance also provide specific employment facilitation for TP holders: no Employment Service labour-market decision for the first employment-based TRP after TP (or a same-employer replacement), a 2026 work-quota exception, and work rights while TP continues. These are application-based and conditional. They do not establish automatic conversion, a new standalone TP transition title, or the end-date mechanics of TP after another permit is granted. [LT-RES-S01–S07]

The current public sources reviewed do **not** expressly resolve whether time holding only TP counts toward the five-year residence period for a Lithuanian long-term/permanent residence permit. The statute requires five years’ continuous residence with a qualifying residence document for its general route, while the EU Long-Term Residents Directive excludes persons residing solely on temporary protection from its scope. The Lithuanian statute expressly treats the TP digital document as a temporary residence permit, but no reviewed Lithuanian authority explains whether that specific TP period counts under the five-year calculation. Do not claim that it counts fully, partly, or not at all without an explicit authoritative determination. A separate 2026 rule does expressly count qualifying time spent on a digital foreigner-registration certificate issued for a pending residence-permit application. [LT-RES-S01, S08–S10]

No dedicated Lithuanian TP-to-national-permit transition route or deadline/window was established in the reviewed sources. The Council’s transition Recommendation is nonbinding and is not a Lithuanian filing entitlement. [LT-RES-S11]

## Eligibility / conditions boundaries

- **Employment:** keep the 2026-05-22 alternatives tied to the ordinary employment ground: relevant qualification, relevant work experience, or employer undertaking to pay at least the last published calendar-year average monthly gross wage, as summarized by the Department (S02, S07). The actual applicable branch and wage figure should be verified at filing. Employer disqualification grounds and permit-specific conditions remain applicable (S01–S02).
- **Labour-market test and quota:** distinguish an Employment Service decision from the annual work quota. The TP-specific first employment-permit exemption from the former and TP exemption from the latter are expressly reported by the Department; neither means all job requirements are waived (S04–S05).
- **Blue Card:** use its separate highly qualified route, not the ordinary employment threshold. Qualification, suitable employment and statutory salary thresholds apply; the current public summary notes changes but is not a complete Blue Card checklist (S01–S03, S07).
- **Study:** qualifying admission and category-specific documentary/means/insurance rules apply under the Law and implementation process. The reviewed current sources do not establish a complete checklist specifically for a TP-holder transition. Student employment-hour limits introduced in 2026 are distinct from the right to obtain study residence (S01–S02).
- **Family:** eligible relationship and sponsor route matter. Since 2026-05-22, public Department guidance says second-cycle students, residents, doctoral students, specified graduates and job-search permit holders can bring family immediately; other foreigners generally need two years’ residence, a TRP valid at least one year, and reasonable permanent-residence prospects. Do not assume TP alone is an eligible sponsor status (S01, S15).
- **Business and humanitarian grounds:** there is no universal checklist. Confirm the exact statutory ground, activity/business requirements, means, insurance and any security/refusal condition against current implementing material (S01).
- **Long-term residence:** the general statutory route requires continuous lawful residence and a qualifying document for five years, plus Lithuanian-language and Constitution exams unless an exception applies. The exact treatment of TP-only residence remains unresolved in current official Lithuanian materials reviewed here (S01, S08–S09).

## Filing and pending-application boundaries

1. **Authority and channel.** The Migration Department is the deciding authority. MIGRIS is used for electronic services and the 2026 pending-case registration certificate. External service providers (VFS Global) accept certain overseas applications; the availability of an appointment may be capacity-limited. In-country eligibility and channel are category-specific (S01–S03, S06, S10).
2. **Work and bachelor-study in-country rule from 2026-11-01.** A legally present person may submit an ordinary work-based or first-cycle bachelor-study TRP application to the Migration Department only if one of the listed lawful-presence bases applies, including a valid Lithuanian TRP. A valid TP digital residence document is such a Lithuanian TRP. A person present only on a Schengen visa or another country’s national visa must use VFS for those categories. Highly qualified work remains under its separate ordinary in-country process (S03).
3. **Filing is not residence status.** The 2026 Department guidance says a qualifying holder who applies to replace a TRP or apply for a new ground while the current TRP remains valid may stay and continue working for the same employer while a decision is pending. If the current permit expires during processing, the person must qualify for and request the digital foreigner-registration certificate. The certificate is not a travel document and does not itself grant the requested TRP (S02, S10).
4. **Work while pending.** Law Art. 94(1)(5) specifically grants a TP-eligible person who has applied for/changed a TRP on the statutory employment bases (Art. 40(1)(4) or (10)) the right to work/self-employ while the application is pending and TP applies. This narrow rule and the same-employer pending-certificate summary do not create universal work authorization for every pending application (S01–S02).
5. **Documents, biometrics, timelines, fees and review.** They vary by permit basis. The current fee table differentiates general and student applications, and an external-service provider may have separate service charges. The evidence reviewed does not establish one current processing time, universal biometric/personal-attendance rule, uniform pickup method or appeal path across every category; consult the exact current MIGRIS instructions and decision letter (S01, S03, S06, S14).

## TP time and long-term residence

Lithuanian law’s general route to the permit of a long-term resident to reside in the EU requires five years’ uninterrupted residence and a qualifying residence document; the standard route also requires Lithuanian-language and Constitution examinations subject to statutory exceptions. Lithuanian law separately identifies the TP digital document as a temporary residence permit. The sources reviewed do not expressly say whether the five-year calculation includes a period spent holding **only** that TP document. [LT-RES-S01, S09]

The EU Long-Term Residents Directive excludes persons whose residence is solely temporary protection from the Directive’s scope. That exclusion is important context but does not by itself resolve the treatment of past TP time under Lithuania’s national wording once the person changes status. Therefore the TP-period result is **Not established**: do not say fully counted, half-counted, excluded, or that a TP-only holder can apply on that basis. [LT-RES-S08–S09]

By contrast, the Migration Department expressly says a qualifying period on a digital foreigner-registration certificate issued because a TRP issue/change application is pending counts toward the lawful-residence period for permanent residence. This is an express and narrower rule; do not extend it to TP time. [LT-RES-S02, S10]

Terminology: Lithuanian public guidance calls the national `leidimas nuolat gyventi` a “permanent residence permit” and also describes it as a permit of a long-term resident to reside in the EU. Do not conflate the general national-law document and the EU Directive’s personal-scope test; where the exact legal consequence matters, retain the Lithuanian title and describe the statutory route. [LT-RES-S01, S09]

## 2026–2028 transition findings

- Lithuania’s national TP framework is extended through **2028-03-04**, following the EU extension and Government Resolution No. 742 effective 2026-10-09. This is the TP period, not a transition application deadline. Existing individual digital documents and replacement actions must be checked against current Department instructions; the extension does not automatically issue another national residence title. [LT-RES-S12–S13]
- From 2026-05-22, the Law includes the specified pending-application stay certificate and expressly counts a qualifying certificate period toward permanent residence. It also provides a narrow pending-work right for TP holders applying on specified employment grounds. These are procedural/status measures, not a dedicated post-TP status. [LT-RES-S01–S02, S10]
- From 2026-11-01, domestic intake rules for ordinary work and first-cycle bachelor applications require an eligible legal-presence basis, including a valid Lithuanian TRP; highly qualified employment continues under its ordinary in-country process. This creates a usable general change-of-ground filing path for a current TP permit holder in those categories, but no automatic outcome. [LT-RES-S03]
- The 2026 EU military-obligation condition concerns eligibility for relevant TP grants/continuation and is subject to the exact EU and Lithuanian transition wording. It is not shown in the reviewed sources as a condition for ordinary national employment, study, family, business or long-term residence grounds. Do not apply that TP condition to an unrelated route. [LT-RES-S01, S12–S13]
- **Dedicated TP-to-national route/window:** **Not established.** General filing rights and work-permit/quota facilitation are established; no standalone Lithuanian transition permit, universal TP conversion rule, or deadline window was found in the reviewed statute and official guidance. The EU Recommendation encouraging Member States to facilitate transition is nonbinding. [LT-RES-S01–S07, S11]

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- Lithuania has ordinary national residence grounds for employment, highly qualified employment / Blue Card, studies, family reunification, qualifying business, humanitarian grounds and long-term/permanent residence, each with its own conditions and decision process.
- A Lithuanian TP digital residence document is a temporary residence permit; while TP applies, TP holders have the specific rights established by the TP provisions, including work/self-employment subject to the statutory terms.
- A TP holder applying for the first employment-based TRP after TP grant does not need the Employment Service labour-market-compliance decision identified in current Department guidance; TP holders are exempt from the 2026 employment quota. Substantive job/employer/permit conditions remain.
- A legally present TP holder with a valid Lithuanian TRP can use the current in-country filing route for ordinary employment and first-cycle bachelor study; highly qualified employment has a separately confirmed in-country process. Application is not automatic approval.
- For a qualifying pending application, a digital foreigner-registration certificate may support stay after the prior TRP expires; qualifying certificate time is expressly counted toward the long-term/permanent-residence period. The certificate is not a travel document or new TRP.
- Lithuania’s national TP framework runs through 2028-03-04. The TP-specific military condition should not be transferred to unrelated residence grounds.

### Require qualification

- Say “may apply” only for the route and filing location whose current legal-presence and eligibility conditions are met; do not imply a general TP-to-residence conversion.
- Present employment facilitation as narrow: no Employment Service decision for the specified first post-TP employment permit / same-employer replacement, plus a distinct work-quota exemption.
- Describe family sponsorship using the precise sponsor category and current statutory conditions; do not treat all TP holders as qualifying sponsors.
- Identify the five-year national route and exams, while stating that TP-only residence counting has not been established in reviewed current Lithuanian guidance.
- Separate TP validity, another permit application, a pending certificate, and an issued national residence permit.

### Do not assert

- That TP automatically converts to a work, study, family, business, humanitarian, Blue Card or permanent residence title.
- That a TP holder can apply in Lithuania for every residence category, or that all applications are wholly online.
- That TP status continues alongside another permit, or automatically ends on a particular event, absent direct authority.
- That time on TP counts fully, partly, or not at all toward the five-year permanent/long-term residence period, or that a TP-only holder may apply under the five-year route.
- That the EU Recommendation itself gives an individual a right to transition or that Lithuania has enacted a special transition permit/window.
- That the EU military condition applies to national residence routes unrelated to TP.
- One universal income, accommodation, insurance, security, biometric, fee, processing-time, collection or appeal checklist for every route.
