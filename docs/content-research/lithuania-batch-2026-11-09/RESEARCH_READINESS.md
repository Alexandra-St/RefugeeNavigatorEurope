## 6. Cross-topic consistency checks

The package findings can be drafted together if their boundaries remain explicit. No direct contradiction requiring a topic/aspect to be blocked was identified.

- **TP status, dates and military condition:** `temporary-protection-application` and `after-temporary-protection` use the amended Lithuanian scheme through 2028-03-04 and the corrected 2026 military-condition dates. Do not conflate the EU/national protection endpoint with validity of each physical/digital document or a separate national residence permit.
- **TP work versus ordinary permits:** `employment-rights` supports work from the qualifying TP application point and a TP-group work-permit exemption. `after-temporary-protection` covers distinct ordinary work/study/family routes and pending-certificate rules. Do not generalize TP work rights to every pending TRP or `ricevuta`; employer reporting obligations remain separately applicable.
- **Social assistance versus unemployment insurance:** `social-assistance` places TP beneficiaries in specified national means-tested support categories, subject to scheme rules. `finding-a-job` separates UŽT registration/services from contributory Sodra unemployment insurance; Ukrainian insurance-period credit is not established.
- **Healthcare:** `healthcare` distinguishes emergency/necessary care for TP beneficiaries from full CHI eligibility and active register status. Work authorization by itself does not prove CHI enrollment; routine maternity/dependant and urgent-care payment boundaries remain qualified.
- **Housing:** `housing` distinguishes reception/accommodation, private tenancy, residence declaration, rent compensation, social housing, utilities and one-time local assistance. Social-assistance claims must not imply a guaranteed housing placement or universal cash payment.
- **Education:** `education` supports school access/compulsory education while keeping municipal placement, language support, online Ukrainian schooling, higher recognition and fee/funding decisions separate.
- **Benefits:** `social-assistance` covers social/child benefits while `housing` describes housing-specific routes; neither establishes one universal TP benefit. Municipal means tests and program-specific criteria remain in force.

## 7. Unresolved but non-blocking boundaries

These are package-confirmed limits for qualified drafting; they are not findings that the relevant right or process does not exist. Refresh the relevant official source before adding operational claims.

- **Temporary-protection application:** current initial-applicant checklist, biometrics/photo, fee, appointment, processing time, and categorical address requirement are not established; use only supported authority/document distinctions and current replacement instructions.
- **Transition/residence:** TP-only time counting toward permanent residence, TP as family sponsor, a dedicated transition title/window, concurrent TP effect of another permit, and some route-specific filing outcomes remain unestablished.
- **Employment:** exact TP-document/cohort edge cases for employer reporting, VMI documentation for self-employment, regulated-profession authorization, and UŽT prerequisites for every TP cohort should be confirmed. Ukrainian insurance-period aggregation and open intake/remaining capacity for Ukraine-specific employment projects are not established.
- **Healthcare:** exact CHI registry start date/document display; full CHI for someone working while a TP application is pending; routine maternity/postnatal, dental and mental-health access for uninsured TP; dependant eligibility and borderline urgent-care charges need individual VLK/provider confirmation.
- **Housing:** current national reception availability/capacity, additional accommodation extension, local declaration checklist, current rent-compensation calculations/funding, deposit cap and uniform municipal one-time support are not established nationally.
- **Education:** current school-level intake and no-seat procedure, uniform enrollment documents, current language-support hours, Ukrainian online-only exception, exact VSF cohort/continuation eligibility, and current scholarship availability require local/institution-specific confirmation.
- **Social assistance:** personal-code/SPIS authentication routes, universal payment method/schedule, unusual household cases, local emergency schemes, municipal housing-support implementation, benefit appeal/cross-border coordination and foreign disability recognition can require individual municipal/Sodra determinations.
