# Lithuania — Social Assistance and Family Benefits

**Research checked:** 2026-11-09
**Scope:** `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, `social-child-benefits`
**Research basis:** current official Lithuanian ministry, SPIS, Sodra, and legislation sources. This is a national rules review, not an individual eligibility decision. Amounts and eligibility are stated as at the research date.

## Scope

This review covers national means-tested cash social assistance, utility compensation, municipal discretion and services, application channels and residence/address rules, child and family benefits, school support, Ukraine-specific measures, and the distinction between municipal benefits and social-insurance payments. It does not determine individual eligibility, cover every municipal supplement, or replace a municipality’s decision.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** A person granted temporary protection (TP) in Lithuania is expressly within the eligible status group for national means-tested cash social assistance and statutory child/family benefits. TP does not itself guarantee a payment: the ordinary cash assistance remains subject to household, income, property, work/activity and application rules; each child-related scheme has its own conditions. In 2026 the principal national cash routes include the social benefit and heating/water compensation, the universal child benefit and a conditional additional child benefit, plus specified birth, pregnancy and non-insurance childcare benefits. Applications and first-instance decisions for these municipal benefits are generally handled by the municipality; SPIS is an electronic application/calculation channel, not the decision-maker. Sodra separately decides insurance benefits for people with the requisite insured status and contribution history.

For Ukrainians with TP, current 2026 official information also lists a one-time accommodation/settlement allowance subject to specified applicant and registered-lease conditions, a TP-specific part-rent compensation route distinct from ordinary means-tested rental support, six-month compensation for children’s pre-school education, and ordinary child benefits. Municipalities may additionally provide one-off, targeted, periodic or conditional support from local funds. None is an unconditional recurring “Ukraine cash payment.”

## Subsistence / ordinary welfare boundaries

- **Main national cash route:** the social benefit plus compensation of eligible housing heating, drinking-water and hot-water costs. A TP grant places a foreign national in the statutory status group, but does not remove the resource tests. [S01–S04, S10]
- **2026 reference values:** state-supported income (VRP/SSI) €233. Social-benefit income threshold: counted average below 1.1 VRP = €256.30 per household member/month. Single-person calculation reference is 1.4 VRP (€326.20) up to six months, 1.2 (€279.60) for 6–12 months, then 1.1 (€256.30), minus counted income. Family formulas vary by household-member rank. These are formulas/references, not guaranteed awards. [S01–S02, S16]
- **Property:** property value must ordinarily stay within the statutory/municipal norm. The authority counts property specified in law and computes norm values using place-of-residence/rented-dwelling territory; individual holdings and exceptions require municipal calculation. [S02, S10]
- **Utilities:** heating eligibility uses household size, income, housing-area and consumption norms. The 2026 formula protects a specified share above 2 VRP for a family or 3 VRP for a single resident; drinking-water and hot-water thresholds are 2% and 5% of income respectively. [S01–S02]
- **No general minimum-income benefit tied to TP alone:** there is no basis to describe these schemes as a flat allowance payable on arrival. Other contributory benefits/pensions and disability support have separate statutes and conditions; this review does not collapse them into social assistance. [S02–S05, S13–S14]
- **In-kind / services:** non-monetary forms exist under long-term social-benefit rules and municipalities separately organize needs-assessed services and local material support. Explain the nature and local assessment; do not call accommodation, food aid or a social service cash income. [S01, S03–S04]

## Means / income / household rules

- **Household:** “persons living together” is a legal concept (including spouses/registered partners, dependent children and specified common-household members), not simply everyone at the same address. A qualifying household may include certain studying adult children; children under formal guardianship are treated by specific rules. Do not calculate per capita income by counting every roommate or automatically combining unrelated tenants. [S02]
- **Income:** 2026 social-benefit threshold is below 1.1 VRP (€256.30 counted income per person monthly). Child benefit itself is excluded from social-benefit income. A share of work/self-employment income and unemployment-insurance benefit is excluded: 20% for childless family/single; 25% for 1–2 children in family; 30% for 3+ children in family; 35% for one person raising 1–2 children; 40% for one person raising 3+ children. Other income may count. [S01–S02, S16]
- **Assets:** a household’s legally enumerated property is assessed against the property norm, with statutory first-application and other exceptions. This is not simply a bank-balance test or only Lithuanian real estate; municipality computes under current law. [S02, S10]
- **Employment/activity:** adults and 16–17-year-olds must meet a listed statutory qualifying circumstance. UŽT registration is a common route for a capable unemployed adult, but is not universal: working, studying, disability, pension, caregiving, pregnancy/childcare and defined other statuses can satisfy/exempt the test. Do not impose a job search on a parent or household member who meets an exception. [S02]
- **Award duration and prolonged unemployment:** normal social-benefit award is three months. For capable working-age non-employed recipients, reductions can apply after 12 months (20%, then 30%, 40%, 50% by periods); after 60 months a reduced amount may be non-cash, subject to exceptions and participation rules. This does not apply uniformly to every household/member or to the universal child benefit. [S01–S02]
- **Heating/water calculations:** separate utility compensation has its own housing-size, use and expense norms; being under the social-benefit income limit is not by itself a guarantee that all costs will be paid. [S01–S02]

Apply each benefit’s own age, residence, parent/child and circumstance rules; entitlement to one benefit does not transfer to another. [S04–S07, S11]

TP status alone does not meet service/work conditions. [S05–S06] |
| Multiple-birth benefit | For twins, 4 BSA/BSI monthly for the shared birth group (2026 BSI €74 = €296); increased accordingly for each additional child, until age 2. | Specific statutory family benefit; confirm municipality’s claim/document requirements and TP status route. Not part of the universal child-money amount. [S05–S06] |
| Single-parent / large-family support | There is **not one blanket single-parent child allowance** established here. Single parents may qualify for the means-tested child supplement, and family composition changes the work-income disregard for social assistance; 3+ child households qualify for the additional child benefit irrespective of income. Other specific childcare/pregnancy support has its own test. | Do not describe all single parents or all families as having a separate guaranteed cash supplement. [S01–S02, S05–S06] |
| Pupil support | National free meals and school-supply assistance are available under pupil-support rules. Pre-primary and grades 1–2 receive free lunch without income assessment; other pupil aid is generally based on per-person income below 1.5 SSI (€349.50), with defined exceptions up to 2 SSI (€466); municipalities may assist in specified cases up to 2.5 SSI (€582.50). | SADM’s 2026 Ukraine guide says school-age Ukrainian families can access school support. Exact request, school-supplies award and local paperwork are municipal/school-administered; do not promise automatic free meals beyond the stated grade exception. [S04, S12] |
| Disability-related child benefit | Additional child benefit is available for a child with disability under the statutory recognized disability category; individual disability recognition / documents have separate process. | No automatic equivalence from a foreign document should be asserted for every benefit. The SADM family-benefit page states BSA/BSI is €74 and SSI/VRP €233 in 2026. Amounts are indexed and scheme-specific; check dates where a law changed mid-year. [S05–S06]

## Ukraine-specific support: current vs historical

**Current official 2026 measures identified:**

1. **General cash social assistance:** individual TP permit holders may apply for national means-tested social benefit and heating/drinking/hot-water compensation on the same statutory framework, subject to its ordinary criteria. This is not a Ukrainian-only fixed grant. [S01–S04]
2. **Child and family benefits:** statutory child benefits, including child money, additional child benefit and other separately qualified family benefits, are available to eligible TP households. The benefit-specific conditions still apply. [S04–S07]
3. **One-time accommodation/settlement allowance:** current SADM 2026 guide describes a one-off allowance for specified TP household members: people working in Lithuania or registered with the Employment Service, persons studying in general education, people of retirement age, single pregnant women, or single parents raising a child under 2. Amount depends on number of co-resident TP holders. Apply through the municipality; submit a residential lease for at least six months, registered in the public register, stating how many TP holders live there. This is a restricted one-time measure, not a universal recurring allowance. [S03–S04]
4. **Rent compensation:** distinguish ordinary means-tested rental support from the TP-specific partial-rent compensation route. The Foreigners Law provides a TP-specific route exempt from specified ordinary Housing Support Law income/property and lease-duration criteria; an application, qualifying TP status, rental agreement evidence/registration and local implementation still matter. Amount and operation are handled under the statutory framework locally; do not substitute ordinary rent-compensation criteria or promise a fixed payment. [S03–S04, S18–S19]
5. **Pre-school education compensation:** the 2026 Ukraine guide lists six months’ compensation for eligible children’s pre-school education for TP holders; it does not establish indefinite or automatic payment. The guide identifies an income test below 1.5 SSI (€349.50 per person/month); the municipality confirms qualifying child age/placement, application evidence and start/end date. [S03–S04]
6. **Municipal discretionary support and social services:** a municipality may provide one-time, targeted, periodic or conditional local assistance and needs-assessed services. Contact local social assistance/social service contacts; amounts, funds, process and in-kind help vary. [S01, S03, S20]
7. **School support:** official national pupil assistance includes free meals/school-supply support under the income/grade rules, with the 2026 Ukraine guide expressly identifying Ukrainian school-age families. [S04, S12]
8. **Accommodation while a TP application is pending:** separate national temporary-accommodation rules may provide up to six months of free state/municipal accommodation for the covered applicant group from TP residence-permit application registration, subject to placement/capacity. This is in-kind accommodation under immigration/housing arrangements, not a cash subsistence benefit; see the Lithuania housing research for full rule and limits. [S04; Lithuania housing research, LT-HOUS-S01–S02]

**Historical or not established as active:** early 2022–2025 emergency food/hygiene parcels, child-hygiene kits and other arrival aid are not described in the current 2026 SADM guide as a uniform open recurring national programme for current TP residents. Municipalities/NGOs may provide case-specific aid, but do not promise historical emergency kits or vouchers. No separate active universal monthly Ukraine-only cash allowance was found. This review does not treat older announcements as evidence of current funding or intake. [S03–S04]

## Employment / benefit interaction

- **Work does not automatically end social assistance:** if counted per-person income remains below threshold, property and household tests pass, and activity conditions are met, a person may continue to qualify. Part of work income is disregarded at the statutory 20–40% rate according to household composition/children. A separate additional in-work benefit may be possible after meeting its prior-benefit, employment, registration/exemption and application deadline conditions. [S01–S02, S16]
- **UŽT:** registration can satisfy the activity condition for an otherwise unemployed working-age person and is expressly a condition for some restricted Ukraine one-off settlement support. It is not a blanket requirement for all social assistance or child benefits; statutory exemptions apply. [S02–S04]
- **Unemployment benefit:** this is social insurance, not a means-tested municipal “unemployment assistance” payment. UŽT establishes unemployed status; Sodra decides/pays where insurance/contribution conditions are satisfied. [S13–S14]
- **Interaction:** the 2026 Ministry information states that unemployment-insurance benefit and a portion of work income are excluded when calculating cash social assistance; the exact exclusion and other counted-income rules must be checked against the current household circumstances. Child money is excluded from social-assistance income. Receiving social benefit does not itself establish Sodra insurance eligibility; conversely, Sodra benefit eligibility is not based solely on poverty/TP. [S01–S02, S13–S14, S16]
- **Report changes:** income, work, household and property changes may affect an award and its reassessment; the applicant should answer municipal requests and report changes under the relevant award conditions. Do not claim one identical reporting deadline across every benefit. [S02, S06]

## Municipal variation

National law sets core cash-assistance and child-benefit rules, but the municipality is the local decision and service administration. Municipal councils can set additional one-off, targeted, periodic or conditional benefits, and determine local procedure, evidence, social-service access/fees and local budget limits. A discretionary payment in one city cannot be presented as nationwide. For TP holders without declared residence, a cash-assistance application goes to the municipality of the no-declared-residence registration or, if none, factual residence; a tenant applies where the rented dwelling is located. [S01–S04, S20]

## 2026 changes / stale guidance

- **2026 indexation:** VRP/SSI = €233; BSA/BSI = €74. The 2026 social-benefit per-person threshold is €256.30; universal child money is €129.50 monthly; the additional child benefit is €76.22; one-off pregnancy benefit €475.82; 14-BSI birth grant is €1,036 from 2026-06-01. Use correct effective dates and formulas. [S01–S02, S05–S06, S16]
- **Childcare change effective 2026-06-01:** SADM’s current family page distinguishes a former education/study-linked childcare allowance through 2026-05-31 from the non-insurance childcare benefit from 2026-06-01. Current rule: 6 BSI (€444) per month until age 2 for the covered parent/adoptive parent/guardian not entitled to insured childcare benefit, subject to no duplicate specified Sodra/adoption benefit for same child/period. Older summaries describing the payment solely as a student allowance are stale after May. [S05–S06]
- **TP status evidence:** SADM’s current support-by-status guide distinguishes collective protection/initial registration from an individual TP residence permit for cash assistance and child benefits. Do not use older emergency-stage status tables as current permission to claim all cash supports while awaiting individual status. [S03–S04]
- **Legislation version caution:** the e-Seimas child-benefit law result reviewed displays consolidation through 2026-05-31, despite SADM guidance incorporating changes effective June 1. SADM detailed guidance was updated 2026-06-01 and is the operational source for post-June child benefit amounts and rules. The money-social-assistance statute’s consolidated-edition listing may likewise lag; check the operative act and current ministry page for an individual claim. [S05–S06, S10–S11]
- **Ukraine emergency programmes:** current official 2026 guidance describes continuing eligibility for named TP-related national schemes, but not the historical 2022 emergency food/hygiene aid as an open universal entitlement. Verify local-funded programme intake with the municipality. [S03–S04]
- **SPIS:** current ministry description confirms electronic service use but does not establish a universal personal-code exemption, identical authentication route or cross-municipal payment method. Avoid stale one-route instructions; check the current SPIS authentication and municipal acceptance channel. [S08–S09]

## Unresolved issues

1. **Personal code / online authentication edge cases:** SPIS requires authenticated identity, but the reviewed national public guidance does not establish that lack of a Lithuanian personal code legally bars a TP holder from applying in person, nor does it give one guaranteed authentication path for every foreign digital permit. Municipality/SPIS must confirm the applicant’s individual route.
2. **Exact payment method:** public national guidance reviewed here does not state a single disbursement method, account requirement or schedule applicable to all municipal cash and child-benefit claims. Confirm with municipal decision-maker; Sodra’s separate method is scheme-specific.
3. **Household treatment of recently arrived or shared accommodation:** law defines household members beyond address alone, but unusual co-residence, separated family, missing civil records, or a TP household split across municipalities requires municipal assessment. Do not infer roommate income is pooled or ignored without the statutory relationship facts.
4. **Municipal emergency awards/services:** no national directory of every local award, amount, funding status, evidence list or processing time was found. SADM provides contacts, but entitlement remains local.
5. **Ukraine rent and pre-school benefit specifics:** the TP-specific rent route is distinct from ordinary means-tested rent support, but current municipal checklist, proof/registration, calculation and available funding need local confirmation. The 2026 SADM summary confirms six-month preschool compensation and its income threshold but not every case-specific interaction, child-placement threshold or document substitution. Confirm details locally and do not promise a fixed amount beyond the published conditions.
6. **Child-benefit appeal deadline / cross-border cases:** national administrative appeal routes and deadlines were not independently established for every individual family-benefit type; EU coordination priority rules may apply if a parent works/resides in another EU state. Follow the formal decision notice and competent municipality/Sodra instructions.
7. **Foreign disability evidence:** child-disability supplement depends on legally recognized disability status. Recognition/equivalence and evidence are a separate process; foreign evidence alone should not be presented as automatically triggering a supplement.

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- A person **granted temporary protection** in Lithuania is in the eligible status category for national means-tested cash social assistance and child benefits, subject to each scheme’s other rules.
- National low-income cash assistance includes a means-tested social benefit and heating, drinking-water and hot-water compensation.
- The 2026 per-person social-benefit income threshold is below €256.30 counted average monthly income; property and statutory activity/circumstance requirements also apply.
- Work does not automatically end the award; 20–40% of certain work/unemployment-insurance income is disregarded according to household circumstances, while other tests continue.
- Cash-social-assistance and eligible child-benefit applications can be made to the relevant municipality, with SPIS as an electronic application route; the municipality decides.
- Without a declared address, cash-assistance rules identify the non-declarant-register municipality or, if the person is not listed there, the municipality where they actually live; renters use the municipality of the rented dwelling.
- The 2026 child money amount is €129.50 per month per eligible child; the conditional additional benefit is €76.22, under its own income/large-family/disability criteria.
- A one-off birth benefit is €1,036 from 2026-06-01; pregnant-woman, childcare, multiple-birth and nanny-compensation schemes each have separate requirements.
- Sodra insurance benefits are separate and require insured status and the scheme’s contribution/eligibility record; TP alone does not grant unemployment/maternity/childcare insurance benefit.
- Municipalities may offer additional needs-based local assistance or services, but local terms and availability vary.

### Require qualification

- Describe social benefit with household, counted income, property, qualifying activity, formula, duration and municipality assessment—not as guaranteed support for every TP holder.
- Present utility compensation separately from cash social benefit; eligibility and calculation use housing/consumption norms.
- State that the additional child benefit is conditional: low per-person income **or** 3+ children **or** recognized child disability, with age/education and calculation rules.
- Distinguish SADM’s one-time TP settlement support, rent compensation and six-month preschool support from ordinary welfare; specify lease, status, applicant and local conditions.
- Give Ukraine-specific support only based on the 2026 official guide and current municipal confirmation; do not reuse a closed or historically announced emergency scheme.
- Say SPIS is an application platform, not the benefits authority; exact online credentials and payment method depend on the current service/municipality.
- For school support, distinguish the no-means-test pre-primary/grades 1–2 free lunch from means-tested assistance for other pupils.
- For childcare-related national benefits, use the post-2026-06-01 regime and distinguish non-insurance municipal family benefits from Sodra insurance benefits.

### Do not assert

- That TP, poverty, Ukrainian nationality, a migration registration receipt, or a temporary shelter address automatically results in a cash award.
- That €256.30 is a flat monthly payment, an absolute universal household cut-off, or a guarantee independent of property/activity requirements.
- That all income is counted, all work terminates social support, or every adult must register with UŽT without statutory exceptions.
- That a single-parent or large-family household automatically receives every supplement or a separate universal single-parent child benefit.
- That child-money award establishes eligibility for birth, pregnancy, childcare, disability, school, rental or social-insurance benefits.
- That Sodra, SPIS or a national ministry makes the local means-tested award; municipality decides that application.
- That local emergency support has a uniform national amount or that historical food/hygiene packs, cash grants or temporary programmes remain open.
- That TP alone gives unemployment, maternity or childcare insurance benefit, or that the no-address rule for cash assistance automatically applies identically to every housing/family benefit.
- That every municipal payment requires a Lithuanian bank account, that a personal code is a universal legal eligibility test, or that every benefit has one payment and appeal deadline.
