## Important limitations and unresolved questions

- UVM’s current Ukraine pages state that the temporary municipal flexibility rules for school education expired on 17 March 2026, while the separate law for some virtual and English/Ukrainian teaching remains. Reception classes under specific rules are still described. These are different provisions; do not summarize them as either “all exceptions ended” or “all Ukraine-specific arrangements continue.”
- For children whose Ukraine Special Act application is pending, the reviewed sources do not resolve every residence pattern. In particular, children accommodated at an asylum centre are expressly subject to a different legal framework. The draft avoids giving a blanket answer and directs families to an individual assessment.
- The reviewed official course framework says residence basis determines language-course placement and S-course participants generally pay a deposit, with exceptions. It does not conclusively classify every Ukraine Special Act permit holder or pending applicant. The article therefore does not promise free language classes or state a fee amount.
- The UCPH tuition and application-deposit exemption is institution-specific. No national tuition exemption for every Ukraine Special Act permit holder was established by the reviewed sources. Check the target programme and institution directly.
- SU equal status is an application route, not an automatic consequence of temporary protection. The SU conditions and responsible administration may change; confirm current instructions with the institution and SU/Udbetaling Danmark.
- L 43 was introduced on 8 October 2026 and remained a bill on 15 October 2026. Do not treat its text or proposed commencement dates as law. Recheck status and the relevant amended provisions immediately before publication. The source was indexed and its bill text/status reviewed through Retsinformation; the live page did not render in the browser reader, so direct page rendering remains a publication-time link check.
- Some official pages are undated. An undated page was still checked on 15 October 2026; absence of a displayed update date is not evidence that its contents cannot change.
- Exact municipality-specific application dates, waiting times, childcare prices, language-course offers, VUC schedules and programme admission rules vary. The article intentionally sends readers to the relevant local authority or institution.
