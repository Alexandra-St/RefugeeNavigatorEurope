# Accessing Healthcare in Poland

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Since 5 March 2026, NFZ care depends on insurance status or a defined temporary-protection category. Check your coverage before routine treatment.


## RULES

From 5 March 2026, the former special healthcare rules changed. NFZ says temporary-protection beneficiaries with PESEL UKR have access to a defined range of free care, including primary healthcare (POZ) and reimbursed medicines, if they are under 18, pregnant or in the postnatal period, survivors of torture or rape, or hold a certificate confirming residence in collective accommodation. This is not access to every public healthcare service.

## TASKS

Full public health-insurance coverage may apply where there is a compulsory-insurance basis, for example certain employment contracts, registration as unemployed or lawful business activity. Other bases and exceptions depend on the law and individual status. PESEL UKR alone does not mean everyone has the same coverage.

## PLACES

For routine care, contact a primary healthcare (POZ) provider or use the NFZ provider directory. Before an appointment, ask whether the provider accepts your document or insurance proof and whether a referral is required. Prescription and specialist-care rules depend on eligibility for the particular service; confirm them with the clinician or NFZ.

## NEXT STEPS

For an immediate threat to life, call 112 or 999. For an urgent problem outside POZ hours, you can contact the nearest night and holiday healthcare point (NiŚOZ). Do not delay emergency care because you are unsure about an insurance document; after urgent needs are addressed, ask NFZ or your insurer about coverage and costs.
