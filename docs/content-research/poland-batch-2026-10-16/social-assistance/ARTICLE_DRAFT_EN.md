# Financial and Social Assistance

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Benefits have different conditions and responsible institutions. From 2026, 800+ includes work-activity and schooling conditions with exceptions; PESEL UKR alone does not automatically qualify someone for general social assistance.


## RULES

There is no single benefit for everyone from Ukraine. 800+, family benefits, housing allowance and general social assistance have different conditions and institutions. PESEL UKR alone does not establish entitlement to a payment. Social assistance for foreign nationals has statutory conditions relating to residence status and the specific need.

## TASKS

For 800+, the Ministry says the child must live in Poland with a parent and a school-age child must attend the Polish education system. For Ukrainian citizens with UKR status, a work-activity condition has applied since 1 February 2026, with exceptions including parents of children with disabilities and some parents receiving support for a child who is a Polish citizen. A registered unemployed person who is not working may qualify for a limited period. Check your current individual condition with ZUS.

## PLACES

Applications for 800+ go to ZUS, generally online; ZUS verifies the data and decides entitlement. A gmina or ZUS may handle other family benefits depending on the programme. General social assistance is handled through the local OPS/MOPS; prepare identity, residence, income, household and need-related documents. Depending on the family and its circumstances, other programmes may include family allowance, a one-off birth grant, parental benefit or care benefits. Their income, residence and family conditions differ; ask the gmina or ZUS which institution handles the specific programme. If disability, care responsibilities or another urgent need affects the household, tell the local OPS/MOPS when requesting an assessment; an assessment does not guarantee a payment. ## NEXT STEPS

Find the relevant programme on ZUS or gov.pl and check the application period, documents, exceptions and duty to report changes. For a complex or urgent situation, contact an OPS/MOPS social worker. Asking for an assessment can identify possible support, but does not guarantee a payment. If a child is not attending a Polish school or the family has another residence status, ask ZUS to confirm the applicable rules in writing.
