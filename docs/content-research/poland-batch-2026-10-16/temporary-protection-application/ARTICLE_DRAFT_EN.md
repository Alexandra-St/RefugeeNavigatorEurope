# Applying for Temporary Protection in Poland

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Since the changes of 5 March 2026, PESEL UKR is the key Polish record of temporary-protection status. Check the conditions and apply through a gmina office.


## RULES

EU temporary protection, Polish temporary-protection status, a PESEL number marked UKR, an older protection certificate and a CUKR residence card are different legal concepts. Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 extends EU temporary protection to 4 March 2028, with that extension applying from 5 March 2027. Under Article 106 of Poland’s 2026 Act, the stay of a covered beneficiary who entered Poland legally during the period specified in the Council decision for the purpose of temporary protection is lawful while that decision remains in force, unless the Act provides otherwise; statutory exclusions and ending events still apply. Since the decision now extends EU protection through 4 March 2028, Article 106 links the lawful stay of that statutory group to the extended decision period. The Ministry’s 26 March 2026 page stating 4 March 2027 predates the later Council decision and should not be read as overriding Article 106. Separately, UdSC says older paper certificates remain valid until 4 March 2027 subject to earlier-ending events. Do not infer an individual record or document’s validity; ask the competent authority to check it. The 5 August 2026 Official Journal corrigendum to Decision (EU) 2026/1912 corrects the military-obligation dates: Article 2 applies from 5 August 2026, and its exception refers to people who had temporary protection in a particular Member State by 4 August and retained it continuously in that same Member State. UdSC’s page gives the corrected dates. A separate KSI page on gov.pl still displays the superseded 31/30 July dates; it does not change the corrigendum. Check current official instructions and ask the competent authority about an individual case.

## TASKS

To obtain a new PESEL UKR record, apply at any gmina (municipality) office within 30 days after crossing the Polish border. The official service describes an in-person application; prepare the form, a photograph and identity document. Check the current gmina instructions for children, missing documents or other special circumstances.

## PLACES

The gmina office accepts PESEL UKR applications; the Office for Foreigners (UdSC) publishes temporary-protection rules. Since 5 March, UdSC no longer issues the former certificates. UdSC says certificates issued earlier remain valid until 4 March 2027 unless an earlier invalidating event applies or PESEL UKR is assigned to the holder.

## NEXT STEPS

By 31 August 2026, people whose PESEL UKR had been issued only on the basis of a declaration, without showing a valid passport, had to confirm their identity at a gmina office. That deadline has passed. UdSC says that for people covered by the requirement, failure to confirm led to loss of UKR status and Polish temporary protection from 1 September 2026. If this may apply to you, ask the gmina urgently to check your record and obtain individual advice; do not infer your status from the PESEL number alone.
