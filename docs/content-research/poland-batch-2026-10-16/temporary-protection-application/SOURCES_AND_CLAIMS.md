## Claim-by-claim review

| Claim ID | Material statement and source passage | Status | Conditions, exceptions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TP01 | Article 106 of the 2026 Act treats the stay of a covered beneficiary who entered Poland legally during the Council-decision period for temporary protection as lawful until that decision ceases to apply, unless the Act provides otherwise. (LAW2026) | **SUPPORTED** | Subject to arts. 109a–109b exclusions and ending events; do not infer an individual record/document validity. |
| TP02 | Eligible beneficiaries apply for PESEL UKR at a gmina within 30 days of crossing the Polish border. (LAW2026, PESEL) | **SUPPORTED** | The online service page is a prior special service; current statute is controlling. Confirm document checklist locally. |
| TP03 | UdSC stopped issuing prior certificates on 5 March; earlier certificates are stated to remain valid until 4 March 2027 subject to exceptions. (CERT) | **SUPPORTED** | Do not conflate certificate validity with EU protection duration or another permit. |
| TP04 | Declaration-only PESEL UKR holders had to show a valid passport by 31 August 2026; failure means loss of UKR and Polish protection from 1 Sep. (IDENTITY) | **SUPPORTED** | Applies only to the defined cohort; deadline has passed. |
| TP05 | Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 extends EU temporary protection through 4 Mar 2028, applying from 5 Mar 2027. Article 106 of the 2026 Polish Act makes the stay of the defined beneficiary group lawful while that Council decision remains in force, unless the Act provides otherwise. (EUTPACT, LAW2026, UDSC2028) | **SUPPORTED** | Article 106 applies only to the statutory group and is subject to arts. 109a–109b. MRPiPS guidance dated 26 Mar 2026 preceded the later Council decision; it is not a distinct 2028/2027 statutory rule for the covered group. Old paper certificates are a separate transitional rule through 4 Mar 2027, subject to earlier-ending events. No individual record or permit validity is inferred. |
| TP06 | The 5 Aug 2026 corrigendum corrects the Article 2 application date to 5 Aug 2026 and its same-Member-State continuous-protection exception reference date to 4 Aug 2026. (EUTPCORR; UDSC2028) | **SUPPORTED** | The corrigendum is the directly checked primary legal source. A separate KSI gov.pl page still shows the superseded 31/30 Jul wording; the drafts identify that stale guidance and do not treat it as the governing legal text. No individual applicability is inferred. |

| TP07 | Older temporary-protection certificates are stated to remain valid until 4 Mar 2027 subject to earlier-ending events and status changes. (CERT, LAW2026 transitional art. 20) | **SUPPORTED** | This is a document-specific transition and is not the end date of EU protection or Article 106 lawful stay. |
