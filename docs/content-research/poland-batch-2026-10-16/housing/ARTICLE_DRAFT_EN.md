# Finding Accommodation in Poland

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Temporary accommodation and support depend on the group, location and available funding. Private renting is separate; check the agreement and local assistance.


## RULES

From 5 March 2026, public accommodation assistance rules changed. The law provides for short-term crisis support and separate arrangements for defined vulnerable groups. Delivery depends on statutory conditions, available funding and local implementation; a place or housing is not guaranteed to every PESEL UKR holder.

## TASKS

For a private rental, ask for a written agreement in Polish or a translation you understand. Check the landlord’s identity, address, term, rent, utilities, termination rules and the amount and return of any deposit. Do not transfer money based only on an advertisement. Housing allowances have separate income, floor-area and legal-title conditions.

## PLACES

For accommodation assistance, start with your gmina or city office, local social assistance centre (OPS/MOPS) or voivodeship office. The service and documents vary by location. The government housing-allowance service directs applicants to OPS or the local authority; an award and amount depend on checks of eligibility, income, floor area and expenses.

## NEXT STEPS

Keep the rental agreement, payment records, handover report and correspondence. If an administrative matter requires address evidence, ask the authority handling that matter what documents it accepts. Do not assume that address registration, a rental contract, PESEL UKR and entitlement to housing support are interchangeable.
