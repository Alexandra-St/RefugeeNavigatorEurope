## Claim-by-claim review

| Claim ID | Material statement and source passage | Status | Conditions, exceptions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ED01 | Foreign pupils may enter public schools under statutory procedures; prior-school records are used for ordinary admission and the school head assigns the class. (EDUCATIONMEN, EDUACT art. 165) | **SUPPORTED** | Do not imply unconditional admission to every school type; special school types may have additional criteria. The Ministry page has older legal references, so the current statute controls. |
| ED02 | Compulsory preschool preparation applies from the school year in the calendar year a child turns 6; compulsory schooling starts in the school year the child turns 7 and lasts through primary-school completion, no later than 18; the learning obligation lasts until 18. Children from Ukraine have been covered by Polish school rules since September 2024. (EDUACT arts. 31, 35; EDUUKR) | **SUPPORTED** | Schooling/learning obligations are distinct; individual school procedure and any applicable statutory exception must be checked. |
| ED03 | Some school types may have additional admission criteria. (EDUCATIONMEN) | **SUPPORTED** | Not all public school admissions are automatic. |
| ED04 | NAWA information supports recognition processes for foreign secondary credentials but does not guarantee admission. (NAWA) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | Ukrainian-specific recognition and intended use must be checked in the specific case. |
| ED05 | Every adult can access a free Polish course from the state anywhere in Poland. (KBI) | **UNVERIFIED** | Excluded; availability varies by provider/location. |
| ED06 | Pupils subject to the learning obligation who do not know Polish sufficiently are entitled to free additional Polish lessons for up to 24 months; a preparatory class may be organized, and a person knowing the pupil’s language of origin may be employed as a teacher’s assistant for up to 12 months. (EDUACT art. 165) | **SUPPORTED** | This does not guarantee a particular class, assistant or local capacity; school implementation and other statutory conditions apply. An intercultural assistant may be employed under the statutory conditions. |
