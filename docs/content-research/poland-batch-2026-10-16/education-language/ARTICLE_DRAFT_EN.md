# Education and Polish-Language Learning

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Children arriving from abroad can enter Polish public schools using records of prior education; the school determines the grade. Compulsory education and support depend on age and current rules.


## RULES

Compulsory pre-school preparation applies to children who turn six in the relevant calendar year. Compulsory schooling begins in the school year in which a child turns seven and continues until primary school is completed, but no later than age 18; the learning obligation lasts until age 18. Since September 2024, children from Ukraine have been covered by Polish education rules. Ask the school or gmina which procedure applies to the child.

## TASKS

Contact a public school near your home. Bring prior-school records if available; the Ministry says pupils arriving from abroad are admitted on the basis of such records without nostrification for ordinary school admission. If records are missing, ask the headteacher what can be provided instead. The school determines the grade in light of records and circumstances; some school types may apply additional criteria.

## PLACES

Pupils subject to the learning obligation who do not know Polish well enough are entitled to free additional Polish lessons for up to 24 months. A school may organize a preparatory class; a person who knows the pupil’s language of origin may be employed as a teacher’s assistant for up to 12 months. An intercultural assistant may also be employed where the statutory conditions are met. Ask the school what support is available in the pupil’s circumstances. For preschool or childcare, contact the gmina: places, recruitment and care arrangements can differ.

## NEXT STEPS

For university admission, check the institution’s requirements and NAWA information on recognition of your school-leaving certificate. Recognition for admission does not guarantee acceptance; language, competition and programme requirements may apply. Adult Polish courses depend on the city and provider; a universal free state course for everyone was not confirmed in this review.
