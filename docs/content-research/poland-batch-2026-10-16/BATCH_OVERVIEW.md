## Focused findings

- **EU and Polish protection dates:** Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 extends EU temporary protection through 4 March 2028 from 5 March 2027. Article 106 of Poland’s 2026 Act ties the lawful stay of its defined beneficiary group to the period while that Council decision remains in force, subject to statutory exclusions and ending events. The Ministry’s 26 March 2026 guidance stating 4 March 2027 predates the later Council decision; it does not establish a separate current end date for the covered statutory group. Older paper certificates are a distinct transitional document rule stated through 4 March 2027, subject to earlier-ending events. No individual record or permit validity is inferred.
- **Military-obligation dates:** The official 5 August 2026 corrigendum was opened and read directly. It corrects the Article 2 start date from 31 July to **5 August 2026** and the same-Member-State exception reference date from 30 July to **4 August 2026**. Continuous protection in that same Member State is required. UdSC reflects the correction; a KSI gov.pl page still contains the replaced dates, which the drafts attribute as outdated page content.
- **CUKR and other residence procedures:** UdSC confirms four cumulative general CUKR conditions, distinct child rules, the 4 March 2027 application deadline and online MOS filing. MOS permits electronic applications for specified ordinary routes from 27 April 2026 and lists exceptions; the drafts do not imply all residence applications are online or that filing itself grants a permit or automatically changes protection status.
- **Healthcare:** NFZ identifies limited statutory groups and exclusions; compulsory insurance is a separate route to broader public coverage. PESEL UKR alone is not described as guaranteeing comprehensive free care. Emergency and routine/out-of-hours routes remain distinct.
- **Housing:** Act 203, Article 112, allows funded short-term accommodation/meals subject to statutory time limits and sets a separate resource-dependent route for defined vulnerable groups unable to secure housing. Mazowieckie programme details are kept regional. Housing allowance and private tenancy are treated separately.
- **Education:** the in-force Education Law codified PDF prepared 16 September 2026 (incorporating later 2026 items) was checked for age-based duties, school access and additional language support. Free additional Polish lessons may run up to 24 months for pupils within the statutory scope; preparatory classes and assistants are not guaranteed everywhere. Universal free adult Polish courses remain excluded.
- **Social assistance and 800+:** the law says social-assistance benefits may be granted under the Social Assistance Act rules; PESEL UKR alone does not guarantee an award. ZUS’s 2026 guidance describes status/residence, schooling, work-activity checks, exceptions and suspension conditions for 800+. Each benefit has separate eligibility.
- **Employment:** the existing articles remain substantively unchanged. PIP supports the employer notification rule and contract information; the wage amount is time-sensitive. ePraca listings and PUP service navigation are distinguished from guaranteed placement or access to every service.
