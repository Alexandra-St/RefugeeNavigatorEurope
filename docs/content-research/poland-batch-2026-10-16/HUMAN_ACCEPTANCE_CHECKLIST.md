## Article-by-article acceptance

### 1. Applying for Temporary Protection in Poland — Temporary protection

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** PESEL UKR, EU temporary protection, Polish stay under Article 106, and older paper certificates are distinct. Article 106 applies to its defined statutory cohort and remains subject to exceptions. The corrected 5 August / 4 August dates and same-Member-State continuity condition are preserved. The KSI page's superseded 31/30 July dates are attributed as outdated guidance; no individual's record validity is inferred.
- **Important sources to maintain:** Act of 23 January 2026 (Journal of Laws item 203); Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 and its 5 August corrigendum; UdSC guidance; KSI's page displaying superseded dates; Gov.pl PESEL service.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** None recorded from the owner's approval. A person's current PESEL UKR record requires individual confirmation by the competent office.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Keep the Article 106 cohort and exceptions explicit, distinguish the EU protection period from document-specific validity, retain attribution of the stale KSI wording, and make no individual-status determination.

### 2. Residence Options After Temporary Protection — Temporary protection

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** CUKR conditions and child-specific rules remain distinct; MOS filing deadlines and eligible application routes are not generalized. Filing an application is not the same as receiving a permit, and an individual application does not establish its effect on protection status.
- **Important sources to maintain:** Act 203; UdSC CUKR procedure and MOS guidance; UdSC guidance on changes to Ukrainian citizens' stay and older certificates.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Individual effects under the applicable statutory provisions are outside the general article.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Preserve cumulative CUKR conditions, child distinctions, the stated filing deadline and route limits; do not recommend an individual permit route.

### 3. Finding a Job in Poland — Employment

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** ePraca is a job-search resource. Public employment-service support through PUP is conditional; local service availability and registration requirements must not be presented as identical for everyone.
- **Important sources to maintain:** Ministry ePraca/Central Job Offers Database and National Integration Centres guidance; Public Employment Services jobseeker information; relevant Ministry guidance.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Local PUP service access depends on the person's circumstances and local procedures.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Keep general job-search navigation separate from conditional public-service access; make no unsupported eligibility or legal-right claim.

### 4. Work Authorization and Employee Rights — Employment

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** Work access is tied to lawful stay and the applicable route, not Ukrainian nationality alone. Employer notification to PUP has a stated seven-day period. Contract protections depend on the contract and statutory rules. PLN 4,806 is the date-specific 2026 gross monthly minimum-wage figure.
- **Important sources to maintain:** National Labour Inspectorate lawful-employment and complaint guidance; Ministry pay and working-time information; Act 203; the 2026 minimum-wage regulation.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Refresh the date-specific wage figure when maintaining the article; do not imply every contract has identical rules.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Retain route qualifications, identify employer notification as the employer's responsibility, and distinguish monthly wage figures from hourly or contract calculations.

### 5. Accessing Healthcare in Poland — Healthcare

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** The post-5 March 2026 free-care scope for PESEL UKR applies to specified statutory groups and services. Insurance-based access is a separate route. Emergency care is not described as comprehensive routine coverage; out-of-hours primary care and provider availability vary locally.
- **Important sources to maintain:** NFZ's post-5 March 2026 guidance and provider finder; Patient.gov.pl emergency and out-of-hours guidance; Act 203.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Individual coverage and provider acceptance depend on the person's circumstances and must be checked with NFZ or the provider.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Keep limited statutory groups, exclusions, insurance route and emergency-only guidance distinct; promise no universal free coverage.

### 6. Finding Accommodation in Poland — Housing

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** Act 203 provides bounded, conditional routes; it does not promise every person a free room or long-term housing. The Mazowieckie information is regional. Housing allowance assessment, private tenancy and temporary accommodation are separate matters.
- **Important sources to maintain:** Act 203 Article 112; Gov.pl housing allowance guidance; consolidated Tenant Rights Protection Act; Mazowieckie Voivodeship Office page, explicitly limited to that region.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Local availability and individual funding or vulnerability criteria are assessed by the relevant office.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Preserve duration, funding, statutory and vulnerability qualifications; do not generalize regional services or equate address registration, tenancy and residence status.

### 7. Education and Polish-Language Learning — Education

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** Preschool preparation, compulsory schooling and the learning obligation have distinct age rules. Statutory additional Polish-language support is time-limited; a preparatory class or language-of-origin assistant is not guaranteed at every school. Adult course availability varies.
- **Important sources to maintain:** In-force Education Law codification and listed amendments; Ministry of National Education guidance; NAWA credential-recognition information.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Local capacity, school admission criteria and individual credential recognition depend on the case and location.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Keep the separate age rules and 24-month / 12-month limits; promise no class, assistant, adult course or admission outcome.

### 8. Financial and Social Assistance — Social assistance

- **UK / EN / RU:** Approved by the project owner, 16 October 2026.
- **Critical qualifications:** PESEL UKR alone does not guarantee 800+ or general social assistance. Benefit rules may depend on residence/status, work activity, schooling, evidence and assessment. OPS/MOPS assesses individual need; assessment is not an award.
- **Important sources to maintain:** Act 203 Article 112a; current ZUS 800+ rules and foreign-beneficiary guidance; Ministry 2026 assistance information; Gov.pl social-assistance procedure.
- **Outstanding editorial questions:** Current programme conditions and application periods are time-sensitive; no individual entitlement is determined by this article.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Keep programme-specific conditions and assessment language; do not imply automatic payments or eligibility from PESEL UKR alone.
