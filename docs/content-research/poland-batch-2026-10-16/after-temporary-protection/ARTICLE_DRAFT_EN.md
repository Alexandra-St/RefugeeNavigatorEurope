# Residence Options After Temporary Protection

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** A CUKR card is a separate three-year temporary residence permit with specific conditions. Other permits follow their own rules; no change is automatic.


## RULES

PESEL UKR records temporary-protection status in Poland; it is not itself a temporary or permanent residence permit. A CUKR card is a separate three-year temporary residence permit for a defined group of people with UKR status. Under the 2026 Act, Polish temporary protection ends on specified events, including the grant of certain residence permits or international protection. Filing an application alone is not the same event; check the effect of your specific procedure before applying. Do not assume that a general protection date automatically extends another document.

## TASKS

UdSC lists four cumulative CUKR conditions: valid PESEL UKR when applying; valid UKR status on 4 June 2025; valid UKR status when the card is issued; and at least 365 uninterrupted days with UKR status. Children born in Poland have separate conditions. Applications may be filed until 4 March 2027. Check the full requirements, fees and consequences in the UdSC procedure before starting.

## PLACES

A CUKR application is filed only online through MOS (Moduł Obsługi Spraw). Since 27 April 2026, MOS has accepted electronic applications for temporary residence, permanent residence and EU long-term resident permits; specified categories, including some applicants outside Poland, still file on paper. Check whether your procedure is an exception. From 5 March 2026, the special basis for certain simplified permits under the Ukraine assistance law ended; UdSC says the general Foreigners Act requirements apply again.

## NEXT STEPS

Before choosing a procedure, check your document, basis of stay, work, study, family circumstances and deadlines with UdSC, MOS and the competent voivode. The authority decides each case. Do not withdraw or submit parallel applications based only on a general summary: legal effects depend on the application type and case stage.
