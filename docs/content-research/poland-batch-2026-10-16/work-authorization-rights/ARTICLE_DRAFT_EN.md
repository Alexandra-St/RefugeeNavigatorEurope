# Work Authorization and Employee Rights

> Research draft — unpublished; pending human editorial review. No content in this draft is approved or marked Verified.

**Summary:** Work eligibility depends on lawful stay and the applicable route. For Ukrainian citizens, an employer generally must notify the PUP within the statutory period.


## RULES

The law taking effect on 5 March 2026 changed special assistance rules, but work eligibility still depends on lawful stay and the specific status. The State Labour Inspectorate (PIP) says Ukrainian citizens with temporary protection have access to the labour market; the employer must notify the powiat employment office through praca.gov.pl within 7 days after work starts. For other Ukrainian citizens, lawful stay and labour-market access must be checked separately.

## TASKS

An agreement with a foreign worker must be in writing before work begins, and the worker must receive a copy. If you do not speak Polish, the employer must provide the written agreement in a language you understand before you sign. Check pay, duties, schedule, workplace and dates before signing.

## PLACES

In 2026 the national monthly minimum wage is PLN 4,806; separate rules apply to part-month work and some contract types. Working-time rules have exceptions depending on the work system and role. Check gross pay and hours against the applicable contract rather than comparing only take-home pay.

## NEXT STEPS

Keep your agreement, schedules, payslips and messages. Contact the State Labour Inspectorate (PIP) about employee rights, pay or whether work was lawfully assigned. An employer notification does not replace checking your own status. If your documents or terms are unclear, ask PIP or a qualified adviser to review your circumstances.
