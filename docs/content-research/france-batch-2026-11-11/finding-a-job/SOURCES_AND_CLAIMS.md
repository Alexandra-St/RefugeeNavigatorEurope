# France — Finding a Job

Research checked: 2026-11-11
Baseline: `43ae9c177c62cc6c5630aa61284455f3cff04de2`
Scope: exactly `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, `employment-assistance-conditions`.

This is an evidence record, not applicant-facing article text. It covers job search, public employment services and employment-related assistance for a person under temporary protection (TP) in France. It does not cover employment rights, pay, contracts or residence transitions.

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS** for a practical article on where to search for work and what support may be available.

Official sources establish free public vacancy browsing, including vacancies from France Travail and selected partners; the ability of a person with a current French TP APS authorizing salaried work to register with France Travail; public-facing job-search support; and conditional routes to training funding. France Travail's official Ukraine page and a live, dedicated intake form add Ukraine-specific signposting. A current APS-BPT is a qualifying work-authorizing document for registration, but the public guidance does not give a complete Ukraine-specific registration checklist. If a social-security number is missing, France Travail says the person must later visit an agency to update the file. Registration is not an award of ARE or another benefit [FR-JOB-S02–S06, S19–S20].

The article can describe the main channels and assistance if it says that adviser actions, workshops, course places and funding depend on individual assessment, programme criteria and local availability. The Ukraine-specific explainer has no visible current update date; its detailed appointment and interpreting arrangements should be treated as unverified/legacy unless reconfirmed. The dedicated online contact form was live when checked on 2026-11-11, but it is not formal France Travail registration and does not guarantee a dedicated adviser, course, job or funding [FR-JOB-S03–S04, S12–S18].

## France Travail registration

### TP beneficiary eligibility

**Yes, conditionally.** France Travail says a non-EU adult aged 19 or over must hold a current document issued by France that authorizes salaried work; that can include a valid APS where the APS authorizes the activity [FR-JOB-S02]. The French TP APS expressly authorizes professional activity [FR-JOB-S06], and France Travail's Ukraine page confirms the APS allows work [FR-JOB-S03]. A person must also actually reside in France, be seeking work and be available for the employment/adviser process; the general international guidance requires an email address [FR-JOB-S05]. Under 19, France Travail's foreign-worker page describes a separate document/age rule; this does not dispense with any separate work authorization required to take a job [FR-JOB-S02].

### Documents and process

- **Online or in person:** Formal registration is normally requested online through “S’inscrire / Se réinscrire.” France Travail checks most work documents automatically, but can ask for an agency appointment to inspect the original APS [FR-JOB-S02, S05, S08].
- **Stay document:** Be ready to enter the French document number (AGDREF number) and expiry date. The valid APS-BPT is the relevant document if it continues to authorize work [FR-JOB-S02, S06].
- **Identity:** France Travail requires identity verification. The general France Travail sign-up page lists identity documents and basic personal details [FR-JOB-S07–S08].
- **Social-security number:** France Travail says a provisional or permanent number is useful; if the person has none at the time of online registration, an agency visit will be needed later to update the file [FR-JOB-S05]. No official source reviewed establishes that the lack of this number alone prevents submitting the initial request.
- **Address:** A person must live in France and give a place of residence. The sources reviewed do not establish one national proof-of-address document checklist specifically for TP registration or the exact procedure for an unsettled address [FR-JOB-S05].
- **Bank account/RIB:** A RIB is listed as useful if an allocation is payable; it is not established as a general precondition for seeking employment services [FR-JOB-S08].
- **First appointment:** The first interview/assessment is used to agree an individualized employment plan. The adviser may identify workshops, offers/recruiters or training needs; not every listed service is automatic [FR-JOB-S16].

Registration gives the status of demandeur d’emploi and access to the relevant France Travail services, subject to the person's obligations and assessment. It does not promise a placement, training funding, or an unemployment payment. The registration form can include a separate ARE application, which is examined separately under benefit rules [FR-JOB-S08, S26].

## TP / Ukraine-specific support

- **Current, direct signposting:** France Travail maintains a Ukraine-specific page that states a TP APS permits work and points displaced Ukrainians to employment support and language resources [FR-JOB-S03]. The page has no visible 2026 update date; its detailed appointment/interpreting workflow is therefore treated as **legacy/qualification**, not a national guarantee.
- **Current as checked:** the official France Travail-domain Ukrainian displaced-person form was live on 2026-11-11 [FR-JOB-S04]. It allows an expression of interest for contact about job search/training and asks for consent to contact and specified data use. It does not replace ordinary jobseeker registration or guarantee a follow-up.
- **Language resources:** the Interior Ministry still lists the “Travailler en France” professional French MOOC and related Ukraine pages, but the Ukraine page is from the 2022 emergency response with no current update date [FR-JOB-S22]. Use it only as a qualified online signpost after confirming its links/content still work; do not claim a current France Travail-funded course or Ukrainian-language instruction.
- **Dedicated Ukrainian adviser/programme:** current evidence supports an intake/contact route, not an entitlement to a dedicated adviser, interpreter, job offer or training. Ask France Travail/local agency to confirm what is currently available [FR-JOB-S03–S04].
- **Young adults:** the Ukraine page suggests local Mission Locale support for people under 25 [FR-JOB-S03]. This is a local network option and not a uniform Ukraine-only programme.

## Benefits boundary

**France Travail registration ≠ automatic unemployment benefit.** Registration and ARE may be submitted together, but France Travail assesses ARE separately. Eligibility includes separate work-history/affiliation, job-loss, residence, availability and search conditions [FR-JOB-S08, S26]. This package does not establish entitlement for any individual TP holder, calculate ARE, or research RSA/other social assistance. Future copy must not imply registration guarantees ARE, RSA or any payment.

## Regulated professions / recognition

Some occupations are regulated and may require a specific qualification, recognition or authorization from the competent authority. France Travail vacancy search, APS work authorization and general job-search support do not replace that requirement [FR-JOB-S24–S25]. ENIC-NARIC comparability is academic guidance; it does not license a regulated occupation [FR-JOB-S24]. Detailed profession-by-profession rules are outside this package.

## Open gaps

- A single exhaustive France Travail registration checklist for TP APS holders was not found. Current guidance gives the core eligibility and process but does not list a universal proof-of-address set.
- The exact handling of every TP holder who has no social-security number is not fully detailed; France Travail says an agency visit is needed later to update the file. Initial online filing appears possible, but that interaction should be confirmed with the agency for individual cases.
- No current national instruction says every bank account/RIB is required for job-search registration; sources list it for payment if a benefit is payable.
- The Ukraine page's interpreting/appointment details have no visible 2026 update. The separate Ukrainian intake form was live but makes no guaranteed-response commitment.
- It is not established that the DGEF “first residence title” French-course eligibility explicitly includes APS-BPT. No universal, free language-training entitlement for TP holders was found.
- Course places, AIF decisions, POEI recruitment needs, adviser services and local events vary by territory and individual assessment.
- France Travail and EURES do not claim to list every vacancy in France/the EEA.
- This research does not determine an individual's ARE or other social-benefit entitlement.
- Detailed rules for regulated professions remain outside scope.

## Editorial exclusions

Future article must not say, without further evidence:

- France Travail guarantees a job or that one portal contains all vacancies in France.
- A person is formally registered just because they browsed offers or submitted the Ukraine contact form.
- Any TP holder automatically receives a dedicated Ukrainian-speaking adviser, interpreter, job match, workshop or course.
- Registration automatically grants ARE, RSA or another payment.
- France Travail pays for any course a person selects or a training place is guaranteed.
- POEI is an open training course without an employer/job opportunity.
- A 2022 Ukraine emergency course or contact workflow is a currently guaranteed service without checking its active status.
- APS alone satisfies authorization requirements for a regulated profession.
- EURES grants work authorization or residence rights in another country.
