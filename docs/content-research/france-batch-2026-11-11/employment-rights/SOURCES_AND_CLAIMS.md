# France — Employment Rights During Temporary Protection

Research checked: 2026-11-11

Baseline: `bce029f21eac15408374545d6b5dc154633f6d2e`

Scope: exactly `work-permission`, `work-employer-duties`, `work-minimum-pay`.

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS**

French legislation and current official guidance establish that a valid APS bearing the temporary-protection beneficiary wording itself opens the right to exercise professional activity. For salaried hiring, the employer does not apply for a separate work authorization, but must still perform the general prior authenticity check with the prefecture (subject to the statutory France Travail registration exception) and keep the relevant title copy with the personnel register. Ordinary employment and minimum-wage rules apply. Official current sources establish the SMIC rate in force on 2026-11-11. Qualify the scope to a currently valid TP APS and to the activity and profession rules that separately apply; an expired document or merely pending initial application is not established as sufficient evidence to work.

## Required distinctions

- TP work right under a valid APS-BPT ≠ ordinary `salarié` / `travailleur temporaire` residence route.
- APS-BPT itself opens the TP professional-activity right; it is not an application for a separate employer `autorisation de travail`.
- Exemption from a separate work-authorization application ≠ exemption from employer authenticity verification.
- DPAE is an ordinary employee hiring/social-registration declaration ≠ immigration work authorization.
- Social-security number or tax administration ≠ source of work permission.
- Gross SMIC ≠ net pay.
- SMIC ≠ every sector’s final applicable minimum; check the collective agreement.
- TP work authorization ≠ a licence, regulated-profession recognition or occupational qualification.
- Ordinary employment rights apply to TP workers as employees, subject to the same statutory exceptions and collective rules.

## Readiness per aspect

### `work-permission`

Status: **Ready with qualification**

Evidence: CESEDA Article R. 581-4 and current Service-Public guidance establish right from holding APS-BPT and no separate employer work-authorization application for salaried employment; EU TP Article 12 separately names self-employment.

Qualification: Limit the practical instruction to a currently valid TP APS/equivalent accepted official evidence. Pending initial claims and expired APS extension scenarios are not established here. Ordinary professional licensing/registration remains separate. Do not imply ordinary residence transition.

### `work-employer-duties`

Status: **Ready with qualification**

Evidence: Current Service-Public TP APS guidance plus Code du travail establish prefecture verification timeline and France Travail exception, title copy annex, and ordinary hiring/payroll duties; general working-time/leave/equality/safety rules are codified.

Qualification: DPAE and contract-information details vary by employment regime/contract form; collective agreements and statutory derogations can add or modify working conditions. No separate Ukraine-specific employer notification found. General title-copy annex rule is not TP-specific.

### `work-minimum-pay`

Status: **Ready with qualification**

Evidence: 22 May 2026 SMIC order plus Urssaf and Service-Public sources establish the current gross hourly and standard monthly values effective 1 June 2026, territorial difference, minor reductions, special alternate-training grids and reckonable-pay guidance.

Qualification: State metropolitan-France rate explicitly; Mayotte differs. Check the applicable sector collective minimum and exact contract/age scheme. Do not present net figures or calculate a personalized payslip.

## Open gaps

- No broad TP-specific listing of all employer counts, working-hour combinations or employer changes was found. State only that the sources grant professional activity and avoid claiming special limits—or a universal “unlimited” rule.
- A specific general employer process for renewal-pending, expired, lost or replacement TP APS documents was not established here; follow the exact currently valid prefecture/ANEF evidence rather than assuming automatic extension.
