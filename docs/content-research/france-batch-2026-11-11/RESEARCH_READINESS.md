## 4. Cross-topic consistency checks

### TP dates, APS and documents

The packages agree that the current EU Ukraine-related TP endpoint is **4 March 2028**, while the French APS is a separate document normally valid for six months and renewed during the TP period. The TP application package preserves CESEDA’s one-year periods/three-year maximum wording and says the reviewed sources do not reconcile that general formulation with the later EU extension for each individual document. Accordingly, neither “all APS documents are valid until 2028” nor “TP ends after three years” is safe. Check the APS and current prefecture instructions. The EU Decision’s military-obligation condition applies to new grants from 5 August 2026, with the same-Member-State continuous-status exception; the nationwide accepted proof format remains unclear. These are qualifications, not blockers to a scoped overview.

### TP work permission, France Travail and residence transition

A currently valid APS-BPT grants professional-activity permission; the TP-specific salaried-employment guidance says the employer does not apply for a separate work authorization. This does not remove the employer’s authenticity check, ordinary hiring duties, job-search eligibility checks, professional licensing or the conditions for an ordinary residence permit. France Travail registration requires a valid work-authorizing document for adult non-EU applicants and is separate from ARE. Local prefectures publish some ordinary-title transition procedures, but no nationwide filing or pending/bridging rule is established. Do not describe work permission as an automatic `salarié` title or a nationwide post-TP conversion.

### Residence options

The Residence Options package explicitly identifies local prefecture transition procedures, including Seine-Maritime and Essonne, but no uniform nationwide filing procedure. It says applicants in Seine-Maritime are directed to keep renewing APS while the ordinary application is examined. National pending/bridging rights after APS expiry remain unresolved; the ordinary application itself is not shown to extend APS. No automatic post-TP conversion scheme was identified. Therefore `residence-routes` and `residence-conditions` support qualified drafting, while filing instructions require local confirmation and the national aspect remains blocked.

### Healthcare

The immediate PUMa plus CSS and no-advance arrangement for 12 months is explicitly tied to the specified Ukraine arrivals/families in Assurance Maladie guidance. It is not a universal TP guarantee. The ordinary PUMa route, APS status evidence, CPAM affiliation, NIA/NIR, attestation de droits and Carte Vitale have different functions. A Vitale card is not the source of entitlement, and an APS is not an attestation of health-insurance rights. Urgent admission without status/reimbursement papers is supported, but later FPU/hospital charges can apply and are not waived by emergency access.

### Housing

National guidance identifies 115 as the emergency homelessness contact, not guaranteed TP accommodation. The 2026 Haute-Garonne orientation description is local; 2022 “sas”/hosting arrangements are legacy. CAF housing aid is not social housing; an APS-BPT document accepted for submitting an HLM application does not guarantee allocation or priority. Local FSL conditions differ by département. The national TP initial-accommodation request path, placement criteria and continuation/re-entry rules remain a material gap for `housing-initial`.

### Education

School access for a child does not require a residence permit, though ordinary documents (identity/age, address, vaccination and guardian information) and local allocation steps still apply. Primary provisional admission exists for compulsory-age children with specified missing documents; it does not erase every practical documentation need. EANA assessment, CASNAV and UPE2A support are needs-based and locally organized; no fixed personal number of hours is promised. Higher-education admission is separate from ENIC-NARIC diploma comparability, credit transfer, fees/CVEC and CROUS financial or accommodation decisions. The 2026/27 domestic-fee categories do not expressly list TP; institution-level classification should be checked.

### Social assistance

The social package establishes a CESEDA TP route to ADA administered by OFII (ASP payment under the general rules), while noting unresolved application of the post-June-2026 ADA transition/rates to TP. RSA and Prime d’activité are not automatic from APS/work; the five-year work-authorized-residence condition generally remains, with narrow majorated isolated-parent exceptions. CAF child-benefit TP derogations are limited; ARS exclusion is supported by Cnaf guidance and current Yvelines CAF information. A CAF temporary identifier without NIR enables account access but does not create entitlement. The social package and housing package both describe possible CAF housing support for qualifying APS-BPT holders; this is distinct from family benefits, social housing and a guaranteed award.

### Cross-package CAF findings

The common safe boundary is: CAF account access, a temporary identifier, APS evidence and benefit entitlement are separate. Social assistance specifically verifies a CAF account can be created without NIR after a claimant starts a benefit request. Housing identifies possible housing-aid access for APS-BPT, with conditional national/local evidence and no automatic payment. Education’s current Ukraine higher-education information points applicants to the institution and CROUS for possible support; it is not a CAF housing or family-benefit rule. No conflict was found in the eight packages, provided articles keep CAF housing aid, child/family benefits and CROUS support separate and do not infer that one CAF route grants another benefit.

## 5. Drafting constraints

### Safe to state

- France’s TP route uses the APS-BPT; the APS and EU TP duration are distinct, and the current EU framework date is 4 March 2028.
- A valid APS-BPT authorizes work; salaried TP hiring has a separate-permit exemption but still involves employer document verification and ordinary work rules.
- Public job vacancy and France Travail/EURES channels exist; services, matching and training funding depend on individual assessment and availability.
- Children may access French schooling without a residence permit; local enrolment and language-support procedures remain.
- Ordinary private rental protections, conditional CAF housing aid, FSL and social-housing application routes exist, but application does not guarantee housing or payment.
- TP beneficiaries have a statutory ADA route; ordinary asylum ADA application instructions should not be transplanted to TP.
- An APS or administrative number/account is not itself a benefit decision, healthcare attestation or ordinary residence permit.

### Require qualification

- TP eligibility cohorts and the 2026 Ukrainian military-obligation rule, including exception and proof-format uncertainty.
- APS renewal, printed expiry and EU 2028 date; CESEDA three-year wording remains unreconciled at individual-document level.
- Any work statement must be scoped to valid APS-BPT and distinguished from ordinary residence transition, employer steps, ARE and professional licensing.
- Ukraine-specific healthcare immediate PUMa/CSS/no-advance policy; ordinary TP applicants may follow different standard routes.
- ADA rate/process after 2026 reform and the current TP initial-claim route.
- RSA/Prime d’activité exceptions, child-specific family-benefit derogations, and the ARS finding.
- Local prefecture transitions, local accommodation, local CAF/FSL decisions, academy language support, university admission, fees, ENIC-NARIC and CROUS support.

### Do not assert

- Every APS-BPT is automatically valid until 4 March 2028, or CESEDA’s three-year wording automatically terminates protection before the EU date.
- The 2026 military-obligation evidence format or a local prefecture checklist is uniform nationwide.
- TP work permission automatically grants an ordinary `salarié` residence permit, ARE or regulated-profession licence.
- France has one national APS-holder residence-filing route, or a pending ordinary filing automatically extends stay/work after APS expiry.
- Every TP beneficiary receives a guaranteed State accommodation place, or 115 guarantees a bed.
- The Ukraine-specific health arrangement covers every nationality under TP, or urgent admission means treatment/hospitalisation is free.
- A CAF account, NIR/NIA, temporary ID, APS, school/university application or benefit claim guarantees approval or payment.
- All TP children automatically receive AF/ARS/PAJE, that ordinary ARS rules override the documented TP exclusion, or a family-benefit derogation applies to every benefit/child condition.
- Schooling, language support, university admission, fee classification, credit transfer, CROUS funding/housing or accommodation is automatic.
- CAF housing aid equals social housing, a social-housing application guarantees allocation, or a local FSL/CAF/prefecture process is nationwide.

## 6. Blocking gaps

Only aspects whose focused package says `Not ready` are blocked. Qualifications in the other 22 aspects are not blockers.

### `residence-filing` — Blocked — material evidence gap

**Known:** ordinary routes and legal conditions exist. Seine-Maritime and other named prefectures document APS-holder change-of-status routes; Seine-Maritime specifies local filing channels and tells applicants to keep renewing APS while an ordinary application is reviewed.

**Missing:** a current national TP-specific filing rule/channel for ordinary permits, route-by-route accepted evidence and timing, and national treatment of pending applications/bridging lawful stay and work after APS expiry. Local instructions cannot be generalized.

**Official evidence needed:** current national CESEDA/Interior/DGEF guidance or binding law explaining whether and how APS-BPT holders may file each relevant change-of-status route from within France, what status document follows submission, and how APS/work rights continue while pending; otherwise current official instructions from the responsible prefecture for the specific applicant and route.

**Scope blocked:** France-wide practical instructions for filing a change of status and describing pending rights. It does not block qualified drafting of route types and their substantive conditions, or clearly labelled local filing examples.

### `housing-initial` — Blocked — material evidence gap

**Known:** call 115 for emergency assessment/orientation, with no guaranteed placement; Haute-Garonne describes a local TP orientation route; the 2022 national Ukraine accommodation model is historical.

**Missing:** a current nationally usable TP-specific first-request channel, responsible actor/hand-off, eligibility and prioritization, actual availability, placement duration/contribution, and continuation/re-entry procedure.

**Official evidence needed:** a current Interior/ministry national instruction or service page defining the TP initial accommodation request route and assessment/placement rules; if the process is implemented only locally, current prefecture/department/operator instructions must be labelled for that jurisdiction and cannot clear a nationwide claim.

**Scope blocked:** a France-wide “how to obtain initial State accommodation under TP” section. It does not block qualified drafting of the general 115 fallback, private rental, housing aid, FSL or social-housing applications.
