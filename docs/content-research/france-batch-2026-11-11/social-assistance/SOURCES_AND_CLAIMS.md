## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** A practical article can safely identify the statutory ADA route for people under temporary protection, distinguish it from ordinary asylum intake, describe the ordinary CAF/MSA rules for RSA and the prime d’activité, bound France Travail/ARE, and explain which child benefits a Cnaf instruction and CAF guidance say may be considered for APS-BPT households. Each payment requires a separate assessment. Qualifications: the 2026 asylum-reception reform's transition rules are not expressly reconciled with TP ADA; current nationwide initial ADA intake instructions specific to TP were not found; and TP child-entry exceptions are benefit-specific. No award or universal CAF/OFII checklist should be promised.

## Required distinctions

- TP status can open conditional ADA through a special legal route; it is not automatic income.
- APS-BPT evidences status and work permission; it is not an ADA, RSA, prime or family-benefit award.
- NIR and temporary CAF identifier are administrative; neither creates entitlement.
- A CAF account/application is not approval.
- ADA is OFII-managed TP subsistence; RSA is means-tested minimum income; prime d’activité is separate in-work support.
- France Travail registration differs from contribution-based ARE.
- Housing aid differs from general income support and is outside this research.
- Social benefits do not equal State accommodation; local CCAS help is not a nationwide benefit.
- Child benefits have separate residence, immigration and scheme tests; TP exceptions are limited.
- Work authorization alone does not satisfy RSA/prime residence-duration tests.
- Work does not automatically end every benefit, but income can change means-tested rights and must be reported.

## TP-specific subsistence

**Scheme:** allocation pour demandeur d’asile (ADA), not a separate named Ukraine payment. CESEDA L. 581-9 and D. 581-7 expressly provide the TP route [FR-SOC-S01, FR-SOC-S02].

**Status and administrator:** in force for TP beneficiaries on 2026-11-11; allowance access is during TP, with chapter III ADA provisions applying. OFII determines/manages; ASP pays for OFII under general rules. OFII requests cessation when TP ends or beneficiary transfers to another EU state [FR-SOC-S02, FR-SOC-S03].

**Eligibility:** age and resources conditions; household resources are considered under the incorporated general ADA rules and RSA-linked test. APS-BPT is evidence, not guaranteed approval [FR-SOC-S01, FR-SOC-S03, FR-SOC-S07]. Exact individual adult/household handling belongs with OFII.

**Amount:** daily and household-sensitive. The published current tariff lists €6.80/day for one person and €7.40/day additional where an adult expressed accommodation need and lacks free lodging [FR-SOC-S03, FR-SOC-S04]. Free accommodation excludes the additional amount, not necessarily base ADA. **2026 qualification:** Decree 2026-463 transition language is keyed to asylum applications; the sources reviewed do not expressly reconcile the effect of all post-12 June changes for every TP recipient under D. 581-7. Service-Public’s ADA page says it is being updated. Do not promise a monthly TP amount; direct the person to OFII for current individual calculation [FR-SOC-S05, FR-SOC-S06].

**Employment:** report work/income to OFII. Earnings/resources may reduce the amount or mean conditions are no longer met; work alone does not prove automatic cessation [FR-SOC-S03, FR-SOC-S07].

**Accommodation:** free lodging removes the additional housing amount; individual arrangements require OFII assessment [FR-SOC-S03, FR-SOC-S07]. Detailed housing-aid policy is outside scope.

**Payment:** ordinarily monthly in arrears via OFII-issued payment/withdrawal card; bank transfer only by specified exception [FR-SOC-S03].

**Application:** current nationwide TP initial-claim steps and checklist were not established. Contact competent territorial OFII and ask for the current TP initial ADA process; do not assume GUDA/OFPRA is required. Existing recipients have an OFII prolongation form posted 2024; verify it and local contacts before use [FR-SOC-S06, FR-SOC-S07].

## Ordinary income support

### RSA

General status rules for most non-EU applicants require age 25+ (with young-active/parent pathways), stable residence, and five years with work-authorized title or another listed status such as resident card/refugee/stateless/subsidiary protection [FR-SOC-S13]. APS-BPT is not a general exception. Cnaf says the five-year test remains applicable to TP except defined majorated RSA cases for certain isolated pregnant applicants/parents; children benefiting only from the special family-benefit derogation do not count as dependants for that RSA/PPA majoration [FR-SOC-S09].

**Verdict:** TP alone ordinarily does not qualify. Narrow parent-majoration exceptions may apply and require individual CAF/MSA review. Somme CAF's page lists RSA “subject to conditions” without explaining a national exception [FR-SOC-S15]. CAF for general regime, MSA for agricultural regime [FR-SOC-S27].

### Prime d’activité

Requires age 18+, work and modest income, plus applicable residence/status condition. For most non-EU applicants, five years with a work-authorized title or specified alternative status is required; TP alone is not listed. Cnaf indicates the five-year rule remains opposable except narrow majoration cases [FR-SOC-S09, FR-SOC-S14].

**Verdict:** work authorization is insufficient by itself; TP alone ordinarily does not meet the status test. Somme CAF's local “subject to conditions” wording does not establish a nationwide exception [FR-SOC-S15]. CAF/MSA must decide individual claims.

### ARE boundary

ARE is employment insurance, not TP subsistence. APS-BPT work permission may support France Travail registration, but registration does not award ARE. Qualifying prior employment/affiliation, eligible job loss and other search conditions still apply [FR-SOC-S16]–[FR-SOC-S18]. No calculation is attempted.

## Applications

- **ADA:** OFII; contact the current territorial channel. The available TP-specific OFII form is for continuing an existing award, not initial claim. Do not transfer ordinary asylum GUDA/OFPRA instructions to TP [FR-SOC-S02, FR-SOC-S06, FR-SOC-S07].
- **CAF/MSA:** apply separately to the relevant general-regime CAF or agricultural MSA service. A first-time CAF claimant can apply then create an online account without NIR using the temporary identifier [FR-SOC-S19, FR-SOC-S27].
- **Prime/family benefits:** use the specific benefit claim; do not assume one application covers all benefits [FR-SOC-S14, FR-SOC-S20], [FR-SOC-S22], [FR-SOC-S25].
- **ARE:** register with France Travail using valid work-authorizing status evidence and request separate ARE assessment [FR-SOC-S16, FR-SOC-S17].
- **Documents vary:** ADA continuation form requests identity/contact, household, accommodation and resources. CAF/MSA may need APS/status, child relationship/charge/residence and applicable entry documents. RSA/prime evidence depends on income and household. No universal checklist is supported [FR-SOC-S03], [FR-SOC-S07]–[FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S20].
- **RIB:** Service-Public generic asylum checklist mentions one, but general CESEDA provides an OFII card and limited transfer exceptions; the TP extension form does not establish a universal RIB requirement [FR-SOC-S03], [FR-SOC-S06], [FR-SOC-S07].
- **Report changes:** ADA address/accommodation, family, work, resources and assets go to OFII; report relevant changes to CAF/MSA under the specific benefit rules [FR-SOC-S03], [FR-SOC-S07], [FR-SOC-S22].
- **Application is not award:** an APS, identifier, account or claim does not guarantee payment [FR-SOC-S01], [FR-SOC-S13], [FR-SOC-S19], [FR-SOC-S20].

## Child/family benefits

### Allocations familiales

An APS-BPT household can be considered under Cnaf's TP-specific rules, subject to ordinary benefit conditions and a decision. Normally AF requires at least two dependent children under 20 residing in France; amounts are income-tiered [FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S20], [FR-SOC-S22], [FR-SOC-S23]. The TP child-entry derogation is limited to named benefits/cases; do not apply the broader foreign-worker waiver to TP [FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S11].

### ARS

Although ordinary ARS has age/school and means criteria [FR-SOC-S24], Cnaf TP guidance and current CAF Yvelines say APS-BPT holders cannot receive it [FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S10], [FR-SOC-S23]. A school certificate may still be required to maintain other family benefits. Do not promise ARS based on ordinary tests alone.

### PAJE

Cnaf and Yvelines CAF list birth premium and base allowance as possible under applicable criteria. Yvelines says CMG and PréParE may be possible for children born in France under ordinary rules [FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S23], [FR-SOC-S25]. Do not generalize to all PAJE parts or children born abroad.

### Child residence and documents

Family-benefit law requires claimant/child residence and has separate immigration/child-entry rules [FR-SOC-S08], [FR-SOC-S20], [FR-SOC-S21]. TP beneficiaries remain subject to entry proof in principle; only Cnaf's named 2022 derogations relieve it [FR-SOC-S09], [FR-SOC-S11]. The 2025 instruction covers select schooling/family situations, not a blanket 2026 exemption [FR-SOC-S10]. The September 2026 professional guide was announced, but its content was not accessible [FR-SOC-S12]. Verify the child's birth/arrival, residence, school and benefit with CAF/MSA.

## Emergency assistance

No nationwide TP-specific emergency cash entitlement beyond statutory benefits above was identified in reviewed official sources. For local assessment, contact the commune's CCAS/CIAS, mairie, or departmental social services. Extra-legal support varies by authority, may be cash or in-kind, and has no universal amount/entitlement [FR-SOC-S26]. No named local emergency or food programme was researched.

## Open gaps

- TP ADA entitlement is current, but exact nationwide initial application channel/checklist is not established; the 2024 OFII continuation form may be outdated.
- The statutory 2026 ADA table is published; the precise transition of post-12 June 2026 components/rate to TP beneficiaries under D. 581-7 needs OFII confirmation.
- Operational age/household treatment and precise income/work effects in all TP cases are not fully detailed in TP-specific public materials.
- Cnaf's national guidance indicates five-year residence condition for TP RSA/prime with narrow majorated isolated-parent exceptions. Somme CAF wording is unexplained; September 2026 national guide contents were inaccessible.
- Child-entry evidence remains the rule for TP except Cnaf's named derogations. Individual child arrival/birth/residence/school evidence should be confirmed.
- 2025 instruction's temporary remote-school handling ended September 2025; later practice for each schooling pattern is not established.
- Local emergency aid varies; no specific municipal grant was researched and no nationwide TP cash programme was found.
- Mayotte is outside the D. 581-7 ADA rule as provided by D. 591-13; separate research is needed before generalizing there.

## Editorial exclusions

Do not state without new evidence that:

- all TP beneficiaries automatically receive ADA or a fixed monthly amount;
- TP ADA requires ordinary asylum GUDA/OFPRA steps;
- every TP recipient receives the post-June-2026 asylum components without transition qualification;
- free accommodation ends the whole ADA rather than the additional accommodation amount;
- work automatically ends every benefit or never affects ADA;
- APS automatically grants RSA or Prime d’activité;
- no income automatically creates RSA entitlement;
- France Travail registration automatically grants ARE;
- CAF account, temporary ID, NIR, APS or application means approval;
- every CAF claim needs the same documents, RIB or NIR;
- APS work permission removes child-entry evidence requirements for all benefits;
- every TP child receives AF, ARS or every PAJE component;
- ordinary ARS criteria alone establish TP eligibility;
- children covered by a TP family-benefit derogation count for RSA/PPA majoration;
- Somme CAF wording establishes a national RSA/prime exception;
- CCAS cash/food aid is available nationwide;
- housing aid equals general income support or proves family-benefit eligibility.

## Scope limits

Not researched: detailed housing calculations; disability benefits; pensions; maternity medical coverage; unemployment calculations; tax credits; every family-benefit component; maintenance/alimony; named municipal food/emergency programmes; residence transition.
