# Romania — Residence Options Beyond Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-05  
**Scope:** focused evidence review for `residence-routes`, `residence-conditions`, and `residence-filing`. This is research, not article copy, individualized legal advice, or publication approval.

## Research verdict

**Ready**

Official sources support a bounded filing answer for all three aspects. Current and former temporary-protection (TP) beneficiaries have an expressly established Romanian work-purpose residence route without a long-stay employment visa while the EU-set TP period remains in effect. Ordinary study, family, and other purpose-specific routes are documented separately; the sources reviewed do not establish a general TP-specific in-country transition to those purposes. This is an evidence boundary, not a finding that transition is prohibited. Filing does not guarantee approval. The general rule preserving lawful stay while an application is decided does not itself establish a pending work right or resolve every TP-transition case.

The controlling source for the current text of OUG 194/2002 is the Romanian Legislative Portal's consolidated record, whose history lists **27 April 2026** and whose text annotates amendments introduced by OUG 32/2026. OUG 32/2026 was published on 27 April; its employer-change provisions took effect from **8 August 2026**. The IGI-hosted PDF is an informational snapshot, not the primary current source. Operational filing instructions must still be checked immediately before publication: WorkinRomania currently announces suspension of employer registration/authorization and the D/AM2 single-application flow, but does not identify the TP-specific, visa-exempt residence-extension route as suspended.

## Long-term residence and counting temporary-protection time

- **Romanian national route:** OUG 194/2002 calls this the right of long-term stay/residence. IGI describes a general five-year continuous-stay framework. The statute and IGI list limits on absences (not more than six consecutive months or ten months total), count study residence at half, and exclude short-stay, diplomatic/service visa, seasonal-worker and au-pair periods from the calculation.
- **Current TP status:** OUG 194/2002 Art. 70(2)(b) expressly excludes TP applicants and beneficiaries from grant of Romanian long-term residence while in that status.
- **Past TP time after a later change:** **unresolved.** The national five-year wording refers to temporary residence or international protection, while TP is separately excluded from grant. The materials checked do not expressly say whether prior TP time counts, is excluded, or is counted in part after a person obtains another qualifying status. Do not infer from the label “residence permit.”
- **EU long-term-resident framework:** Directive 2003/109/EC Art. 3(2)(b) excludes current TP-authorised persons and TP applicants awaiting a decision. It does not answer the treatment of earlier TP time once a person later enters a qualifying status. EU long-term-resident status must not be conflated with Romania's national long-term residence right.
- **Other qualifying periods:** the cited Romanian provisions explicitly provide half-counting for study and exclude listed stay categories. Do not extend these rules to TP without a direct legal basis.

## Unresolved boundaries

1. The reviewed official sources establish ordinary visa/residence routes for study and family but do not establish a general direct in-country TP transition to those purposes. This is an evidence boundary, not a finding that transition is prohibited.
2. The general stay-pending-decision rule is conditional on lawful stay when filing; its exact effect in each TP-transition case, and any pending work right, is not established. No pending-work right should be claimed.
3. Whether TP ends automatically when another Romanian permit is granted, whether concurrent statuses are possible, and whether separate notice or renunciation is required.
4. Whether prior Romanian TP time counts toward Romanian national or EU long-term-resident qualification after a status change. Keep this unresolved; it does not block `residence-filing`.
5. Whether the TP work-purpose exception can be used for an EU Blue Card; the reviewed sources do not expressly establish that interaction. Keep Blue Card separate from the ordinary TP work route.
6. Current operational submission channel for a TP work-purpose extension. WorkinRomania's live notice suspends employer registration/authorization and D/AM2 flows but does not address the TP-specific visa-exempt route. Check current IGI instructions before publication; do not claim either universal outage or guaranteed portal availability.
7. Any legal or operational change after the Portal Legislativ consolidated date shown as 27 April 2026. Recheck immediately before publication.

These are research limits, not conclusions that an application is impossible or that a right does not exist.

## Drafting constraints

1. Do not say TP automatically converts into another residence permit or that all residence routes can be filed from Romania.
2. Describe the OUG 96/2024 employment exception separately, with its TP-duration limit and the ordinary route's remaining conditions.
3. Keep employment, high-skilled / EU Blue Card, family, study and long-term residence as distinct routes; do not merge their visa or filing rules.
4. Do not present family reunification as available solely because a person holds TP. IGI's ordinary sponsor list does not include TP status.
5. Do not claim a TP holder can switch to study or family status in-country without direct official evidence.
6. Do not say filing guarantees approval, preserves work rights, or necessarily allows the applicant to stay pending a decision in a TP-transition case.
7. State Romania's statutory “long-term residence” terminology. Do not promise permanent residence after five calendar years.
8. Do not say temporary-protection time counts or does not count toward long-term residence after a status change unless an authoritative source directly resolves it.
9. Do not imply a new permit automatically ends TP or that TP and another status can be held together.
10. Recheck OUG 194 amendments and IGI operational instructions before publication; avoid fixed fees, salary amounts, and processing times unless freshly verified.

## 2026 amendment and filing-date note

OUG 32/2026, published on 27 April 2026, replaced OUG 194/2002 Article 56's work-purpose extension procedure. As of the 2026-11-05 check, the amendment's staged employer-change rule had been effective since **8 August 2026** for covered holders of pre-existing work-purpose residence. For qualifying visa-exempt work-extension categories, Article 56(10) directs an employer or placement agency to submit the single application electronically; after an extension is granted, Article 56(11) requires the applicant to book within 10 days at the territorial IGI unit for biometric capture and issue of the single permit or EU Blue Card. These are statutory process details, not a guarantee that every live platform flow is available.

The TP-specific legal basis remains OUG 96/2024 Article 19: current and former beneficiaries may work without an employment authorization until TP ends, and work-purpose residence may be extended without the long-stay employment visa until the EU-set TP period expires. OUG 194 purpose-specific residence conditions continue where relevant; the visa/authorization exceptions do not guarantee approval. This does not establish a direct TP switch to study/family or an automatic Blue Card route. OUG 32/2026's temporary measure for certain persons whose work residence has expired, available until **31 December 2026**, is a separate transitional provision and should not be presented as the TP route.

The current WorkinRomania home page warns that employer registration, employer authorization, and D/AM2 single-application flows are deactivated following a court suspension concerning Order 1073/2026. The notice does not name visa-exempt TP work-purpose extensions. Therefore, the legal route and its statutory filing sequence can be described, while the precise live channel must be confirmed with IGI at the time of publication.
