# Romania — Education and Language Learning: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 2026-11-05. **Scope:** bounded evidence for school access, current Ukrainian-minor enrolment procedure, Romanian-language support, recognition, and higher-education admission. This is research for future content, not individualized legal advice or a decision about a person's eligibility. No production catalog was changed.

## Research verdict

**Ready** — there is current primary evidence for the main school-access route for minors from Ukraine, the present `audient` procedure, Romanian-language accommodation groups, and the national higher-education/recognition framework. Future content can be drafted safely if it separates school access from recognition, presents local placement and course availability as conditional, and does not promise admission, a funded university place, or a language course to a particular person.

This verdict does not mean every local procedure or individual document case has been established. The boundaries below remain explicit drafting constraints.

## Unresolved boundaries

- **Local school capacity and placement:** the national right and commission procedure do not identify a guaranteed school, location, class, or immediate available seat for a particular child.
- **Grade/class placement:** age, learning history, available records, assessments, and equivalence can affect level. No single placement outcome can be promised.
- **Academic records missing:** the general regulation has an assessment process, but the exact evidence accepted and interaction with the special Ukraine audient procedure may need confirmation by the relevant inspectorate. Do not claim that every missing-document case can be resolved by a declaration alone.
- **Audient details in practice:** current national rule exists, but county intake contacts, form links, appointment practices, and operational document requests vary and should be checked before publication.
- **Pupil language groups:** a request route exists, but actual local group availability and timing depend on applications/resources. No general Ukrainian-language teaching entitlement was established in the reviewed national sources.
- **Adult language courses:** adult course categories distinguish international protection from a right of residence; current sources do not expressly settle the TP-permit classification for the 2026 fee category. ANOFM vocational training is conditional on service eligibility/registration and is not evidence of a general Romanian-language-course entitlement.
- **University-specific admission:** tests, calendars, language proofs, documents, recognition steps, and application fees are institution/programme-specific.
- **Annual funded university places:** OUG 100/2022 states the annual place measure for TP beneficiaries from Ukraine, but this review did not establish the current year’s exact allocation, application channel, or guaranteed individual eligibility. Do not promise a funded place.
- **Missing university diplomas:** no current general route allowing a new applicant to enter a degree programme without the required prior qualification was established. Historical Ukraine emergency/mobility announcements should not be presented as a current general admission rule.
- **Scholarships:** no general scholarship entitlement for all Ukrainian or TP students was established. Any relevant scholarship should be researched by its specific programme and beneficiary category.
- **Remote Ukrainian curriculum:** the reviewed current Romanian sources do not establish a general entitlement to combine Romanian schooling with Ukrainian remote schooling. Do not infer it from practice or historical emergency notices.
- **Diploma recognition:** equivalence/recognition does not itself guarantee a school place, university admission, a scholarship, or professional recognition. Regulated-profession recognition is outside this topic.

## Drafting constraints

- Limit the school-right statement to the cohorts named in OUG 96/2024; keep general Romanian compulsory-education rules separate from special Ukraine provisions.
- Describe the current audient route using Order 5679/2024 and OUG 96/2024. Do not recycle Order 3363/2022 as the current procedure or quote old emergency steps as current.
- Say the inspectorate coordinates assignment; do not promise a particular school or grade. Distinguish enrolment/attendance as audient from ordinary pupil status after recognition/equivalence.
- Explain that missing school records trigger an assessment/equivalence route, not automatic recognition or a guaranteed grade.
- Describe Romanian-language accommodation groups as requestable, locally organized support subject to resources. Do not promise a separate class/place or Ukrainian-language instruction.
- Keep adult language courses separate from school language support. Do not call them universally free or claim all TP holders fit one course fee category.
- For universities, separate admission, credential recognition, language preparation, and fee/funding status. Refer readers to the institution’s current rules and CNRED’s relevant procedure.
- Mention state-funded places for TP beneficiaries only as the annual plan-level measure; never imply automatic admission, a guaranteed funded seat, or free study for all Ukrainians.
- Do not assert a universal missing-diploma exception, automatic recognition, or current right to remote Ukrainian schooling based on old emergency arrangements.
- Do not conflate academic recognition with professional recognition or licensing.
