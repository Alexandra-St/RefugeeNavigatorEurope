# Romania Financial and Social Assistance — Sources and Claims

Research checked **5 November 2026**. This is a dated evidence matrix for future drafting, not article copy or an individual eligibility decision. It separates ordinary means-tested support, temporary accommodation support, and benefit-specific child/family routes. A right to apply or a route in legislation does not guarantee that an application will be approved.

## 1. Research verdict

**Ready** for carefully bounded drafting of `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, and `social-child-benefits`.

Primary Romanian legislation establishes a specific VMI application route for current and former beneficiaries of the Ukraine-response support regime after the relevant three-month accommodation-sum period, subject to VMI eligibility rules. It also establishes separate, explicit TP-specific routes for child state allowance and certain child-raising support. The evidence does not support a blanket claim that every TP holder or every child is entitled to social benefits. The 2026 sources reviewed do not establish a general nationwide cash benefit solely by virtue of TP beyond the particular statutory schemes described below.

## 5. Comparison mapping

### `social-subsistence`

**Readiness: Ready — bounded answer.** VMI is an established means-tested scheme, and OUG 96/2024 provides a specific route for covered current/former beneficiaries to apply after the temporary accommodation-sum period. A safe answer may name the two VMI components and say that Romanian income/assets, household composition and other statutory criteria are assessed. It must say “may apply / subject to assessment,” not “TP holders receive VMI.”

### `social-application`

**Readiness: Ready — scheme-specific answer.** For VMI, the application route is the local public social-assistance service at the relevant mayor's office, paper or electronic, followed by checks and a mayoral decision. Child benefits have separate routes: child allowance via the local authority for permit address and AJPIS payment; child-raising routes through AJPIS; disability registration through DGASPC. A future article should not present one universal application checklist or imply all benefits are filed with the same body.

### `social-child-benefits`

**Readiness: Ready — separate, conditional schemes only.** OUG 96/2024 gives direct TP-related bases for the state child allowance, child-raising allowance under OUG 111/2010, and a distinct monthly payment for specified single caregivers. The safe answer can summarize these routes only with their separate eligibility, address, application, residence-proof and income/age conditions. Children's school attendance alone does not establish benefit eligibility. Do not imply automatic payment to every child or caregiver.

## 6. Unresolved boundaries

- **Covered-person scope:** verify the person's current or former beneficiary status under OUG 96 and the applicable extension/transition date before describing a live application.
- **VMI duration/status:** OUG 96 Art 17 links its route to the period in Art 3(2), and separately includes previous beneficiaries. Do not broaden this into an immediate general VMI entitlement for every current TP holder.
- **Household and means calculation:** Law 196 and the OUG 96 derogations require household, income and asset assessment. Exact treatment of a specific household, foreign assets, income evidence, and excluded property must be checked in the current forms/rules and local practice.
- **Amounts and indexation:** all cash amounts and thresholds are omitted. Verify current effective values for the relevant month and benefit before publication.
- **Local implementation:** national law identifies filing authorities, but appointment availability, electronic channels, local documents and case-handling arrangements may vary; verify with the relevant municipality/AJPIS/DGASPC before publishing operational details.
- **Documents:** the matrix records only the statutory core items expressly identified. It is not a universal or exhaustive checklist. Additional evidence may depend on benefit, family situation and current forms.
- **Child allowance:** verify that a child's parent/representative is within the covered OUG 96 group, that the permit has the required address, that dependency is declared, and that quarterly residence declarations are made. School enrolment is not a substitute for these conditions.
- **Child-raising allowance:** benefit-specific contribution/income history, qualifying periods and other OUG 111 conditions remain; the TP-specific address rule does not waive them. Foreign/assimilated income treatment beyond OUG 96's stated calculation rule requires individual review.
- **Interaction between schemes:** the reviewed provisions do not establish that the listed benefits are mutually exclusive, cumulative in every case, or automatically offset in a particular way. Do not state a universal interaction rule.
- **Additional payments:** this focused review did not establish a general nationwide cash benefit based solely on TP beyond the named statutory routes. Do not conclude that no other individual, local or emergency support exists.
- **Housing boundary:** the time-limited accommodation sum is not VMI, a child benefit, or a continuing rent subsidy. Eligibility and conditions for its later months should be checked separately if operational details are included.
- **Disability:** a foreign disability document does not itself establish a Romanian certificate or a specific Law 448 payment; DGASPC assessment and benefit-specific requirements matter.

These are drafting boundaries, not evidence that an unverified benefit does not exist.

## 7. Drafting constraints

Future content must:

- distinguish VMI, accommodation support, child state allowance, OUG 111 child-raising allowance, OUG 96 single-caregiver support, and disability-related rights;
- state that applications are assessed and do not guarantee an award;
- avoid saying that TP alone guarantees cash assistance;
- avoid treating school enrolment as proof of child-benefit entitlement;
- avoid merging the two child-raising schemes or calling either a universal child allowance;
- use benefit-specific authority and documents rather than a universal checklist;
- avoid unsupported claims that employment automatically ends all support;
- omit amounts unless checked against current effective law and official instructions at publication;
- keep the three-month accommodation sum separate from continuing social assistance;
- describe a source gap or unresolved eligibility as unknown, not as proof that no right or service exists.

## Research scope note

This is a bounded national-law review of VMI and selected clearly defined Ukraine-related cash/support pathways. It is not a complete Romanian welfare catalogue, does not determine an individual's eligibility, and does not include new production content, comparison answers, or source records.
