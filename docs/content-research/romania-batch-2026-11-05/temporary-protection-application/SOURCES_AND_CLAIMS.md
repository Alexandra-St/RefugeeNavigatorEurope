## Research verdict

**Ready**

Primary sources now establish a safe, bounded account of Romania's application route, the EU military-obligation rule and Romania Border Police's listed proof pathways, and the current EU scheme endpoint. The article can be drafted without asserting unverified appointment, processing-time, exhaustive-document-checklist, printed-card-expiry, or transfer details. Some operational details remain explicitly out of scope for factual claims; their absence does not block a carefully qualified article. An older IGI public information page still states that permits are valid only until 4 March 2026. Do not use that page's obsolete date as current guidance.

## Application and registration evidence

- **Authority:** IGI. The consolidated Romanian government decision says IGI grants temporary protection to the covered categories present at a Romanian border crossing (including transit) or in Romania, subject to statutory exclusions.
- **Route:** IGI points to its territorial immigration structures and regional centres; Romanian law says IGI grants protection for eligible persons at a Romanian border crossing or in Romania. Border Police separately addresses entry for Ukrainian citizens intending to apply in Romania. The current IGI hub links the relevant information. A safe article can direct readers to IGI and its current locations without promising a single universal appointment route.
- **In-person steps:** Romanian rules require a facial image and specified personal data. IGI's 2024 page says each person, including children, must be physically present for photographing. Do not infer fingerprints.
- **Permit / fee:** IGI issues residence permits free of charge. The permit evidences temporary-protection status and authorizes stay during implementation; the sources do not support treating an initial enquiry alone as a grant.
- **Documents:** Residence/address evidence can be documented or declared under HG 505/2024. This is an address-data fallback, not a waiver of identity/eligibility proof. For some cases the law assigns identity-establishment measures to border/police authorities. For the military condition, Border Police lists the passport-stamp and paper/electronic Reserv+ evidence pathways but does not say whether the list is exhaustive. No single current complete applicant checklist was found; an article can say to bring available identity/eligibility documents and follow current IGI instructions without enumerating a universal checklist.
- **Appointment and time:** No current official appointment requirement or processing-time standard was found. Do not state a processing estimate or call the procedure fast.

## Dates, validity, and extension

Keep three things separate:

1. **EU scheme:** Decision 2026/1912 extends the covered EU measure to 4 March 2028, with that extension applying from 5 March 2027.
2. **New-grant condition:** the military-obligation rule applies from 5 August 2026, subject to the decision's continuous same-Member-State status exception.
3. **Romanian document:** HG 505/2024 ties earlier permits to the EU decision “as subsequently amended”; the 2024 FAQ says old and new permit forms remain valid during implementation and that the 180-day exchange window is not a forfeiture deadline. IGI reported permits issued/reissued in 2026 under the legal framework. This supports legal continuity of existing permits through the extended measure, but no reviewed source establishes a new printed expiry for every card or a universal mandatory 2027 replacement/renewal procedure.

No official Romanian source reviewed announces a 2026–2027 renewal window, physical-replacement deadline, or individual-document deadline. The IGI page that gives 4 March 2026 is stale. A future article may state the EU endpoint and the dynamic statutory validity rule, while directing people to IGI for individual-card instructions; it must not claim that all cards print 4 March 2028 or that every holder must/not replace a document.

## Leaving Romania, return, and another Member State

The Border Police page concerns entry by Ukrainian citizens intending to apply in Romania, not the travel rights of existing permit-holders. IGI's 2024 page describes return subject to valid temporary protection and entry conditions, but also contains an obsolete 2026 date. The reviewed Romanian sources do not settle prolonged absence, restarting rights after a long stay abroad, or the practical process to transfer protection from another Member State. Keep these as unresolved individual-case boundaries; do not imply simultaneous or automatic protection in two countries.

## Unresolved boundaries

- **Third-country nationals/stateless people:** Romanian law and IGI explain several cohorts, but case eligibility depends on the exact Ukrainian status, departure date, family relationship, sponsor recognition, and safe-and-durable-return assessment. Do not generalize to every person who lived or studied in Ukraine.
- **Military-obligation condition:** the EU scope/continuous same-Member-State exception is explicit. Border Police lists a passport exit-stamp pathway and paper/electronic competent-authority evidence through Reserv+. Its page does not state whether the list is exhaustive, does not reproduce the EU exception, and does not set age bands or explain every special case. Do not invent those details or characterize the listed documents as the exclusive accepted forms.
- **Military rule — Romanian authority boundary:** no separate Romanian legal eligibility restriction beyond the EU rule was found. The Border Police evidence page is narrower and should not be used to erase the EU exception or to answer every applicant cohort's document question.
- **Missing or ambiguous documents:** law permits identity-establishment measures and a declared residence locality, but no blanket exemption from identity or eligibility evidence is supported.
- **Transfer / previous EU protection:** no sufficiently clear Romanian operational source found for transfer, prior registration elsewhere, or whether/how a new Romanian grant may be requested.
- **Future validity/renewal:** IGI confirms 2026 permit issuance/reissuance and the law uses dynamic validity wording, but no current Romanian announcement reviewed for a universal renewal window, mandatory replacement, 2027 appointment, or card-specific printed expiry under the extension.
- **Appointment and processing time:** no current official commitment found. Do not add estimates or assert appointments are/are not required.
- **Travel and return:** IGI's 2024 statement is date-stale; prolonged absence, current re-entry documentation, and any status consequences need updated confirmation.

## Drafting constraints

1. Date-stamp any time-sensitive explanation and recheck IGI immediately before publication.
2. Name the cohort conditions; do not merge Ukrainian nationals, family members, protected people from Ukraine, and third-country/stateless permanent-residence holders into a single unconditional rule.
3. State the safe-and-durable-return assessment only for cohorts to which Romanian law applies it.
4. Distinguish EU scheme duration, Romanian protection status, and a permit's printed/document validity.
5. Describe IGI's role and official intake points without promising an appointment, processing time, outcome, or universal document list.
6. Explain that missing address proof can lead to a declared locality entry; do not convert that into “no documents required.”
7. Do not say fingerprints are taken; facial image capture is what the checked Romanian regulation expressly establishes.
8. Do not claim a 2027 renewal/replacement procedure is open or required until IGI publishes current instructions.
9. Avoid definitive rules for lengthy absence, return, or transfer between Member States without updated official evidence.
