# Romania — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 2026-11-05. **Scope:** bounded evidence for official job-search channels, ANOFM services, EURES, conditional counselling/training, and foreign-qualification navigation for the future `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, and `employment-assistance-conditions` aspects. This is research, not individualized legal advice, an eligibility decision, or publication approval. No production catalog was changed.

**Ready** — a safe future article and comparison answer can identify ANOFM as the national public vacancy/service channel, distinguish EURES as a European mobility and adviser network, and describe counselling, mediation, registration and training as conditional services. ## Unresolved boundaries and operational cautions

- **Registration documents:** the public checklist is generic and does not explain how the county agency handles every foreign identity document, temporary-protection permit, missing original, translation or transcript. Confirm directly with the relevant AJOFM/AMOFM before describing a definitive document checklist.
- **Individual Art. 16 category:** the service category for a foreign national requires a legal work right (or prior Romanian employment/income). Use the existing TP work-right matrix only for its covered groups; do not imply that all Ukrainians, family members, or people with any Romanian stay document meet the condition.
- **Vacancy listing language/completeness:** the official listings are Romanian-language in the reviewed interface, and the matrix does not establish that all openings are published or translated. Do not present ANOFM as the sole way to find work.
- **Training supply and eligibility:** statutory eligibility does not guarantee a current local course, a place, a desired qualification, travel reimbursement or a particular outcome. Availability and prerequisites can vary by county and programme.
- **Unemployment benefit:** registration for job-search services is not itself an award of unemployment benefit. Benefit eligibility and any cross-border export are separate questions and are not assessed here.
- **Qualifications:** CNRED handles specified academic-document recognition; regulated professions may have different competent authorities and requirements. A qualification assessment does not guarantee hiring or authorization to practise.
- **WorkinRomania:** the reviewed live notice is about named employer/placement/visa procedures. It does not replace ANOFM's vacancy channel or settle the separate temporary-protection work route.
- **No outcome guarantee:** no source establishes that registration, counselling, mediation, an EURES profile, or course eligibility guarantees employment, income, benefit, admission to training, or a qualification recognition decision.

## Drafting constraints

- Keep **job search**, **registration for employment services**, **work authorization/residence**, **unemployment benefit**, and **qualification recognition** distinct.
- Describe the ANOFM vacancy search as a live national/county public listing; note the reviewed job details are in Romanian.
- Describe free mediation and counselling within their stated scope; do not promise placement.
- For free training, state the statutory-category and ANOFM-registration conditions; verify current local courses and places.
- For temporary-protection beneficiaries, state only that the law's foreign-jobseeker category refers to having a legal right to work, and direct individual registration/document questions to the county agency. Reuse the existing employment-rights research rather than reopening the work-permission question.
- Describe EURES as a separate European channel and explain that profile visibility requires sharing the Europass profile with EURES.
- Mention qualification recognition only as a signpost; check the specific occupation and authority before giving instructions.
- Do not promise vacancies, a job, a course, benefit eligibility, qualification recognition, or a specific administrative outcome.
- Recheck dynamic portal screens, course availability, WorkinRomania notices and any operational instructions immediately before publication.
