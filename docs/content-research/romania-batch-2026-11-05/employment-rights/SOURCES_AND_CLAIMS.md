## Work authorization: separate legal concepts

1. **Work authorisation:** OUG 96/2024 Art. 19(1) is the specific TP-linked rule: the covered beneficiaries/previous beneficiaries may be employed without the OG 25/2014 employment authorisation until temporary protection ceases.
2. **Residence:** Art. 19(2) separately addresses extension of the right of residence for work without the long-stay employment visa, with the statutory endpoint tied to the EU-set TP period. The detailed immigration filing procedure is in OUG 194/2002, as amended; the prior Romania residence research found that filing does not guarantee approval and that a work-purpose route should not be generalized into other permit purposes.
3. **Employment conditions:** The exemption does not disapply the Labour Code. Contract, age/capacity, wage, working-time, safety and other applicable employee rules continue to matter.
4. **Activity type:** The cited text says a person may be employed. It does not expressly establish an equivalent exception for self-employment or independent economic activity.
5. **After TP:** Do not infer an indefinite right to work after temporary protection ends. The statute ties the exception to cessation of TP; post-TP permission depends on the person’s later legal basis and is outside this refresh.

## Minimum wage: date and sector boundary

- **1 January–30 June 2026:** the prior general statutory minimum was **4,050 lei gross/month**. Do not use it as the current amount for a claim checked on 5 November 2026.
- **From 1 July 2026:** HG 146/2026 sets the general minimum at **4,325 lei gross/month**, equivalent to **25.949 lei/hour** for an average normal schedule of 166.667 hours/month. The statutory base excludes bonuses and other additions; this is not a guaranteed net/take-home amount.
- **Construction:** OUG 156/2024 Art. LXIX separately states **4,582 lei gross/month** (27.714 lei/hour) from 1 January 2025. OUG 29/2026 repealed the agricultural provision but not the construction provision. The sector classification and any later amendment must be checked before quoting this special figure for an individual job.
- **Agriculture and food industry:** OUG 156/2024 Art. LXX’s separate 4,050 lei rate was repealed effective 1 July 2026 by OUG 29/2026. From that date the applicable floor aligns with the general rate in force, 4,325 lei gross/month.
- A future article can safely state the general figure with the effective date and flag that construction has a separate statutory rate. Avoid implying a universal amount across every sector, quoting net pay, or presenting the gross floor as a promised salary offer.

## Unresolved boundaries

1. **After TP ends:** the cited exemption ends with TP. This refresh does not determine each person’s subsequent work/residence basis.
2. **Pending application:** no general claim is made that filing a residence application preserves a right to work, or creates a pending-work right for a TP-related transition.
3. **Self-employment:** OUG 96/2024 Art. 19 addresses employment; it does not expressly establish independent/self-employed activity permission.
4. **WorkinRomania outage:** the live notice disables named registration/authorisation and D/AM2 flows but does not address the TP-specific route. Exact operational submission channel must be confirmed with IGI before publication; do not infer that the legal exemption is suspended or that a particular portal route is available.
5. **Construction coverage:** the special 4,582 lei rate depends on the statutory sector definition. This research did not determine whether a particular employer, activity, worker category or schedule falls within it.
6. **Minimum-pay scope:** the amount checked is a statutory gross basic wage for the described normal schedule, not net pay or a complete answer for every employment arrangement. Recheck amendments before publication.
7. **Collective agreement:** whether a specific collective agreement applies depends on the bargaining level and employer/unit coverage. The existence of collective bargaining does not mean universal coverage.
8. **Individual employment disputes:** ITM is an official complaint route; this review does not determine jurisdiction or remedy for a specific claim, limitation period, litigation step or emergency/abuse response route.

These are limits on what this evidence supports, not findings that a right or remedy does not exist.

## Drafting constraints

- Keep work authorisation, residence permission, residence-extension filing and the residence document’s date distinct.
- Tie the OUG 96/2024 exception to the law’s defined covered groups and to cessation of TP.
- Do not imply that the exception authorizes independent work, guarantees hiring, or continues after TP ends.
- Do not promise that a pending residence application preserves work rights.
- State **gross** wage and effective date. Do not call it net/take-home pay or imply that bonuses count toward the statutory basic-wage floor.
- Distinguish the general 4,325 lei floor, the construction-sector 4,582 lei provision, and the repealed agricultural/food provision; check sector classification and current law before publication.
- Do not claim that every employee has the same collective-agreement coverage.
- Use concise high-value worker protections; avoid turning the article into a full Labour Code guide or individual legal advice.
- If linking the complaint portal, say it is the official labour petition route, note that it is Romanian-language and requires identifying details, and do not present it as emergency assistance.
- Recheck legislation, minimum-wage amounts and WorkinRomania/IGI operational notices immediately before publication.
