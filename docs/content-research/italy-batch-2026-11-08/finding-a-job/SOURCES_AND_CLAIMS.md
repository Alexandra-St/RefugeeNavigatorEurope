# Italy — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 2026-11-08
**Scope:** exactly `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, and `employment-assistance-conditions`.
**Method:** official-source research only; this is not an individual eligibility determination.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Italy has multiple official ways to find vacancies: SIISL, the EURES portal, and regional employment-service/CPI portals. Their coverage and access rules differ. SIISL is open to Italian and foreign citizens for vacancy and training search but requires digital identity access (for example SPID or CIE); regional public vacancy pages can allow browsing without a login, as shown by Emilia-Romagna. A vacancy search is distinct from formal jobseeker registration and a DID. CPI services, regional systems, GOL participation and unemployment insurance have separate conditions. The reviewed national guidance does not establish one universal foreign/temporary-protection document checklist for CPI registration. NASpI is a separately assessed insurance benefit, not an automatic consequence of looking for work or registering. The Ministry currently lists the Skills Partnership for Employment Italia-Ucraina under GOL for regularly present Ukrainian citizens who sign a GOL Patto di Servizio at a CPI; the evidence checked does not establish that new enrollment remains open on 2026-11-08. [IT-JOB-S01–S23]

## Public vacancy search boundaries

- **SIISL:** the Ministry states that Italian and foreign citizens can access vacancy and training opportunities and upload a CV using digital identity such as SPID or CIE. The live service prompts sign-in before viewing announcements. The reviewed sources do not make DID or unemployed status a general prerequisite for browsing. [S01, S03]
- **EURES:** public “Find a Job” and several other services are available without registration; registered My EURES features are separate. Its listings largely import national vacancy data and should not be described as complete. EURES cannot waive Italian immigration, residence or work rules for non-EU nationals. [S10–S11]
- **Regional portals/CPI:** the Ministry points jobseekers to regional employment portals and CPIs. Emilia-Romagna, for example, makes vacancy browsing available through Lavoro per Te, including CPI/employer, seasonal, agriculture, targeted-placement and public-administration lists. Its official act describes a no-login public offer area. This is a regional example, not a national rule. [S02, S12–S13]
- **Formal registration:** browsing an advertisement, submitting an application and expressing interest in SIISL do not by themselves create a DID, unemployment status, CPI service plan or NASpI claim. [S04–S06, S15–S16]

## Employment institutions and registration

**Centri per l’Impiego (CPI)** are local/regional public employment-service points. Ministry guidance describes help with DID, orientation, counselling, short training and matching vacancies to jobseekers. The responsible CPI/service route can depend on where the person lives or has domicile and the region. Regional portals and service offerings differ. [S02, S04, S14]

**Regional employment services** operate or commission local portals and active-policy delivery. Their services may include public vacancy listings, registration support, profiling, training pathways and referrals, but scope and access requirements are not uniform nationally. [S02, S12–S14]

**Ministero del Lavoro / SIISL** provide the national platform and policy framework. SIISL permits job/training search, CV upload and expression of interest; it does not replace CPI assessment or the INPS decision on NASpI. Former **ANPAL** functions were assigned to the Ministry from 2024-03-01; do not identify ANPAL as the current independent authority. [S01–S03, S17]

**EURES** supports European mobility with vacancy information, advisers and cross-border guidance. EURES advisers work in many CPIs. EURES does not register a person as unemployed under Italian law, grant immigration status or decide Italian benefits. [S04, S10–S11]

**DID (Dichiarazione di immediata disponibilità)** is the formal declaration that begins unemployment status. The national Ministry says it can be filed at a CPI, through a regional portal where available, or online through the national active-policy portal. An online DID is initially provisional; the person confirms unemployment status and identity with a document at first CPI contact. For online access the Ministry specifies an Italian codice fiscale and SPID or CIE. The sources do not provide a complete universal document list for in-person registration of a TP holder; regional instructions must be checked. [S04–S06]

DID, CPI registration and a **Patto di Servizio** are employment-service steps; they are not immigration or temporary-protection registration. Conversely, a TP document or work authorization does not itself complete DID registration or establish eligibility for a particular employment programme. [S04–S08]

## Assistance / counselling / training conditions

CPI services described by the Ministry include DID assistance, orientation, counselling, short training and matching. Regional sources describe additional profiling, referrals and training pathways. These descriptions establish types of service, not an individually guaranteed appointment, course, funding, employer referral or job placement. Assessment, availability and regional programme rules matter. [S04, S07, S14]

The Ministry associates profiling/assessment with issuing DID to acquire unemployment status or access GOL, and with updating an individualized service pact. GOL measures have their own target groups and procedures; DID alone does not guarantee GOL placement. [S07–S08]

For the formal online DID route, the national guidance identifies Italian codice fiscale and SPID/CIE; initial online identity/state confirmation is completed through CPI contact. The reviewed national material does not establish that a TP receipt alone is sufficient for every region’s in-person jobseeker registration, nor does it define one document checklist for all non-EU TP holders. Check the responsible regional CPI for operational requirements. [S04–S06]

## Unemployment-benefit boundary

**NASpI is an insurance cash benefit administered by INPS**, separate from public vacancy browsing and employment-support services. INPS states the main baseline conditions as involuntary termination of an employee relationship and at least 13 weeks of unemployment contributions in the four years before unemployment, with statutory details and exceptions. A person must submit a benefit claim and meet the applicable requirements; jobseeker/CPI registration or TP status alone is not entitlement. [S15–S16]

INPS describes certain foreign employment periods as relevant where an applicable EU or bilateral coordination/totalization instrument provides for it. INPS’s current list of bilateral agreements with non-EU states does not list Ukraine. The reviewed official evidence does **not establish** that Ukrainian insurance periods count toward NASpI; do not promise aggregation or infer it from pension rules or TP status. INPS must determine an individual case. [S15, S18]

SIISL onboarding for NASpI/DIS-COLL recipients is a separate beneficiary workflow. The 2025 INPS clarification says technical CV/PAD platform issues do not themselves stop payments where statutory entitlement continues; this does not remove distinct legal cooperation obligations connected to active-policy interventions. Do not turn SIISL technical onboarding into a general condition for browsing vacancies. [S19–S20]

## Ukraine-specific programmes

The current Ministry GOL page lists **Skills Partnership for Employment Italia-Ucraina**, under Skills Alliance for Ukraine. It aims to support employment integration and skills adaptation for Ukrainian citizens regularly present in Italy who have signed the GOL Patto di Servizio at a CPI. This is an identified Ukraine-specific employment/training measure, but the Ministry page links to a potential-beneficiary list updated 2026-02-06 and monitoring data through 2025-12-31; these records do not show that applications or new placements remain open on 2026-11-08. Participation, regional delivery and available places must be confirmed with CPI/GOL. [S08–S09]

No current nationwide Ukraine-specific Italian-language course with verified open enrollment on 2026-11-08 was established in reviewed official sources. The Emilia-Romagna 2022 language/employment initiative is historical. The 2022 Lombardy refugee active-policy call is explicitly closed. Do not present either as current support. General regional courses may exist, but they are not evidence of a nationwide TP entitlement. [S22–S23]

## 2025–2026 changes / stale guidance

- **SIISL broadened access:** the Ministry announced that from 2024-12-18 Italian and foreign citizens could access the platform for vacancies/training and upload a CV with digital identity. The October 2026 SIISL implementing notice concerns platform operations and employer-side vacancy/notice obligations; it does not establish that jobseekers need DID just to browse. [S01, S21]
- **SIISL for benefit claimants:** NASpI/DIS-COLL beneficiary registration and CV/PAD workflows were introduced separately, with subsequent operational clarification in 2025. Do not transfer those beneficiary steps to every public portal user. [S19–S20]
- **ANPAL:** suppressed effective 2024-03-01, with functions assigned to the Ministry. Older ANPAL pages may remain online as archives and are not current independent-authority guidance. [S17]
- **Ukraine-specific regional programmes:** 2022 announcements and closed calls are historical. The current GOL Ukraine Skills Partnership is listed by the Ministry, but current enrollment/open capacity on the research date is not established. [S08–S09, S22–S23]

## Unresolved issues

1. **TP-specific CPI document checklist:** national guidance states online portal identity requirements and first CPI identity validation, but does not establish a complete nationwide list for a temporary-protection holder registering in person. **Not established in reviewed official sources.**
2. **Ukraine insurance periods for NASpI:** INPS sources describe aggregation only under applicable coordination/totalization arrangements; a current INPS treaty list does not include Ukraine. Whether any individual Ukrainian record can qualify under another legal basis was not determined. **Not established; no automatic credit should be stated.**
3. **Open enrollment for Skills Partnership for Employment Italia-Ucraina:** ministry materials identify the project and target, with beneficiary/monitoring data current through early 2026, but not a live November 2026 intake or remaining capacity. **Not established.**
4. **A universal national vacancy catalogue:** systems aggregate or publish different subsets; no reviewed official source says SIISL, EURES, or a regional portal lists every Italian vacancy. **Not established.**
5. **Common access rule for every regional portal:** login and application steps vary. Emilia-Romagna’s public-search example cannot establish rules in other regions. **Not established nationally.**

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- Italy has public vacancy search through SIISL, regional employment-service/CPI portals and EURES, with different content and access rules.
- SIISL is open to Italian and foreign citizens for vacancy/training search using digital identity such as SPID or CIE; the live portal requires sign-in to view announcements.
- EURES offers public search and mobility information; several tools are available without registration.
- CPI services include DID assistance, orientation/counselling, short training and job matching, with regional delivery.
- DID formally establishes unemployment status and is separate from immigration/TP registration and from a NASpI claim.
- NASpI is an INPS-administered insurance benefit with separate eligibility requirements.
- The Ministry lists a GOL Skills Partnership for regularly present Ukrainian citizens who have signed a GOL Patto di Servizio at a CPI.

### Require qualification

- Regional service availability, registration steps, login requirements, programme eligibility and training capacity vary.
- A TP holder’s precise CPI documentation and any service access must be confirmed with the responsible region/CPI; the reviewed national sources do not establish a universal checklist.
- Skills Partnership is a current listed programme, but open intake and available places as of 2026-11-08 are not established.
- Foreign insurance periods matter only where a relevant coordination instrument applies; Ukrainian periods are not established as counting toward NASpI.
- Listings and matching systems are not exhaustive and do not guarantee an employer response or placement.

### Do not assert

- One portal lists all Italian vacancies.
- Anonymous/no-login SIISL viewing, or that DID is universally required just to browse.
- Every TP holder can register at any CPI with only a TP receipt, absent regional confirmation.
- CPI/jobseeker registration grants work authorization, residence status, GOL enrollment or NASpI.
- Every jobseeker receives counselling, training, a referral, a funded course or a job.
- TP or DID automatically qualifies a person for NASpI, or Ukrainian insurance periods automatically count.
- ANPAL remains an independent current authority.
- 2022 Ukraine-specific regional programmes remain active in 2026.

## Final topic verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** The official evidence supports careful descriptions of public vacancy channels, CPI/regional services and benefit boundaries. Content should explicitly preserve regional variation, the distinction between browsing and DID registration, and the separation between employment support and NASpI. Do not promise universal TP-holder CPI documentation, Ukrainian insurance aggregation or an open place in the Ukraine-specific GOL measure. Refresh dynamic service and programme pages before publishing operational instructions.
