# Italy — Healthcare Access, Proof and Urgent Care

**Research checked:** 2026-11-08
**Scope:** exactly `healthcare-access`, `healthcare-proof`, and `healthcare-urgent`. Research only. This package distinguishes national SSN rules from regional administration and individual entitlement.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** On 2026-11-08, the Ministry of Health says Ukrainian citizens with temporary protection (TP) may be compulsorily enrolled in Italy’s SSN through 31 December 2026, provided they hold a TP permit or have applied for one. The TP application receipt is therefore sufficient to approach the local ASL for enrolment before the plastic permit arrives. This is not automatic activation: the person must complete a separate local SSN registration. The Ministry says that after 31 December 2026 registration cannot be extended without a renewal receipt or a new permit request; no nationwide post-2026 renewal mechanics were established here, even though EU-level TP has been extended to 4 March 2028. A codice fiscale identifies a person for tax and administrative purposes; it does not by itself prove current SSN enrolment. The Tessera Sanitaria (TS) is normally issued after SSN enrolment and is a proof/access instrument, not the legal source of the entitlement. Emergency access, STP interim access, ticket exemptions and comprehensive free care are distinct. [S01–S06, S13–S16]

## SSN access and enrolment boundaries

The national Ukraine-specific basis is **temporary protection permit status or an application for that permit**, with compulsory SSN enrolment ensured through **31 December 2026**. The legal extension is in the 2026 conversion law, while the Ministry’s current operational page describes the practical conditions. Employment is not listed as a condition of this TP-specific enrolment. **A separate registration step at the local ASL is required**; neither TP status nor a receipt alone proves the person has already been enrolled (S01–S03).

The Civil Protection Ukraine FAQ says to use the ASL nearest the domicile stated in the TP application, presenting the application receipt or permit, and it allows choosing a family doctor and pediatrician for eligible minors. The Ministry’s general non-EU guidance uses registered residence or, when residence is not yet established, effective domicile. ASL, ATS, district and counter names and local document handling can vary. This is an operational local process, not a promise that every applicant will be assigned the same provider or that every doctor has capacity (S03, S05).

The **codice fiscale** is an identification/tax code. A provisional code may be usable by ASL for registration, and the TP receipt may itself contain the code, but the code alone does not establish active SSN coverage. The **Tessera Sanitaria** is generally requested through ASL after SSN enrolment and transmitted to the Sistema Tessera Sanitaria. It functions as identity/health-access proof and carries the codice fiscale; the physical card does not create entitlement or prove that a time-limited special route is still current (S07–S08).

After enrolment, the Ministry describes local paper identification with a personal code and chosen GP/pediatrician. If the plastic card has not arrived, ask the ASL for written enrolment confirmation or the temporary identifier/certificate used locally; present available identity and TP application/permit documentation. **No single nationwide document checklist or identical paper-proof format across every ASL is established.** Do not delay urgent care to obtain a plastic card (S01, S03–S04, S07).

The TP-specific healthcare provision is currently extended through 2026-12-31. Ministry guidance says registration cannot be extended after that date without a renewal receipt or request for a new permit. EU temporary protection lasts until 2028-03-04, but that EU-level extension does not automatically extend Italy’s current healthcare enrollment date. No nationwide instructions reviewed here establish exactly how 2027–28 enrolment, TS validity, or local renewal will work; refresh official guidance before drafting future-dated advice (S01–S02, S17).

## Tessera Sanitaria / proof of entitlement

Keep these separate:

- **TP permit or application receipt:** evidence of TP status/request; the receipt is accepted as a basis to seek SSN enrolment.
- **ASL registration:** the administrative act that places the person in the SSN and enables GP/pediatrician choice and covered pathways.
- **Codice fiscale:** personal tax/administrative identifier; it is not a health-insurance contract or active-entitlement proof.
- **Tessera Sanitaria:** card normally issued after ASL enrolment and associated with the person’s SSN record; a delayed card does not itself cancel completed enrolment.
- **TEAM:** European Health Insurance Card function on the reverse of the TS, usable for medically necessary care during a qualifying temporary stay abroad, under host-state rules. Planned treatment is a separate prior-authorization route. Do not state that every TP beneficiary automatically receives TEAM (S01, S03, S07–S09).

In the Ukraine guidance, ASL registration is the operative step. A person who has the receipt but not a physical permit can approach the ASL. A person who is enrolled but waiting for a physical TS should ask the ASL for the local proof/identifier; available records and local issuance mechanics are not fully standardized in the reviewed national material. The generic foreigner page describes a substitute certificate in a regularization context, but that is not a universal Ukraine-specific certificate guarantee (S01, S03–S04, S07).

## Primary and specialist care

After ASL enrolment, the TP guidance allows selection of a `medico di medicina generale` and, for eligible minors, a `pediatra di libera scelta`. The GP/pediatrician is the usual route for general visits, referrals/prescriptions and non-emergency care. Ministry guidance says hospital admission normally follows written physician request, while emergency admission can be ordered by the Pronto Soccorso doctor. Availability, territorial lists, referral pathways, waiting times and service capacity depend on local organization (S01, S03, S05).

Do not claim that all specialist visits, diagnostics, medicines, dental services or mental-health care are free or immediately available. Ordinary SSN services follow national LEA and regional procedures; ticket rules and pharmaceutical copayments vary. No distinct universal TP-specific mental-health package or service-capacity guarantee was established. For pregnancy, the Ministry identifies protections and defined pregnancy-related services; this is not a statement that every pregnancy-related or private service is cost-free. The Ministry’s Ukraine guidance also describes maternal care and vaccination assessment for displaced people and children; local health authorities determine applicable assessment/pathways (S01, S10–S14).

## Costs, ticket and exemption boundaries

The Ministry’s national ticket guidance lists specialist visits and diagnostic tests, non-urgent white-code Pronto Soccorso services not followed by admission, and thermal care as potentially ticketed. GP/pediatrician visits and inpatient treatment are listed among services without a ticket, and defined maternity/prevention services have exemptions. A white-code ticket varies by Region and is not charged in the stated exceptions, including children under 14 and assisted persons; avoid claiming a blanket rule for all TP applicants without confirmed enrolment/status (S10).

For medicines, the Ministry says most Regions may impose co-payments on Class A medicines; Class C medicines are fully paid by the patient, and choosing a branded product over an equivalent may require payment of the difference. Exemptions may be total or partial and depend on recognized conditions/codes. This rules out an unqualified “healthcare and medicines are free” statement (S10).

**X22 is a distinct, time-limited ticket exemption, not SSN registration.** The Ministry published a 27 February 2026 operational note and correction. Official regional examples report X22 through 2026-12-31 for TP holders enrolled in SSN who declare they are not working; exact entry, declarations and follow-up vary locally. Lombardia says the exemption covers its specified NHS services; AUSL Romagna publishes its own application steps. These examples support explaining the national extension and the qualification, but they do not prove one uniform procedure or the same treatment of every medicine/copayment in every Region. Confirm current X22 coding and local handling with the ASL/CUP. Do not imply all TP holders qualify, that X22 makes every private service free, or that the exemption continues after 2026 (S06, S10–S12).

## Urgent and emergency care

For an emergency, attend the nearest `Pronto Soccorso` or call **112** or **118**. Civil Protection says that if an STP code has not already been issued, the first point of access can issue it or explain how to obtain it; eligible facilities include local health authorities and specified hospitals. The FAQ describes STP as nationally usable for six months and renewable. Before a TP permit application is filed, STP is the stated interim route to basic healthcare. Once the TP request is filed, use the specific TP receipt/permit route to register with the ASL for SSN care (S03–S04, S13, S15).

Art. 35 of Legislative Decree 286/1998 protects defined urgent and essential outpatient/hospital care, including continued care, for foreigners without regular stay, plus specified maternity, child-health, vaccination and prevention protections. This is not the same as comprehensive ordinary SSN enrolment. Treatment may be billed under applicable regional tariffs and rules; indigence-related protections may affect payment, but no universal no-cost episode, ambulance, hospitalization, medicine or follow-up is established (S13–S14).

For non-emergency low-priority care, **116117** is a non-emergency channel where activated; the Ministry lists it as available in specified Regions and says organization varies. It is not a replacement for 112/118 in emergencies, and availability should be checked locally (S16).

## Regional variation

The national basis for TP-related compulsory enrolment to 2026-12-31 and the national X22 extension come from national sources. The actual SSN registration counter, ASL/ATS/district organization, local proof, doctor lists, X22 declaration/entry, medicine copayments, white-code ticket amount and 116117 availability are operationally regional or local. Lombardia and AUSL Romagna are examples only, not national rules. Do not generalize either region’s forms, exact steps or service coverage to all of Italy (S01–S02, S06, S10–S12, S16).

## 2025–2026 changes

- The 2026 law extended the relevant Ukraine healthcare provisions of DPCM 28 March 2022 art. 5(3) through **2026-12-31**; the Ministry’s updated Ukraine page confirms the current enrollment boundary (S01–S02).
- The Ministry says prior TP permit holders whose SSN enrolment was due to expire on 2025-12-31 needed to apply for renewal for 2026; renewal was not automatic. It says no extension beyond 2026-12-31 without a renewal receipt or new permit request (S01).
- A Ministry operational note dated 2026-02-27 covers healthcare and X22, with a correction listed 2026-03-02. Official regional guidance reports the X22 extension through 2026-12-31 for qualifying non-working TP beneficiaries enrolled in SSN (S06, S11–S12).
- EU TP was extended to **2028-03-04** by Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912. This does not itself extend Italian healthcare enrolment or X22 beyond their current national date (S17).
- The generic six-year TS validity must not be equated with six years of TP-specific healthcare registration; for foreign nationals the registration period commonly follows permit/ASL record, and the Ukraine-specific rule currently ends 2026-12-31 absent required renewal evidence (S01, S07–S08).

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- Ukrainian TP holders who have the permit or have applied for it can seek compulsory SSN enrolment through 2026-12-31.
- Registration is a separate step at the local ASL; TP status or receipt alone is not proof that enrolment is complete.
- A TP application receipt may be used before the physical permit arrives to seek enrolment.
- Codice fiscale is not proof of active SSN coverage; TS is normally issued after ASL enrolment.
- Before filing for TP, Civil Protection describes STP as an interim route to basic care; emergencies go to Pronto Soccorso or 112/118.
- Ticket charges and exemptions vary by service and Region. X22 is a separate, time-limited exemption with a non-work qualification in current official regional guidance.
- EU TP through 2028 does not, by itself, establish Italian SSN or X22 coverage through 2028.

### Require qualification

- Registration location is the ASL corresponding to residence/effective domicile or the domicile stated in the permit application; local organization and doctor availability vary.
- GP/pediatrician choice follows registration and local lists; referrals, specialist access, medicines, hospital pathways and mental-health care follow ordinary national/regional rules.
- A missing plastic TS need not prevent an already enrolled person from receiving care; ask ASL for the local paper/identifier or certificate and have entitlement checked.
- TEAM may be available to eligible compulsory SSN enrollees resident in Italy, but confirm TP-specific issuance with ASL; TEAM covers medically necessary temporary-stay care abroad, not planned care without prior authorization.
- Maternity, child-health and vaccination protections cover defined services and do not establish that every service is free.

### Do not assert

- “TP automatically activates SSN,” “the TP card is health insurance,” or “codice fiscale proves active coverage.”
- “Every TP holder gets healthcare free of charge,” “all medicines/specialists are free,” or “X22 applies to every person or every private service.”
- “The receipt alone means registration is already complete,” or “a plastic TS is always required before urgent care.”
- “STP replaces ordinary SSN registration for someone who has already applied for TP.”
- “TP through 2028 means Italian SSN/X22 automatically continue through 2028,” or that a six-year TS date guarantees six years of eligibility.
- Any one Region’s ASL/ATS procedure, X22 form or amount as a nationwide process.
