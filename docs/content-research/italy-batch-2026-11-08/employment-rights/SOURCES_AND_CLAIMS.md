# Italy — Employment Rights During Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-08
**Scope:** exactly `work-permission`, `work-employer-duties`, and `work-minimum-pay`. Research only; this is a national overview, not an individual employment or immigration decision.
**Baseline:** current `main` after `c44f426d12d72f7d1bf6d7ecd7e669d0859d7d0b`.

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Italian TP-specific rules allow covered people displaced from Ukraine to undertake subordinate (including seasonal) and self-employed work on the basis of the TP permit request submitted to the competent Questura; official government FAQs confirm that the Questura receipt (`ricevuta`) allows work while the physical permit is pending. This is a special TP rule, not a rule that every residence-permit receipt authorizes employment. No separate ordinary work permit or quota entry is required for this TP work access, but professional licensing and ordinary labour, tax, social-security, and employer duties remain. [S01–S04, S06–S07]

For private subordinate employment, the employer generally makes a prior electronic hiring notification (UNILAV/Comunicazione Obbligatoria), gives the worker the required employment information, registers and pays social contributions, and meets applicable INAIL and occupational safety duties. The 2026 SIISL channel was introduced as an experimental alternative for mandatory communications; regional systems remain available. The sources do not establish a separate Ukraine/TP-specific employer notification or a universal duty to retain a copy of the TP card for a prescribed period. [S08–S13]

As of the research date, Italy has **no single nationwide statutory hourly or monthly minimum-wage amount**. However, the conclusion is more nuanced than simply “pay is set by CCNL”: Law 112/2026 converted DL 62/2026, whose Article 7 establishes the applicable “fair wage” framework for private subordinate work, using representative sectoral CCNL total economic treatment as its benchmark, with a fallback where a sector has no collective bargaining coverage. This does not create one uniform euro rate; amount depends on sector, employer activity, category, worker classification and applicable terms. Article 18 excludes public employees and their applicable collective agreements from this decree’s scope. [S15–S18]

## Work authorization boundaries

For the TP route, the relevant start point is the filing of the temporary-protection residence-permit request at the competent Questura: OCDPC 872 Art. 7 says the covered person may undertake subordinate (including seasonal) and self-employed activity on that basis. The Civil Protection Department’s official Q&A states that the person may start on the Questura `ricevuta` before the physical permit is delivered. Thus the legal basis is the **specific TP request/receipt**, not the bare fact of arrival, a general registration document, or any immigration receipt. [S02–S04]

The TP work right is separate from ordinary work-based immigration procedures such as employer-led authorization for a person entering from abroad. There is no separate ordinary work permit or quota place for the TP route described by Art. 7; normal employment communications, contributions, professional registration, qualification recognition, tax and social-security rules continue to apply. [S02, S06–S08, S11]

D.Lgs. 83/2026 implements the EU single-permit reform and excludes TP holders and pending TP applicants from the TUI single-permit provisions. Its amended TUI Art. 5(9-bis) is a conditional pending-work rule for other covered permit applications: work requires the qualifying filing, receipt from the competent office, other legal requirements, and remains subject to possible notice of obstacles. It neither turns every `ricevuta` into work authorization nor creates a general TP conversion route. [S06–S07]

TP work access does not waive licensing or qualification rules. An occupation may be regulated under national or regional rules; foreign qualification recognition, registration with an order, or other authorization may still be needed. This package establishes that boundary only and does not determine the recognition route for any profession. [S01]

## Employer duties

For ordinary private subordinate hiring, the employer generally sends the electronic mandatory hiring communication before work begins. The Ministry describes the UNILAV/CO channel and confirms the communication serves the competent labour, INPS and INAIL administrations. Since 1 April 2026, authorized employers/intermediaries may also use SIISL experimentally; regional and autonomous-province systems remain available. This is a transmission-channel change, not a TP-specific hiring procedure and not a universal duty to post a vacancy before hiring. [S08–S09, S20]

The employer must provide required written terms using a written individual contract or the prescribed hiring communication, with any remaining statutory information supplied within the periods in D.Lgs. 152/1997. This information duty is not the same as a universal rule that every employment contract must be signed in writing to be valid. [S10, S14]

Employers must meet ordinary social-security contribution duties and, for covered workers and work activities, INAIL insurance duties. Workplace risk assessment, prevention, information and training obligations arise under ordinary occupational-safety law. These obligations do not disappear because the worker’s immigration work authorization comes from TP; coverage details differ between subordinate, self-employed and other work arrangements. [S11–S13]

The employer should establish that the person and document fit the TP-specific authorization basis before relying on it. The sources reviewed do not establish a special TP-only employer filing, card-copy retention period, or a different UNILAV workflow. The worker must provide accurate identity/status evidence and comply with ordinary contractual, tax and professional requirements as applicable; no additional TP-specific worker registration duty was established here. [S02–S04, S07–S09]

## Minimum-pay / collective-agreement boundaries

There is no single statutory Italian hourly/monthly amount applicable to every worker. Article 36 of the Constitution protects remuneration proportionate to the work’s quantity and quality and sufficient for a free and dignified existence. The 2026 “salario giusto” legislation now makes representative collective bargaining the statutory reference mechanism for private subordinate work: identify the representative CCNL by sector/category and employer activity, scale and legal form; for another CCNL the total economic treatment cannot be lower than that benchmark, and a reference CCNL is specified where the sector lacks bargaining coverage. [S15–S16]

The statutory term `trattamento economico complessivo` (TEC) is broader than one base hourly figure. Article 7(4-bis) includes fixed and continuous direct, indirect and deferred pay items, additional monthly payments, fixed/continuous allowances and specified contractual welfare; it excludes discretionary and variable items. Which wage table, job level, working-time basis, increments, allowances and other terms control must be checked for the actual job. Do not compare a net-pay figure or one monthly figure with the TEC floor without applying the relevant legal and collective terms. [S16]

Article 18 limits this decree’s scope to private subordinate employment and excludes public employees and their applicable collective agreements. Do not apply this specific Article 7 floor mechanically to public employment, self-employment or every non-subordinate work relationship. Those groups remain subject to their own constitutional, statutory, collective and contractual rules. [S15–S16]

Law 144/2025 delegated legislation on remuneration/collective bargaining but did not itself enact a numerical national wage rate. DL 62/2026, converted by Law 112/2026 and effective in converted form from 28 June 2026, is enacted law and changed the framework as described above. The C.2179 minimum-wage proposal remained a bill in committee in the official parliamentary status reviewed; do not describe it as law. [S16–S19]

## Unresolved issues

- No current public operational instruction was identified that prescribes one uniform document-checking or record-retention procedure specifically for every employer hiring a TP holder. Do not invent a TP-specific card-copy term; the employer must establish the applicable lawful work basis. [S02–S04, S07–S09]
- The sources support the TP request receipt as the TP-specific pre-card work basis, but an individual case can turn on whether the application is the qualifying TP application submitted to the competent Questura and whether the person is within the protected class. [S02–S04]
- The precise applicable CCNL, representative comparator, job classification and TEC calculation require facts about employer activity, the actual work and contract. No generic pay figure can resolve this. [S16]
- Article 7’s wage framework applies to private subordinate employment; public-sector and non-subordinate work requires separate analysis. [S16]
- Current official legislative status should be checked again before use after 2026-11-08; this package records the date-specific status of the C.2179 proposal. [S18–S19]

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- Covered people under Italy’s Ukraine TP framework may work in subordinate, including seasonal, and self-employed forms under the TP-specific rule; no separate ordinary work permit or quota place is required for that route. [S02–S03]
- Work can start on the TP application receipt from the competent Questura before the physical TP permit is issued, according to the official Civil Protection FAQ. [S02, S04]
- Employer CO/UNILAV, written employment information, social contributions, applicable INAIL insurance and workplace safety duties are separate from immigration authorization. [S08–S14]
- No one nationwide statutory hourly/monthly minimum-wage amount existed on the research date; for private subordinate work, enacted 2026 law uses representative CCNL TEC benchmarks and a fallback where a sector lacks bargaining coverage. [S15–S16, S18]
- C.2179 was a proposal, not enacted legislation, in the parliamentary status checked. [S18–S19]

### Require qualification

- Pre-card work requires the receipt for the relevant TP application filed with the competent Questura; do not generalize to any `ricevuta`. [S02–S04, S06–S07]
- A TP holder’s permission lasts only while the applicable TP work basis remains in effect; any post-TP route or pending-work right requires separate legal qualification. [S05–S07]
- The applicable wage floor depends on representative CCNL, employer activity, sector/category, worker classification, work schedule and TEC components; the 2026 decree applies to private subordinate relationships and excludes public employment. [S16]
- Employer work-right checks should confirm the actual TP status/document basis, but no universal TP-specific photocopy-retention rule is established by these sources. [S02–S04, S07]

### Do not assert

- That all foreign nationals can work on any permit receipt, or that a pending application automatically grants work rights.
- That TP status automatically converts to a work permit or allows work after the relevant TP entitlement ends.
- That the TP rule removes licensing, professional recognition, tax, registration, social-security or ordinary employment conditions.
- That a TP-specific UNILAV, employer approval, or fixed card-copy retention procedure exists without a current source.
- That Italy has one universal minimum-wage figure, or that the absence of one figure means there is no enforceable remuneration floor.
- That any selected CCNL or any one gross/net monthly number automatically determines every worker’s lawful pay.
- That Law 144/2025 or bill C.2179 created a statutory euro rate.

## Final topic verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Content may explain TP-specific work access from the qualifying Questura application/receipt, ordinary employer duties, and the 2026 private-sector CCNL-based fair-wage benchmark. It must not generalize TP receipts into universal work permission, promise automatic post-TP work status, invent a Ukraine-only employer procedure, or publish a single Italian minimum-wage amount. For any individual wage calculation, determine the applicable representative CCNL, activity, classification and TEC elements; recheck current official instructions for the actual status and hiring channel.
