## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Official Italian measures provide for continuing some reception of displaced people from Ukraine through 4 March 2027, through specified CAS/SAI and related arrangements. This is a continuation of defined publicly funded reception pathways, not an individual guarantee of admission, a bed, or a particular location. Temporary-protection status alone does not establish automatic access to housing support. The separate 2022–2025 `contributo di sostentamento` and several civil-protection reception measures have ended; the one-time 2025 exit contribution had a closed eligibility window.

Private renting follows ordinary Italian tenancy rules; no TP-specific prohibition on entering an ordinary lease was identified. For residential leases within Law 431/1998, valid lease agreements require written form. Leases exceeding 30 days in a year with the same tenant generally require tax registration within 30 days; the housing-law registration duty is on the landlord, while the Revenue Agency allows either party to submit the registration. Law 392/1978 sets a three-month-rent security-deposit ceiling for residential leases within its scope. Residence registration at the Comune is separate from lease tax registration and the landlord’s foreign-guest notification.

No universal, automatic TP-specific rent subsidy, deposit grant, or social-housing entitlement was established. The national rent-support fund is implemented through regional/municipal rules and calls, and parliamentary material reports no ordinary state-budget funding for that fund since 2023. `Wohnbeihilfe` is not an Italian national scheme; the Italian equivalents and public/social housing rules are regional and local. A narrow 2026 national contribution for eligible separated/divorced parents is not a general TP housing benefit and its application window had closed by the research date.

## Public reception and arrival boundaries

The current national evidence is **OCDPC 1175/2025**, not the old 2022 emergency pages. It allows specified forms of reception for displaced Ukrainians holding TP permits to continue until **4 March 2027**, including CAS/SAI pathways and certain agreements taken over by Prefetture. This confirms a current legal/administrative continuation for covered arrangements; it does not establish that every TP holder is eligible, that new intake is open in every province, or that an available place exists. Ask the competent Prefettura or the responsible reception project about current intake, criteria, relocation, contributions, house rules, and termination (S01–S03).

Do not tell a newly arrived person that a nationwide emergency centre will provide an immediate free bed. The sources reviewed do not establish such a guarantee as of 2026-11-08. Earlier `accoglienza diffusa`, residual civil-protection reception and 2022 first-arrival arrangements must be identified as historical or transitional where relevant. The 2025 one-time exit contribution (up to €250 per person, household cap €1,200) applied only to qualifying people/families already housed at public expense who voluntarily left by 31 January 2025; it is not current arrival money (S02–S05).

The EU extension of temporary protection to 4 March 2028 extends the protection framework, not every Italian housing programme or benefit. The Italian reception authorization currently reviewed runs through 4 March 2027 and is narrower in the arrangements it covers (S03, S06).

## Private rental and registration boundaries

No TP-specific prohibition on ordinary private renting was identified. A TP document does not guarantee that a landlord will accept an applicant or that the applicant will meet the agreed rent, references, income, deposit or other ordinary contract conditions. Avoid presenting a universal landlord document checklist; confirm practical requirements directly and distinguish contract formation from public support (S07–S10).

For residential tenancy contracts governed by Law 431/1998, written form is required for validity. Tax registration is generally due within 30 days for leases exceeding 30 days in a year with the same tenant; either party may submit the RLI registration, though the landlord has the statutory duty to register and notify the tenant. Residential deposit rules under Law 392/1978 art. 11 cap the security deposit at three months’ rent and provide legal interest within that law’s scope. These rules do not establish a rent ceiling or show that support will cover rent (S07–S10).

Keep three address-related procedures separate:

1. **Tax registration of the lease** with Agenzia delle Entrate, where required.
2. **Anagraphic residence declaration** to the Comune based on actual habitual residence, generally within 20 days of the relevant change/event. A local Comune assesses its process and evidence; the Rome Prefettura page is only a local guide (S12–S13).
3. **Accommodation notification to public security** by the person providing accommodation to a foreign national, generally within 48 hours under D.Lgs. 286/1998 art. 7. It is not a replacement for either of the above (S11–S12, S22).

The landlord/host notification obligation should not be misrepresented as an additional universal personal address-reporting filing by every TP holder. The reviewed housing sources do not establish a single TP-specific BFA/immigration address-change procedure beyond the applicable permit/Comune rules; check official TP instructions for a particular change.

## Housing financial support and regional variation

The national rent-access fund in Law 431/1998 art. 11 is a framework administered through regional/local criteria and calls. The MIT overview identifies housing instruments, but does not prove current funding or automatic beneficiary access. The Camera dossier reports no ordinary state-budget allocation to the national fund since 2023; local and regional calls can have their own budgets, criteria and dates. Thus a local call may exist while no universal nationwide allowance is available (S07, S14–S17).

No current nationwide Ukraine-specific private-rent amount was established. Do not reuse the historical monthly `contributo di sostentamento` or confuse the 2025 one-time exit contribution with rent assistance. Likewise, no nationwide deposit grant/loan/guarantee for TP holders was identified. Any municipal or regional “agenzia casa,” arrears, rent or deposit programme requires a local source and an eligibility check (S02–S05, S14–S17).

Public/social housing is another distinct system. MIT describes regional/local responsibility; the Lazio public-housing call and eligibility pages illustrate that allocation may depend on a public call, lawful status, residence/work connection, income, assets and property circumstances. They are not national rules. TP is not automatic qualification for a municipal, ERP, or cooperative dwelling (S20–S21). The 2026 separated-parent contribution is a narrow, time-limited programme with a closed application window, not a general rent allowance (S18–S19).

The current sources do not establish one nationwide income threshold that automatically ends reception or housing support. Employment and household resources can be relevant to needs-based or means-tested programmes, but the decision depends on the specific reception project, regional call or assistance law. Verify obligations to report changes and the effect of income with the responsible authority; no universal amount or threshold is asserted here (S02–S03, S16–S17).

## 2025–2026 changes and stale Ukraine guidance

- OCDPC 1123/2024 transitioned or closed several emergency Ukraine reception measures during 2025; its dates and the former `contributo di sostentamento` deadlines have expired (S02, S04–S05).
- OCDPC 1175/2025 authorizes specified reception continuations through **4 March 2027**. It does not reopen all ended measures or guarantee capacity (S03).
- EU temporary protection currently extends through **4 March 2028**. That is not an automatic extension of Italy’s separately governed reception, private-rent, or cash-support measures (S06).
- The 2026 separated/divorced-parent rent-contribution application window ran 7–28 October 2026 and was closed on the research date. It is not Ukraine-specific (S18–S19).
- Older Civil Protection FAQs and pages still describing 2025 as the final reception date, the 2022 €300/month support, or the former widespread emergency arrangements are historical/stale for current housing availability. Use the later ordinance for the current limited continuation (S02–S05).

## Drafting boundaries for Phase 23 content

### Safe to state

- Specified publicly funded CAS/SAI and related arrangements for displaced Ukrainians with TP permits are authorized to continue through 4 March 2027; this does not guarantee a bed to every person.
- TP status is distinct from a decision to admit someone to public reception or award a rent benefit.
- Several Ukraine-specific emergency reception/cash measures from 2022–2025 have expired; the old `contributo di sostentamento` is not current.
- Ordinary private rental is a separate landlord–tenant process. For residential contracts within Law 431/1998, written form is required; statutory tax registration and residence registration are different procedures.
- Law 392/1978 provides a three-month-rent deposit cap within its residential-tenancy scope.
- The host’s 48-hour foreign-national accommodation notification is separate from tax registration and the tenant’s Comune residence declaration.
- Italian housing support and public-housing allocation are substantially regional/local; calls and criteria must be checked in the relevant location.
- No nationwide TP-specific rent/deposit grant or universal social-housing qualification was established in this research.

### Require qualification

- Current CAS/SAI availability: specify that the 2027 continuation concerns defined arrangements and that local intake/eligibility/capacity must be confirmed.
- Rent-fund status: explain the statutory national framework alongside the regional/municipal implementation and funding limits; do not imply a current open call.
- Tax registration: state the >30-day annual threshold and 30-day deadline with the relevant statutory scope and clarify who may file.
- Residence registration: state the general 20-day requirement and Comune process while directing readers to current local instructions and accepted TP documentation.
- Social-assistance/income interaction: describe only the competent programme’s rule; do not claim one national cutoff.

### Do not assert

- Every TP holder has a right to a free bed, immediate reception, or a guaranteed chosen location.
- EU TP through 2028 automatically extends Italian reception or cash benefits through 2028.
- The former `contributo di sostentamento`, 2025 exit contribution, or 2022 `accoglienza diffusa` is open to new applicants in November 2026.
- A universal nationwide monthly rent subsidy, deposit grant/loan, or TP housing payment exists.
- A federal/administrative budget allocation is cash paid directly to every beneficiary.
- `Wohnbeihilfe` is an Italy-wide benefit; or TP alone qualifies someone for municipal/social housing.
- All accommodation has the same lease form, registration rule, deposit treatment, rent-control regime or landlord documentation requirements.
- One regional/municipal call, amount or eligibility test is an Italy-wide rule.
- Comune residence registration, Revenue Agency lease registration, and the host’s foreign-guest notification are interchangeable or one automatically performs the others.
