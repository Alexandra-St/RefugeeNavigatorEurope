## 5. Cross-topic consistency checks

The reviewed package findings are compatible when their stated scopes are preserved; no cross-topic contradiction requiring a topic or aspect to be blocked was found.

- **TP legal status and physical permit:** the packages distinguish the temporary-protection entitlement from the physical permit/receipt. The 4 March 2028 EU endpoint does not establish individual Italian renewal or reissue mechanics.
- **TP work access and ordinary residence routes:** employment-rights evidence concerns the specific qualifying TP application/receipt basis. It does not conflict with residence research, which finds no currently open general TP-to-work/residence conversion route and warns that pending lawful stay is not automatic work permission. Do not generalize the TP receipt rule to every `ricevuta`.
- **Dates and healthcare:** 4 March 2028 is the EU TP endpoint; 31 December 2026 is the end date stated by reviewed sources for the special Italian SSN-registration framework. These concern different rules. National post-deadline healthcare registration mechanics remain unresolved.
- **Reception and cash assistance:** housing and social-assistance packages distinguish specified, capacity-dependent CAS/SAI/reception arrangements through 4 March 2027 from the closed `contributo di sostentamento` and ordinary welfare. Reception is not a guaranteed bed or ordinary cash award.
- **Employment services and unemployment benefit:** job browsing/CPI registration and assessed services are separate from work authorization and from contributory NASpI; the packages do not establish automatic Ukrainian insurance-period aggregation.
- **School access and TP status:** education findings concern minors’ access/compulsory schooling and do not depend on asserting a TP-only entitlement. Ukrainian online schooling alone is not treated as a replacement for Italian compulsory education.
- **Child/family benefits:** no other reviewed Italy package establishes a conflicting child-benefit rule. Within the social-assistance evidence, current INPS AUU application guidance explicitly lists the TP permit while older INPS guidance differs; retain that documentary discrepancy and the ordinary AUU conditions. Current Bonus nido evidence and the unresolved nationwide TP position for Comune maternity aid remain distinct.

## 6. Drafting constraints

### Safe to state

- The research packages support bounded drafting for all eight topics and all 24 comparison aspects, each with qualifications.
- TP runs to the EU endpoint of 4 March 2028; keep the scheme separate from physical permit renewal details.
- TP-specific work access, ordinary immigration routes, CPI/job-search services, NASpI, health registration, reception, education, and welfare/family benefits are distinct legal or administrative questions.
- The reviewed social-assistance package supports no automatic nationwide subsistence payment from TP alone; ADI/SFL have independent status, residence, means, and activation criteria.

### Require qualification

- Preserve all regional, municipal, provider, programme, and case-specific boundaries in the aspect matrix.
- Use the packages’ stated 2026 dates. In particular, do not carry the special SSN registration framework beyond 31 December 2026 without refreshed evidence; do not treat housing reception continuation to 4 March 2027 as a placement guarantee.
- Keep the AUU current-manual/older-guidance discrepancy visible and require ordinary benefit conditions; do not infer a universal TP entitlement.
- Before operational instructions are published, refresh time-sensitive Questura procedures, dynamic job-service/programme availability, and healthcare rules after the stated 2026 deadline.

### Do not assert

- A nationally uniform Italian military-document procedure, a current general TP-to-residence conversion route, or automatic work rights from any permit receipt.
- That the TP endpoint guarantees an individual physical permit valid to that date, or that SSN access automatically continues to that date.
- Guaranteed CAS/SAI placement, universal TP rent/deposit aid, or that closed Ukraine cash schemes remain available.
- Automatic ADI/SFL or general subsistence eligibility from TP; automatic AUU/Bonus nido/municipal benefit entitlement from TP alone; or nationwide Comune maternity eligibility.
- A national minimum-wage figure, guaranteed CPI/training placement, Ukrainian insurance-period credit, fixed language-course hours, Ukrainian online schooling as an automatic compulsory-schooling exemption, or universal university-fee exemption.
