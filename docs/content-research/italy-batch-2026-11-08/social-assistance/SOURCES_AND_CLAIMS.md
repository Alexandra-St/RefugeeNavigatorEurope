# Italy — Social Assistance and Family Benefits

**Research date / `LastCheckedAt`:** 2026-11-08
**Exact scope:** `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, `social-child-benefits`
**Research basis:** Official Italian legislation and administrative guidance. This is a national-boundary review, not an individual eligibility decision.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Temporary protection (TP) does not itself create a general national minimum-income payment. Ordinary `Assegno di Inclusione` (ADI) and `Supporto per la Formazione e il Lavoro` (SFL) have enumerated residence/status tests plus means and/or activation rules; TP alone is not one of the ordinary qualifying statuses, and holding TP plus five years’ residence does not by itself meet the listed status test. Separate, narrow statutory rules for named “special case” permits must not be extended to TP.

Two distinct forms of support do apply to some displaced Ukrainians: a needs-assessed place in the reception system may continue under a 2025 Civil Protection order through 2027-03-04 for covered existing reception arrangements; and the old one-off `contributo di sostentamento` is closed, with its last request deadline in April 2025. Reception is in-kind and capacity-dependent; it is not ordinary cash welfare or a guaranteed placement.

For families, INPS’s AUU service-user manual expressly lists the Ukrainian temporary-protection permit among permits accepted for `Assegno Unico e Universale` (AUU). This remains conditional on the general tax/residence or work requirements, eligible dependent-child rules, and application. The 2026 INPS `Bonus asilo nido` circular also expressly includes the Ukrainian TP permit, subject to residence, child-age, expense/medical and ISEE rules. AUU’s “universal” character means a minimum amount may be paid without an ISEE; it does not remove immigration/status, residence, tax, child or application conditions. Local social assistance and the Comune maternity allowance require local/case-specific confirmation.

## Subsistence / ordinary welfare boundaries

There is no general national minimum-income payment created by TP status. ADI and SFL are separate anti-poverty/activation measures with enumerated applicant status and residence requirements, household/resource tests and obligations. The statutory recognition of refugees or international-protection beneficiaries in those routes must not be extended to temporary protection by analogy.

Ordinary local social services are another route. The integrated social-services framework is implemented through regions and municipalities; local emergency aid, social-service casework or family support may exist, but no uniform nationwide TP cash amount, eligibility checklist or deadline was established. The Comune can assess a person’s situation and direct them to local services. A CAF or patronato can provide application/ISEE assistance where offered; it is not a benefit and cannot waive legal tests.

A separate reception-in-kind route remains current: OCDPC No. 1175 authorizes through 2027-03-04 the continuation of covered arrangements and reception in specified CAS/SAI structures for displaced Ukrainians with extended TP. This order continues the reception system, not a guaranteed place for every TP holder and not cash in lieu of a place. The competent Prefettura/territorial authority must assess individual need and availability.

## ADI / SFL eligibility

### Assegno di Inclusione (ADI)

ADI is a means-tested household benefit. At least one household member must meet a target-category condition (minor, disability, age 60 or older, or qualifying disadvantaged status with certified inclusion in a social/health care programme). The applicant’s ordinary immigration/status route is Italian/EU citizen or qualifying family member, EU long-term permit holder, or holder of international-protection/stateless status. The general residence rule is at least five years in Italy, with the last two continuous, and applicable household members must remain resident. INPS lists 2026 ISEE up to €10,140 plus family-income/assets, durable-goods, household and other legal tests. Exact amount depends on the calculation and circumstances; do not infer it from this summary.

TP alone is not an enumerated ordinary ADI title. INPS describes an additional statutory special route for specifically named `casi speciali` permits (arts. 18, 18-bis and 18-ter TUI); that route is not a TP exception. An individual may qualify on another independent legal basis, but that requires separate assessment. The claimant applies to INPS directly or through a patronato/CAF. If admitted, they register in SIISL and sign the household Digital Activation Pact; municipal social services assess inclusion needs and coordinate the personalized inclusion pact. Payments use the electronic inclusion card. The 2026 renewal payment/timing changes do not alter the immigration-status threshold.

### Supporto per la Formazione e il Lavoro (SFL)

SFL is an individual activation allowance for people aged 18–59. INPS lists Italian/EU citizenship or qualifying family-member status, EU long-term residence, international-protection/stateless status, and at least five years’ residence including the last two continuous; TP alone is not listed. Published 2026 requirements include valid ISEE no higher than €10,140, a family-income/resource test and other statutory conditions. SFL is linked to DID, SIISL registration, the Digital Activation Pact, a Personalized Service Pact with the CPI/work service and effective participation in an accepted training/activation measure. INPS’s current page describes €500/month for effective participation, for up to 12 months and possibly a further 12 where a qualifying course is continuing and the service pact is updated. Do not portray it as an unconditional allowance or general TP support.

Identify eligible 0–3 service and submit actual invoices/proof of payment or the required medical certificate for home support. | INPS verifies permit/residence/eligibility and allowable provider/cost. ISEE affects the ceiling; payment does not exceed eligible expense. |
| Comune maternity/local social assistance | Apply to the Comune or local social-service office within the relevant local or statutory period; ask the Comune whether the TP permit is accepted for the specific measure. | Comune grants/assesses the basic maternity allowance and local social assistance; INPS pays the Comune maternity allowance. CAF/patronato help is not the decision. |
| ISEE/DSU | File through INPS (pre-filled or ordinary) or obtain assistance from an authorized CAF. Adult family members may need to authenticate/authorize access for pre-filled data. | ISEE is calculated through the national system; it is not itself an award. Each benefit authority applies its own threshold and means rules. |

The INPS pages do not establish a single exhaustive identity-document checklist for all schemes. In practice, the application must correctly identify the applicant and child/household and provide the required residence/title, tax-code, bank/payment and expense information for that measure. If the TP permit/renewal receipt is not recognized automatically, contact INPS or the Comune with the current permit/renewal evidence; do not assume a CAF can override a system or eligibility refusal.

## Ukraine-specific support: current vs historical

### Current reception, not general cash support

OCDPC No. 1175 of 17 December 2025 authorizes continuation through 4 March 2027 of certain arrangements taken over by Prefectures and reception in specified CAS/SAI facilities for people displaced from Ukraine who hold extended TP. It is a current public reception framework for covered cases. It does not promise that every TP holder has a bed, that a new arrival is automatically admitted, or that a cash equivalent is paid. Refer a person needing accommodation to the Prefettura for the relevant Comune and state that placement depends on the applicable arrangement and availability.

### `Contributo di sostentamento`: closed

The former Civil Protection subsistence contribution was €300/month for up to three months after the TP application, for people who arranged accommodation independently (with an additional child-related amount under the historical rule). Official Civil Protection guidance says it cannot be granted to TP permits issued after 31 January 2025 and the last request deadline for the final installment was 20 April 2025. It is not available as a new 2026 application. Older pages describing its €300 amount or 2022 platform are historical and must not be presented as current.

### Transition into ordinary schemes

Ending the one-off emergency payment does not automatically make a TP holder eligible for ADI/SFL. Those use separate statutory qualifying-status and residence tests. Families may apply for AUU or Bonus nido if the relevant INPS title and other conditions are met. Local Comune aid remains locality- and assessment-specific. Reception in kind remains distinct from those cash-benefit routes.

## Child and family benefits

### Assegno Unico e Universale (AUU)

AUU is INPS’s monthly dependent-child allowance. It covers each dependent minor; a dependent child from 18 to 21 only if the statutory education/training, internship/work, registered job-search or universal civil-service conditions and income limit are met; a dependent child with disability may qualify without an age limit. A new-born benefit can start from the seventh month of pregnancy. INPS’s citizen application manual lists a Ukrainian temporary-protection residence permit as an eligible title. The applicant must also meet the general current tax and Italian residence or qualifying insured-work requirements and provide the child/family information. It is not a TP-only award.

ISEE affects the amount and increases. In 2026, the minimum can still be requested without an ISEE or where the ISEE is above the indexed maximum, provided all non-ISEE legal requirements are met. An accepted resident claim continues automatically without a new annual application; update the ISEE and report family changes/new children. INPS’s older 2022 immigration-title note does not name TP, while the later online application manual does; record this version discrepancy and use the later explicit operational manual, with individual INPS follow-up if automated title validation fails.

### Bonus asilo nido and at-home support

The INPS 2026 circular expressly names the Ukrainian TP title as a qualifying residence permit, subject to Italian residence and other requirements. The nursery contribution reimburses eligible costs for authorized early-childhood education for a child under three; a separate home-support contribution is limited to a child under three with a serious chronic pathology preventing nursery attendance, supported by the required paediatrician attestation. From 2026, the application can remain effective through August of the year the child turns three, subject to annual resource reservation/eligible month and document steps. ISEE determines the maximum, and reimbursement cannot exceed eligible expenditure. It is separate from and compatible with AUU.

### Comune maternity and local benefits

The basic Comune maternity allowance is granted by the Comune and paid by INPS for eligible birth/adoption events, subject to income and insurance conditions and the applicable application deadline. INPS says foreign applicants must ask their Comune which exact residence-permit types qualify. The reviewed national guidance does not establish that TP alone is accepted everywhere, so this remains unresolved pending local confirmation. Other municipal/regional child benefits can exist and may be compatible with AUU, but require current local research; do not invent a national amount or TP eligibility.

Former `Bonus bebè`, the three-minor-child allowance and other listed national child measures were replaced by AUU from March 2022. Do not present them as separate current universal payments. Other maternity/working-mother or insurance-based benefits have distinct conditions and are outside a general TP child-benefit promise.

## Regional/municipal variation

- ADI and SFL are national INPS benefits; their decision criteria are national, though municipal social services and CPI/work services have roles after filing.
- Reception referral and local placements run through the competent Prefettura and covered facilities; the continuation order is not a guaranteed bed.
- General emergency social-service aid and local family schemes depend on region/Comune rules, available programmes, residence and means assessment.
- The Comune grants the basic maternity allowance; INPS pays it. The Comune must confirm the exact permit category and any local application/document steps for a TP holder.
- AUU and the national Bonus nido use national INPS rules. Authorized childcare service categories and local supporting services can be governed by regional rules, but the benefit’s TP title is expressly addressed in the 2026 INPS circular.
- An ISEE threshold or amount for one national benefit must not be carried over to another benefit or local call.

## 2025–2026 changes

- The former Ukraine `contributo di sostentamento` ended; last installment requests closed on 2025-04-20. Permits issued after 2025-01-31 are excluded from that historical contribution (S16, S19–S21).
- Although a Civil Protection FAQ still contains a 2025-12-31 reception end date, OCDPC No. 1175, issued later, authorizes covered reception continuation through 2027-03-04. Use the later order for the current period and retain the qualification that it is not a universal placement guarantee (S18–S19).
- INPS’s 2026 AUU page confirms updated 2026 amounts and continuation of already accepted resident claims without annual re-application; ISEE and household changes still need updating (S04, S09).
- INPS’s 2026 Bonus nido circular explicitly lists the Ukraine TP permit and introduces longer application validity through the child’s third year, annual booking and 2026 ISEE-for-specific-benefits treatment (S07–S08).
- INPS’s 2026 SFL page publishes current thresholds/activation amount; ADI’s 2026 page reflects revised renewal mechanics and the separate special-permit route. Neither change adds TP to the ordinary status list (S01–S03).
- INPS’s January 2026 change for `attesa occupazione` concerns that distinct permit and is subject to possible recovery pending litigation. It cannot be inferred to include TP holders (S22).

## Unresolved issues

1. INPS’s 2022 AUU title message omits temporary protection, while the later online citizen application manual expressly lists it. The present research treats the later operational listing as support for TP eligibility, but the current AUU overview does not display a title-by-title list. If INPS’s automated application rejects a valid TP permit, individual confirmation/review may be necessary.
2. TP eligibility for the Comune maternity allowance is not resolved nationally; INPS directs applicants to their Comune for exact permitted-title rules.
3. The current national reception order continues covered arrangements, but does not establish a universal new-arrival intake route, current capacity, priority order, or guaranteed accommodation for an individual. Ask the relevant Prefettura.
4. No single municipal emergency cash-assistance programme for all TP holders, common ISEE threshold, nationwide application form, or universal local deadline was established.
5. Applicants with expired permit cards or renewal receipts should ask INPS/Comune to verify the current permit validity in the case. No single documentation outcome for all digital databases and benefit types was established.
6. A TP holder who also holds an independent qualifying ADI/SFL status (for example, a later long-term permit or a separately recognized status) requires individual assessment; this review does not determine mixed-status or family-member edge cases.
7. Exact 2026 benefit amounts are indexed and can change; this package focuses on eligibility boundaries and records only amounts/ceilings needed to explain the scheme. Refresh official INPS and local notices before drafting a dated payment amount.

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- TP alone does not create a general national minimum-income cash entitlement.
- TP alone is not one of the ordinary ADI or SFL status categories, and those schemes also require residence, means, household/age and activation conditions.
- The 2022 Ukraine `contributo di sostentamento` is closed; its last request deadline was 2025-04-20.
- Specified reception arrangements for displaced Ukrainians can continue through 2027-03-04 under OCDPC 1175; placement is not guaranteed to each TP holder and this is in-kind reception, not cash welfare.
- INPS’s AUU application manual lists the Ukrainian TP permit, subject to the general applicant and child requirements.
- The 2026 INPS Bonus nido circular expressly lists the Ukrainian TP permit, subject to residence, age, eligible service/expense and other requirements.
- AUU’s minimum amount may be available without ISEE if all other eligibility requirements are met; ISEE changes the amount.
- CAF/patronato support is application assistance, not an entitlement or eligibility decision.

### Require qualification

- Describe ADI’s special-permit exception as applying only to permits named by INPS/statute, not TP.
- Describe local social-service help and municipal maternity allowance as dependent on Comune/region rules and individual assessment.
- Describe reception support as continuation for covered arrangements, subject to referral and available placement.
- State AUU TP eligibility with the later INPS manual citation and explain that tax/residence or work, child, application and ongoing conditions still apply; note the older 2022 INPS title-page discrepancy if material.
- State Bonus nido TP eligibility by citing the 2026 circular, and distinguish nursery reimbursement from the narrow serious-pathology home-support route.
- Label every indexed amount by benefit, year, ISEE band, child/household unit, eligible expenditure or participation condition.

### Do not assert

- Automatic ADI/SFL or universal ordinary-welfare eligibility solely from TP, poverty or family status.
- International-protection eligibility as if it were TP eligibility.
- CAF/patronato as a benefit authority or separate payment.
- The old €300 Ukraine contribution as open in 2026.
- A guaranteed reception bed, a fixed cash substitute, or access without referral/capacity review.
- AUU as unconditional or unrelated to immigration/title, tax/residence/work, dependent-child and application requirements.
- Bonus nido as flat cash or as available without a qualifying expense/medical basis.
- Universal TP access to the Comune maternity allowance or local family grants.
- Former `Bonus bebè`/three-child ANF as separate current national benefits.
