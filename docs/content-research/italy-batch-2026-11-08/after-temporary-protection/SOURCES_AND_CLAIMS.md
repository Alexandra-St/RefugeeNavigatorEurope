# Italy — Residence Options During and After Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-08
**Scope:** exactly `residence-routes`, `residence-conditions`, and `residence-filing`. Research only. This is a national-law overview, not an individual eligibility determination.

## Research verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Italian law confirms that TP holders may work while their TP entitlement applies, and it created a specific, application-based conversion to work permits for defined earlier TP permit cohorts. The renewal/conversion periods in the 2023 and 2024 provisions ended no later than 2026-03-04. I did not establish a currently open, general Italian TP-to-work conversion route for applications made on 2026-11-08. General employment, self-employment, family, study, highly-qualified/Blue Card and protection routes exist under their own laws, but TP status alone does not establish that each route can be requested in-country or that its ordinary eligibility requirements are waived. The EU transition recommendation is nonbinding. [IT-RES-S01–S10]

There is a qualified basis to count lawful TP residence toward the five-year period for EU long-term residence after transition to a title that permits applying: Italian TUI Article 9(3)(b) bars granting long-term residence while the person is currently under TP, while Article 9(5) excludes only periods in paragraph 3(d) and (e) from the calculation. An official regional immigration information portal likewise says periods under (a)–(d), including TP under (b), are counted. Because this is not an explicit TP-specific nationwide Ministry FAQ and the EU Long-Term Residents Directive excludes TP holders from its scope, state the counting rule with qualification and do not promise a grant. [IT-RES-S01, S11–S12]

## Available residence routes

1. **Continue under temporary protection.** While the individual remains within the TP decision’s scope and has the corresponding right/document, TP provides residence and work access under the TP framework. This is not an ordinary work permit or a guarantee of a future residence title. EU Decision 2026/1912 extends EU temporary protection to 2028-03-04, applying from 2027-03-05; specific Italian document renewal mechanics beyond the current 2027 national guidance were not located. [S02, S07, S15]
2. **TP-to-work conversion under the earlier special Italian provisions.** Application-based conversion existed for defined TP permit cohorts as they applied for renewal, for work actually being performed. The 2024 extension’s renewal period ended 2026-03-04. As of the research date, no new general TP-holder conversion period was established. [S03–S04, S15]
3. **Ordinary employment.** TUI subordinate-work routes usually require employer-led SUI authorization, applicable quota or a statutory exception, visa where required, and residence-permit application after entry. A TP-holder’s existing permission to work is separate from qualification for this ordinary route. [S01, S10, S12–S13]
4. **Self-employment.** TUI Art. 26 has a separate route with its own entry and eligibility requirements. TP status alone does not establish a conversion right into this title. [S01, S12–S13]
5. **EU Blue Card / highly qualified work.** TUI Art. 27-quater remains a route with route-specific job, qualification/experience, salary and procedural criteria. The EU Council recommends allowing qualified TP beneficiaries to apply to relevant EU statuses, but Italy’s recommendation-specific in-country transition procedure was not found. [S01, S05, S08]
6. **Family residence.** TUI Arts. 29–30 provide family-reunification/family residence routes where sponsor, relationship, income/housing and other statutory conditions are met. TP status does not make all family members eligible or waive requirements. [S01, S10]
7. **Study residence.** TUI Art. 39 et seq. contains study routes. Admission and route-specific resources/insurance/visa conditions apply. Neither TP nor the Council Recommendation establishes an automatic change from TP to a study title in Italy. [S01, S05, S08]
8. **International protection.** Asylum/refugee or subsidiary-protection procedures remain separate individual processes. TP does not automatically convert into them. [S01–S02]
9. **EU long-term residence.** Not available for grant while a person remains under TP. After moving to a qualifying residence title, the statutory framework and official regional guidance support counting prior TP residence in full toward the five years, with qualification and individual calculation. [S01, S09, S11, S16]

No special, automatic Italian post-TP residence status was identified. The EU Recommendation asks Member States to facilitate transitions but is not itself a permit or an Italian filing rule. [S07–S08]

## Conditions and conversion boundaries

- **Temporary protection work rights:** work access applies under TP while the right is in force. No conversion is needed to exercise that TP-based access; this does not transform the TP document into a regular employment title. [S02]
- **Historical conversion:** both the 2023 and 2024 statutory provisions were application-based and linked to specified expiring permits and renewal requests. The statute described conversion for the activity actually carried out. The latest renewal/conversion window identified ended 2026-03-04. [S03–S04]
- **Ordinary work:** employment and self-employment routes have separate requirements. Work from abroad is generally employer/SUI-led, subject to the relevant flow decree or an exception and visa requirements. A job offer alone does not equal authorization or an in-country conversion entitlement. [S01, S10, S12–S13]
- **Highly qualified work:** the Blue Card route has its own eligibility. The Council Recommendation does not waive qualification, salary, job, employer or procedural conditions; no Italian administrative instruction implementing an in-country TP-to-Blue-Card transition was identified. [S01, S05, S08]
- **Family and study:** statutory sponsor/relationship and admission requirements apply. No uniform document list or automatic TP conversion was established. [S01, S10]
- **Long-term residence:** current TP holders cannot receive the EU long-term residence permit while under TP. TUI Art. 9(5) excludes from the five-year calculation only the paragraph 3(d) and (e) periods, not TP under 3(b); the official regional portal says periods under (a)–(d) count. The supported reading is full TP-time credit after transition to an eligible title, but an individual case should confirm continuous lawful residence and all current requirements. [S01, S09, S11, S16]
- **Do not mix TP and `protezione speciale`:** DL 201/2025 as converted in 2026 extended a limited group of Ukrainian `protezione speciale` permits; it is not a general TP conversion or renewal rule. [S06]

## Filing / pending-status boundaries

### Where an application goes

- **Ordinary work recruitment from abroad:** employer submits the work authorization request through the competent SUI; the worker then follows the applicable visa process with the Italian consular authority and applies for the residence permit after entry. [S10, S12–S13]
- **Initial TP permit request:** the 2022 DPCM directs submission to the Questura for the place where the person is domiciled. This TP issuance procedure is distinct from later ordinary permit applications and from the expired special conversion window. [S02]
- **Ordinary conversion:** only the route and permit types allowed by current law can be converted. SUI commonly handles clearance/eligibility steps; the Questura issues the residence permit. Do not use Ministry pages listing study, internship or seasonal conversion to infer that all TP holders currently have a conversion option. [S01, S10, S12]
- **Postal kit / Questura:** selected permit applications can use the `Sportello Amico` post-office kit; other titles have different submission channels. The available official category table states it was last updated 2024-05-30 and is not evidence that a TP-specific conversion remains open. Check the competent Questura and current national instructions for an actual application. [S12, S14]
- **Expired special TP conversion:** the earlier Italian law described filing at renewal. Its relevant renewal deadline has passed; no nationwide current deadline or filing channel for a new TP conversion was found. [S03–S04]

### Pending stay and work

From 2026-06-04, TUI Art. 5(9-bis), as amended by D.Lgs. 83/2026, states that while issue, renewal or conversion is pending, a foreign national may lawfully stay and temporarily work where the other legal requirements are met. For work, the competent office must have issued a receipt confirming the request; the temporary-work rule continues until a public-security authority communicates grounds opposing the requested issue, renewal or conversion. The rule is not an automatic permit, does not establish a valid underlying conversion route, does not extend TP itself, and does not make every receipt a work authorization. [S05]

The pending rule must be kept separate from work rights under active TP. While TP is active, the TP-specific framework supports work; after TP ends, do not infer either a residence right or work authorization from a pending-status statement without checking whether the particular filed route, receipt and all other statutory requirements qualify. [S02, S05]

## Long-term residence and residence-time counting

Italian TUI Art. 9(3)(b) excludes a person currently residing under TP from receiving EU long-term residence. That is a restriction on grant while the TP ground remains current. Article 9(5) says that only residence periods under paragraph 3(d) and (e) do not count; TP is paragraph 3(b). Tuscany’s official PAeSI information portal expressly says that periods under (a), (b), (c) and (d) are counted toward the five years. Accordingly, the supported reading is that lawful TP time counts in full after the person moves to a title under which they can apply, rather than starting the five-year clock from zero or applying the student half-time rule. [S01, S09, S11, S16]

This is **supported with qualification**, not a promise of eligibility: the applicant must hold a qualifying current title at application and satisfy the other requirements, including the applicable income, residence-continuity, language and security rules. The Directive excludes TP holders from its scope while they are on TP; it does not expressly exclude TP time in Art. 4(2)’s list of non-counted periods. No Italy-specific CJEU judgment on this exact TP-time question was identified in this research. [S01, S09, S11, S16]

## Unresolved issues

- A currently open general TP-to-work conversion on 2026-11-08 is **not established**. A Ministry migration FAQ retains a broad “yes” answer but cites the 2023 conversion provision without clearly stating its expiry/cohort boundaries; the statutory texts reviewed are more specific. [S03–S04, S15]
- No Italian national law or administrative instruction implementing the Council Recommendation as a TP-specific in-country Blue Card, single-permit or study conversion was found. [S05, S08]
- The Italian documentation/renewal procedure for TP beneficiaries during the EU extension after 2027-03-04 was not established in current national instructions. The EU extension itself is in force on its stated schedule. [S07, S15]
- TP-time counting for long-term residence has a strong textual and official regional-guidance basis, but no TP-specific nationwide Ministry clarification or relevant national case-law determination was located. Treat individual calculations as authority-dependent. [S01, S09, S11, S16]
- The exact route-specific documents, appointment channel, fee and application timing depend on the title and competent office; this package does not establish a universal checklist. [S01, S10, S12, S14]

## Drafting boundaries

### Safe to state

- TP itself permits work while the TP entitlement applies. [S02, S07]
- Italy created an application-based TP-to-work conversion for specific earlier permit cohorts, with conversion linked to work actually being carried out. [S03–S04]
- The latest special TP renewal/conversion window located ended on 2026-03-04; a current general conversion route is not established. [S04, S15]
- Ordinary work, self-employment, Blue Card, family, study, asylum and long-term residence each have separate legal conditions and procedures. [S01, S08, S10–S13]
- TUI Art. 5(9-bis) provides a conditional pending stay and temporary-work rule when all statutory conditions, including the receipt requirement for work, are met. [S05]
- The EU decision extends TP to 2028-03-04 on its stated schedule; it does not automatically issue another Italian residence permit. [S07]

### Require qualification

- Prior TP time is supported as countable in full toward the five-year long-term residence period after transition to an eligible title, but current TP holders cannot receive the long-term permit while still under TP; individual calculation and all other requirements remain. [S01, S09, S11, S16]
- The EU Recommendation promotes transitions to national/EU legal statuses but is nonbinding and requires qualification under national law. [S08]
- Ordinary route filing from within Italy depends on the specific permit, current lawful status and statutory in-country filing rules. [S01, S10, S12–S14]
- Pending work after TP expiry depends on the filed route, statutory requirements, competent-office receipt and absence of communicated obstacles; do not infer it from lawful stay alone. [S05]
- The Italian administrative implementation of the EU TP extension after March 2027 requires a fresh source check as guidance is updated. [S07, S15]

### Do not assert

- Automatic TP-to-work conversion, automatic TP-to-any-residence conversion, or automatic status after TP ends.
- A currently open general TP conversion window based only on the broad older migration FAQ.
- That the Council Recommendation itself creates an Italian visa, in-country filing right, conversion route or permit.
- That lawful pending stay alone grants permission to work, or that every application receipt is sufficient.
- That all Blue Card, study, family or self-employment routes can be filed in-country by TP holders, or that TP waives their conditions.
- That an applicant can receive EU long-term residence while currently holding only TP, or that prior TP time is automatically approved without checking eligibility and continuous residence.
- That DL 201/2025’s `protezione speciale` renewal rule applies to TP holders.
- That an Italy-issued TP document is automatically renewed through 2028 without checking updated Italian authority instructions.
