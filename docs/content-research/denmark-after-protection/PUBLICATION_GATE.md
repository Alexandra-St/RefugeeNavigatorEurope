# Publication gate — Denmark after the Ukraine Special Act

> **PUBLICATION BLOCKED.** These three language drafts are unpublished editorial material, not verified guidance. Do not transfer them to production catalogs or publish them while the official-source conflict remains unresolved.

## A. Claims suitable for human editorial review

The following narrow statements have official support in the reviewed material, subject to human source and legal-language review:

- The Special Act is a temporary residence basis.
- BEK no. 828/2026 states a possible extension through 17 March 2028 subject to the section 10 exception, with own-initiative review and suspensive effect during processing. This is not an individual guarantee.
- The Labour Market Attachment and Educational Attachment pages describe specific routes for some current Special Act permit holders, with pre-expiry filing and substantial scheme-specific criteria.
- The cited general work and study schemes have their own entry requirements and do not establish an automatic transition.
- The Immigration Service’s page states that Special Act residence is not itself a basis for permanent residence and is excluded from the eight-year calculation described there.
- On 15 October 2026, the Folketing page listed L 43 as introduced, with first reading scheduled for 13 November 2026.

The claim-level record and exact source passages are in [ARTICLE_CLAIM_REVIEW.md](ARTICLE_CLAIM_REVIEW.md). “Suitable for review” does not mean verified, publication-ready, or applicable to a person’s individual case.

## B. Claims to omit or hold until the conflict is resolved

1. **Do not publish a definitive current extension date or say that a particular reader’s permit lasts until 17 March 2028 or 17 March 2027.** BEK 828/2026 says 2028, while two accessible Immigration Service pages still say 2027 and include old March 2026 processing dates. A disclaimer does not resolve this conflict.
2. **Do not reuse the Immigration Service’s old processing instructions or rights statements tied to March 2026 / 2027 as current steps.** The pages contain visibly outdated timelines.
3. **Do not state that filing another residence application automatically preserves or ends residence/work rights.** The reviewed evidence does not establish a rule common to these routes.
4. **Do not present L 43’s proposal as law or predict its enactment.** Recheck the legislative record after its scheduled first reading and before publication.
5. **Do not state that any named route is available to every person with a Special Act permit, or that meeting a summary of criteria guarantees approval.**

- For Labour Market Attachment, how does SIRI apply “legal, continuous and unsupported employment” and the same-employer condition to applicants whose current permit is under the Special Act? What evidence and exceptions apply?
- For Educational Attachment, what exact institution, programme, enrolment and timing criteria apply to current Special Act permit holders, including after a refusal or revocation?
- How does a later permit on another basis affect the permanent-residence qualifying period in a person’s individual circumstances?
