## Legislative and administrative changes

- Act No. 1655 of 30 December 2024 reformed the kontanthjælp system; the main changes took effect on 1 July 2025. Current pages and the September 2026 consolidation use kontanthjælp and the current rate structure. Drafts do not use historical payment rates.
- Current Borger.dk and SIRI/STAR guidance says Special Act permit holders are not currently covered by arbejdspligt. They may still be covered by an integration programme and individually applicable requirements.
- The earlier 15 October 2026 research check recorded L 43 as introduced on 8 October and ongoing, not enacted. The bill record and hearing portal could not be read again in this integration pass, so that procedural status is not newly confirmed here. The proposal concerns removing the current work-obligation exception and includes proposed 2027 dates; it remains research-only. Recheck live status, final text and commencement before publication or deployment.
- No amounts are included. Current 2026 rates exist, but they are volatile and unnecessary for this navigation article.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

1. SIRI’s Ukraine FAQ is the most specific source on process applicants, permit holders, spouse/assets, child allowances and special-instance help, but is undated. Reopen relevant passages and cross-check current law and practice before publication.
2. An applicant’s route depends on procedural basis, whether an immigration authority has a maintenance duty, accommodation and any other residence ground. The draft does not classify every pending applicant as eligible or ineligible.
3. The current family-benefit page is general and does not confirm every Special Act permit holder satisfies child and youth benefit qualifying rules. The specific child-allowance FAQ is undated; Udbetaling Danmark should confirm the benefit and qualifying period for the individual.
4. MitID, CPR and document requirements vary by service and person. No universal prerequisite is stated.
5. L 43 may change the work-obligation explanation. Recheck its current status, final text and commencement before publication or deployment.
6. Local application channels, language support and documents vary. The article cannot predict an individual award, amount, start date or sanction.
7. Housing benefit/deposit loans, detailed childcare guidance and employment-service navigation are covered in Denmark × Housing, Education and Employment and are not repeated here.
