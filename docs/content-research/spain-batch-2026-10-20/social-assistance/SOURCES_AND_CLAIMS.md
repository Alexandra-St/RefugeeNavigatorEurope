# Spain — Financial and Social Assistance: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Official sources below were opened and checked on **20 October 2026**. No exact verification times are invented. This matrix distinguishes basic social services, the conditional state reception system, the Minimum Vital Income (Ingreso Mínimo Vital, IMV), its child supplement, and regional/local support. It does not determine individual eligibility.

## Scope and result

Spain’s law and current Ministry information identify several different routes. The System of Reception for International and Temporary Protection can include temporary-protection applicants and beneficiaries who lack sufficient resources and meet access/permanence conditions; it provides individualized reception conditions and services. The IMV is a separate means-tested Social Security benefit with residence, household, income and asset requirements. Regional minimum-income schemes and local social services have their own rules.

The reviewed official sources do not establish a universal cash payment for every temporary-protection holder, a current Ukraine-specific nationwide cash grant, or automatic IMV/child-supplement eligibility based solely on temporary protection. Do not state that absence of a program has been definitively proved.

## Conflicts, limitations and claims excluded

- **No universal payment:** the reviewed laws and pages do not establish that every temporary-protection holder receives a cash allowance. Reception access is resource- and condition-based; IMV is a separate means-tested benefit.
- **Temporary-protection status and IMV:** the IMV law sets a general residence requirement and exceptions. The sources reviewed do not settle how a particular TP document or period is treated for a particular applicant; do not promise that TP alone is sufficient.
- **Historical 2022 aid discrepancy:** the dated Ministry page states “400 euros per family” plus €100 per minor, while Article 7 of RD 673/2022 states €400 per eligible adult plus €100 per dependent minor. The underlying decree is the primary legal text for the historical scheme, but the discrepancy is not needed for current navigation; omit all amounts and do not portray the old measure as available now.
- **No proof of current Ukraine-specific grant absence:** the bounded review did not identify a current nationwide Ukraine-specific cash grant. This is an evidence limitation, not proof that no later or regional measure exists. Recheck before publication.
- **Child supplement:** general IMV law specifies a child supplement and additional means tests. The reviewed sources do not confirm its applicability to every TP holder; do not describe it as an independent or guaranteed child payment.
- **Regional and local support:** autonomous-community minimum-income programs, emergency aid, local eligibility, forms, appointment routes and document lists are outside this national review. Article 14 of LO 4/2000 and the Local Regime Law do not substitute for local program rules.
- **MOP excluded:** no claim about extraordinary immediate assistance (MOP) or its individual categories is made.
