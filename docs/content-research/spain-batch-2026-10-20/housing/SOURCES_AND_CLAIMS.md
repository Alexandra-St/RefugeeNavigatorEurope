# Spain — Finding Accommodation: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. All sources below were opened and read on **20 October 2026**. The matrix distinguishes the state reception system from private rental and municipal registration. It does not promise accommodation or rental support.

## Scope and result

Official guidance supports directing a person with insufficient resources to the national reception-system information and assessment route, and describing a small set of practical rules for habitual-residence rentals under the Urban Leases Act (LAU). Registration in the municipal Padrón records actual residence and is handled by the municipality; it is not a ruling on private housing rights. The lawful document route can vary by address and circumstances. Housing subsidies, municipal housing access, reception placement availability and an individual's eligibility remain unresolved.

## Material source note: 2026 LAU amendment history

The current BOE consolidated LAU opened on 20 October 2026 displays later entries for 30 September, 2 October and 7 October 2026. It explicitly records that the RDL 26/2026 modification to Article 20 was left without effect by the 2 October resolution. The reviewed Article 20 text says named real-estate-management and contract-formalization costs are for the landlord. Because this is date-sensitive and recently amended, recheck the consolidated text immediately before publication. This is not treated as a contradiction in the checked current text, but it is a publication-time freshness gate.
