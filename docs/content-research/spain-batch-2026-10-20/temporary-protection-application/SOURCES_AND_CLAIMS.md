## Contradictions, evidence limits, and publication gates

### EU protection endpoint versus Spanish TIE validity

The EU act of 30 July 2026 sets the scheme endpoint at **4 March 2028**. Orden INT/96/2026 explicitly extended the relevant Spanish TIE cards only through **4 March 2027** and says no replacement card is needed for the cited Article 209 obligation. The Ministry overview and FAQ still say 4 March 2027; the FAQ also describes renewal as automatic and directs card renewal if it expires. Those statements pre-date the July 2026 EU extension. This is not evidence that individual Spanish permission or TIE validity automatically continues through 2028. No Spanish national extension past 4 March 2027 was located in the official sources reviewed. Before publication, recheck for a later Spanish measure and authoritative instructions about document evidence after that date.

### Military-obligation evidence

The EU act’s Article 2, corrected on 5 August 2026, is applicable from 5 August and contains a continuous-existing-holder exception through the 4 August cut-off. Spain’s Ministry overview repeats a broad new-applicant condition but does not provide a Spanish age split or exact evidence format. The sources do not resolve the detailed Spanish application procedure or expressly explain the existing-holder exception on the Spanish page. Report the EU scope separately from the Spanish notice; do not claim Czech age/document rules apply in Spain and do not extend the condition to routine continuity/extension of existing status without authority support.

### Older procedural guidance

The procedure page and FAQ date to 2022 or have no displayed current revision date. The FAQ contains historical duration and TIE statements now inconsistent with the 2026 EU act. The current overview gives live Ministry contacts but operational details can change. Recheck intake location, appointment method, document instructions, card handling, and any current military-evidence instructions before drafting or publication.

### Current conclusion

The evidence supports Spain-specific application cohorts, the documented Police/CREADE procedure, an EU scheme endpoint of 4 March 2028, and a Spanish TIE extension instrument through 4 March 2027. The detailed Spanish military-evidence requirements and Spain’s TIE handling after 4 March 2027 remain unresolved. Travel to Ukraine/other Member States, renunciation to move protection, and cessation events are not developed here because this review did not establish a current, complete Spain-specific account for those situations. Asylum/international protection is a separate regime and is not presented as an automatic successor or recommended alternative. These gaps must remain explicit; no personal eligibility or document-validity conclusion may be inferred.
