# Spain — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Every source below was opened and read on **20 October 2026**. Where an official page displayed an update date, it is recorded; “not displayed” means no publication/update date was visible on the reviewed page. This is an internal evidence matrix, not user advice or approved article content.

## Scope and result

Navigation to official vacancy search and public employment services: **research-ready with regional caveats**. The evidence supports signposting SEPE/Empléate, EURES and the competent autonomous-community employment service. It does not support promises of job placement, benefits, admission to a particular course, free training, or recognition of a qualification.

## Excluded or narrowed claims

- Do not promise that SEPE/EURES will find or place a person in a job.
- Do not promise unemployment benefits, a free course, admission to a course, or recognition of foreign qualifications.
- Do not state Spanish is universally a legal prerequisite for all work. EURES's broad practical wording is not a legal eligibility rule.
- Do not publish a universal registration checklist: the competent region and individual case matter.
- Qualification recognition is a separate navigation route through the official REGPROF database and National Assistance Centre for regulated professions. Academic homologación/equivalencia procedures were not reviewed; do not conflate them with professional recognition or imply an employment service recognizes a diploma.
