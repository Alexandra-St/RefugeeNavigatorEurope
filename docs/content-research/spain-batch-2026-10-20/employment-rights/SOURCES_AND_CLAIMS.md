# Spain — Work Authorization and Employee Rights: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Sources listed below were opened and read on **20 October 2026**. No exact verification time is recorded. This is an internal evidence matrix; no individual work authorization or legal outcome is determined.

## Scope and result

This matrix supports a narrowly qualified overview of work authorization attached to Spanish temporary protection and basic employment signposts. It supports the **2026** minimum wage, selected contract/pay/working-time protections, and selected employer Social Security, contract-reporting and authorization duties. It does not support individual permit advice, a net-pay calculation, a complete employer-compliance checklist, or generalized deadlines and termination entitlements.

## Publication caveats and exclusions

- The Ministry's Ukraine page contains stale protection-duration material. It is used only as a limited description of work modes; it must not be used for dates, permit validity, or new application evidence.
- Do not write that every applicant may work. The legal source ties work authorization to the grant decision; current instructions should be rechecked for any receipt-stage rule.
- Do not state “no separate work permit” as a universal rule unless the target person's current Spanish protection document and current operational rules are verified. The grant decision itself includes work authorization.
- €1,221/month, €40.70/day and €17,094 annual floor are **2026 only**. No 2027 figure is established.
- Do not state a universal termination payment, notice period, or guaranteed outcome. Article 59 timing should be rechecked on publication and tailored by qualified support.
- Avoid asserting that a particular collective agreement applies. It can improve or specify statutory terms.
- A separate ITSS webpage about anonymous mailbox communications could not be opened directly. No claim is made about an anonymous channel; the complaint routes in WORK-S08 were directly opened and read.
