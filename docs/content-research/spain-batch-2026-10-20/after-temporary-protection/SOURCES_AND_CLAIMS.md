Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Research only; not article copy, translation approval, legal certification, or personal route recommendation. `Confirmed` describes direct official support; `Conditional` indicates route-specific requirements or context.

## Article

- Working English title: **Residence Options Beyond Temporary Protection in Spain**
- Category: `temporary-protection`
- User question: What Spain’s current official framework says about applying for other residence authorizations while holding temporary protection, and what is known about transition and long-term residence counting.
- Scope boundary: Signpost only a narrow set of routes expressly relevant to current TP holders. Do not rank routes, calculate eligibility, or imply TP must expire first.
- Architecture: [Spain V1 proposal](../../../features/SPAIN_CONTENT_V1_PROPOSAL.md).

## Contradictions, limits, and publication checks

### Dedicated transition framework, not an automatic conversion

A February 2026 Spanish order states that no transition “passerelle” had yet been regulated. That description was accurate when written, but was superseded chronologically by RD 316/2026 (14 April) and SEM 2/2026 (22 June). Together these establish a Spain-specific administrative framework that lets eligible TP holders seek applicable ordinary authorizations, with route-specific requirements. They do not establish one independent permit automatically granted to all TP holders.

### National versus EU long-term residence

SEM 2/2026 expressly says TP time counts in full toward the five-year national long-term-residence period. The regulation sets a separate EU long-term route with its own five-year legal-continuous-residence test and enumerated counting rules; the sources reviewed do not say TP time counts there. Keep the national credit out of any EU long-term-residence statement. Clarification of TP-time counting for the EU route remains a publication question; do not infer either full or partial credit.

### Dated historical statement

SEM 2/2026’s opening background says the scheme remains in force through 4 March 2027, reflecting the legal position before Decision (EU) 2026/1912. The operative transition instructions remain relevant where consistent with later law, but this historical date must not be repeated as the current EU endpoint. Recheck the instruction’s continued administrative use and later amendments before publication.

### Publication boundary

This research does not recommend which route an individual should apply for. Before drafting/publishing, recheck current application channels, route eligibility requirements, individual procedure/fees, and authoritative treatment of TP time under EU long-term residence. Keep asylum and temporary protection distinct; this phase does not research or compare individual asylum eligibility, and does not present asylum as an automatic successor or preferred option.
