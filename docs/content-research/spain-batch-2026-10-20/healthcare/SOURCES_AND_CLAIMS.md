# Spain — Accessing Healthcare: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Sources were opened and read on **20 October 2026**. No exact verification times are invented. This matrix keeps the national framework separate from autonomous-community procedures and does not describe healthcare as universally free.

## Scope and result

A current 2026 decree lists applicants for temporary protection among groups whose health rights follow specific rules, and health-card administration is regional. The Ministry's official FAQ page currently offers its Q&A PDF as a frequently-asked-questions document, but the PDF viewer title is “Draft Concept Note,” it has no clear revision date, and it contains outdated duration statements. A separate current Ministry of Health pathway concerns people without legal residence and does not establish the TP-specific receipt/card procedure. Therefore the detailed practical route remains unresolved for publication; do not present receipt/resolution-to-card steps as settled. A 2022 Ukraine page makes broader “free healthcare” statements and is dated; those broad statements are not relied on as current proof of universal coverage or payment treatment.

## Conflicts, limitations and claims excluded

- **Document-status limitation:** the Ministry's current FAQ page publicly offers the Spanish and Ukrainian Q&A PDFs as FAQ documents, but the Spanish PDF's viewer title reads “Draft Concept Note” and no revision date is displayed. This establishes that the Ministry currently presents the file publicly as FAQ material; it does not establish that the PDF is a final, current revision. HEALTH-S07 is current but scoped to people without legal residence and does not replace the TP-specific route. Do not rely on exact receipt/card steps as settled until a current TP-specific instruction is confirmed.
- **Stale broad guidance:** the Ministry's 7 June 2022 page says all Ukrainian TP applicants receive free healthcare under the same conditions as local residents. This research does not use that statement as proof that every service, medicine or user charge is universally free in 2026.
- **No copayment conclusion:** pharmacy copayments, insurance contributions, individual exemptions, regional service pathways and coverage limits were not sufficiently established in the sources reviewed. Exclude amounts and universal-payment claims.
- **No card interchangeability:** a TP receipt/resolution is evidence the Q&A tells the person to present; an individual health card is issued/managed through health authorities. Do not call a TIE, NIE, TP receipt and health card interchangeable.
- **No universal primary-care/referral workflow:** appointment, assigned provider, referral, interpretation and regional contact details require autonomous-community-specific research.
