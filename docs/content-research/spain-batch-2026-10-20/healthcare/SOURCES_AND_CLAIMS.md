# Spain — Accessing Healthcare: Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Sources were opened and read on **20 October 2026**. No exact verification times are invented. This matrix keeps the national framework separate from autonomous-community procedures and does not describe healthcare as universally free.

## Scope and result

The clearest safe navigation is to present the temporary-protection application receipt or grant resolution to the health centre/unit designated by the competent autonomous community and ask how to obtain the regional health document/card. A current 2026 decree lists applicants for temporary protection among groups whose health rights follow specific rules. The Ministry's detailed Q&A PDF provides the practical evidence route, but its viewer title is “Draft Concept Note”; its final status should be confirmed before production use. A 2022 Ukraine page makes broader “free healthcare” statements and is dated; those broad statements are not relied on as current proof of universal coverage or payment treatment.

## Conflicts, limitations and claims excluded

- **Document-status limitation:** the Ministry-hosted Q&A PDF is useful for the receipt/resolution route but its viewer title reads “Draft Concept Note.” The content is not treated as settled final instructions until a human editor confirms the version's status or replaces it with an authoritative current page.
- **Stale broad guidance:** the Ministry's 7 June 2022 page says all Ukrainian TP applicants receive free healthcare under the same conditions as local residents. This research does not use that statement as proof that every service, medicine or user charge is universally free in 2026.
- **No copayment conclusion:** pharmacy copayments, insurance contributions, individual exemptions, regional service pathways and coverage limits were not sufficiently established in the sources reviewed. Exclude amounts and universal-payment claims.
- **No card interchangeability:** a TP receipt/resolution is evidence the Q&A tells the person to present; an individual health card is issued/managed through health authorities. Do not call a TIE, NIE, TP receipt and health card interchangeable.
- **No universal primary-care/referral workflow:** appointment, assigned provider, referral, interpretation and regional contact details require autonomous-community-specific research.
