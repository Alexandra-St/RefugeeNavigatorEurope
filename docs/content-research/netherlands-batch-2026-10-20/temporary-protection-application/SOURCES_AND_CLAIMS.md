## Scope

Temporary-protection cohorts, municipal/IND intake, evidence and proof of residence, military-obligation screening, and validity dates. Do not merge Ukrainian nationals, family members, third-country nationals, prior EU-protection holders, or people with pending/appeal cases into one rule.
