## Scope

## Research gaps / drafting gate

- The full current consolidated English Decision (EU) 2026/1912 was reviewed through the official EUR-Lex full-text indexed presentation. Direct browser access to EUR-Lex HTML/PDF still returns robot verification. The corrected text establishes the 4 March 2028 scheme horizon and effective dates; it does not establish a person’s individual proof-document validity.
- The IND PUC lists IB 2026/34 as a current, externally accessible information bulletin (published 7 Sep 2026; modified 8 Oct 2026), but its document body/PDF was not extractable. Do not treat the metadata as confirmation of its substantive rules.
- Current IND public guidance supports the 23–60 evidence examples and a family-joining exception, while Decision Art 2’s corrected continuing-holder exception is worded for the same Member State. Clarification of their exact reconciliation, younger/outside-band cases, other military-obligation cases, and the full municipal screening cohort remains necessary. Do not invent an 18–22 rule.
- Confirm the response/review route after negative triage or an individual refusal. The handbook does not establish a complete appeal process.
- Government.nl’s English page still says 2027, in conflict with the Decision and current IND/Rijk guidance. Record it as stale public guidance; recheck that it is corrected before publication. Recheck each IND proof-of-residence document cohort separately.
- Third-country-national status, appeals, previous TPD in another EU country, and family applications remain separate pathways.
