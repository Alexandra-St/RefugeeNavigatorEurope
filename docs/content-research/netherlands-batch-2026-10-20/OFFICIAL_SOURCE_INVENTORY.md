## Inventory interpretation

- The inventory contains 34 distinct official URLs. Of these, the EU operative text and 25 September attachments remain inaccessible; those entries are access records, not verified substantive evidence. The October municipal instruction, June policy letter and new RMO provider page were actually opened and relevant text reviewed.
- A displayed update date, effective date, or version date is reproduced as the page presents it. “Not displayed” means no date was visible in the inspected page view, not that the source is timeless.
- Search snippets, inaccessible linked documents, and uninspected legislation are not promoted to verified evidence here.
- Labels are provisional article-scoping aids. Phase 18.1B should create article-owned claim matrices with direct passages, conditions, source language, source-check timestamps and claim-level support decisions.
- The inventory intentionally includes repeated URLs only once in the unique source set. A future article-owned matrix may assign its own article-local source label to a shared official URL.
