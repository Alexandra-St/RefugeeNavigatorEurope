## Article research scopes

### NL-TP-01 — Temporary protection application

**User questions:** Who may fall within the EU Temporary Protection Directive (TPD) in the Netherlands? Where does a new applicant start? What are BRP/municipal registration, IND screening or appointment, BSN and proof-of-residence steps? What do the IND sticker, O-document and extension letter each evidence?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-TP-01–06 in the [official source inventory](OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md).

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Separate nationality and pre-war residence cohorts, Ukrainian and eligible third-country-national situations, previous protection in another EU state, public-order conditions, application date, family-joining exceptions and individual IND decisions. The municipality’s 17 September 2026 triage guidance describes referral before BRP registration and a military-obligation screening pathway; older public step pages describe BRP-first sequencing. Resolve the operational sequence from the actual current municipal instruction and IND route before drafting. The military evidence detail and its exact applicant scope need primary-document confirmation; do not infer age bands or paperwork from another country’s rules.

**Research questions:** What exact cohorts remain eligible, including third-country nationals and people with prior EU protection? Which authority currently performs triage, and in what order do triage, BRP, BSN and IND proof occur? What documents satisfy the military rule and which applications/family cases are excepted? What action is needed for each sticker/card cohort for the 2028 extension?

**Comparison mapping:** `protection-scheme`, `protection-application`, `protection-dates`.

### NL-TP-02 — Residence after temporary protection

**User questions:** Which ordinary residence routes might be relevant (work, study, family or another route)? Where are their official requirements and procedures? What is the status of the announced transition document?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-TP-07–08, NL-RES-01–04 and NL-TP-01 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Work, study and family permits have distinct sponsor, income, qualification, application, timing and evidence rules. Permanent residence requires its own statutory conditions; temporary protection must not be treated as automatically counting toward those conditions. Government transition-document pages conflict in tone and dates with current TPD pages and with the Dutch-language policy page, which describes the document as a cabinet plan. No transition procedure is treated as operational in this scope.

**Research questions:** Obtain and review the June/September 2026 parliamentary letters, any explanatory notes and any implementing legislation. Determine whether a transition document is merely proposed, enacted for future commencement, or operational; identify exact cohorts, dates, application/notification path and appeal rights. Separately check the eligibility and in-country filing rules for each ordinary permit route. Clarify whether TPD time counts for any long-term or permanent route from applicable law and current IND guidance.

**Comparison mapping:** `residence-routes`, `residence-conditions`, `residence-filing`.

### NL-EMP-01 — Finding a job

**User questions:** How can a person search official vacancies, use UWV/werk.nl resources, contact a municipality or regional Werkcentrum, seek counselling, consider Dutch-language/retraining options, or request qualification evaluation?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-EMP-01–02 and NL-EDU-05 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Distinguish self-service vacancy tools from registration as a jobseeker, benefit-linked duties, eligibility for individual UWV/municipal programmes, work authorization and regulated-profession authorization. Public pages inspected do not establish that every TPD holder is eligible for the same counselling, retraining, course funding, placement or qualification-recognition outcome.

**Research questions:** Identify the current work.nl vacancy and registration routes; determine what non-benefit jobseekers can access through UWV and municipality/Werkcentrum; verify Dutch language-course and retraining access and costs; identify free or paid credential-evaluation options and professional licensing authority per regulated field.

**Comparison mapping:** `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, `employment-assistance-conditions`.

### NL-EMP-02 — Work authorization and employee rights

**User questions:** When does valid TPD proof permit paid employment without a TWV? What must the employer notify UWV about? What differs for self-employment, internships, third-country-national cohorts or changed status? Where can workers check contracts, pay, hours, leave and report suspected underpayment?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-EMP-01–04 and NL-TP-01 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** The IND describes the exemption for covered people in employment, requires proof and an employment contract, and distinguishes self-employment and volunteering. Employer notification is a separate duty. Cohort/status and valid proof matter; no blanket work-right statement should be based on Ukrainian nationality alone. The official minimum-wage page gives age-based hourly rates, including a rate effective 1 July 2026; any rate must carry its effective date and be rechecked before publication. CAO and contract terms may add detail.

**Research questions:** Review current statutory provisions and UWV forms for employer notice timing and changes; verify rules for self-employed work and internships; check current employment-contract disclosures, hours/rest, leave, termination, agency work and CAO scope; confirm the correct Labour Authority complaint channel and any language/access route.

**Comparison mapping:** `work-permission`, `work-employer-duties`, `work-minimum-pay`.

### NL-HEALTH-01 — Healthcare

**User questions:** How does the RMO pathway work? How do BRP/BSN, TPD status, employment or an ordinary residence permit affect the applicable healthcare arrangement? Where does a person seek routine or urgent care, and what may involve limits or patient costs?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-HEALTH-01–02 and NL-TP-01 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Keep RMO separate from compulsory Dutch health insurance, RMA and CAK reimbursement arrangements. Official information describes a defined RMO package and specific exclusions/conditions; some coverage and enrollment pathways depend on registration/BSN, while unregistered or differently-statused people may fall under another route. Never claim that all care is free or that every service is covered. Clinical urgency, referral, service type and scheme eligibility are distinct.

**Research questions:** Read the current RMO policy terms and operational provider guidance directly; establish patient-facing access route, registration requirements, covered care, exclusions, referral and cost rules; confirm provision for people without BSN and any status transition after TPD. Identify current primary-care access and emergency services without assuming a universal regional pathway.

**Comparison mapping:** `healthcare-access`, `healthcare-proof`, `healthcare-urgent`.

### NL-HOUSING-01 — Housing

**User questions:** How can a newly arrived person ask the municipality about reception? What is the difference between municipal reception, private hosting and renting independently? What practical checks apply to rental agreements, deposits, rent assistance and municipal contributions?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-HOUSING-01–02 and NL-SOC-01 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Official arrival guidance says reception capacity is constrained and people may be asked to find a place themselves; it does not guarantee a bed, municipality, region or facility. Municipal reception contribution depends on age, placement, income/benefit, household and current indexed rates. Ordinary rent rules vary by property/contract type. Housing benefit is a separate tax-benefit decision and the inspected Government.nl summary was last updated in 2022; do not use it to state current eligibility.

**Research questions:** Confirm the 2026 municipal reception / DFR rules and individual allocation process from current legal and national guidance; identify local contact routes without implying uniform capacity. Verify current rental law, contract and deposit rules and current Dienst Toeslagen rent-benefit conditions. Clarify whether protection status satisfies any benefit residence/document requirement.

**Comparison mapping:** `housing-initial`, `housing-private-rental`, `housing-financial-support`.

### NL-EDU-01 — Education and Dutch-language learning

**User questions:** How do parents approach school admission for minors? What compulsory-education rules apply? What language support can schools arrange? How can adults find language/integration courses? What are the higher-education admissions and credential-evaluation steps?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-EDU-01–05 and NL-RES-03 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** School access and compulsory attendance are related but distinct. School placement and language support depend on age, school capacity, local organization and learner needs; no specific school, immediate place or guaranteed support hours should be promised. Civic integration duties have their own status-based scope; temporary protection alone must not be assumed to create a mandatory integration duty. Higher-education admission is decided by institutions; credential evaluation is not admission, a professional license or permission to work. Tuition/fee exemptions require separate current checks.

**Research questions:** Verify current newcomer admissions and compulsory-school duty with primary education sources; identify municipal school-navigation support and age-specific newcomer classes; check free/low-cost Dutch language and integration options; review current university entry, language, tuition and exemptions for TPD holders.

**Comparison mapping:** `education-school-enrolment`, `education-pupil-language`, `education-higher-admission`.

### NL-SOC-01 — Financial and social assistance

**User questions:** Who administers leefgeld and how do household, accommodation, food provision, income or benefits affect it? What are the current application/reporting steps? How do child benefit and child-related budget differ from the Ukraine-specific subsistence allowance? What support can a municipality assess?

**Potential inspected sources:** NL-SOC-01–05, NL-HOUSING-02 and NL-TP-06 in the inventory.

**Conditions, exceptions and dependencies:** Leefgeld is part of the Ukraine-specific reception framework, not a universal refugee allowance. Household composition, accommodation type, meals, income and benefits affect payment. The 2026 municipal contribution has a dated 115% threshold and current maxima; rates are volatile and local decisions/exceptions matter. Child benefit and child-related budget have separate insurance, residence, income and asset conditions; generic eligibility pages do not prove an individual TPD holder qualifies. Do not treat housing costs, leefgeld, municipal assistance and national benefits as interchangeable.

**Research questions:** Verify current RooO and Temporary Act text, application/reporting channel, household calculation, partial allowance/partial contribution rules, child/minor treatment and any ordinary social-assistance route. Confirm current child benefit / child-related budget residence and legal-status conditions with SVB and Dienst Toeslagen. Research urgent municipal aid only with specific evidence; do not imply entitlement to MOP-equivalent support.

**Comparison mapping:** `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, `social-child-benefits`.

## Unresolved research questions

1. What is the current legal instrument and exact individual proof-of-residence validity after the EU extension to 4 March 2028? What official action applies to each document cohort?
2. What are the complete 17 September 2026 municipal triage instructions, and how do they interact with the older public IND page sequencing? What documentation and review route is used for the military-obligation scope restriction?
3. How are non-Ukrainian nationals with different Ukrainian residence statuses, prior EU protection, family links or pending appeals treated under current IND guidance and court decisions?
4. What was decided after the June 2026 policy letter? What does the September 2026 parliamentary package and 28 September advice say? Has a bill, enacted law, commencement order, implementing instruction or applicant-facing transition procedure appeared?
5. Which ordinary residence routes can be filed in-country by a person currently under TPD, and what are the exact sponsor, financial, employment, study, family and timing conditions? Does any TPD period count for a later status?
6. What is the current 2026–27 municipal reception process, availability, transfer/allocation policy and household contribution law under the new national funding framework?
7. What patient-facing RMO route applies with/without BRP and BSN, and what happens on employment, a transition document or another residence permit? What care is excluded or subject to referral/indication?
8. Which UWV, municipality and Werkcentrum services are available to a person not claiming unemployment benefit? Are language courses, vocational retraining or credential evaluations free, subsidized or individually assessed?
9. What current residence/document conditions apply to rent benefit, child benefit, child-related budget and any municipal income support for each TPD cohort?
10. Which school placement routes and compulsory attendance rules apply to newcomer children of different ages? What current language support and adult Dutch-learning options are actually accessible?
11. What are the current legal employment conditions for covered TPD employees, including notification timing, written particulars, working time/rest, leave, agency work, CAO and complaint channels?

## Proposed Phase 18.1B workflow

1. Resolve the high-risk TP/date, new-applicant/military, transition-document, healthcare and assistance questions against current official primary sources and legislation.
2. Build eight article-owned `SOURCES_AND_CLAIMS.md` matrices, recording exact passages, conditions, exceptions, current/in-force status, source language and real `LastCheckedAt` dates. Keep an inaccessible item explicitly inaccessible; do not promote a search snippet into evidence.
3. Map each article’s claims to the 24 contract aspect IDs, flag evidence gaps, and assess readiness without writing comparison answers.
4. Independently check source freshness and contradictory/legacy language; obtain editorial rulings for unresolved material conflict before drafting.
5. Only after research approval, draft EN/UK/RU articles and review cross-language equivalence; keep all publication statuses out of this research phase.

## Publication boundaries

- This research does not authorize any article draft, comparison fact, country catalog entry, status change or deployment.
- Do not infer entitlement from nationality, BRP/BSN registration, employment, family status or an EU-wide measure alone.
- Do not present a proposed transition as enacted or operational, or a permit route as automatic.
- Do not imply universal job placement, free courses, healthcare, housing, benefits, a specific municipality, rent assistance or recognition of qualifications.
- Report source conflicts and limits in the body of future research documentation; do not conceal them in generic disclaimers.
- The [source inventory](OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) records pages actually opened and the limits of what each can support. It is not a complete evidence matrix or legal audit.
