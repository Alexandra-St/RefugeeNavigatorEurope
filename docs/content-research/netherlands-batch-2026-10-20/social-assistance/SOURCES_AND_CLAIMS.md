# NL-SOC-01 — Sources and Claims

Research check: **20 October 2026 (UTC; exact check time not recorded)**. Research only. No benefit eligibility is inferred from nationality, TPD, BRP or household composition alone. See the [shared official source inventory](../OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md).

## Scope

Leefgeld under municipal reception, household/income conditions, application navigation, personal contribution, child benefit and child-related budget. Ordinary municipal social assistance is not treated as a universal benefit for TPD holders.
