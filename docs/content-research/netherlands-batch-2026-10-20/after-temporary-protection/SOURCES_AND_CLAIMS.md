# NL-TP-02 — Sources and Claims

Research check: **20 October 2026 (UTC; exact check time not recorded)**. Research only; no route is an individualized recommendation. See the [shared official source inventory](../OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md).

## Scope

Examples of ordinary residence routes after or alongside TPD, their separate eligibility and filing requirements, permanent-residence limitations, and the proposed transition document. “Beyond TPD” does not mean TPD must first expire.

## Research gaps / drafting gate

- The 25 September letter and “Advies verkenning verblijfsvergunning transitie uit de RTB” PDF were not readable; the separately requested 28 September advice was not located as a distinct document.
- No implementing legislation or commencement provision for a transition document was verified. Status classification: **UNRESOLVED**, not enacted-not-effective or operational.
- Confirm route-by-route whether a TPD holder can file from the Netherlands, timing/continuity requirements, permit expiry interactions, and whether TPD time counts toward residence-duration tests.
- Government.nl and current IND/Rijksoverheid transition wording remains conflicting; preserve that discrepancy in any later editorial review.
