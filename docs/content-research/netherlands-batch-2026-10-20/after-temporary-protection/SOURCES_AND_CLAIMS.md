## Research gaps / drafting gate

- The official 25 Sep 2026 parliamentary record and attached 22 Sep DG Migration decision memorandum were reviewed; the parent ministerial letter PDF and separate “Advies verkenning verblijfsvergunning transitie uit de RTB” attachment remain unreadable. The memo is a request for approval to start realization and legal consultation, not evidence that the request was approved. No distinct 28 September advice was identified.
- The latest public Rijksoverheid page and IND page describe future Cabinet intent/possibility. No enacted Vreemdelingenbesluit/TWOO amendment, commencement provision, effective procedure, or usable individual filing route was verified in the official materials reviewed through 20 Oct 2026. The public page’s “IND will contact” description is future policy, not an active application step.
- Policy proposal/planning is confirmed; a government decision establishing a legally enforceable three-year permit with final cohort and conditions is not independently verified; enacted legislation, effective procedure and operational application route are not established. A person cannot be advised from this evidence that they may apply now or will receive a document.
- Confirm route-by-route whether a TPD holder can file for other regular status from the Netherlands, timing/continuity requirements, permit-expiry interactions, and whether TPD time counts toward residence-duration tests.
- The English Government.nl page still presents the older 2027 version as settled and automatic, while current Dutch pages use future/intention wording and the September memo shows legal work still required. Preserve and attribute this webpage discrepancy in any later editorial review.
