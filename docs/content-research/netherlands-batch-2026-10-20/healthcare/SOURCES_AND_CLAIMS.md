## Research gaps / drafting gate

- Read the March 2026 RMO terms and patient-facing GP information. RMO applies to its defined registered/status cohort, contracted providers and service-specific rules; provider reimbursement is not an individual patient coverage decision.
- Material unresolved conflict: UWV says TPD-covered workers must take out Dutch insurance; current CAK and Dienst Toeslagen pages say working Ukrainians need not and may choose it. Applicable current legislation was not read. Do not advise a worker to switch insurance until this is resolved by competent legal authority.
- CAK provider-side SOV for eligible care without BSN is distinct from RMO; OVV is separately described for specified third-country cohorts. RMA rules were not reviewed and must not be inferred.
- Urgent-care navigation is bounded: 112 for life-threatening situations; otherwise local GP/HAP/central doctors service. Verify regional contact details and individual coverage before publication; do not imply all care is free.
