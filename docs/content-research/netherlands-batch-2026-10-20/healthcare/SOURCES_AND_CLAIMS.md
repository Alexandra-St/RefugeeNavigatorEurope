## Research gaps / drafting gate

- Read the March 2026 RMO terms and patient-facing GP information. RMO applies to its defined registered/status cohort, contracted providers and service-specific rules; provider reimbursement is not an individual patient coverage decision.
- Targeted review clarified the apparent conflict but did not safely resolve an individual's obligation: the Temporary Act art. 2(2) provides the ministerial medical-care scheme basis; 2024–25 parliamentary/ministerial explanations say employed displaced people are formally Zvw-insurance-liable, while policy is not to enforce individual policy-taking during RMO coverage; current TPD-specific Rijksoverheid guidance says workers may insure themselves but do not need to, while UWV's general page still says they must. The consolidated Zvw text could not be directly retrieved in this review; self-employment and exact work/status-change triggers remain unverified. Preserve CONFLICTING and do not advise a person to cancel, take out or change insurance.
- CAK provider-side SOV for eligible care without BSN is distinct from RMO; OVV is separately described for specified third-country cohorts. RMA rules were not reviewed and must not be inferred.
- Urgent-care navigation is bounded: 112 for life-threatening situations; otherwise local GP/HAP/central doctors service. Verify regional contact details and individual coverage before publication; do not imply all care is free.
