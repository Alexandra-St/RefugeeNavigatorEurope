## 2. TPD date conflict

The current consolidated English text of Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 was reviewed from the official EUR-Lex full-text indexed presentation. Article 1 extends the temporary protection introduced by Decision 2022/382, as previously extended, through **4 March 2028**. The preamble cites Directive 2001/55/EC, Articles 4(2) and 5(3)(a), as the legal basis. Article 3 says the Decision enters into force the day after Official Journal publication: publication was 4 August 2026, so entry into force was 5 August 2026. It generally applies from 5 March 2027, while Article 2 applies from 5 August 2026. Article 2’s corrigendum exception protects persons who had temporary protection in a given Member State before/on 4 August 2026 and continuously retained it in that same Member State afterwards.

The ordinary browser open of EUR-Lex HTML/PDF still returned robot verification. The full operative text, including the corrected provisions, was nevertheless present in the official EUR-Lex indexed full-text result; it was reviewed directly there, not replaced with a secondary summary or the corrigendum alone. This is an access-method limitation, not an unreviewed operative date.

IND’s current pages and Dutch-language Rijksoverheid guidance state the 2028 scheme horizon. IND’s proof/document pages separately describe stickers, O-documents and extension letters by cohort; some current proof evidence can still show a 2027 date while IND says protection itself is automatically extended through 2028. The statutory scheme horizon must not be substituted for a person-specific document rule.

Government.nl’s English “Long term policy: return and stay” page was reopened on 20 October 2026. It still states that the TPD runs through 4 March 2027 and presents a three-year transition document after that date as an automatic future process. This remains a real official-publication discrepancy, but the legal scheme date is settled by the current EU act. The English page is recorded as stale guidance, not as the governing law.

**Verdict change:** `NL-TP01-05` **CONFLICTING → CONDITIONAL**; `protection-dates` **UNRESOLVED → CONDITIONAL**. The scheme date is supported by the operative act. The verdict remains conditional because the stale English page and individual document cohorts must remain visible, and no person-specific validity can be inferred.

## 3. Municipal triage and military screening

The previously reviewed 1 October 2026 municipal handbook is not reread in full. Its existing finding remains: clear cases are handled BRP-first; where identity/nationality or eligibility is in doubt, municipal staff may refer the case for IND triage before BRP. The municipality handles identity/nationality checks; IND conducts referred eligibility triage. The handbook describes negative advice leading to refusal/non-registration, but it does not establish a complete appeal or review route.

The EU Decision’s Article 2 states, subject to Union law and fundamental rights, that TPD is granted only to people who satisfy applicable Ukrainian military obligations, with proof where applicable. The corrected Article 3 makes Article 2 applicable from 5 August 2026. The EU text does not set out a Dutch municipal evidence checklist or age-band table. Its corrected prior-holder exception is limited in wording to continuous protection in the same Member State.

Current public IND guidance says that liable persons/registered reservists aged 23–60 must show compliance or exemption, and gives examples: a recent Ukrainian exit stamp, official Reserv+ certificate, military-registration certificate, exemption/deferment decision, or demobilisation evidence. It says paper/electronic documents are accepted and screenshots/photos are not. IND’s announcement also says the new condition does not apply to persons who already had TPD in the EU before 5 August and separately excludes people joining family members who already hold TPD. Those public exceptions are not phrased exactly like the Decision’s same-Member-State exception; this review does not harmonize them or decide the legal reach.

The official IND PUC record identifies `IB 2026/34 RTB scopebeperking dienstplichtigen`, version 1, available/published 7 September 2026, valid through 7 March 2027, modified 8 October 2026, and listed as externally accessible. Its PDF/body could not be extracted. The record confirms the bulletin’s existence and metadata only. It does not close the exact military cohort, endpoints, evidence exceptions or legal interpretation. Do not invent an 18–22 rule or other age band.

**Verdict:** `NL-TP01-02`, `NL-TP01-03` and `NL-TP01-04` remain **CONDITIONAL**. The triage order is bounded; the complete screening scope, family-exception reconciliation, exact age edges, and negative-triage review route remain open.

## 4. Transition document

| Question | Evidence-based result as of 20 October 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Policy proposal | **Yes.** Official Dutch government pages describe a future transition document as policy intent; the 22 September DG Migration memo develops a proposed model. |
| Government decision | **Limited/uncertain.** The Cabinet publicly says it wants to offer a document, but the 22 September memo itself asks decision-makers to approve starting realization, legal amendments and consultation. No separate operative approval establishing final rights/eligibility was verified. |
| Enacted legislation | **Not verified.** No enacted Vreemdelingenbesluit/TWOO amendment establishing the document was identified in reviewed official material. |
| Effective procedure | **Not verified.** No commencement provision or current procedure was found. |
| Operational application route | **Not verified.** Current pages describe future IND contact; no route for an individual to apply now was identified. Do not advise a person that they can apply or will receive the document. |

The current Rijksoverheid page says the Cabinet wants to offer a document for up to three years to people who do not return and cannot obtain another residence permit; it describes future IND contact, public-order review and withdrawal of the pending asylum application. The IND page describes a possible future temporary permit. These are public descriptions of intended policy, not proof of an operative entitlement.

The 22 September DG Migration decision memorandum, attached to the 25 September parliamentary package, proposes a three-year document for people under TPD on 4 March 2028 who do not have another regular permit. It says legal amendments to the Vreemdelingenbesluit and Temporary Act for displaced persons from Ukraine must be prepared for consultation and asks for approval to start realization and implementation preparation. The cohort, duration, rights, conditions, scope restrictions and procedure are still subject to legal and operational work. The separate “Advies verkenning verblijfsvergunning transitie uit de RTB” PDF and the parent 25 September letter could not be substantively read.

Government.nl’s English page still describes a three-year document after 4 March 2027 with automatic IND contact and specific conditions. That page’s 2027 date is stale; its certainty about the future process also exceeds what the later memo proves.

**Verdict change:** `NL-TP02-03` **CONFLICTING → CONDITIONAL**. The existence of government planning is supported; final legal status, entitlement, scope, rights and application remain unverified. `residence-routes` remains CONDITIONAL.

## 8. Drafting boundaries

- A narrowly framed statement that the EU TPD scheme is extended through 4 March 2028 is supported by the current consolidated Decision. State that this is the scheme horizon, not a guarantee about an individual sticker, O-document or letter. Refer readers to current IND cohort-specific proof instructions.
- Attribute the 4 March 2027 statement to the stale English Government.nl page; do not present it as equal legal authority or hide that the page remains online.
- Describe municipal intake as clear-case BRP-first with municipality-requested IND triage before BRP in specified uncertain cases. Do not tell every applicant to approach IND triage directly.
- If mentioning military screening, keep the EU condition, IND’s published 23–60 evidence examples and the stated exceptions attributed to their respective sources. Do not invent age rules, a complete evidence list, or a universal military-service rule. State that individual decisions depend on the applicable criteria.
- Do not describe the transition document as current law, available to apply for, guaranteed, or automatic. At most, explain that the Cabinet is planning a future transition measure and that its final law, scope and process were not yet verified as of the research check date.
- Do not reproduce the old 2027 transition schedule as current. Do not tell readers to withdraw an asylum application or take any transition-specific step based only on a future-policy page.
- No individual eligibility conclusion is supported.
