## Remaining gaps and access limits

### Healthcare

- The insurance requirement conflict between UWV and CAK/Dienst Toeslagen is material and unresolved. The applicable consolidated legislation was not read. This blocks advice about whether employment requires ordinary Dutch insurance or a change from RMO.
- RMO terms and a patient-facing GP/HAP route were checked, but RMO eligibility still depends on the current status/registration cohort, provider contract and service-specific requirements. Provider subsidy is not proof that an unregistered patient has individual coverage, a guaranteed appointment or no costs.
- The CAK SOV/no-BSN material is provider-facing. RMO, SOV/CAK and OVV are separate routes. RMA rules were not reviewed and must not be inferred or merged with them.
- Do not say healthcare is universally free. Recheck the RMO terms, service limits, referral rules and local contacts at publication.

### Education

- Current official Ukraine-specific guidance supports the stated school-age summary and nearby-school route, but a case-specific statutory exemption/late-arrival review still needs current law. The generic government exemptions page remains dated 15 September 2022.
- The 2026/27 DUO remission route is specifically conditional MBO course-fee relief. It does not establish adult Dutch-language courses, universal funded retraining or university tuition relief.
- Availability, eligibility, language requirements and funding for adult Dutch-language courses remain unresolved. That gap must be researched before a full article covers adult course options.

### Housing and assistance

- Huurtoeslag is a conditional route for a person with temporary protection who meets general conditions; no individual qualification or amount was calculated. Recheck current statutory implementation, calculator inputs and the host-household rule before publication.
- Reception availability is local and time-sensitive. The April 2026 occupancy figure remains historical, not current capacity. No accommodation location or placement is guaranteed.
- Leefgeld and the municipal reception contribution are separate mechanisms. Current amounts and conditions must be rechecked; they do not establish ordinary bijstand or MOP eligibility.
- The current consolidated RooO/Temporary Act provisions were not directly reviewed. Do not add legal conclusions about bijstand, MOP, leefgeld or contributions beyond the checked official guidance.

### Other open items

- Werkcentrum and UWV pages do not establish the same services for every TPD holder. Adult language courses, funded retraining and job placement remain unpromised.
- Minimum wage, employer reporting/proof, benefit rates, housing conditions and DUO school-year rules are date-sensitive and must be rechecked immediately before publication.
- Existing Phase 18.1C limitations remain: the EUR-Lex direct page is robot-blocked although the official indexed full text was reviewed; the 25 September parent letter/advice attachment and IND bulletin IB 2026/34 body remain inaccessible or metadata-only. Their substance must not be inferred.

## Drafting boundaries

- Do not translate any bounded verdict into individual eligibility. Place cohorts, conditions, exceptions and effective dates beside the relevant claim.
- Employment: vacancy portals are search tools; UWV/Werkcentrum are navigation points with their own conditions. Do not promise a job, benefit, course, training funding or professional recognition.
- Healthcare: preserve the insurance conflict internally and omit employment-triggered insurance advice from any article until resolved. Explain RMO as defined/conditional; distinguish provider reimbursements from patient access. Use 112 only for life-threatening emergencies; otherwise direct to local GP/HAP/central doctors service without a universal phone number.
- Housing: describe municipal reception as dependent on route, decision and availability; separate private rent, huurtoeslag and reception contribution. Do not quote individual rent-benefit amounts or promise accommodation.
- Education: state only the bounded school rule and conditional MBO route; exclude current adult Dutch-course availability/eligibility until researched.
- Social assistance: confine the draft to supported leefgeld/contribution/child-benefit navigation. Exclude general bijstand/MOP rights or eligibility conclusions.
