## Material conflicts and limitations

### Temporary-protection dates

The current consolidated English text of Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 was reviewed from EUR-Lex’s official full-text indexed presentation. Article 1 extends the TPD scheme through 4 March 2028; Article 3 sets entry into force on 5 August 2026 and general application from 5 March 2027, while Article 2 applies from 5 August 2026. Its corrected prior-holder exception concerns continuous protection in the same Member State. Direct EUR-Lex HTML/PDF access remained robot-blocked. The English Government.nl long-term page still states 4 March 2027 and describes a future transition permit, so the official-page discrepancy remains visible even though the EU-law scheme date is established. Do not infer that an individual sticker, O-document or extension letter is valid through the scheme date.

### Municipal intake and military screening

The 1 October 2026 municipal handbook supports BRP-first handling for clear cases and municipality-requested IND triage before BRP in uncertain cases. Current public IND guidance describes a military-obligation condition, evidence examples for liable/registered reservists aged 23–60, and a family-joining exception. The corrected EU Decision exception instead refers to continuous protection in the same Member State; do not silently harmonize the difference. The PUC metadata confirms IB 2026/34 exists and is listed as current through 7 March 2027, but its text was not extractable. Exact military scope, age boundaries, full exceptions/evidence and the negative-triage review route remain conditional or unresolved. Do not invent an 18–22 rule.

### Transition document

The current IND and Dutch-language Rijksoverheid pages describe a possible/future transition measure. A directly read 22 September DG Migration decision memorandum proposes a three-year document for a defined future cohort but asks approval to begin realization, legal amendments and consultation. It is evidence of planning, not final government approval, enacted legislation, an effective procedure or an operational application route. The 25 September parent letter and separate advice attachment remain unreadable; the advice record supplies metadata only. Do not advise an individual that they can apply or will receive a document.

### Other boundaries

- TPD cohorts, Ukrainian nationals, third-country nationals and family members must remain distinct; do not collapse them into “all Ukrainians.”
- RMO provider reimbursement guidance is not proof that all care is free or that a particular person is covered. Urgent-care instructions need patient-facing regional sources.
- Municipal reception, private rent, housing benefit and the personal contribution are separate concepts. A 2022 housing-benefit page is not a current source for 2026 thresholds.
- The general school-attendance overview is dated 2022; the reviewed DUO form/deadline was for 2025–26 and expired 31 July 2026. No current 2026/27 process was confirmed.
- Leefgeld is not ordinary bijstand. MOP and child benefits have separate eligibility rules; no universal entitlement is established.
- The Werk.nl registration page was not opened and was removed from the finding-a-job evidence matrix; no search-result snippet is treated as evidence.

## Next research gates

Before article drafting or any production decision:

1. Recheck the consolidated Decision/corrigendum at publication time, confirm whether Government.nl has corrected its 2027 page, and verify IND proof-document cohorts individually.
2. Obtain the text of `IB 2026/34` or an authoritative public equivalent for military scope, age boundaries, exceptions and evidence; confirm the negative-triage review route.
3. Obtain the unreadable 25 September letter/advice attachment; verify whether approval followed, legislation was enacted and commenced, and an individual operational route exists before describing transition-document terms or process.
4. Recheck current consolidated reception/support legislation and time-sensitive 2026 amounts, including leefgeld and municipal contributions.
5. Verify current patient-facing RMO/urgent-care routes, 2026/27 education requirements and current DUO conditions, and current housing-benefit rules.
6. Confirm local UWV/municipality/Werkcentrum service access before presenting any training, counselling or referral as available to a particular reader.

Any later draft must preserve all cohort limits, conditions, exceptions, effective dates and source conflicts close to the claims they qualify. No content in this research set is approved for publication.
