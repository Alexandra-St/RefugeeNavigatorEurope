## Material conflicts and limitations

### Temporary-protection dates

IND and Dutch-language Rijksoverheid guidance state a 4 March 2028 TPD horizon. The English Government.nl long-term page still states 4 March 2027 and describes a future transition permit. The official EUR-Lex operative decision text could not be read directly in the available source view; the corrigendum was read, but that does not substitute for reading the decision itself. Record this as a source conflict. Do not infer that an individual sticker, O-document or extension letter is valid through either date.

### Municipal intake and military screening

The full 14-page municipal triage handbook dated 1 October 2026 was opened and read. It describes BRP-first handling for clear cases and a municipal referral to IND triage before BRP where identity/nationality or eligibility is in doubt. The municipality verifies identity/nationality; IND conducts the referred eligibility triage. The handbook’s military-screening language is limited and references internal IND instruction `IB 2026/34`, which was not opened. Do not infer a complete evidence checklist, resolve the “23/60” boundary, or invent a separate 18–22 rule from this material.

### Transition document

The 26 June 2026 ministerial letter describes ordinary routes and a transition document as work under exploration, with legal and implementation details not final. A 25 September parliamentary record and attachment names were accessible, but its letter and the “Advies verkenning verblijfsvergunning transitie uit de RTB” attachment could not be read; no separate 28 September advice was independently identified. No enacted transition instrument or operational application route was verified. Treat status, terms, dates, rights and process as unresolved.

### Other boundaries

- TPD cohorts, Ukrainian nationals, third-country nationals and family members must remain distinct; do not collapse them into “all Ukrainians.”
- RMO provider reimbursement guidance is not proof that all care is free or that a particular person is covered. Urgent-care instructions need patient-facing regional sources.
- Municipal reception, private rent, housing benefit and the personal contribution are separate concepts. A 2022 housing-benefit page is not a current source for 2026 thresholds.
- The general school-attendance overview is dated 2022; the reviewed DUO form/deadline was for 2025–26 and expired 31 July 2026. No current 2026/27 process was confirmed.
- Leefgeld is not ordinary bijstand. MOP and child benefits have separate eligibility rules; no universal entitlement is established.
- The Werk.nl registration page was not opened and was removed from the finding-a-job evidence matrix; no search-result snippet is treated as evidence.
