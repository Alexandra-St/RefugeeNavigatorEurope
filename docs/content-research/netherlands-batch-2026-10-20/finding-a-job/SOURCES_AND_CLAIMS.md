## Research gaps / drafting gate

- UWV’s published job-search route may be described with its stated identity/BSN and in-person registration conditions. Werkcentrum is a national point to ask about work and development; confirm local availability and TPD-specific access directly.
- Adult Dutch courses, funded retraining, counselling eligibility and placement are not established for every TPD holder; do not promise them.
- Credential evaluation is a non-binding diploma comparison, not admission, licensing or work permission; check fees and regulated-profession requirements separately.
