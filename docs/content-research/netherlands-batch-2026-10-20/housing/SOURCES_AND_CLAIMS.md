## Research gaps / drafting gate

- Reception remains contingent on TPD/intake route, municipal decision and real-time capacity; the April 2026 occupancy figure is historical, not current availability. Recheck local routes and capacity at publication.
- Current TPD housing-benefit page and general conditions support conditional signposting only. Do not quote individualized thresholds or promise amounts; use the official calculator and Mijn toeslagen.
- Date-sensitive future rule: Dienst Toeslagen says a hosted TPD refugee does not count as a co-resident through 31 Dec 2026, but counts from 1 Jan 2027. Recheck before publication.
- Keep municipal reception, private rental, personal contribution and huurtoeslag distinct. Recheck 2026 contribution amounts and conditions before any publication after a rule change.
