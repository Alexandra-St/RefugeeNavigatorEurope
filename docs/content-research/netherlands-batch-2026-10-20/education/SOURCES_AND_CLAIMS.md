## Research gaps / drafting gate

- Bounded: the current Ukraine-specific Rijksoverheid page states compulsory attendance through the school year of age 16 and qualification duty for 16–17-year-olds without a start qualification. The generic exemption page is dated 2022; verify current statute/exemptions before advising a particular family.
- School registration, newcomer classes and Dutch lessons in the described child programme are documented; placement and support hours depend on school/local capacity.
- DUO has a current 2026–27 MBO course-fee remission form/deadline and conditions. It is not a promise of free courses, adult Dutch classes or university fee relief.
- Current adult Dutch-language course availability, eligibility and funding remain unverified. Ask municipality/ROC for local information without describing a universal route; this gap requires targeted research before a full article draft.
- Nuffic/SBB evaluation is non-binding; school admission, language requirements and regulated-profession recognition are separate decisions.
- The civic-integration page was inaccessible in this review and is not evidence of a duty or entitlement.
