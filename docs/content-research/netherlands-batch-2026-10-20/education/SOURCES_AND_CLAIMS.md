## Research gaps / drafting gate

- Bounded: the current Ukraine-specific Rijksoverheid page states compulsory attendance through the school year of age 16 and qualification duty for 16–17-year-olds without a start qualification. The generic exemption page is dated 2022; verify current statute/exemptions before advising a particular family.
- School registration, newcomer classes and Dutch lessons in the described child programme are documented; placement and support hours depend on school/local capacity.
- DUO's 2026–27 route is conditional MBO course-fee remission only. It is separate from adult Dutch-language courses and does not establish a free course or university fee relief.
- Adult-language navigation is now conditionally supportable: adults 18+ in the described Ukraine TPD cohort with BRP registration may access adult education and existing municipal WEB-funded courses; municipalities may decide whether to offer Dutch courses. People in municipal reception may be offered optional educational activities including Dutch lessons. Availability, current price/funding, exact local registration steps and online options are not established; ask the municipality or local adult-education provider without promising a course/place.
- TPD holders in the described arrangement are not required to civic-integrate because the stay is temporary. Do not infer that voluntary courses are free or that all other residence categories share the same rule.
- Nuffic/SBB evaluation is non-binding; school admission, language requirements and regulated-profession recognition are separate decisions.
- The civic-integration page was inaccessible in this review and is not evidence of a duty or entitlement.
