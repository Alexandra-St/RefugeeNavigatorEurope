# Employment rights in Hungary under temporary protection

*Draft checked 16 November 2026. The guidance below reflects the law in force on that date; get advice for an individual dispute.*

## Permission to work

Act L of 2025 §20 allows a person recognized by Hungary as a temporary-protection beneficiary (`menedékes`) and a person who has formally applied for that recognition to work in Hungary without a separate work permit, including through a temporary-work agency. The provision does not list general restricted sectors or named employers. For an applicant, formal filing follows the required in-person application and identity steps; merely intending to apply or completing online data preparation is not enough. The rule does not establish self-employment rights. A profession regulated in Hungary may still require recognition or a licence. Work authorization is separate from the Batch 1 EU/hungarian implementation uncertainty for certain new TP decisions.

## Employer notifications and registration

Immigration reporting and ordinary employment reporting are separate duties. For a recognized `menedékes`, the employer reports the start to the competent government office no later than the first day of employment, and the end no later than the following day, under Government Decree 445/2013 §16. Separately, Act L §20(2) expressly applies the reporting provisions in subtitle 88 of Act XC/2023 to both beneficiaries and formal applicants. OIF’s applicant factsheet repeats the reporting requirement. The public materials do not give a bespoke deadline/channel checklist for the permit-free applicant category; employers should confirm the adapted procedure with the competent authority and must not assume that no report is due.

The employer must also report insured employment to NAV using the current 08E process within the required deadline (normally no later than the start of insured work). If a foreign employee has no TAJ number, NAV says the employer requests it through the competent authority. A TAJ number is a social-insurance identifier, not a work permit. Tax identifiers and payroll must also be handled; do not assume the physical cards themselves are a universal prerequisite to signing the contract.

## Contract and ordinary employee rights

A written employment contract should identify the parties, position, base wage and start date; the employer must provide written information on key working conditions within 15 days, subject to statutory exceptions. Probation has to be agreed and is normally capped at three months; a collective agreement may permit up to six. Ordinary full-time work is generally 8 hours a day / 40 a week. Breaks are at least 20 minutes after more than 6 hours and another 25 minutes after more than 9; daily rest is generally 11 consecutive hours and weekly rest generally 48 hours, subject to statutory exceptions and schedules. The basic annual paid leave is 20 working days plus age-based and other statutory supplements. Employer-paid sick leave is generally up to 15 working days per calendar year at 70% of absence pay; it is separate from social-insurance sickness benefit. Overtime, rest, leave and sector/collective rules have exceptions; no single work-time summary covers every schedule.

Wages are paid in forints at least monthly, normally no later than the 10th of the following month, with an itemized wage statement. Cash payment can be agreed in writing. Deductions are limited by law and cannot be arbitrary. Termination rules depend on who ends the contract and the circumstances; ordinary notice is generally at least 30 days, while immediate termination is exceptional. Final settlement and employment documents are generally due within five working days after employment ends. Required working-time and payroll records must be kept; the separate immigration-employment notification documents for a recognized beneficiary are retained for three years after the employment ends.

## Minimum wages on 16 November 2026

For full-time work, the gross base rates effective since 1 January 2026 are:

| Rate | Monthly | Weekly | Daily | Hourly |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Minimum wage | HUF 322,800 | HUF 74,210 | HUF 14,850 | HUF 1,856 |
| Guaranteed wage minimum | HUF 373,200 | HUF 85,800 | HUF 17,160 | HUF 2,145 |

The guaranteed minimum applies when the job itself requires at least secondary education or a secondary vocational qualification. It does not apply merely because the worker holds a qualification. Part-time monthly, weekly and daily rates are prorated; for a full-time day longer or shorter than eight hours, the hourly rate is adjusted under the decree. The amounts are gross, not take-home pay; a collective or sectoral minimum may be higher.

The government reported on 11 November that 2027 wage talks were continuing. No 2027 rate was enacted in the sources reviewed by 16 November. Do not use conditional projections as an adopted rate.

## Regulated work and complaints

Permit-free employment does not waive professional recognition or licensing for regulated work. Ask the competent Hungarian professional authority about foreign-qualification recognition and the required licence. For employment-law concerns, contact the employment-supervision authority of the capital or county government office, generally connected to the place of work. Occupational-safety matters follow the relevant safety authority. The reviewed sources do not promise anonymous handling, interpretation or a multilingual complaint form.
