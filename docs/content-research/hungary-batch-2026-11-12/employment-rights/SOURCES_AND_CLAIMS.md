# Hungary: employment rights and work authorization

**Research checked:** 12 November 2026  
**Canonical comparison aspects:** `work-permission`, `work-employer-duties`, `work-minimum-pay`  
**Scope:** Official-source research for a future article. No content draft or catalog integration is included.

## Executive finding

**Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Current Hungarian law expressly gives both a recognized `menedékes` and a person formally applying for recognition as one (`menedékesként elismerését kérő`) the right to work without a work permit, including through a temporary-work agency. For an applicant, the legal application is submitted only after the statutory in-person identity check and recording; merely intending to apply or preparing/submitting an online data form is not enough. The law states no general sector or employer limitation for these two categories. A recognized beneficiary has a clear employer notification under Government Decree 445/2013; the precise interaction of the 2025 Act's cross-reference to immigration reporting rules with a pending applicant's permit-free status remains insufficiently clear in the official material reviewed and should be qualified in practical hiring guidance.

The 2026 statutory wage amounts are clear. General employment duties are well supported by the Labour Code and NAV instructions. Documentation evidence for an employer should be described by category and verified with the authority; sources do not establish one exhaustive universal document checklist or a general requirement to retain a TP card copy.

## 2–3. Work authorization: recognized beneficiary and pending applicant

Act L of 2025 §20(1) says both a person recognized as `menedékes` and a person applying for such recognition may work in Hungary without a permit, including via a temporary-work agency. Section 20(2) separately provides that employment notification is governed by the relevant part of Act XC of 2023, applied correspondingly. Section 16 sets the formal application steps. [HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S02]

For a pending applicant, the Act treats the application as submitted after the person appears in person, identity data are checked and recorded, and the required biometric steps are completed where applicable. Online data preparation or an expression of intention alone does not meet that statutory filing point. The applicant therefore may rely on the permit-free work provision from formal submission of the application, not merely from starting the process. The sources do not establish an additional interim humanitarian document as a condition of the statutory work right; such a document may evidence identity, lawful stay and pending status. [HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S05]

| Status | May work? | Separate work permit? | From when? | Evidence/document | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recognized Hungarian `menedékes` | Yes | No | While within the statutory Hungarian status category | Current TP status document is the ordinary evidence of recognition/identity; no exhaustive employer checklist found | HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S05 |
| `menedékesként elismerését kérő` | Yes | No | After formal submission: personal appearance and statutory identity check/recording, plus biometric steps where applicable | OIF-issued applicant/humanitarian document may evidence identity, lawful stay and pending status; law creates the right | HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S05 |

No general sector, occupation, named-employer, labour-market-test or prior employment-authority approval restriction appears in §20. This is a conclusion about the TP statutory exemption, not regulated-profession qualification rules or ordinary residence permits. A foreign TP status from another Member State alone is not expressly included in §20; the safe boundary is to establish Hungarian recognition or a Hungarian application that has reached formal submission before relying on this exemption in Hungary. No wider transfer analysis is made. [HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S02]

Ordinary residence titles such as the National Card or Hungarian Card have their own employment authorization rules. They are outside this TP-specific conclusion; a worker must follow the conditions attached to the relevant ordinary title. [HU-WORK-S02]

### Employer document check

The recognized TP status document and OIF's pending-applicant humanitarian document/data sheet are official evidence categories. Identity should be checked from valid identity evidence available to the person. The legal right under §20 arises from the statutory status/application category, rather than from a separate work permit card. Sources reviewed do not establish a public status-register query for employers, a universal document list, or an explicit general duty to retain/copy the TP document. The employer must still meet applicable identity, contract and tax/social-insurance reporting requirements; do not advise an employer to accept an unverified claim of status. [HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S05, HU-WORK-S06, HU-WORK-S07]

## 4–6. Employer notification and distinct statuses

Government Decree 445/2013 §16 requires the employer to notify employment of a person recognized as a refugee, subsidiary-protection holder or `menedékes` to the competent capital/county government office: commencement no later than the day work starts, and cessation no later than the following day. This is a labour-authority notification and does not reinstate a work permit. [HU-WORK-S03]

For pending applicants, Act L §20(2) cross-refers correspondingly to Act XC of 2023 rules. The reviewed provisions do not yield a clear, directly stated OIF channel and deadline specifically for a permit-free pending TP applicant; Decree 445/2013 §16's explicit TP category is recognized `menedékes`. This interaction is a qualification: employers should confirm the current procedure with the competent authority before treating the recognized-beneficiary notification as exhaustive for applicants. Do not infer that permit-free means no report. [HU-WORK-S01, HU-WORK-S02, HU-WORK-S03]

## 7–10. NAV, tax ID and social insurance

From 1 January 2026 NAV uses form `08E` for employer notification of insured persons (replacing the familiar T1041 label for this function). The employer must report the employee's insured relationship no later than the start of insured work, under the applicable social-security reporting deadline; this applies to employees generally, not only Hungarian nationals. NAV's instructions require a tax ID and TAJ number in the insured-person data. For a foreign national without a TAJ, NAV says the employer requests one from the competent district/administrative office. Do not tell a foreign worker that possession of a plastic TAJ card is itself a universal immigration precondition to work; the official material instead describes identifier issuance and reporting steps. Exact start timing where identifiers are still being processed should be coordinated by employer/payroll with NAV and the issuing authority. [HU-WORK-S06, HU-WORK-S07, HU-WORK-S08]

An employee needs a Hungarian tax identifier for payroll/tax administration. NAV's foreign-person information explains the tax-number application route; the employer should arrange required payroll identifiers and tax withholding. Reviewed sources do not establish a blanket rule that the employment contract cannot be signed until a physical tax card arrives, nor a universal right to start without the required insured-person data being reportable. [HU-WORK-S06, HU-WORK-S07]

Employment under Hungarian law generally creates insured status when statutory conditions are met; employer reporting is not optional. The exact benefit scope and healthcare entitlement are outside scope. NAV's 08E guide is operational and reflects required identifiers; it is not evidence of a special TP exception. [HU-WORK-S06, HU-WORK-S08]

## 11. Employment contract and core terms

The Labour Code requires a written employment contract. It must state at least the parties' agreement on the job position and base wage; the employment start date is the contract date unless otherwise agreed. If duration is not specified, the relationship is indefinite. Workplace may be specified in the contract; statutory defaults apply if omitted. The employer must give written information about key working conditions within 15 days after commencement, subject to the Code's listed exceptions. Reviewed law does not establish a universal requirement that the contract be translated into a language the employee understands. A practical article may advise the worker to ask for an explanation/copy before signing but should not present that as a statutory translation rule. [HU-WORK-S04]

### Trial period and fixed term

A probationary period (`próbaidő`) must be agreed in the written contract. The general maximum is three months from commencement; where a shorter period was agreed, it may be extended once, but the total may not exceed three months. A collective agreement may allow a maximum of six months. Fixed-term duration is a separate contract feature and must not be described as probation. [HU-WORK-S04]

## 20–28. Minimum pay and practical wage boundary

Government Decree 426/2025 sets the rates effective 1 January 2026 for full-time employment: [HU-WORK-S11]

| Statutory rate | Monthly | Weekly | Daily | Hourly |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Minimum wage (`minimálbér`) | HUF 322,800 | HUF 74,210 | HUF 14,850 | HUF 1,856 |
| Guaranteed wage minimum (`garantált bérminimum`) | HUF 373,200 | HUF 85,800 | HUF 17,160 | HUF 2,145 |

The guaranteed wage minimum applies where the **job position requires at least secondary-school education or secondary vocational qualification**; it is not triggered solely because the worker personally holds such a qualification. Amounts are gross base-wage minima, not net take-home pay. For part-time work, monthly/weekly/daily rates are reduced proportionally to working hours; hourly rates follow the decree's stated rule. Higher collective-agreement or sectoral terms may apply; the decree is the floor, not proof that every workplace's applicable rate ends there. The existence and amount of any particular sectoral floor must be checked for that job and agreement. [HU-WORK-S04, HU-WORK-S11]

No reliable official evidence was established in this batch for a general recruitment/placement fee rule or a special blanket accommodation-deduction rule. These should be omitted or separately researched rather than inferred. The Labour Code's limits on wage deductions remain applicable. [HU-WORK-S04]

## 29. Complaints, inspection and undeclared work

Employment-supervision matters fall to the competent government office's employment-supervision authority, generally determined by the place of work. Official guidance describes complaints/public-interest reports and investigation procedure; it does not support a promise that a personal wage complaint can always be anonymous. Occupational-safety concerns follow the competent occupational-safety authority route. Labour-court remedies are available through the courts, but procedural detail is outside scope. Check the current county/capital government-office contact before filing; the reviewed official complaint notice gives the authority route but is not a centralized multilingual form. [HU-WORK-S12, HU-WORK-S13]

An employer must formally register insured employment with NAV and meet the applicable employment notification obligations. Permit-free status does not legalize undeclared cash work or remove contract, tax, wage or safety duties. NAV publishes information on employers found employing people without declaration and offers official routes for checking declared employment status. [HU-WORK-S06, HU-WORK-S14]

No reviewed official source establishes a guaranteed English interpreter, multilingual complaint form or universal right to a translated Labour Code contract. Do not promise language assistance; direct the person to the competent authority and verify what support it currently offers. [HU-WORK-S12, HU-WORK-S13]

## 34. Drafting boundary

**“Working legally and understanding employment rights in Hungary” — Draftable with constrained scope.**

### Safe to state

- Hungarian recognized TP beneficiaries and formally registered TP applicants may work without a separate work permit under Act L of 2025.
- For applicants, work-right start follows formal application submission after statutory in-person identity steps, not mere intent.
- No general sector list or employer-specific permit condition is stated in that TP provision.
- Employers must register insured employment with NAV; recognized TP beneficiary hire/termination has a separate employment-authority notification rule.
- The exact 2026 gross minimum and guaranteed wage minimum and their distinct trigger.

### Must qualify

- Applicant-specific immigration/labour notification: Act L cross-reference exists, but reviewed official material does not clearly establish the separate channel/deadline for this permit-free applicant category.
- Employer evidence: identify official status/identity documents by category; do not claim exhaustive required checklist or a special employer status-register check.
- Foreign tax/social-security identifiers: coordinate onboarding with NAV and competent issuing office; do not promise the employee can start with missing reportable data.
- Labour Code working-time, leave, termination and complaint statements have exceptions and require case-specific application.
- Prior EU TP status: do not treat another state's status alone as the Hungarian §20 category.

### Do not assert

- That permit-free means no employer report, no contract or no tax/social-security formalities.
- That a pending person can start work before formal submission of the Hungarian TP application.
- That an employer can rely on a verbal claim alone or that one specific document is always the only proof.
- That the 2026 statutory wage is net pay, or that every qualified worker is automatically owed the guaranteed minimum.
- That complaint channels are anonymous, multilingual or guaranteed to provide an interpreter.

## 35. Scope limits

This research does not cover jobseeker registration in depth, unemployment allowance, healthcare, housing, social assistance, post-TP immigration routes, detailed tax calculation, pensions, parental benefits, every Labour Code exception, or sector-specific collective agreements. It creates no article draft, catalog integration, country addition, comparison feature, application/runtime change, localization, or deployment.
