# Hungary: finding a job

**Research checked:** 12 November 2026  
**Canonical comparison aspects:** `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, `employment-assistance-conditions`  
**Scope:** Official-source research for a future practical article. This is not an article and does not integrate content into the catalog.

## Executive finding

**Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Official vacancy search and the public employment-service structure are well documented. Current binding rules and NFSZ guidance establish that a recognized `menedékes` aged 16 or older can register with the district employment office as a `szolgáltatást kérő` (employment-service requester), with the competent office determined by the person's accommodation or designated residence. EURES says anyone, not only a registered jobseeker, can request public employment-office assistance. However, the reviewed sources do not establish that a TP beneficiary is automatically or generally registrable as an `álláskereső` (registered jobseeker) under the ordinary Employment Act process. The TP-specific service-seeker route has no published complete registration-document checklist or full online procedure. Those boundaries must be explicit.

## 4. Jobseeker registration and TP status

### Recognized TP beneficiary

Current consolidated Government Decree 106/2022 expressly defines an “employment-involvement target person” as someone aged 16 or older who is either a recognized `menedékes` or a specified Ukrainian-Hungarian displaced citizen. Such a target person **may be registered as a `szolgáltatást kérő` at the district employment authority**. The office is competent by accommodation or residence designated for the purpose of stay. Registration and cooperation are mandatory before the first regular subsistence support payment under the special scheme; do not generalize that payment condition to every TP holder's access to job-search services. [S03, S12]

This is not the same as registration as an ordinary `álláskereső`. The current Employment Act's general definition requires work-establishment conditions, no full-time study, no disqualifying pension/benefit, no employment or other gainful activity (subject to statutory exceptions), cooperation and actual registration as jobseeker. NFSZ's ordinary registration checklist assumes a Hungarian identity/address/TAJ/tax-document set. The reviewed official sources do not explicitly say that a `menedékes` is generally registrable in this ordinary category, or provide TP-specific handling of those ordinary documentation conditions. **Result: TP-specific service-seeker registration is established; ordinary registered-jobseeker eligibility for a TP beneficiary is not established in this research.** [S01, S02, S03, S05]

### Pending TP applicant

The TP-specific target-person definition in Decree 106/2022 refers to `menedékes` (recognized beneficiary), not `menedékesként elismerését kérő` (pending applicant). Therefore the reviewed sources do **not** establish eligibility for the specific service-seeker registration under that provision while TP recognition is pending. The 2025 Act says TP applicants may work without a permit, but that does not itself create a jobseeker registration category. EURES says any person may request local government-office employment assistance, so a pending applicant can be described as able to ask for general public assistance; this is not a registration guarantee. Public VMP/EURES browsing is open. [S03, S04, S08, S09]

### Minimal work-right boundary

Act L of 2025 §20 states a recognized TP beneficiary or person applying for TP recognition may work in Hungary without a permit. This is included only to demonstrate the legal distinction between jobseeker/service registration and work permission. Employer reporting and job-specific rules are outside this topic. [S04]

## 7. Training and language support

NFSZ's training incentive page gives a current 2026 calculation for the `álláskeresést ösztönző juttatás` (ÁÖJ): it can include subsistence, training, travel and childcare elements; the minimum/maximum monthly totals for 2026-start courses are stated on the page, and language/basic-skills/bridging training has a cost-element cap. These figures describe programme calculation, **not a universal individual entitlement**, and the page addresses a training-support request by a registered jobseeker under applicable programme rules. A recognized TP beneficiary's eligibility for this ordinary route is not established simply by being a `szolgáltatást kérő`. [S10]

The official NFSZ materials reviewed did not establish a current, general free Hungarian course for all TP beneficiaries. A separate employer-training programme allowed language training but the NFSZ page reports applications have been closed since 1 June 2026 until further notice; this is not an open jobseeker entitlement. Course and programme access can vary by current call and county. [S11, S15]

## 9. EURES

EURES Hungary is part of NFSZ. Trained mobility advisers operate through county/capital government offices; district employment departments also have EURES assistants. Published services include EU/EEA vacancy information, application advice, cross-border living/working information and tailored counselling. Advisers are available in person, by phone or online according to the EURES contact page. The public EURES vacancy database can be searched without treating this as an NFSZ registration step. [S09, S10]

An EURES account/profile is distinct from Hungarian `álláskereső` registration and from TP-specific `szolgáltatást kérő` registration. EURES advert language and hiring conditions remain specific to each vacancy; EURES access does not determine work authorization. [S04, S09, S10]

## 10. Unemployment-benefit boundary

`Álláskeresési járadék` is the relevant ordinary unemployment/jobseeker allowance. NFSZ says ordinary registered-jobseeker registration is a prerequisite to an allowance claim, and Employment Act §25 separately requires at least 360 days of qualifying time in the preceding three years, plus other statutory conditions. Registration does not create entitlement; TP service-seeker registration is not in itself an allowance award. Detailed calculations and foreign work-history aggregation are outside scope. [S01, S13, S14]

## 12. Foreign qualifications and regional differences

The employment-office materials support job matching and career assistance based on a person's profile, but do not establish a universal foreign-qualification recognition shortcut. Whether a qualification must be recognized depends on the profession and the role; recognition for regulated professions is outside this research. Do not imply that NFSZ registration validates a foreign diploma. [S02, S05]

Services sit in a national NFSZ framework and are delivered through local district offices and county/capital structures. Official pages show region-specific current training programmes and regional vacancy inventories. A reader should ask the competent office which current training/support calls are available locally; do not claim every county offers the same course or budget. [S02, S06, S08, S10, S11]

## 15. Readiness, blockers and drafting boundary

### Blocking gaps

No blocking evidence gap prevents a carefully scoped article about public job-search channels, public-office help, and the explicit TP service-seeker pathway. Two material qualifications remain:

- **Ordinary `álláskereső` status for recognized TP beneficiaries — not established.** The sources needed to resolve this are an explicit current NFSZ or county-office instruction, or an authoritative administrative interpretation applying Employment Act §58(5)(d) and its documentation requirements to `menedékes` holders.
- **TP service-seeker procedure and document list — not established.** A current NFSZ/competent-office checklist or step-by-step procedure is needed to confirm documents, appointment/walk-in expectations, online access and address evidence. The standard jobseeker list is not a substitute.
- **Pending applicant under TP service-seeker route — not established.** Official guidance would need to state whether the pending category is included; the current special decree names recognized `menedékes`. General assistance and vacancy browsing can still be described.

The gaps matter only if a draft tells someone how to complete formal TP registration, promises the ordinary registered-jobseeker category, or promises a programme payment. Safe general content remains available.

### Drafting boundary

**“Finding a job in Hungary” can be drafted with constrained scope.** Explain VMP and EURES browsing, the public NFSZ/government-office network, free general public assistance, and the recognized TP beneficiary's statutory `szolgáltatást kérő` option. Direct the person to the local office to verify the TP-specific checklist and procedure. Keep allowance and work permission separate.

#### Safe to state

- VMP and EURES are official public vacancy channels; browsing is possible without becoming an NFSZ registered jobseeker.
- NFSZ services operate through local district employment departments and county/capital government-office structures; Budapest uses district offices.
- Anyone can ask local government-office employment services for help; recognized 16+ TP beneficiaries are expressly included in the TP-specific service-seeker category under current consolidated Decree 106/2022.
- The TP-specific responsible office is tied to accommodation/designated residence; confirm required documents and appointment details locally.
- Job matching, vacancy information, CV/interview help and EURES advice are available through public services, subject to service/case and local availability.
- Ordinary `álláskeresési járadék` has separate qualifying conditions; ordinary registration does not automatically pay it.
- A current employer-side accommodation support programme for eligible TP employees runs through 4 March 2028 under the current NFSZ page; it is applied for by the employer and is not a direct jobseeker cash allowance.

#### Must qualify

- A TP beneficiary's service-seeker registration is not ordinary `álláskereső` registration; the latter's TP-specific eligibility is not established by this review.
- The pending applicant has public help/browsing access but is not established as eligible for the TP-specific service-seeker registration.
- Training, language-course costs, subsidies and referrals are conditional; confirm current local/programme rules.
- A vacancy does not establish work authorization, employer eligibility, qualification recognition or availability.
- The 45-day suitable-job rule is attached only to the special subsistence-support scheme and has exceptions.

#### Do not assert

- That all TP beneficiaries can register as ordinary jobseekers or receive unemployment allowance.
- That every TP service-seeker must present a particular TP card, TAJ, tax ID, bank account or qualification record; that the TP process is fully online; or that walk-in service is guaranteed.
- That pending applicants qualify for the TP-specific service-seeker registration solely because they may work.
- That a particular job is open to TP beneficiaries just because it appears on VMP/EURES.
- That free Hungarian lessons or training subsidies are universally available.
- That old 2022–2024 Ukraine employment programmes remain open.
