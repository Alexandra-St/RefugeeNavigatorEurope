# Finding a job in Hungary

*Draft checked 16 November 2026.*

## Search for vacancies

Search the National Employment Service (NFSZ) Virtual Labour Market Portal ([VMP](https://vmp.munka.hu/allaskeresoknek)) and [EURES Hungary](https://eures.munka.hu/Lapok/eures_allasok.aspx). Public listings can be browsed without registering as an ordinary jobseeker or creating an EURES profile. Follow each advert’s application instructions. A listing, referral or service registration does not establish that you meet the job’s immigration, language, qualification or employer requirements.

## Contact public employment services

Local registration and services are handled by the district government-office employment department (in Budapest, the relevant district office). County and capital government offices provide coordination and EURES advisers. The municipality is not the employment registration office. EURES says anyone may ask a local public employment service for general help; formal registration is a separate step.

If you are a recognized temporary-protection beneficiary (`menedékes`) and are at least 16, you may ask the competent district office to register you as a `szolgáltatást kérő` (employment-service requester). The competent office is linked to your accommodation or designated residence. This category differs from ordinary `álláskereső` registration and does not itself establish eligibility for unemployment allowance. The special TP rule does not expressly include an applicant whose TP case is still pending; that person can still browse vacancies and ask for general assistance. The TP-specific document checklist, appointment system and full online registration process are not published in the reviewed guidance, so check with the local office before attending.

## Training and support

An office may offer job-search help, matching, career advice or information about training. Training, language courses, funded support and referrals depend on your circumstances, programme rules, budget and local availability; they are not guaranteed benefits of temporary protection. The identified employer training-subsidy programme was not accepting new applications from 1 June 2026 until further notice. A separate housing-support programme is applied for by eligible employers hiring certain recognized beneficiaries; it is not a direct payment to a jobseeker.

## Qualifications and permission to work

A foreign qualification may need recognition, particularly for regulated work such as healthcare or teaching. Ask the relevant Hungarian professional authority about recognition, licensing and language requirements. Employment-service registration and a job offer are not work permission.

Under Act L of 2025 §20, both a recognized Hungarian `menedékes` and a person who has formally filed a Hungarian TP application may work without a separate work permit. For applicants, mere intent or online preparation is not formal filing. This rule does not replace professional licensing and does not itself establish a right to self-employment. The military-obligation qualification for new TP decisions is described in the temporary-protection article; do not assume an uncertain TP grant.
