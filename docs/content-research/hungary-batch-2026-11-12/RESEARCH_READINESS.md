## 11. Social-assistance consistency

- The special regular subsistence allowance is established for recognized `menedékes`; the pending-applicant route is not established.

- TP alone is not a general status basis for family allowance, maternity support, GYES or GYET. School meals and housing programmes are separate from household cash support.
- Payments to an employer, housing provider or municipality are not direct social cash paid to a household.
