## Executive finding

**12 November snapshot: PARTIALLY READY — BLOCKING EVIDENCE GAPS.** Ordinary private renting and the current limits of housing support are sufficiently documented for carefully qualified guidance. The law confirms limited, provider-side temporary accommodation support, but the official public guidance does not tell a newly arrived person who needs a bed tonight where to present, how to request a placement, whether self-referral is accepted, or what to do when there is no capacity. For recognized beneficiaries, the 2025 rules expressly remove entitlement to accommodation and care at a reception centre. Budapest has a general public night-shelter network, but it is not a TP placement route and current live availability is not published.

## 4. Current facilities, placement, duration and exit

OIF's official reception-centre/community-shelter information describes facility types and services under the general rules, but it is not a current walk-in intake instruction for TP cases. The current special decree makes two distinctions: an applicant or beneficiary placed outside a reception centre may be treated as being at a designated other accommodation for specified asylum-rule purposes; a recognized beneficiary is not entitled to reception-centre accommodation/care. No official evidence found allows choosing a city, presenting without referral, guaranteeing meals or fees at a TP placement, returning after leaving, or determining what happens when capacity is full. Do not publish facility addresses as TP intake points without current explicit confirmation.

Specific accommodation funding under Decree 196/2025 §11 is ordinarily limited to one use up to one month after entry for eligible people arriving from, and resident in, a war-affected area. If the person applies for TP during that period, §§12/13 support can continue through the last day of the month after recognition. Continued support beyond that window is available for a pregnant person, a child under 18, one co-resident legal guardian, a person with disability and caregiver, and a person over 65, if support began no later than 90 days after recognition or the later onset of the qualifying condition and Ukraine residence remains in a war-affected area. These are conditions on funding after accommodation is arranged, not a promise of placement. No general income/employment-based end date, notice period, duty to locate replacement housing, or right to return was established for the local/provider accommodation stream. The employer allowance ends if employment or the accommodation contract ends, among listed events.

## 5. Vulnerable persons and families

Current Decree §11(3) expressly names pregnancy, persons under 18, one co-resident legal guardian, disability plus caregiver, and age over 65 for continued local/provider accommodation funding under the conditions above. This does not establish a priority ranking, guaranteed bed, family-unit placement, or automatic extra duration for all families. The focused current housing sources did not establish a separate TP housing entitlement for unaccompanied minors or victims of violence/trafficking. The article should avoid inventing priority rules.

## 6. Emergency homelessness after initial period

The Social Administration Act defines a night shelter as providing night rest and crisis-night accommodation to homeless people able to care for themselves and observe communal rules; temporary homeless accommodation is a distinct service. Section 7 provides emergency social assistance where lack of assistance endangers life or physical integrity. Municipalities organize local services; service availability and admission details vary. A general homelessness route is not the same as the TP-specific accommodation arrangements.

### If you have nowhere to sleep tonight

- Contact the municipality's social-service office or a local night shelter and ask for its current intake point.
- In Budapest, contact or attend a BMSZKI night shelter matching the facility's published admission group; the official BMSZKI page lists addresses and phone numbers.
- Ask the local authority/social service for urgent help if being without shelter puts life or physical safety at risk.
- Do not assume a bed is available, that a TP-specific housing placement is automatic, or that an old transit-centre address remains open.

## 7. Private renting and eligibility

The Housing Act (Act LXXVIII of 1993) applies to residential leases. A lease may be fixed-term, indefinite, or tied to a condition; the parties agree rent. A lease for a privately owned dwelling must be in writing for validity. The statute does not prescribe a TP-specific rental application checklist. No official source establishes that TP status alone compels a landlord to accept an applicant, or that a Hungarian address card, Hungarian tax number, TAJ card, local bank account, employment contract, or guarantor is a universal statutory prerequisite to a private lease. A landlord may set lawful contractual/business conditions; distinguish those preferences from legal requirements. This research did not establish a national official checklist for identity documents a private landlord must accept.

## 8. Address reporting and tenancy

OIF says a pending applicant must report a change of Hungarian accommodation address to the asylum authority and is not issued a `lakcímkártya`; the OIF-issued stay document evidences the accommodation address. The current OIF instruction located gives no specific deadline in its page text. Do not transfer the 15-day rule applicable in unrelated status/benefit contexts. A private lease is a civil-law contract; it does not itself issue an address card or replace OIF reporting. No official housing source established landlord consent as a general prerequisite to OIF address reporting, whether a lease alone is sufficient proof in every case, or that lacking an address card prevents a TP holder from renting.

## 10. Tenant protections and discrimination boundary

The Housing Act and Civil Code govern landlord/tenant duties, maintenance, use, termination, and return of the dwelling. The landlord cannot be described as having unrestricted entry or self-help eviction rights; however, this focused research did not establish a complete universal residential eviction procedure or all notice periods. Use only the contract and applicable statutory provisions for case-specific disputes; do not promise that a tenant cannot be removed after termination.

The Equal Treatment Act (Act CXXV of 2003) applies to persons and groups in Hungary and lists protected characteristics, including nationality/ethnic origin; the review did not establish a simple blanket rule specifically resolving every private landlord's refusal to rent. Public housing allocation is plainly subject to public-law/local eligibility rules. State the general equal-treatment protection with qualification; do not promise an individual private rental offer or imply that protected status removes ordinary lease conditions.

## 11. Municipal/social housing and general aid

Municipal housing is governed nationally by the Housing Act but allocation conditions, social eligibility, rent and application procedure are set through local municipal ordinances. No national TP-specific right to municipal/social housing, uniform waiting-list route, or guaranteed allocation was located. A TP beneficiary may ask the municipality about its current application rules, but eligibility must be checked locally; Budapest housing stock and application rules can be district/municipality specific.

The Social Administration Act §45 authorizes municipalities to create `települési támogatás` for regular housing expenses and housing-cost arrears and provides for extraordinary municipal support to people in hardship. This is not a national TP rent allowance, not proof that every TP beneficiary qualifies, and not an entitlement to deposit/first-rent assistance. Local decree, residence/competence, income and other conditions control. No current nationwide TP-specific deposit assistance or direct rent/utility payment to the beneficiary was established.

Keep separate: social-assistance payment ≠ housing entitlement ≠ rent reimbursement ≠ public-housing allocation.

## 13. No fixed address, leaving accommodation, and moving

OIF's current applicant guidance requires reporting a changed Hungarian accommodation address and says no `lakcímkártya` is issued to the applicant. The linked OIF administrative rulebook lists accommodation-change reporting as an available procedure, but the reviewed material did not establish a universal deadline for this TP-specific case, a correspondence-only reference-address scheme, or the precise impact of homelessness on every benefit. A lack of fixed accommodation is not evidence of loss of TP status; do not conflate registration with housing.

Leaving a designated public/provider accommodation may end the underlying accommodation arrangement or related support, but the official public source does not establish a general move-approval requirement, automatic re-entry right, or guaranteed replacement placement. Report the new address to OIF. Local social support and municipal-housing applications may change when moving between municipalities; portability is not established.

## 16. Readiness, blocking gaps and drafting boundary

### `housing-initial` — NOT READY

- **Known:** recognized beneficiaries do not have reception-centre accommodation/care entitlement; conditional local/provider accommodation funding exists; homeless night-shelter services exist generally; Budapest BMSZKI lists current facilities and opening window.
- **Missing:** current official first contact and referral path for a newly arrived TP applicant/beneficiary needing a bed tonight; self-referral/eligibility procedure; same-night and no-capacity alternatives; current destination/contact for county/capital accommodation support.
- **Why material:** without a working route, guidance could send a newly displaced person to a closed or irrelevant facility and leave them without shelter.
- **Official evidence needed:** OIF / county or capital defence committee public instructions naming the current intake office/contact, hours, eligibility, required referral and fallback when full.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** general Budapest and local homelessness-service route, clearly separated from TP accommodation placement; the legal exclusion and conditional-funding limits.

### `housing-private-rental` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** written lease is required; terms and rent are agreed; deposit rule is court-reducible above three months rather than a statutory cap; applicant accommodation reporting is separate from tenancy.
- **Missing:** no official universal private landlord document checklist; case-specific deposit settlement and address-proof acceptance differ.
- **Why material:** these matters prevent universal claims but do not block a bounded overview of renting.
- **Official evidence needed:** no blocker for high-level drafting; local OIF confirmation may be needed for a particular evidence/deadline question.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** short legal overview, no invented TP-specific document requirement, explicit contract/local-practice qualifications.

### `housing-financial-support` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** current employer-side support and current municipal/provider temporary-accommodation funding; general municipal housing and hardship-aid powers.
- **Missing:** no national TP-specific direct deposit/rent/utility benefit; local ordinance eligibility varies; no guarantee of an accommodation provider's capacity.
- **Why material:** relevant for directing users, but the known limits allow accurate qualified guidance.
- **Official evidence needed:** municipal ordinance for any named locality and current NFSZ programme notice for an individual employer claim.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** state the recipient, eligibility and time limits; say no nationwide direct rent/deposit entitlement was identified; refer to local rules without promising approval.

**Drafting boundary: Draftable with constrained scope.** A general article can explain private renting and the limited, recipient-specific programmes, while marking `housing-initial` as a blocking gap. Do not publish a purported first-night TP housing route until the missing official intake evidence is available.

### Safe to state

- TP registration and housing placement are distinct.
- Recognized beneficiaries are not entitled to reception-centre accommodation/care under the current special rule.
- Designated accommodation and time-limited local/provider funding exist, subject to specific eligibility and vulnerable-group extensions.
- Budapest BMSZKI night shelters are free overnight services; availability is not promised.
- A private residential lease must be in writing; the deposit rule is not a hard three-month cap.
- OIF address reporting is separate from the tenancy, and a TP applicant does not receive a `lakcímkártya`.
- Current employer housing support is employer-side, not a general direct cash benefit.

### Must qualify

- Any first-night route, admission, family placement, capacity or local social-aid claim.
- Which IDs/documentation a private landlord may request.
- Deposit return, advance rent, utility allocation, entry, termination and eviction details.
- The local availability of municipal/social housing and discretionary aid.

### Do not assert

- Automatic or indefinite public accommodation for every TP applicant/beneficiary.
- Current availability of 2022 transit-centre/railway/BOK help points.
- Guaranteed bed, municipality choice, or re-entry after leaving designated accommodation.
- A universal statutory three-month deposit maximum or nationwide TP rent/deposit/utility subsidy.
- That employer housing support is paid as cash to the worker or covers an employee's deposit.
