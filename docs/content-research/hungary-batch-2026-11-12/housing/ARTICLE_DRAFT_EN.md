# Housing in Hungary under temporary protection

**Research draft — checked 16 November 2026.**

## If you need a place tonight

The official sources reviewed do not establish a complete current nationwide first-night intake route specifically for people under temporary protection. They do not provide one current contact, hours, referral procedure, same-night placement method or fallback if no bed is available. TP status alone does not guarantee accommodation. Ask the local municipality's social-service office or local homelessness service about available options; this is general local assistance, not a verified TP reception route. In Budapest, BMSZKI publishes night shelters operating **18:00–08:00**. These are general homelessness services, not TP arrival centres; admission criteria and capacity vary, and a bed is not guaranteed. [BMSZKI](https://bmszki.hu/hu/szallasok/ejjeli-menedekhelyek).

Current law recognizes designated accommodation and conditional local-authority/provider funding, but the reviewed material does not give a complete self-referral or placement procedure. A recognized `menedékes` is not entitled to accommodation and care in an ordinary reception centre under the current rule. Do not rely on 2022 arrival-point or host-family instructions as current. [Decree 196/2025](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-196-20-22); [Act L of 2025](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-50-00-00.2).

## Conditional housing support

Some accommodation funding is conditional and time-limited; it applies after accommodation is arranged and does not guarantee a bed, priority, family placement or indefinite housing. Separate employer-side support is available through **4 March 2028** for qualifying employers hiring a recognized beneficiary in Hungary after recognition for at least 20 hours weekly. The employer applies for and receives it; it is not a general cash payment to the worker or a deposit grant. The stated monthly maximum is HUF 120,000 in designated preferred small settlements or HUF 80,000 elsewhere, subject to programme and contract rules. [NFSZ](https://nfsz.munka.hu/cikk/4091/Menedekes_munkavallalok_lakhatasi_tamogatasa).

Municipalities may offer local housing-cost, arrears or hardship assistance under their own rules. Eligibility and amounts vary. No nationwide TP-specific direct payment for private rent, deposit, first rent or utilities was established. Ask the municipality about its current rules.

## Private renting

No general TP-specific prohibition on private renting was identified, but the landlord's decision and contract conditions matter. Residential leases must be written. Rent and terms are agreed by the parties. A deposit above three months' rent is not automatically unlawful: a court may reduce grossly excessive security on request; this is not a hard three-month cap. Advance rent is different, and no universal statutory maximum for it was established. Check rent, deposit, utilities, term and other costs in the written contract. [Housing Act](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1993-78-00-00); [Civil Code](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2013-5-00-00).

A lease does not update your immigration accommodation record. Follow OIF rules for reporting a changed address. OIF says a pending TP applicant is not issued a Hungarian address card (`lakcímkártya`) merely through the TP application. No universal landlord document checklist was established.

**Evidence limits:** no complete official TP first-night intake or universal direct rent/deposit benefit was found. Check local shelter availability and municipal aid criteria locally. OIF's reviewed applicant guidance says to report a changed accommodation address but does not provide a clear universal deadline for this situation.
