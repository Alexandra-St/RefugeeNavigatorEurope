# Hungary: education under temporary protection

**Research checked:** 12 November 2026
**Canonical aspects:** `education-school-enrolment`, `education-pupil-language`, `education-higher-admission`
**Scope:** focused official-source research for a future article. This is not a content draft and is not integrated into the catalog.

## Executive finding

**Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Current law and OIF guidance establish access to public education for a foreign minor from the day a temporary-protection application is filed; recognized beneficiaries have the same education access, and OIF identifies the local school by the person's recorded accommodation. The general school system supplies the compulsory framework, but current official sources do not establish a uniform nationwide Ukrainian-language or Hungarian-language preparation entitlement. At higher-education level, a recognized `menedékes` may study in state-funded or fee-paying places under the Higher Education Act's conditions. Admission, funded-place competition, fees for a given programme, and scholarship selection remain separate decisions. The pending-applicant higher-education tuition category is not established. No `lakcímkártya` requirement for school enrolment was found; OIF instead says to present the protection document and official accommodation-address evidence.

## 6. Public-school costs, meals, attendance and secondary/vocational path

OIF says compulsory-age children and eligible kindergarten-age children may use free public education on the same terms as Hungarian citizens. This supports free public instruction, not a promise that all meals, transport, supplies, trips or extracurricular activities are free. Under current Decree 106/2022 §3/A, an eligible recognized beneficiary or pending applicant caring for a child may request free institutional and school-holiday meals for six months from the request without means testing. After that period, continuing eligibility depends on the stated income threshold or ordinary child-meal rules. No TP-specific transport subsidy was established.

Compulsory attendance continues under the Hungarian system. Parents/guardians must ensure enrolment and attendance; unjustified prolonged absence may engage ordinary school and child-protection procedures. Ukrainian online schooling alone is not established as a substitute. Secondary and vocational admission follows the relevant school's programme-specific requirements; interrupted Ukrainian studies may be assessed by the receiving school, but the research does not establish automatic placement into a particular grade or programme. Upper-secondary certificates can be used in higher-education applications subject to equivalence/recognition rules below.

## 7. Higher education, fees and TP status

The central Hungarian admission process is administered electronically through the Higher Education Information System's application interface (`E-felvételi`/Felvi). Some non-central, international, doctoral or institution-specific pathways can differ. TP status does not itself confer admission. Bachelor’s entry ordinarily requires a secondary-school leaving certificate; master's entry requires a relevant bachelor’s degree; doctoral entry normally requires a master's degree plus doctoral-school criteria. Institutions may impose programme-specific subject, language, aptitude, exam and other requirements.

The official Stipendium Hungaricum application itself is stated to be free, including admission examinations. Keep any formal diploma-recognition fee separate from admission fees.

## 9. Student social support and residence-permit boundary

TP status does not create automatic dormitory, study-grant or university social-support entitlement. These depend on the institution's rules, an eligible scholarship programme or the ordinary student-support regulations. The current Students at Risk scholarship can include accommodation support/dormitory for selected 2026/27 awardees; ordinary student social support is application- and eligibility-specific (S07, S10). The educational right to enrol and study is separate from immigration-permit categories. Existing Hungary Topic 2 research establishes that an active recognized TP beneficiary cannot be granted a study residence permit under the relevant provision; do not tell a TP student to obtain a study permit as a precondition to studying while TP remains active. A pending applicant's immigration route is also outside this topic.

## 12. Readiness, blockers and drafting boundary

No aspect is rated Not ready. The identified limits are local or programme-specific and can be described without blocking a narrowly scoped article.

### `education-school-enrolment` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** school-age rules; same-condition school access for recognized TP child; pending applicant access from filing; OIF accommodation-address school route; catchment primary-school duty; incomplete foreign studies decided by school director.
- **Missing:** universal school-by-school documents list; exact fallback when the child has no recorded address; guaranteed place at a preferred non-catchment school.
- **Why not a blocker:** families with an accommodation address have a direct local school and catchment route; the remaining details are local and can be qualified.
- **Official evidence still useful:** current education-ministry or school-district instructions for enrolment without address proof / with no prior documents.
- **What can be safely drafted:** contact the school serving the current accommodation; present the OIF status/address evidence; ask about alternatives if papers are missing; do not require a `lakcímkártya`.

### `education-pupil-language` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** general education and pedagogical-service access; current source does not guarantee a uniform Ukrainian-specific language programme.
- **Missing:** nationwide current schedule, duration and delivery for Hungarian-as-a-foreign-language support; the five-hour/130,000-HUF information is legacy and not corroborated by current law.
- **Why not a blocker:** the task expressly does not block for optional support hours varying locally; safe wording can identify it as school/provider-specific.
- **Official evidence still useful:** current ministry/Klebelsberg implementation notice for any revived/active Ukrainian-language preparation grant.
- **What can be safely drafted:** ask the receiving school what Hungarian-language and catch-up support it currently offers; do not promise a separate course or fixed hours.

### `education-higher-admission` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** recognized TP statutory eligibility for higher education including state-funded and fee-paying study; central electronic admission; institution-led further-study assessment of foreign credentials; current and legacy scholarships separated.
- **Missing:** pending TP applicant tuition category; 2027/28 scholarship calls and future intake fees; case-specific document-language and missing-document treatment.
- **Why not a blocker:** recognized-beneficiary rule is sufficiently clear, and uncertain items can be kept case-specific rather than generalized.
- **Official evidence still useful:** OIF/OH or university admissions confirmation for pending applicants; future Felvi/Tempus 2027/28 call and fee notice.
- **What can be safely drafted:** recognized TP holders may apply under the statutory routes, with no promise of admission, a funded place, scholarship or tuition waiver.

**Drafting boundary: Draftable with constrained scope.** The article can give the school route, ages, application-stage access, OIF-address distinction, and a cautious higher-education overview. It should not promise uniform language lessons, Ukrainian online-school substitution, automatic funded tuition, or current access to already-closed scholarship calls.

### Safe to state

- A TP child can access school from the filing date; recognition need not be complete.
- OIF directs families to the school serving their accommodation and says the TP document/address certificate should be shown.
- A `lakcímkártya` is not a precondition established by the reviewed TP rules.
- A compulsory-age child actually living in a catchment area has a right to the designated public primary school.
- The school director decides how to continue/credit incomplete foreign studies.
- A recognized TP beneficiary may apply for higher education in state-funded, partial-state-funded or fee-paying programmes under statutory requirements.
- A recognized TP beneficiary's eligibility does not guarantee admission, funded place or scholarship.

### Must qualify

- Required identity/guardian documents; missing records; grade placement; health records; address-free admissions.
- Preferred-school capacity, local language support, and school social/psychological services.
- Ukrainian bilingual institutions and any Ukrainian language/curriculum offering.
- Programme language, entrance exams, certificate equivalence, institutional fees and scholarship selection.

### Do not assert

- TP status automatically guarantees the preferred school, a particular grade or a Ukrainian-language class.
- Five weekly Hungarian-support hours or the former school subsidy remain current universal entitlements.
- Ukrainian online schooling alone satisfies Hungarian compulsory schooling for a TP child residing in Hungary.
- Every TP beneficiary gets free university tuition or a state-funded seat.
- Pending applicants have the same stated higher-ed tuition category as recognized beneficiaries.
- Every foreign credential must first receive standalone OH recognition or every document needs certified Hungarian translation.
