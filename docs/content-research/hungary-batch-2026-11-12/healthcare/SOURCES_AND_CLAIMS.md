# Hungary: healthcare access

**Research checked:** 12 November 2026  
**Canonical comparison aspects:** `healthcare-access`, `healthcare-proof`, `healthcare-urgent`  
**Scope:** Official-source research for a future article. This is not an article and does not integrate content into the catalog.

## Executive finding

**Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Current law provides healthcare entitlements separately to both the recognized `menedékes` and the pending `menedékesként elismerését kérő`, where they are not insured through ordinary social insurance. The core statutory set includes primary care, urgent outpatient and inpatient care, post-urgent necessary treatment until recovery/stabilization, pregnancy and childbirth care, defined medicines, mandatory age-based vaccination, and limited dental care. Oncology and other chronic-condition care and certain subsidized medicines are expressly added for both categories by Government Decree 196/2025. The entitlement is not identical to full ordinary insured-person coverage and does not mean all medicines, routine dental care or every planned specialist service is free.

OIF guidance tells TP holders to take their residence/status document and official certificate showing their Hungarian accommodation address. A TAJ number is not the TP status and its physical card is not a prerequisite stated in the TP entitlement provisions. The exact current TAJ-less data-entry/technical identifier procedure is a gap: the former specific 171/2022 decree was repealed in 2025. Urgent-care routes are clear and current: call **112** for life-threatening emergencies and **1830** for urgent, not immediately life-threatening problems outside GP hours; OMSZ operates the unified nationwide on-call system.

## 2–4. Legal basis and covered services

The primary current basis is Act L of 2025 §19, which provides that both a TP applicant and a recognized beneficiary receive healthcare defined by law. Decree 196/2025 §3(1), currently consolidated, makes the 301/2007 Decree §44(2) package applicable to both groups and expressly adds oncology and other chronic-care examinations/treatment and qualifying subsidized medicines used for improvement, maintenance or pain relief. Decree 301/2007 §44(2) sends the uninsured `menedékes` to §§26–28; §26 enumerates the core entitlement. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03]

## 5. Pending applicant: distinct entitlement

The pending `menedékesként elismerését kérő` is separately named in Act L §19 and Decree 196/2025 §3. If not insured through ordinary social insurance, the current official OIF applicant guidance lists free primary care; urgent specialist and hospital care (with expanded specialist/hospital care for specified special-needs persons); prenatal and obstetric care; qualifying medicines; oncology and other chronic care; and emergency dental/tooth-preserving treatment. The underlying 301/2007 §§26–28 explain the service limits and reimbursement route. This is not a right inferred from recognized status. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S05]

The pending applicant's GP route depends on accommodation: at a reception facility or immigration detention, GP care is provided there; a person placed privately is entitled to the territorial GP for that accommodation, and other basic care is through the municipality's service. Specialist care is with the territorially responsible provider; non-urgent follow-up after urgent treatment generally depends on GP referral or specialist recall. Special-needs applicants may receive medically justified additional services after specialist assessment. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S05]

## 6–8. TAJ, proof and electronic checks

The legal entitlement comes from TP category and the statutory package. Neither Act L §19 nor Decree 196/2025 §3 makes possession of a TAJ card a precondition. OIF's TP guidance instructs the beneficiary to show the residence/status document and official certificate containing the Hungarian accommodation address when seeking listed services. OIF's applicant guidance similarly identifies the applicant document and accommodation route. The applicant humanitarian residence document and recognized beneficiary card are distinct evidence; neither is an ordinary TAJ card. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S04, HU-HEALTH-S05]

NEAK says publicly financed providers check eligibility electronically using TAJ when available. It distinguishes a green (valid TAJ, entitlement registered) and red (valid TAJ, relationship data unsettled) indicator; red alone cannot be used to refuse publicly financed care or charge the full price. This ordinary entitlement check does not by itself explain TP-specific services where the patient has no TAJ. [HU-HEALTH-S06, HU-HEALTH-S07]

The old special procedure in Government Decree 171/2022 allowed providers to register people without TAJ using a technical EESZT identifier and status-document number. NJT shows it was repealed effective 20 July 2025 by Act L of 2025. The reviewed current legal sources do not establish the replacement technical workflow in detail. Do not instruct providers to rely on 171/2022 as current law; for a TAJ-less patient, present official TP/applicant and address documents and ask the provider to verify/record under current procedure. [HU-HEALTH-S08]

**TAJ result:** no evidence that TAJ is automatically issued solely because a person has TP. NAV's Topic 4 guidance says an employer requests TAJ for a foreign worker who lacks one; NEAK generally describes application through the competent government office for an eligible person. OIF has no official statement found that it automatically initiates TAJ issuance for every beneficiary/applicant. A TP-specific statutory service entitlement may exist before TAJ assignment, supported by official TP documents. The physical card is not itself the entitlement. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S04, HU-HEALTH-S06, HU-HEALTH-S09]

## 9. Employment and end of employment

If Hungarian employment creates ordinary insured status, the employer reports that status to NAV using 08E under the rules covered in Topic 4. A TAJ identifier may then be requested for the foreign worker by the employer. The TP route is separate: Act L and Decree 196/2025 tie the special package to the TP category where the person is not in a social-insurance relationship; they do not say employment is required to receive healthcare. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S06]

If work ends while Hungarian TP status remains active, the TP-specific basis does not vanish merely because employment ended. However, ordinary insurance law may have transitional post-insurance rules, and the exact point at which the person is treated as not in social-insurance status must be checked with NEAK/NAV for that person's record. Do not state that healthcare stops immediately on the final workday or provide a full post-employment insurance calculation. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S06]

**Topic 4 consistency:** consistent. Topic 4 established that employment triggers ordinary employer insured-person reporting and that a foreign worker without TAJ has an employer-request route. This report independently confirms a separate TP healthcare basis and no general rule requiring a TAJ card before care. Work-based insurance can add an ordinary basis; it does not mean employment is required for the TP package. No Topic 4 changes were made. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S06, HU-HEALTH-S09]

## 10–18. Access, referral and cost boundaries

### GP and location

For a recognized beneficiary, OIF says the listed services are received at the territorially competent health institution and asks the patient to bring their TP residence document and official accommodation-address certificate. The general primary-care system allows a patient to choose a GP, but a GP outside the territory may refuse; the GP responsible for the patient's area has territorial duties subject to lawful exceptions. The exact enrollment steps for every TP accommodation arrangement are not separately described in current TP guidance. For a pending applicant, the rule is clearer: GP care at reception facility/detention, or the GP for the private accommodation territory; other basic services through the local municipality's provision. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S04, HU-HEALTH-S05]

### Referral and hospital

The ordinary referral system generally uses a GP or other authorized referrer for specialist and planned inpatient care, with statutory exceptions. The TP package does not create a general waiver of the ordinary referral system. For pending applicants, non-urgent follow-up in the §26(d) package is free when referred by the GP or recalled by the treating specialist; specialist care is at the territorially responsible provider. Emergency admission and urgent treatment use the urgent-need route, not ordinary planned referral. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S10]

### Medicines, children, maternity and chronic conditions

- **Prescriptions:** Some medicines are free or subsidized under named statutory categories. Decree 196/2025 adds eligible subsidized medicines for oncology/chronic/basic-care improvement, maintenance or pain relief. This is not universal free medication. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S02]
- **Maternity:** prenatal care and obstetric care are expressly included for both TP categories under the applicable package. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S05]
- **Children:** a child must have their own relevant TP/applicant status evidence; do not infer entitlement from a parent's document or TAJ. The statutory age-based mandatory vaccination is included. The specific recordkeeping/proof route is not a parent-child “family TAJ” rule. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S04]
- **Chronic care:** oncology and other chronic-care examinations/treatment are now expressly included by Decree 196/2025 for beneficiary and applicant. It is a defined entitlement, not an unlimited promise of every service or all medicines. [HU-HEALTH-S01, HU-HEALTH-S05]
- **Mental health/rehabilitation:** only the special-needs applicant provision expressly adds psychological/clinical psychological, psychotherapy and medically justified rehabilitation with individual circumstances and specialist opinion. No general promise for every TP holder is supported. [HU-HEALTH-S02]

## 19–21. Emergency, urgent and routine care

| Situation | Route | Official guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Life-threatening emergency, immediate risk, severe breathing/chest symptoms, unconsciousness | Call **112** for emergency assistance/ambulance. | OMSZ distinguishes life-threatening cases and says call 112. |
| Acute problem that cannot wait for the next GP opening but is not immediately life-threatening | Call **1830**, the unified adult/child out-of-hours primary-care number. OMSZ triages and advises, directs to a care point, dispatches an on-call clinician or ambulance when needed. | OMSZ operates it nationwide, including Budapest since 1 October 2024. Weekdays 16:00–08:00; weekends/holidays 24 hours. |
| Routine, deferrable issue (e.g. ordinary prescription renewal) | Contact the GP during ordinary opening hours. | OMSZ warns the on-call service is not a substitute for routine GP appointments. |

For a non-life-threatening acute issue, 1830 is the first point of contact during on-call hours; users should call before going to a location because triage determines the appropriate destination. In Budapest, the national system applies, although OMSZ identifies local facilities. Emergency response is distinct from ordinary planned-care entitlement and should not be delayed to resolve TAJ or immigration evidence. Exact charges for all non-TP uninsured circumstances were not researched. [HU-HEALTH-S11, HU-HEALTH-S12]

## 22–26. Language, address and boundary topics

- **Language:** the health portals provide official Hungarian information. This batch did not establish a guaranteed interpreter, English-speaking provider, Ukrainian hotline or universal translation service. Do not promise one. [HU-HEALTH-S11, HU-HEALTH-S12]
- **Address:** OIF tells a beneficiary to bring the official certificate showing accommodation address, and the service is at the territorially competent institution. For a pending applicant, GP access is tied to reception placement or private accommodation territory. No-address scenarios are not resolved. Emergency calling is not conditioned on a GP district. [HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S05, HU-HEALTH-S11]
- **Moving:** the TP status/TAJ identity is not a GP district. A move may change the territorial provider; update OIF accommodation records where required and ask the new local service about GP registration. The health record is not a new TAJ entitlement. No detailed transfer process researched. [HU-HEALTH-S03, HU-HEALTH-S04]
- **EHIC / planned cross-border treatment:** no evidence found that Hungarian TP status automatically issues an EHIC or grants planned cross-border treatment rights. Do not assert either. NEAK's ordinary EEA guidance describes EHIC for people insured in an EEA state, a separate basis. [HU-HEALTH-S13]
- **Private providers:** private care is not equivalent to public entitlement. No private pricing or insurance research was conducted. [HU-HEALTH-S02, HU-HEALTH-S06]

**“Healthcare in Hungary” — Draftable with constrained scope.**

### Safe to state

- Both recognized beneficiaries and pending applicants have a defined TP healthcare package when not in ordinary social-insurance status.
- The package includes primary care, urgent specialist/hospital care, defined follow-up, prenatal/obstetric care, limited medicines, chronic/oncology care, mandatory age-based vaccination and limited dental treatment.
- Bring the relevant TP/applicant residence/status document and official Hungarian accommodation-address certificate.
- TAJ and its physical card are distinct from TP status; the legal TP package is not employment-dependent.
- Call 112 for life-threatening emergency; call 1830 for urgent non-life-threatening care outside GP hours.

### Must qualify

- Planned specialist/hospital treatment, medicine subsidies, dental, special-needs and chronic-care coverage have statutory conditions and limits.
- Employment can add ordinary insured status; stop-work transitions may depend on a person's NEAK record and social-insurance rules.
- TAJ-less verification/recording mechanism: present official status and address evidence and let the provider confirm current procedure.
- GP provider depends on territory/accommodation; address evidence matters for ordinary planned care.

### Do not assert

- That all healthcare, every medicine, routine dentistry or any planned specialist care is free.
- That a physical TAJ card is always required or that lack of a TAJ cancels TP healthcare rights.
- That old Decree 171/2022's technical-ID workflow remains current.
- That every TP holder automatically receives an EHIC, interpreter, English-speaking clinician or cross-border planned treatment.
- That 1830 replaces 112 for life-threatening emergencies.

## 33. Scope limits

This batch does not cover social assistance, disability benefits, sickness-benefit calculation, unemployment, housing, detailed employment rules, private insurance products, medical advice or treatment recommendations. It makes no content draft, catalog integration, country addition, comparison feature, app/runtime change, localization or deployment.
