# Healthcare in Hungary under temporary protection

**Research draft — checked 16 November 2026.**

Hungarian law provides a defined healthcare package for both a recognized temporary-protection beneficiary (`menedékes`) and a person whose application has been formally filed and is pending (`menedékesként elismerését kérő`). Online data preparation alone is not formal filing: the application route starts when the application is submitted to OIF in person. The applicant-specific basis applies while the case is pending under the statutory rules; a final rejection or withdrawal ends that basis when the pending status ends. A recognized beneficiary has a separate basis. [Act L of 2025 §§19, 249](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-50-00-00.2); [Decree 196/2025 §3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-196-20-22).

The package is defined, not full unrestricted healthcare. It includes primary care; urgent specialist and hospital care; necessary follow-up after urgent treatment until recovery or stabilization; prenatal and obstetric care; mandatory age-based vaccinations; limited emergency and tooth-preserving dental care; specified medicines; and defined oncology and other chronic-condition examinations and treatment. Applicants with specified special needs may qualify for additional services after individual assessment. Planned specialist care generally follows ordinary referral and territorial-provider rules. Not every planned treatment, medicine, dental service, rehabilitation or mental-health service is automatically covered or free. [Decree 301/2007 §§26–28, 44](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2007-301-20-22); [Decree 196/2025 §3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-196-20-22).

Bring your current TP residence/status document if recognized, or your official applicant/humanitarian document while the application is pending, plus official evidence of your Hungarian accommodation address. Show a TAJ number if you have one. TAJ is an administrative identifier, not TP status; the TP entitlement rules do not make a physical TAJ card a prerequisite. The exact current provider workflow for a TP patient without TAJ is unclear: the former procedure under Decree 171/2022 was repealed on 20 July 2025. Ask the provider to verify and record your entitlement under the current procedure. A red indicator in an ordinary NEAK status check does not by itself erase a separate TP basis. [OIF beneficiary guidance](https://oif.gov.hu/menedekeseknek-nyujtott-ellatasok-es-tamogatasok); [OIF applicant guidance](https://oif.gov.hu/tajekoztatok/menedekeskenti-elismereset-keroknek-nyujtott-ellatasok-es-tamogatasok).

Services in the statutory package are provided without charge within their conditions. Some medicines are free or subsidized only in specified categories. This is not a promise that all care, prescriptions, routine dentistry or private services are free. Work is not required for TP healthcare; employment may create ordinary insured status. The reviewed law does not say employment automatically cancels the TP package.

For routine care, contact the GP responsible for your accommodation area and bring address evidence; exact enrollment steps vary. Applicants in a reception facility or immigration detention receive GP care there; privately accommodated applicants use the territorial GP. Planned specialist care generally requires ordinary referral unless an exception applies.

For a life-threatening emergency call **112**. For an urgent problem that cannot wait until your GP is available but is not immediately life-threatening, call **1830** outside ordinary GP hours. OMSZ operates the unified service nationwide for adults and children: weekdays **16:00–08:00**, weekends and public holidays 24 hours. The service triages the call and can advise, direct you to care, send a clinician or dispatch an ambulance if needed. **1830 is not a replacement for 112 in a life-threatening emergency.** [OMSZ](https://www.mentok.hu/ugyelet/).

**Evidence limits:** the current TAJ-less provider workflow, case-specific insurance transitions after employment, and guaranteed interpreter/language support are not established by reviewed official sources.
