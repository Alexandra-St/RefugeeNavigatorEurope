# Temporary protection in Hungary: eligibility and applying

*Draft checked 16 November 2026. Official rules and service instructions can change; verify with OIF before travelling.*

## Who may qualify

Hungary’s temporary-protection status is called **menedékes**. The main group is Ukrainian citizens who lived in Ukraine before 24 February 2022 and were displaced from Ukraine on or after that date. The national rules also cover specified stateless or non-Ukrainian people recognized as refugees or otherwise protected in Ukraine before that date, and qualifying family members of these groups. A Ukrainian residence document by itself does not establish eligibility. The applicant must show which category applies. Hungarian citizens, including dual Hungarian-Ukrainian citizens, are not recognized as foreign beneficiaries under OIF guidance.

### Military-obligation cases

EU Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 makes compliance with Ukrainian military obligations a condition for new grants, with proof where applicable, from **5 August 2026**. The corrected decision exempts people who held temporary protection in the same Member State before or on **4 August 2026** and have continuously retained it there. OIF’s current public notice says Hungary has not changed its national eligibility approach and that military-liable Ukrainians may still receive protection under the previous rules. Decree 145/2026 stated that Hungary maintained its national eligibility scope, but its relevant provisions ceased to be in force on 7 September 2026. It is historical context only, not current operative law. OIF’s current published position still conflicts with the EU rule for new grants. If this may affect you, contact OIF about your circumstances and evidence; this guide cannot determine an individual outcome.

## How to apply

Apply **in person** to the National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing (NDGAP/OIF). You may enter information through the Enter Hungary data-entry interface first, then attend a Government Office (`kormányablak`) for the prescribed personal step. Online data entry is preparation; it is not the application. Without that preparation, attend the OIF regional directorate competent for your Hungarian accommodation address. Confirm the current office and service arrangements before travelling; the published procedure does not establish a universal appointment or guaranteed walk-in rule.

Bring the completed application form and your passport or other identity documents you have. OIF lists alternatives to a passport, but does not publish a complete route for someone with no identity documents at all. The authority checks and records your identity data, takes a facial image, and records fingerprints for applicants aged 14 or older. The form is signed before the authority. Each family member needs their own application.

## While your application is pending

OIF describes a humanitarian residence permit or specified data sheet as interim evidence of identity, lawful stay, applicant status and accommodation while the beneficiary card is being prepared. If the interim document expires first, OIF says to appear in person to request an extension. A granted beneficiary’s biometric document is different from these pending documents and from a Hungarian address-registration card (`lakcímkártya`); OIF says beneficiaries and applicants are not issued that address card. Report accommodation changes as required. The statutory decision period is 45 days; no universal card-delivery estimate is published.

A pending application for another residence permit does not support a universal promise that you may switch from temporary protection or retain stay/work rights after protection ends. Hungarian law describes withdrawal of an existing Asylum-Act title at the same time as grant of a new residence permit, but route-specific filing and pending rights remain separate questions. Ask OIF about the exact permit before acting.

## Dates for current beneficiaries

The EU protection period and Hungary’s current legal extension for beneficiary documents run through **4 March 2028**. OIF says existing documents remain valid by law to that date despite an earlier printed expiry, and no replacement is required solely because of the extension. Distinguish the EU period, Hungarian legal extension, and the date printed on an individual card. One older OIF validity page still contains a stale 2027 statement; use the newer OIF notice and current legislation.
