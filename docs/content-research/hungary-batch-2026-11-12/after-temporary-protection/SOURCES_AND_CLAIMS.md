## 1. Automatic transition and current legal framework

No current Hungarian law or OIF instruction located in this research automatically converts `menedékes` status into an ordinary residence permit, National Residence Card or EU Residence Card. No Hungarian post-4 March 2028 transition permit, grace period or simplified route was identified as enacted. Council/OIF extension material confirms the temporary-protection endpoint and card treatment; it does not establish conversion to another title. A Commission/Council transition recommendation is policy guidance, not an enacted Hungarian permit or entitlement.

Act XC of 2023, §9(5), says that for a third-country national granted temporary protection by the Hungarian asylum authority or court, the Act applies only its National Residence Card, law-enforcement and data-processing provisions. Thus route pages that tell visa-exempt visitors or residence-permit holders how to file inside Hungary cannot by themselves establish that a current `menedékes` can use the same route. TP-specific filing instructions for ordinary permits were not found.

Act LXXX of 2007, §1(3), requires written notice that the existing Asylum-Act residence title will be withdrawn simultaneously with grant of a newly requested residence permit. This gives the transition timing: filing alone does not end the existing title; grant replaces it. Do not substitute the more general one-title wording in Act XC of 2023, §7, for this precise timing rule. The cited law does not establish card-surrender mechanics, what a refusal means for the application, or a lawful-stay/work bridge after temporary protection itself expires.

## 4. Employment routes

### Employment-purpose residence permit

The permit is for remunerated employment with an identified employer (including specified contract arrangements). Current Act §28 and Government Decree 450/2024 apply nationality restrictions. OIF's current employment factsheet lists Georgia, Armenia and the Philippines. The country list excludes Ukraine, so a Ukrainian national cannot newly obtain this permit under the current list. OIF states a maximum total validity of three years, with route-specific extensions/re-issuance and no in-country application for another legal title during or after its validity. Family reunification is barred for sponsors holding this permit. Legacy permits may follow transition exceptions; they do not make the route newly available to a Ukrainian TP holder.

### Guest-worker residence permit

Government Decree 92/2026 changed the rules effective 6 June 2026. OIF states there is no third country whose nationals are currently eligible to apply for a new guest-worker permit. The country list page records transitional treatment for a guest-worker or employment-purpose permit that was valid on 5 June 2026, and for qualified applications already in process by then. Ukraine is therefore not eligible for a new guest-worker permit in November 2026. The usual maximum period is three years, with no family reunification and no long-term residence card eligibility, but this is not a realistic new Ukrainian route under the current suspension.

### Hungarian Card

The Hungarian Card is an employment route for listed highly skilled/professional activity, not an unrestricted skilled-worker title. The current OIF page lists occupational fields including IT, engineering, natural sciences, music teaching/folk music, and other specified performers, professional athletes/coaches and film industry activities. It requires a preliminary agreement or valid contract and the relevant higher-education/professional qualification, except for listed activity-based categories. Ukrainian nationality is not listed as an exclusion, so a Ukrainian may qualify if the activity and evidence meet the rules. OIF's ordinary filing page describes domestic submission in the situations allowed by law; it does not state that an active `menedékes` may switch into this route.

### National Card

The current Government Decree 179/2024 nationality list includes Ukraine and Serbia; the Act separately identifies Serbian and Ukrainian citizens. A Ukrainian citizen can qualify through actual remunerated employment (including employment through an agency) or the specified actual work as an owner/executive of a business. The permit is initially up to two years and may be extended for up to three years at a time, subject to statutory overall conditions. OIF describes application through Enter Hungary, the competent OIF regional directorate, or in some cases the employer. Ukrainian nationality is therefore an affirmative answer for National Card eligibility in November 2026. This is **not** a finding that a beneficiary can apply while active TP continues: §9(5) and OIF's application conditions leave that switch procedure unresolved.

### EU Blue Card

The EU Blue Card requires highly qualified employment, qualifying higher education or permitted professional experience, and a preliminary agreement/contract of at least six months. The 2026 monthly remuneration floor is HUF 1,001,048, with HUF 800,838 for the listed healthcare professions. OIF has already announced 2027 floors of HUF 1,091,244 and HUF 872,995, respectively; apply the amount relevant to the decision/period, not a stale 2025 amount. OIF explicitly says a person granted temporary protection in Hungary shall not be granted an EU Blue Card. Article 3(2)(a) of Directive 2021/1883 also excludes beneficiaries of temporary protection from that Directive's scope. A Ukrainian TP beneficiary cannot obtain this permit while the TP status is active. Eligibility after TP ends is conditional on the then-current route rules.

## 5. Study, family, and business routes

### Study

The ordinary permit requires qualifying full-time school/higher education admission, required language ability, tuition payment, general entry/residence conditions, a real Hungarian accommodation address, means and health coverage. Act §54(2)(b) expressly states that a person whom Hungary has granted temporary protection cannot receive a study permit. This prevents presenting study as a concurrent switch while recognized TP remains active. After TP ends, ordinary nationality-based domestic/consular rules apply; the general OIF page permits domestic filing for visa-exempt persons who are not holders of a residence permit, but it does not specifically explain the TP-to-study transition.

### Family reunification

Temporary-protection family unity is not ordinary family reunification. Ordinary family reunification is available to qualifying family members of listed residence-permit holders, Hungarian citizens, specified long-term-status holders, and certain protected persons. Core family members include spouse/registered partner and minor children; dependent parents and health-dependent siblings/ascendants are only in the conditions specified by law. A `menedékes` is not listed by OIF or §71 as a sponsor for this ordinary residence permit. Further, ordinary sponsors holding employment-purpose, guest-worker, study, White Card and several other specified permits cannot sponsor; guest self-employment has a one-year bar. A family member of an eligible ordinary sponsor may qualify, subject to the particular sponsor's restrictions. The route page does not resolve an applicant who is simultaneously a TP beneficiary.

### Guest self-employment

The current route is `vendég-önfoglalkoztatás`, for independent remunerated work or executive management of a business. OIF requires evidence of the activity, lawful means, accommodation and health coverage; self-employed persons may need to demonstrate annual revenue above 24 times the minimum wage and no tax arrears. For executives, the company generally must employ at least five Hungarian/EEA workers full time for six continuous months, or demonstrate the applicant's essential role and likely sufficient income. Validity is at most one year initially and may be extended up to a total of three years. Family reunification is unavailable during the first year. While holding it, a person may not apply in Hungary for a different title during or after its validity. These rules are not a TP switch permission; use only as conditional post-TP route information.

## 7. Enter Hungary, consular filing, appearance, fees, core evidence

Enter Hungary is the ordinary electronic filing platform for many permit routes, but its existence does not itself establish TP application/switch eligibility. Depending on route and applicant class, OIF pages also permit an in-person application at the regional directorate competent for the Hungarian accommodation address. Some routes require the initial application abroad at the Hungarian diplomatic/consular mission or authorized receiving location, where approval includes the visa for collecting the residence document. EU Residence Card applications are described as Enter Hungary only.

For ordinary permit applications, OIF route guidance requires identity/travel document, route-specific application/appendix and evidence of purpose (such as a work agreement/contract, university admission, family documents or business evidence), plus general requirements such as accommodation, means and insurance. Biometric photograph/fingerprints and signature are generally captured for the biometric permit; Enter Hungary-submitted applications require in-person biometric/signature steps within the period stated in the relevant route page. Exact fees depend on route and filing mode. OIF exempts Ukrainian nationals from permit issuance/extension fees, with an explicit exception for an EU Blue Card application filed in Hungary. This research does not reproduce a single fee number because it is route/mode-sensitive.

Existing TP-derived rights during valid TP do not establish work rights after its end. Do not promise a lawful-stay or employment bridge.

## 9. Permit grant/refusal and coexistence

- **Filing alone:** no source establishes that merely filing an ordinary-permit application terminates TP.
- **Ordinary permit granted:** §7 generally requires one title at a time and withdrawal of the former title when another is acquired. The exact effect on `menedékes` status, effective transition date, record, and card surrender is not stated in TP-specific instructions.
- **Ordinary permit refused while TP remains valid:** no TP-specific rule located expressly states the effect of refusal on recognition/card. Do not assert either automatic loss or guaranteed unaffected status based only on the general one-title rule.
- **Ordinary permit expires later:** no source says temporary protection automatically resumes or is reinstated.
- **Concurrent status:** the general rule disfavors two titles at once, but the exact TP/ordinary-residence relationship is **not established**.

## 13. Readiness and blocking gaps

### `residence-routes` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

Main current routes and major exclusions are sufficiently evidenced for a constrained route overview. State that the National Card is the clear Ukrainian work route by nationality under ordinary law, but do not imply it is currently issuable during active TP. No automatic conversion or enacted post-2028 pathway was identified.

### `residence-conditions` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

Current route criteria, nationality lists and TP exclusions are documented. National Residence Card time-counting is not explicit for TP, while EU Residence Card time-counting is explicit; preserve that distinction. A complete individualized eligibility assessment is outside the research scope.

### `residence-filing` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION (follow-up 13 November 2026)

- **Known:** OIF publishes route-specific in-country, Enter Hungary, personal and consular procedures for ordinary applicants. Ukrainian nationality qualifies for National Card; Blue Card and study grant are barred while TP is active. §9(5) limits ordinary Act applicability for `menedékes`.
- **Resolution for bounded drafting:** Act LXXX of 2007, §1(3), establishes that the existing Asylum-Act title is withdrawn at the same time the new permit is granted. A pending application alone does not end the title. EU/UNHCR transition guidance supports in-country applications for Ukrainian nationals under visa-free arrangements and identifies abroad filing for non-Ukrainian third-country nationals, including relevant family members.
- **Scope limit:** Filing procedures remain permit-specific. No universal domestic filing rule, post-expiry lawful-stay bridge, post-expiry work right, card-surrender rule, or refusal consequence is asserted.

## 14. Drafting boundary

**Current decision (13 November 2026): Draft with qualifications.** HU-TP-01 may explain the sourced transition timing and route-specific filing distinction. It must not guarantee eligibility for a particular permit or a stay/work bridge after TP expiry.

### Safe to state

- No automatic permit/permanent-residence conversion or enacted Hungarian post-4 March 2028 transition was identified.
- Ukraine is on the National Card country list; the guest-worker route has no new eligible countries since 6 June 2026; the employment-purpose route's current OIF list excludes Ukraine.
- EU Blue Card and study permit cannot be granted while the person is a Hungarian TP beneficiary.
- Ordinary family reunification is distinct from TP family unity; TP beneficiary is not identified as an ordinary sponsor.
- Current requirements and time limits for the Hungarian Card, National Card, Blue Card, study, family and guest self-employment routes, with the in-country caveat.
- EU Residence Card TP-time calculation is explicit; National Residence Card TP-time calculation is not established.

### Must qualify

- Whether an active `menedékes` can submit any ordinary permit application inside Hungary; general visa-free filing criteria do not resolve TP status.
- National Card availability while TP is active versus after it ends.
- National Residence Card eligibility/document prerequisite and TP-period calculation.
- Grant/refusal/expiry coexistence or restoration effects.
- Any pending stay/work after TP expiry.
- Future post-2028 law; check again before publication.

### Do not assert

- That applying automatically ends TP or that refusal definitely leaves it unaffected.
- That a granted ordinary permit has a known TP termination date or requires a particular card surrender absent an official instruction.
- That TP automatically resumes after the ordinary permit expires.
- That filing an ordinary application provides a lawful-stay bridge, temporary document, or continued work right after TP ends.
- That TP time counts for a National Residence Card.
- That Ukraine qualifies for a new guest-worker or employment-purpose permit under current rules.
