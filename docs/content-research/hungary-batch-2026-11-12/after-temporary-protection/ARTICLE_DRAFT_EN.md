# Residence options during or after temporary protection in Hungary

*Draft checked 16 November 2026. This is a route overview, not an individual immigration decision.*

## No automatic conversion

Hungarian temporary-protection status (`menedékes`) does not automatically become an ordinary residence permit or permanent residence. No enacted Hungarian transition permit or automatic grace mechanism for the period after **4 March 2028** was identified. Council Recommendation C/2025/5129 and Commission/UNHCR transition materials are recommendations or guidance, not a Hungarian permit or entitlement.

## Routes to check

- **National Card (`Nemzeti Kártya`):** Ukraine remains on the official nationality list. A qualifying job or specified business role is also required. Nationality eligibility does not establish that an active TP beneficiary may file from inside Hungary or switch while TP remains active.
- **Hungarian Card (`Magyar Kártya`):** available only for specified professions or activities, with the required qualification and employment documents. General domestic filing instructions do not establish an active-TP switch.
- **Employment-purpose permit:** the current OIF nationality list names Georgia, Armenia and the Philippines, not Ukraine. It is therefore unavailable to Ukrainian nationals under the current restriction.
- **Guest-worker permit:** OIF says no new applications have been accepted since 6 June 2026. Transitional treatment for qualifying earlier permits or applications is not a new route for Ukrainian applicants.
- **EU Blue Card and study permit:** neither may be granted while a person remains a Hungarian TP beneficiary. Eligibility after TP ends is subject to the rules then in force.
- **Family reunification:** ordinary family unity under TP is a different legal track. A TP beneficiary is not established as an eligible ordinary sponsor; a relative may qualify through another eligible sponsor under the applicable conditions.
- **Guest self-employment:** a separate route for qualifying independent work or business management, subject to its own activity, means, accommodation and insurance requirements. No active-TP switch is established.
- **Long-term residence:** the National Residence Card and EU Residence Card are different. Hungarian law expressly counts TP time for the EU card’s residence period, but a TP beneficiary cannot be granted that card while the status remains active. Whether TP time counts toward the National Residence Card is not established.

## Applying from Hungary and pending rights

Permit eligibility, the right to file from inside Hungary, pending lawful stay, pending work and the effect on temporary protection are separate questions. OIF’s ordinary route pages and visa-free filing options do not establish a universal active-TP switch. European Commission and UNHCR transition materials describe in-country filing options for Ukrainian nationals in some circumstances, but they do not replace each permit’s rules or settle the legal consequences of filing.

Under §1(3) of the Asylum Act, an existing title under that Act is withdrawn simultaneously with the grant of a new residence permit; filing alone does not itself withdraw it. This does not establish that every permit route accepts an active-TP application or create a general stay/work bridge if TP expires while an application is pending. No universal rule was found for TP-card surrender, refusal consequences or restoration after another permit expires. Contact OIF about the exact permit, filing location and your rights during processing before acting.
