# Social assistance and family benefits in Hungary under temporary protection

**Research draft — checked 16 November 2026.**

## Temporary-protection subsistence allowance

Hungary's regular subsistence allowance is for a **recognized** temporary-protection beneficiary (`menedékes`), not someone with only a pending application. Current 2026 amounts are **HUF 22,800 per adult per month** and **HUF 13,700 per minor per month**. OIF/NDGAP decides and pays it; the district employment office handles separate registration/cooperation and appearance duties. Payment is monthly, due by the 15th. The first payment is in the month after recognition under the applicable procedure. [Decree 106/2022, consolidated](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2022-106-20-22.10); [Decree 301/2007 §53](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2007-301-20-22); [OIF allowance information](https://oif.gov.hu/menedekeseknek-nyujtott-ellatasok-es-tamogatasok).

Beneficiaries aged **six or older** must appear at the district office linked to their accommodation/designated residence in the month before payment; a legal representative may appear for a child aged 6–17, while children under six are exempt from this appearance. Missing an applicable appearance or failing to collect a postal payment may mean no payment for that month. Beneficiaries who are employable and aged 16 or older must register as an employment-service requester (`szolgáltatást kérő`) and cooperate before the first payment. After 45 days from receiving the first payment, they generally must accept suitable offered work, subject to exceptions including already working for a Hungarian employer, caring for a child under three, or being under 18 and in full-time education. Refusal without an exception can end the allowance.

The allowance is not paid for a month in which the beneficiary earns income through Hungarian employment/public employment, receives a Hungarian old-age pension, or receives a foreign old-age pension payable in Hungary. The provisions do not provide a complete means-test formula for savings, household resources or other income. Report a change of accommodation to both the district office and OIF. The special allowance application channel, full document checklist and payment option for someone without a bank account are not fully specified in the reviewed official sources; ask OIF for the current form and filing method. Do not apply at the employment office.

## Other assistance and family benefits

Municipal housing-cost, medicine or hardship assistance is governed locally; eligibility and amounts vary, and TP status alone is not established as universal eligibility. A narrow emergency safeguard applies where life or physical integrity is in immediate danger; it is not a guaranteed same-day cash or food payment. Ask the municipality/social service about local rules. Housing-provider or employer subsidies are separate and are not cash benefits to the household.

TP status alone does not establish eligibility for ordinary family allowance (`családi pótlék`), maternity support (`anyasági támogatás`), GYES or GYET. Another qualifying legal status, residence, family and/or insurance conditions may be required. Applications for ordinary family benefits are handled by the competent government office or MÁK, depending on the benefit and case. The current published family-allowance rates are HUF **12,200** for one child in a two-parent household; **13,700** for one child and a single parent; **13,300** per child for two children; **14,800** per child for a single parent with two children; **16,000** per child for three or more; **17,000** per child for a single parent with three or more; and **23,300/25,900** for a child with long-term illness/severe disability (single-parent higher rate). These amounts are not proof of TP-only eligibility. [MÁK family allowance](https://www.allamkincstar.gov.hu/csaladok-tamogatasa/Csalad_gyermek/csaladi-potlek).

For ordinary eligible claimants, 2026 maternity support is HUF **64,125 per child** or **85,500 per twin child**, subject to prenatal-care and six-month application rules. GYES is generally HUF **28,500/month** in 2026 under the applicable base and child-age rules. GYET is for qualifying parents raising at least three minors, generally while the youngest is 3–8; the MÁK amount display is dated and no numeric 2026 amount is stated here. CSED/GYED are insurance-based and generally require qualifying insured history, not TP alone. The family tax allowance is a tax/contribution benefit, not cash social assistance. [MÁK maternity support](https://www.allamkincstar.gov.hu/csaladok-tamogatasa/gyermekvallalas-tamogatasa/anyasagi-tamogatas); [MÁK GYES](https://www.allamkincstar.gov.hu/csaladok-tamogatasa/Csalad_gyermek/gyermekgondozast-segito-ellatas); [MÁK GYET](https://www.allamkincstar.gov.hu/csaladok-tamogatasa/Csalad_gyermek/gyermeknevelesi-tamogatas).

School meals are a separate in-kind support route. A child born in Hungary does not automatically acquire TP or ordinary family-benefit eligibility through a parent; separate status and benefit conditions apply.

**Evidence limits:** current OIF material does not provide a complete public application channel, exhaustive documents, no-bank-account payment route or special-allowance appeal/deadline instructions. Individual family-benefit decisions depend on status, residence, family composition and insurance records. No 2027 rate replacing the current 2026 amounts was identified.
