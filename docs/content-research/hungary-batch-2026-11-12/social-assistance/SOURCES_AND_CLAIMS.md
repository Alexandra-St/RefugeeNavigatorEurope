# Hungary: social assistance and family benefits

**Research checked:** 12 November 2026
**Canonical comparison aspects:** `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, `social-child-benefits`
**Scope:** Official-source research for a future practical article. This is not an article and does not integrate content into the catalog.

## Executive finding

**Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Hungary has a current, TP-specific monthly `rendszeres létfenntartási támogatás` for recognized beneficiaries (`menedékes`). The amount is currently derivable from two statutory references: HUF 22,800 monthly for an adult and HUF 13,700 for a minor. The asylum authority (OIF/NDGAP) decides and pays beneficiary claims by the 15th monthly. Separate appearance, employment-service cooperation, suitable-job acceptance, earnings and pension rules can stop or affect payment. The law states the beneficiary route lasts during temporary protection, but does not by itself ensure payment through 4 March 2028; it remains conditional and depends on the applicable protection period.

The special beneficiary allowance does not cover a pending applicant: the relevant section names `menedékes`, while the general implementing regulation's §53(1) is displaced for beneficiaries by Decree 106/2022 §4(1). A separate route exists for a narrow cohort of displaced Hungarian citizens with permanent domicile in Ukraine. Family cash benefits are different: the Family Support Act does not list temporary protection as a status category. TP alone therefore does not establish entitlement; citizenship, another covered status, residence, insurance and individual statutory conditions matter. Municipal exceptional support is locally regulated and the Social Act's ordinary personal scope does not list TP beneficiaries; immediate statutory emergency aid has a narrower rule. Claims beyond these boundaries need individual review.

### Amount calculation and resources

- §53(2) of Decree 301/2007 sets the beneficiary's adult support at the amount of `foglalkoztatást helyettesítő támogatás` and the minor amount at Family Support Act §11(1)(b). The currently consolidated Social Act sets adult FHT at 80% of the social reference base; the 2026 base is HUF 28,500, giving **HUF 22,800**. Cst §11(1)(b) is **HUF 13,700** monthly. [HU-SOC-S02–HU-SOC-S04, HU-SOC-S07]
- These are person-based statutory reference amounts; the reviewed text does not set an additional household-size multiplier for this TP support. The adult and minor categories differ; §53 does not specify a separate single-parent or disability premium for TP support. Do not add family allowance rates to the support calculation.
- The express monthly non-payment rules are: a beneficiary receives employment income from a Hungarian employer or public employment, receives Hungarian old-age pension, or receives a foreign old-age pension that can be received in Hungary. The law does not in these provisions state a comprehensive means-test calculation for savings, family income, maintenance, ordinary foreign income or Ukrainian property. It does not state that those items are categorically ignored; avoid asserting either. [HU-SOC-S01, HU-SOC-S02]
- Prior to the first payment, a person within the employment-involvement cohort must register as `szolgáltatást kérő` at the district employment office and cooperate. The relevant cohort is a recognized beneficiary aged 16+ (and the separately defined displaced Hungarian citizen). After 45 days from receipt of the first regular payment, a suitable job offer, including public employment, must be accepted unless an exception applies: already employed by a Hungarian employer (proved), responsible for a child under 3, or under 18 and in full-time school education. Refusal ends the payment, subject to these exceptions. Starting Hungarian employment income means no TP subsistence payment for that month. [HU-SOC-S01]
- The act requires monthly appearance for beneficiaries aged 6 and over at the district office competent for accommodation/designated residence in the month before payment; the legal representative can do this for a child aged 6–17. Not appearing or not collecting payment means no retroactive payment for that month. [HU-SOC-S01]
- No 2022-only flat amount should be repeated as current without calculation from these current statutory references. The 4 March 2028 date concerns the protection/document validity horizon established elsewhere; it is not a separate guaranteed subsistence-payment term. [HU-SOC-S01, HU-SOC-S02]

**Evidence checklist for the special TP allowance:** The law establishes that a recognized beneficiary must apply and ties the employment and monthly appearance duties to recorded accommodation/designated residence. It does not establish a universal attachment checklist for identity/status card, bank details, address proof, income, employment, or child/family documents. Treat those items as **not established as mandatory documents** from the reviewed official evidence; do not manufacture a checklist. The authority may request facts/evidence in the individual procedure.

**No bank account:** The special support provisions reviewed establish monthly payment by the asylum authority by the 15th but do not establish whether a beneficiary without an account can choose cash, postal payment, or another method. Do not promise an alternative. (The separate historic accommodation-related benefit under §37/A has a postal mechanism; it is not this subsistence allowance.) [HU-SOC-S01–HU-SOC-S03]

**Timing and remedy:** Beneficiary support is monthly and due by the 15th. The special provisions reviewed give no decision deadline, explicit start-month rule, or appeal route for this claim. The displaced-Hungarian route does require a district-office formal decision, but the details of remedy were not researched. Do not transplant the 45-day TP-recognition decision deadline into this benefit procedure. [HU-SOC-S01–HU-SOC-S03]

A lawfully staying Ukrainian citizen appears within the narrow §3(2)/§7 emergency-assistance scope if life or physical integrity is endangered; the emergency provision does not establish a fixed amount or guaranteed same-day decision. Emergency accommodation and housing subsidy are separate matters.

## 14–20. Family and child benefits

### Status threshold

The Family Support Act's scope covers people living in Hungary who are Hungarian citizens; holders of long-term residence; persons recognized as refugees, beneficiaries of subsidiary protection or stateless; specified EU free-movement residents; and certain covered work-permit/coordination categories. Temporary-protection beneficiary and pending applicant are not listed as standalone categories. Thus **TP alone does not establish entitlement** to `családi pótlék`, GYES, GYET or domestic `anyasági támogatás`. A Hungarian citizen (including a dual citizen) or a person with another qualifying status may qualify under ordinary rules, subject to the benefit-specific conditions. [HU-SOC-S05]

### Family allowance (`családi pótlék`)

|---|---:|---|---|
| One-child family | HUF 12,200 | Per family | HU-SOC-S05 |
| One child, single parent | HUF 13,700 | Per family | HU-SOC-S05 |
| Two-child family | HUF 13,300 | Per child | HU-SOC-S05 |
| Two children, single parent | HUF 14,800 | Per child | HU-SOC-S05 |
| Three or more children | HUF 16,000 | Per child | HU-SOC-S05 |
| Three or more children, single parent | HUF 17,000 | Per child | HU-SOC-S05 |
| Long-term ill/severely disabled child | HUF 23,300; HUF 25,900 for single parent | Per eligible child | HU-SOC-S05 |

The allowance is claimed by an eligible parent/guardian for a child in the claimant's household subject to the statutory age/schooling rules. The application is decided by the applicant's competent capital/county government office (or a qualifying workplace family-benefit payer, where one exists); MÁK lists online, in-person and post routes. The law sets a maximum two-month retroactive period for ordinary family-support claims. A pending applicant does not acquire eligibility just by having applied for TP; another qualifying status or nationality basis would need to apply. [HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S06]

### Maternity support (`anyasági támogatás`)

TP-only status does not appear in the Family Support Act's scope and does not itself confer this benefit. Under the ordinary domestic rules, a qualifying woman generally needs at least four prenatal-care attendances (one in a premature birth); statutory alternative claimant situations include adoption/guardianship. Apply within six months after birth. 2026 one-off amount: **HUF 64,125 per child**, or **HUF 85,500 per twin child**. The government office at the claimant's residence decides; official routes include form, post, government customer service and the online portal. Pending TP alone is not an established eligibility basis. [HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S08]

### GYES and GYET

- **GYES (`gyermekgondozást segítő ellátás`):** TP status alone is not a listed Family Support Act basis. Ordinary qualifying parent/guardian can receive it generally to the child's third birthday; for twins and long-term ill/severely disabled children special extensions apply. 2026 gross base/one child amount: **HUF 28,500/month** (twins multiply by child count); pension contribution is deducted. A parent may generally work after the child's first birthday under the published conditions. Apply to the competent capital/county government office, with official form and online, postal and in-person routes. This is not insurance-based CSED/GYED. [HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S07]
- **GYET (`gyermeknevelési támogatás`):** Relevant only to a parent/guardian raising at least three minors in the same household; payable from youngest child's age 3 to 8, and ends if the eldest turns 18 leaving fewer than three minors. Work is allowed up to 30 hours/week, or without hour limit at home. Amount is one social reference base per month regardless of number of children: HUF 28,500 gross in 2026 based on the current base; the MÁK page still displays a 2025-dated amount, so the 2026 amount here is calculated from the official 2026 base and the law's formula. TP-only status does not establish Act scope. [HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S07, HU-SOC-S09]

### Insurance benefits and tax boundary

- **CSED/GYED:** These are insurance-based parental cash benefits. MÁK's general rules require the relevant insured status and generally 365 days of qualifying insurance during the two years before birth. TP status alone does not supply that record. Employment in Hungary may create insured days if it is a qualifying insured relationship, but eligibility remains individual; no amount calculation is included. [HU-SOC-S10]
- **Family tax allowance (`családi kedvezmény`):** NAV describes a tax/contribution allowance exercised by eligible taxpayers based on the relevant family/benefit status. It reduces tax/contribution otherwise due; it is not a social-assistance cash award and has limited practical value without eligible taxable income/contributions. Rates not researched. [HU-SOC-S11]

## 21–25. Other boundaries

- **School meals:** Decree 106/2022 §3/A separately grants eligible children free institutional and holiday meals on application for the initial period and then applies ordinary income-based rules. This is food-service support, not family cash allowance. The provision expressly includes pending TP applicants in the relevant meal route. No edit was made to the education research. [HU-SOC-S01]
- **Housing:** employer accommodation subsidy, accommodation-provider/municipal funding and municipal housing-cost aid are separate schemes and may have employers/providers/municipalities as recipients. They are not direct TP subsistence or family cash benefits. See the separate Hungary housing research; no housing research was reopened here. [HU-SOC-S01, HU-SOC-S04]
- **Disability/elderly:** the reviewed TP support rule gives no separately stated higher TP subsistence amount for disability or age. Disability can affect ordinary family allowance/GYES conditions; full disability and pension systems are outside scope. No TP-specific caregiver or elderly cash route was established here. [HU-SOC-S01, HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S07]
- **Ukrainian pension:** the TP allowance explicitly withholds payment in a month where the person receives a foreign old-age pension that can be collected in Hungary. The law does not in this clause enumerate other Ukrainian social benefits. TP does not automatically grant Hungarian pension; no pension coordination research was undertaken. [HU-SOC-S01]
- **Child born in Hungary:** Birth alone does not create TP status for a newborn; separate TP application is covered by Topic 1. Family cash-benefit eligibility follows the Family Support Act's eligible claimant/status and benefit conditions. The sources reviewed do not establish automatic family-benefit payment from birth or a special TP newborn entitlement. [HU-SOC-S05, HU-SOC-S12]

[HU-SOC-S01–HU-SOC-S05]

Ukraine is outside the EEA; the sources reviewed do not establish automatic Hungarian TP family-benefit coordination or an across-the-board ban on separate Ukrainian benefits. ## 32–34. Blocking gaps and drafting boundary

### Readiness

- `social-subsistence`: **Ready with qualification**
- `social-application`: **Ready with qualification**
- `social-child-benefits`: **Ready with qualification**
- **Overall: READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS**

No aspect is blocked on the core legal outcome. Material limits for safe drafting are specified below rather than treated as blockers.

### Blocking gaps

#### `social-subsistence` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** Recognized beneficiary route, administrator, current amounts, appearance, employment/cooperation, job-refusal and pension/earnings consequences.
- **Missing:** Special-support form/channel, complete proof list, payment method for someone without an account, decision deadline and remedy details; full rule for non-salary foreign income/assets.
- **Why material:** A practical article must not send users to the wrong office or promise a filing/payment method or categorical means-test result.
- **Official evidence needed:** Current OIF instructions/form for §53 subsistence, payment notice/form, and official case-processing guidance stating required proof, decision timing and review route.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** Benefit name, recognized-beneficiary-only scope, formula/current amounts, monthly timing and exact conditions with explicit limits.

#### `social-application` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** OIF/NDGAP application authority for recognized beneficiaries; district employment office for registration/appearance; separate district-office route for specified Hungarian citizens.
- **Missing:** Beneficiary submission channel, attachments, account-free payment arrangements and benefit decision remedies.
- **Why material:** Prevents incorrect directions and unmet expectations.
- **Official evidence needed:** OIF application form, up-to-date submission instructions and formal procedural notice.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** Direct applicant to OIF for case-specific filing instructions and district employment office for the statutory employment-service/appearance duties.

#### `social-child-benefits` — READY WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Known:** Family-support Act status scope, current amounts, MÁK application routes, and ordinary eligibility/insurance boundaries.
- **Missing:** Individual status/coordination outcomes for mixed-nationality households, Ukrainian treaty interaction and specific child/family facts.
- **Why material:** TP alone cannot be represented as blanket eligibility; other citizenship, residence or insurance facts may change individual outcomes.
- **Official evidence needed:** MÁK/government-office determination for the household's facts and any applicable international coordination/treaty provision.
- **What can still be safely drafted:** State that TP alone is not sufficient under the listed domestic status categories, give ordinary benefit names/rates only with an eligibility caveat, and direct case-specific questions to MÁK/government office.

### Drafting boundary

**“Social assistance and family benefits in Hungary” — Draftable with constrained scope.** Focus the article on the TP-specific regular subsistence support for recognized beneficiaries; distinguish pending applicants; explain the narrow Hungarian-citizen route; and state that general family cash benefits are not automatic based on TP alone. Present local aid and insurance-based benefits as conditional/individual. Direct unresolved filing and payment-method questions to OIF/MÁK or the competent local authority.

#### Safe to state

- Recognized `menedékes` can claim the current monthly support; the pending applicant route is not established for that allowance.
- Current statutory monthly references are HUF 22,800 adult and HUF 13,700 minor.
- OIF/NDGAP awards and pays recognized-beneficiary support monthly by the 15th; a separate local employment-office duty applies.
- TP alone does not establish family-support Act eligibility; ordinary rates must not be presented as automatically payable to a TP household.
- Extraordinary municipal assistance and EEA family-benefit coordination are locally/individually qualified.

#### Must qualify

- Employment, age, appearance, suitable-job and pension/earnings conditions.
- No comprehensive means-test statement for resources not listed in the TP rule.
- Municipal help, CSED/GYED, GYES/GYET and family allowance depend on separate status, residence, household, child or insurance conditions.
- Application channels, documents, bank-free payment and decision remedies for TP subsistence remain unestablished.

#### Do not assert

- Pending applicants receive the regular TP subsistence allowance.
- Every TP household is entitled to family allowance, maternity support, GYES/GYET, a specific cash emergency payment, or municipal support.
- Old 2022 flat amounts/current-sounding accommodation subsidies apply as the current regular TP allowance.
- Support is guaranteed through 4 March 2028, is paid in cash/post when there is no bank account, or is subject to a detailed resource test not found in current provisions.
- Newborn automatically gets TP or a cash benefit from parent's status.
