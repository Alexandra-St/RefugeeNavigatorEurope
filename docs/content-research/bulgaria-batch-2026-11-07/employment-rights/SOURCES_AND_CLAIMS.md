# Bulgaria — Employment Rights During Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-07
**Baseline:** 57e7173da2317760c0ccb4d3f168626b93d4bc10
**Scope:** focused current-law and official-guidance review for exactly work-permission, work-employer-duties, and work-minimum-pay. Research only; not an article, legal opinion, or individual eligibility decision.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** A person currently enjoying Bulgarian temporary protection (TP) may work without a separate labour-market access permit or prior Employment Agency approval. The right lasts only for the TP period. It is a separate status-based right, not a pending ordinary residence application. The Bulgarian Law on Asylum and Refugees states the TP right to work; the Labour Migration and Labour Mobility Act removes labour-market-permit requirements for TP beneficiaries. EU Temporary Protection Directive Article 12 requires access to employed and self-employed activity, subject to professional rules, and applies ordinary remuneration and employment law. Bulgaria's separate permit chapter for independent freelance activity excludes TP beneficiaries. This does not waive business registration, tax, licensing, or professional-recognition rules.

No TP-specific prior employer approval or Employment Agency registration before starting was found. Employers still have foreign-worker duties: written notice to the General Labour Inspectorate within seven days after actual work starts, and a valid residence/status document must be requested, with a notarized copy retained for the employment period. A premature termination triggers a separate three-day written notice to the Employment Agency. Ordinary employment-contract recording in the NRA Employment Register is separate: record the contract within three days of signing and termination within seven days; give the worker the signed contract and proof of registration before starting.

As at the research date, the official national minimum is **€620.20 per month** and **€3.74 per hour**, effective **1 January 2026**. These are official wage-floor figures, not net pay. Higher mandatory floors can apply to a covered occupation or collective agreement; pedagogical specialists have an express 2026 salary table. The national minimum does not set a freelancer's fee.

The existing Bulgaria residence research remains applicable: a pending ordinary residence application alone does not create work authorization; the EU Blue Card route excludes a person currently using TP; and no universal post-TP employment bridge has been established.

## Critical question answers

1. **Does a TP beneficiary need a separate work permit?** **No** for work during current TP: the national statute expressly removes the labour-market access permit requirement.
2. **Is prior Employment Agency approval required?** **No prior approval was found** for TP employment. Employer reporting duties still apply.
3. **Which employer notices are required, and when?** The employer gives written notice to GLI within **7 days from actual start**. A separate written notice goes to the Employment Agency within **3 days** if employment ends prematurely. Ordinary employment records go to the NRA within three days after signing and seven days after termination.
4. **Must the employer separately notify work termination?** **Yes for premature termination** under Labour Migration Act Art. 10(4). Whether every scheduled contract end is “premature” is not established. The NRA termination record is separately required for employment contracts.
5. **Does the TP work right cover self-employment/business?** EU law requires access to self-employed activity during TP, and TP beneficiaries are excluded from Bulgaria's ordinary freelance-permit chapter. The precise business-registration, tax and activity-specific steps remain separate and are not established here.
6. **What happens when TP ends?** The TP-based work right ends with TP. No universal post-TP employment bridge was established; another express work-authorizing status is needed.
7. **Does a pending residence application let a person continue working after TP ends?** **No general such right was established.** Filing by itself is not work authorization.
8. **What minimum wage applied on 2026-11-07?** **€620.20 monthly and €3.74 hourly**, effective 2026-01-01, for the official normal full-time basis. The wage figure is gross, before individual deductions; it is not take-home pay.
9. **Are there profession, grade or sector floors?** **Yes.** The 2026 pedagogical-specialist table includes a €1,144.05 teacher/educator monthly basic floor; a covered collective agreement may set a higher floor. Coverage is not universal.
10. **Do TP employees have the same basic employment/pay protections?** **Yes, under the general Bulgarian employment laws that apply to the employment relationship.** Directive 2001/55 Art. 12 applies general national remuneration and employment-condition law; no lower TP-specific minimum was found. This does not remove occupation-specific licensing rules or convert self-employed fees into wages.

## Employment vs self-employment boundaries

- **Employee:** a worker under a labour relationship may rely on the TP exemption while TP is current. Contract, NRA record, wages and ordinary labour protections still apply. GLI notice is due after actual start.
- **Civil/service arrangement:** do not infer status from the document label. If the facts amount to employment, the Labour Code governs. Genuine independent activity is covered by Directive 2001/55 during TP, and TP beneficiaries are excluded from the ordinary freelance-permit chapter; registration and tax rules are separate.
- **Seasonal work:** a TP beneficiary under an employment contract does not need an ordinary seasonal-worker permit solely because the job is seasonal. Do not import duration caps of an ordinary seasonal authorization into the TP exemption.
- **Temporary-agency work:** the exemption does not erase ordinary registration and employment duties of a temporary-work agency. Identify the legal employer and user undertaking.
- **Regulated professions:** no market-access permit does not waive recognition, licensing, registration, language/fitness standards where lawfully required, or statutory citizenship limits on particular posts.

## Post-TP and residence-application boundaries

1. **Current TP:** supplies its own right to stay and work for the TP period; it is separate from an ordinary permit or Blue Card.
2. **TP ends:** the TP-based right does not continue. No general Bulgarian post-TP bridge was established in the residence research. A separate residence/work authorization may create rights under its own effective terms.
3. **Pending ordinary application:** filing alone does not create work authorization and is not a post-TP work bridge.
4. **Single permit:** an ordinary route remains distinct. Whether a TP card satisfies the in-country filing condition was not settled in the prior residence review.
5. **EU Blue Card:** current TP beneficiaries are excluded from the standard route by Foreigners Act Art. 33k(30)(1). Do not present it as a TP conversion route.

## Evidence boundaries

1. Tie the exemption to current TP, not a pending TP or ordinary residence application; do not assume a future TP extension.
2. The Directive and Labour Migration Act Art. 39a establish TP-period self-employment access and exclusion from the ordinary freelance-permit chapter. They do not establish every company, tax, social-insurance or activity-specific requirement.
3. Civil contract, service contract, employment and freelance activity can describe different legal relationships. Facts and Bulgarian law control.
4. Professional recognition/licensing is distinct from immigration work permission.
5. Art. 10(1) says seven days and written notice to GLI. It does not say seven working days or designate one exclusive channel. GLI publishes a form and electronic portal; follow its current instructions.
6. Art. 10(2) requires a valid residence document and a notarized retained copy. AREF/GLI guidance identifies the TP registration card as status proof, but the operational guidance is older; check current validity.
7. Art. 10(4)'s “premature termination” is narrower than every end of work. Three-day Employment Agency notice is established for the statutory premature-termination case; its application to every planned end during TP is unresolved. NRA's seven-day employment-record notice is separate.
8. Labour-market exemption does not dispense with the signed written contract and NRA record confirmation before an employee starts.
9. Use only the official 2026 wage and effective date. Do not use 2025 values or calculate net pay. Decree provides the official dual-currency figure; use its euro value.
10. Pedagogical salary minima are role-specific. Collective-agreement minima bind within applicable coverage; do not imply a universal sector/grade table.
11. Prior Bulgaria residence research establishes no general pending-application work right, Blue Card exclusion while TP is current, and no universal post-TP bridge. These do not replace checking a distinct permit route.
12. Refresh official law and procedure before applicant-specific advice or use after the 2026 wage period.
