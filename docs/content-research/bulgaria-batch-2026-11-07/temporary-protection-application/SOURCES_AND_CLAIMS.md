## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH EXPLICIT BOUNDARIES.** Bulgarian government guidance describes a national category for foreign citizens and stateless people who left Ukraine because of hostilities, entered and remained in Bulgaria, and expressly request temporary protection. The same official overview lists Ukrainian citizens who lived in Ukraine before 24 February 2022 and their family members, plus people with international or equivalent national protection in Ukraine and their family members. The national category page is undated and also contains a 5–15 minute/immediate-grant claim that is not corroborated by current dated operational guidance; do not repeat that timing as a service promise. Current Bulgarian public operational details are strongest for the 2026 card re-registration process, not a complete initial-application checklist. The EU military-obligation rule and its continuity exception are established, but Bulgarian accepted-proof and screening instructions were not found. The EU scheme is extended to 4 March 2028; the latest Bulgarian State Agency for Refugees notice located says national protection was extended to 4 March 2027 and specifies card-renewal arrangements through 2026. Do not collapse the EU scheme endpoint, the Bulgarian decision notice, and an individual card’s validity into one date. No later Bulgarian operational notice resolving the 2027–2028 national implementation or individual document validity was found as of the check date. These gaps can be stated expressly and do not prevent a carefully bounded draft.

## Application, documents, and transitions

- **Initial request:** the undated official government overview describes an oral request to Border Police, AREF, or other Ministry of Interior bodies (Migration/police). AREF’s older procedure page says the person appears in person. Treat these as published route descriptions, but because the operational page is from 2022 and the current AREF page concentrates on re-registration, verify current locations and intake steps with AREF/MVR. No universal appointment requirement, active online application form, fee, initial-document checklist, fingerprint rule, statutory processing deadline, or guaranteed immediate decision was established in the reviewed current sources.
- **2026 re-registration of existing beneficiaries:** AREF says personal appearance is required and no proxy is accepted. Its listed items are a renewal application showing current address, a national identity document, and surrender of the existing card. This is a renewal list only. After 30 April 2026, the notice directs re-registration to AREF territorial units; a new card may be requested before or after that date. These dates have passed as of this research check and should be re-verified before operational use.
- **Duration:** distinguish (1) the EU scheme extension to 4 March 2028; (2) the latest Bulgarian AREF notice, which says Decision 190/2026 extended national protection to 4 March 2027; and (3) the validity of a person’s particular registration card. The reviewed sources do not establish that every card has a 2028 printed expiry or describe the national post-March-2027 card workflow.
- **Transfer, travel, termination, and return:** not researched as separate claims within these three aspects. Do not promise automatic transfer between Member States, Schengen travel, re-entry, or that leaving Bulgaria has no status effect. Escalate those questions to current AREF/MVR information and the applicable legal instruments.
- **Military rule:** reproduce the EU rule only with “where applicable,” the 5 August 2026 application date, and the same-Member-State continuity exception. Bulgarian accepted evidence and operating steps remain unestablished.
