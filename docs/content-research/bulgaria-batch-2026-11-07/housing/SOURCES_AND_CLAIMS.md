# Bulgaria — Housing

**Research date / LastCheckedAt:** 2026-11-07
**Scope:** exactly `housing-initial`, `housing-private-rental` and `housing-financial-support`. Research only; no country catalog or application content.

## Research verdict

As of 2026-11-07, **Program 3** is an active national humanitarian accommodation and integration programme for people displaced from Ukraine who hold Bulgarian temporary protection (TP). It runs alongside the statutory right to suitable accommodation or means of accommodation **when necessary**. Program 3 supports selected accommodation through eligible facilities and reimburses qualifying providers; it is not a universal free-hotel entitlement, a guaranteed bed, or a rent allowance paid to the beneficiary. A Council of Ministers amendment in September 2026 extended/updated the programme through 4 March 2027 and introduced individualized assessment; all children under 18 are expressly included in the risk group. The public summary does not supply the full revised eligibility, evidence, referral and termination rules.

The 2022 hotel **Program 2 is closed to new applications and claims**. Only already-open administrative proceedings may be completed or reconsidered under the September 2026 closure rules. Do not present its old hotel arrangements as current.

Ordinary private residential rental is a separate landlord–tenant contract. General Bulgarian lease law does not establish a universal statutory deposit cap. The TP card/address process requires an up-to-date current address, but the exact current checklist for address registration in an ordinary private rental, including when landlord consent or a particular declaration is required, was not established in the current primary sources reviewed.

Safe drafting is possible with qualifications. In particular, verify the amended Program 3 mechanism and current euro rate/available places with the responsible authority before making an individual placement or payment promise.

## Beneficiary payment vs host/facility compensation

- **Direct payment to TP beneficiary for rent:** no universal national rent allowance was established.
- **Program 3 reimbursement:** paid to an eligible accommodation provider/operator for qualifying accommodation costs; it is not rent money paid to the displaced person. The last official published schedule located is the July 2025 rate in S04: BGN 16.43 per person/night excluding VAT, with stated VAT-inclusive totals of BGN 17.91 or BGN 19.72 depending on VAT treatment. These are historic published amounts; current 2026 euro rate, grant cap, and any updated eligibility schedule are **Not established**.
- **Accommodation in kind:** qualifying beneficiaries may receive a place if individually assessed/approved and a compliant facility has capacity; no source establishes universal availability.
- **General social assistance:** separate, application-based support may be sought through the Social Assistance Directorate after current-address registration. Current monetary values on MLSP's page are in BGN and should not be repeated as verified 2026 euro values. Whether a particular assistance award is compatible with Program 3 must be checked individually.
- **Private landlord/host compensation outside Program 3:** no universal payment arrangement was found. A normal private lease is paid by the tenant under the contract unless an applicable separate programme expressly pays an eligible provider.

## Critical questions — direct answers

1. **Is there a national free/subsidized accommodation programme on 2026-11-07?** Yes, Program 3 is operating with provider reimbursement periods through the programme end date of 4 Mar 2027; individualized eligibility and an available eligible facility are required. It is not a universal bed guarantee (S02–S04).
2. **Who may qualify?** People displaced from Ukraine holding Bulgarian TP, under the current programme criteria and individual assessment. The full revised eligibility test is not established in the public summary (S02–S04).
3. **Are there separate vulnerable-person rules?** Yes. All children under 18 are expressly in the risk group; individualized family/child needs consider health, income and self-housing ability. Full current categories and proof requirements are not established (S02).
4. **Is there a fixed maximum stay?** Not established in current reviewed public sources. The programme ending date is not an individual's guaranteed stay period.
5. **Who allocates places?** Not established. MoT administers provider reimbursement, but the current beneficiary referral and placement decision-maker are not identified in the reviewed updates (S02–S03).
6. **Is a place guaranteed?** No. No guarantee or capacity assurance was found.
7. **Can a TP holder rent ordinary private housing?** Yes, no TP-specific bar was found; ordinary civil lease rules apply, subject to landlord agreement and case-specific administrative requirements (S06–S08).
8. **Must the ordinary lease be written?** A universal statutory written-form requirement was not established. A signed written contract is strongly advisable; distinguish special accommodation/short-stay contracts.
9. **Is there a universal statutory deposit cap?** Not established; no universal cap was found for ordinary residential leases. Do not apply another accommodation regime's cap.
10. **What address-registration duties are confirmed?** The TP registration card records the holder's current address, and MLSP says general assistance applications follow current-address registration. Exact current change procedure, deadline, acceptable private-rental proof and any host notification duty for an ordinary lease were not established; verify with SAR/MVR/municipality (S01, S07–S09).
11. **Is there a national rent allowance paid directly to the TP beneficiary?** Not established; no universal direct rent subsidy was found. Program 3 is provider reimbursement/in-kind support.
12. **Are there payments to hosts, landlords or facilities?** Yes, Program 3 has provider reimbursement for eligible accommodation under its conditions; ordinary private landlords are not automatically eligible (S03–S04).
13. **Who receives that payment?** The eligible accommodation operator/provider, not the TP beneficiary (S03–S04).
14. **What amounts/caps apply on 2026-11-07?** A current euro rate/cap is not established. Last located official rate: BGN 16.43/person/night excluding VAT (BGN 17.91 or BGN 19.72 including VAT according to VAT treatment) in the July 2025 mechanism. Do not treat it as an updated 2026 amount (S04).
15. **Must vulnerable status be separately confirmed?** Vulnerability is part of the current individual assessment; exact current evidence documents and confirmation process are not established in the public summary (S02).
16. **Does support continue automatically for the whole TP period?** No automatic individual continuation was established. The programme-wide end date is 4 Mar 2027; individual eligibility/placement remains subject to assessment and programme terms (S02–S03).
17. **Are there municipal mechanisms that cannot be promised nationwide?** Yes. Local social housing, emergency assistance and services may vary by municipality and capacity; no universal TP-specific municipal housing programme was established (S09–S10).

## Final topic verdict

**READY WITH QUALIFICATION FOR CONTENT DRAFTING.** Official evidence establishes an active national Program 3, a statutory necessity-based TP accommodation right, closure of Program 2 to new claims, ordinary private rental as a separate contractual route, and the distinction between provider reimbursement and beneficiary cash. Do not promise a bed, universal eligibility, a fixed individual stay, automatic continuation, direct rent subsidy or a current per-night euro amount. Obtain the full amended Program 3 criteria, current rate schedule, current referral/contact route and TP private-rental address-registration checklist before adding case-specific application steps or amounts.
