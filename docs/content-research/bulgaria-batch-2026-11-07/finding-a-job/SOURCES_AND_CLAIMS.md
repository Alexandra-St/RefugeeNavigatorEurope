# Bulgaria — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 2026-11-07
**Scope:** exactly the shared aspects `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, and `employment-assistance-conditions`. Official Bulgarian and EU sources only.
**Purpose:** research evidence for later content drafting; not user-facing legal advice or an individual eligibility decision.

Keep public vacancy browsing, Employment Agency registration, work authorization, programme participation, and unemployment cash benefits distinct. Current temporary protection (TP) is expressly covered by Bulgaria's Employment Promotion Act for jobseeker registration rights. That does not itself qualify a person for cash benefit, guarantee a job, or guarantee a training place.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Public vacancy search is available through the Employment Agency's e-Labour Exchange and EURES without Bulgarian jobseeker registration. Bulgarian Employment Agency registration is available to persons enjoying TP; TP beneficiaries may register at the address where they have settled or reside, with one of the statutory address proofs. Registration as **unemployed** is appropriate only if the person meets the statutory definition: not working, seeking work, and ready to start within 14 days after notification. Employed jobseekers use a distinct group/status.

Registered jobseekers can access statutory information and, depending on their registration group, mediation, counselling, career guidance, training, validation, and referrals. These service categories do not promise a particular vacancy, course, subsidy, or placement. A current Employment Agency page lists a nationwide Refugee Employment and Training Programme that expressly includes TP beneficiaries among its intended participants, but participants must be registered unemployed and programme intake, individual assessment, employer demand, funds, and programme terms still apply.

Unemployment cash benefit is a separate NSSI decision and application. Registration alone does not confer it. NSSI lists insurance contributions for at least 12 of the 18 months before insurance termination, registration as unemployed, no disqualifying pension, and no covered work activity among the conditions. Qualification recognition is not a universal prerequisite for every job; regulated professions have profession-specific recognition/licensing rules.

## Registration and service boundaries

- **Status and eligibility:** The Act distinguishes registered unemployed persons from employed jobseekers, students seeking work during study-free time, and certain nonworking pensioners. TP beneficiaries are expressly entitled to exercise the Chapter's jobseeker rights. A TP holder not working may register as unemployed only when seeking work and ready to start within 14 days of notification; a TP holder who is working belongs in the appropriate employed category. Employment authorization and jobseeker registration are separate legal questions.
- **Address:** TP beneficiaries may register at the address where they have settled or reside. The Act names an address card, accommodation registration card, declaration of the person hosting them, municipal document, or other address-document as ways to verify it. The reviewed provisions do not require a Bulgarian permanent address for TP holders.
- **Documents:** The application-declaration is required. The implementing rules list identity/national travel/residence documents and the TP registration card for TP beneficiaries. Education/qualification evidence is needed only if the Agency cannot obtain the data ex officio. Disability documents and other declarations are conditional on relevance and inability to retrieve records. The TP card confirms the claimed protection status; it is not a substitute for address evidence if separately required.
- **Filing and local referral:** Application may be made in person, online under e-government identification, by licensed post, or by another method the Agency announces. It can be physically delivered to a territorial unit, but registration is organized through the Labour Bureau based on the address rule. After registration, a mediator can provide information, consultation, and matching; an office referral letter for an advertised vacancy is not a guarantee of interview or employment.
- **Service tiers:** The Act lists vacancy/programme information, mediation, psychological counselling, career guidance, adult training, validation, and referrals. Rights differ by registration group. Employment Agency information about mediation is also available to people who are not registered, but that should not be described as full formal placement mediation or referral. EURES public search and several EURES online services are available without a Bulgarian register entry or EURES account; extra features may require an EURES profile.
- **Training and subsidized employment:** Neither registration nor TP status automatically secures training or subsidized work. The Agency's Refugee Employment and Training Programme is a relevant current route for TP beneficiaries; its public page names registration as unemployed as a participant condition. Current eligibility, documentation, budget, intake, training provider/employer, and local availability need confirmation with the relevant Labour Bureau.

## Unemployment-benefit boundary

The NSSI, not the Employment Agency or the Labour Bureau, decides and pays Bulgarian cash unemployment benefits. NSSI says the person must submit a separate application; among its listed conditions are contributions to the unemployment fund for at least 12 of the 18 months before insurance termination, registration as unemployed with the Employment Agency, no disqualifying pension, and no covered work activity. Deadlines also matter: the NSSI page describes an application within three months and Employment Agency registration within seven working days for payment from the termination date. Other rules and individual facts can affect entitlement and amount.

Do not infer benefit eligibility from TP, a TP registration card, work permission, registration as an unemployed jobseeker, programme participation, or the fact that a person has searched for work. This research does not assess whether foreign insurance periods can be aggregated in an individual case or whether any particular applicant meets the contribution and other conditions.

## Qualification-recognition boundary

Formal recognition is not a universal requirement for every job. For non-regulated occupations, NACID states that recognition of the professional qualification is for the potential employer to decide. Regulated professions can require recognition/licensing through the competent authority for that profession. The Agency's registration document rule is a distinct administrative step: education/qualification documents are requested where the information cannot be obtained ex officio; it is not evidence that every foreign diploma must first be formally recognized to work. Identify a profession's regulated status and competent authority before giving occupation-specific steps.

## Evidence boundaries

1. The legal route for TP registration is clear, but a Labour Bureau must determine the correct registration group and validate an individual's documents/address evidence; this research is not a case-specific decision.
2. “Ready to start within 14 days” is part of the statutory unemployed definition and should not be generalized to other registration groups.
3. The Refugee Employment and Training Programme is listed by the Employment Agency and includes TP beneficiaries, but the public page does not establish that applications or funded places are open at every office on the check date. Confirm current intake, funding, selection and local availability.
4. Training, language learning, counselling and employer subsidies are programme/service pathways, not universal free entitlements or guaranteed outcomes.
5. Vacancy portals and EURES listings can change; user-facing guidance should encourage checking the listing and its application contact directly.
6. Unemployment benefit has separate NSSI insurance, timing, and other eligibility rules. No TP status alone creates benefit entitlement.
7. Qualification recognition requirements depend on whether the profession is regulated and on the competent authority's current rules.

## Final topic verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Official sources support all three aspects, including TP-holder registration rights and a TP-inclusive national employment/training programme. Verify local programme intake and any applicant-specific registration or benefit requirements before turning this research into procedural advice.
