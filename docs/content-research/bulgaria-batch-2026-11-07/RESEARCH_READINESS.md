## 5. Aggregated drafting caveats

### Temporary Protection

- EU scheme endpoint 4 Mar 2028 does not establish Bulgarian national/card validity after the Bulgarian notice through 4 Mar 2027.
- Bulgarian military-proof and screening procedure was not established. No complete current initial-application checklist was established.

### Residence Options

- No automatic conversion or post-TP transition route was established. TP-card eligibility for in-country single-permit filing is unconfirmed.
- A pending ordinary application itself does not establish a stay/work bridge. TP-time counting toward Bulgarian permanent residence is not established; Blue Card is excluded while under TP.

### Work Authorization and Employee Rights

- The TP work right applies only while TP is effective. Employer reporting duties remain; self-employment/business formalities are separate.
- The 2026 minimum wage is a dated gross value; do not present it as net pay.

### Finding a Job

- Public vacancy browsing is not registration or placement. Training and subsidized employment are not guaranteed.
- Unemployment cash benefit is a separate NSSI decision. TP employment-program local intake/funding must be checked.

### Healthcare

- TP is a compulsory-insurance category, but state-paid contributions for each adult are not guaranteed for the whole TP period.
- Not all services are free; a universal provider-proof checklist is not established. Qualify post-emergency costs, medicines and dental care.

### Housing

- Program 3 runs through 4 Mar 2027, but placement is not guaranteed. Program 2 is closed to new claims.
- Current 2026 euro reimbursement rate is not established. Provider reimbursement is not refugee rent allowance; no universal private-rental deposit cap was established.

### Education

- School access does not guarantee a chosen school. Ukrainian online study is not an automatic substitute for Bulgarian compulsory attendance.
- Language support availability/hours are not guaranteed. NACID recognition is not university admission or credit transfer; the TP state-university route is not a universal tuition waiver.

### Financial and Social Assistance

- TP enables application for general assistance, but the award is individual and means-tested. Family-benefit eligibility has statutory/case-law ambiguity; do not promise it automatically.
- NSSI insurance benefits and disability assessment are separate. Housing/provider support is not cash social assistance.
