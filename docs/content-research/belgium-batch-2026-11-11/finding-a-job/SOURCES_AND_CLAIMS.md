# Belgium — Finding a Job: Sources and Claims

**Research checked:** 11 November 2026
**Repository baseline:** `28e9ead4d3b7e72a72f6eff70e63729643c29f3b`
**Aspects:** `employment-vacancies`, `employment-institutions`, `employment-assistance-conditions`
**Overall verdict:** **READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS**

This is a research ledger for “Finding a job in Belgium under temporary protection,” not public article copy or legal advice. Belgium has four regional public employment services; there is no single federal jobseeker-registration authority. Vacancy browsing and formal registration are separate. Evidence is limited to official Belgian and EU sources. A source’s inclusion does not make every service or programme universally available. No content draft, catalog, comparison, app, test, localization, or deployment change is in scope.

## Findings at a glance

- The public employment authority follows where a person resides: VDAB in Flanders, Actiris in Brussels-Capital, Le Forem in French-speaking Wallonia, and Arbeitsamt (ADG) in the German-speaking Community. Cross-region job search is possible; for formal registration, the home-region service is the primary route. (S01–S04, S46)
- Each service has a public vacancy portal. EURES offers cross-border search and aggregates vacancies supplied by public employment services, but its coverage is not all Belgian jobs. A public vacancy search, creating a portal account, publishing a profile, and formally registering as a jobseeker are different actions. (S05–S09, S47–S49)
- The clearest current TP-specific directions are regional and not identical: Flanders tells temporary displaced people to register with VDAB; ADG offers a voluntary Ukraine-specific registration route; Forem explicitly accepts a valid Belgian A card for foreign residents in French-speaking Wallonia (and enumerates specific Annex 15 situations). Actiris currently applies its general valid-stay-document/work-authorization criteria; its current material reviewed does not name TP documents individually. (S10–S14, S20–S21)
- No current official source reviewed establishes that a pending TP application, by itself, permits formal jobseeker registration or access to all employment services. Do not infer jobseeker registration, training eligibility, or unemployment benefits from work authorization alone. (S10–S14, S50–S52)
- When there is no e-ID/login, each region has a human/manual alternative, though the required documents and appointment practices differ. The sources do not establish a universal rule for people without a permanent/registered address or in collective/private accommodation. Contact the employment service for the actual place of residence with available residence/status documents; do not promise that a person either can or cannot register. (S10–S14, S20–S21)
- Adviser support, language instruction, vocational training, coaching, and workplace experience are conditional, region-specific services. Benefits and eligibility are not automatic. Major current/legacy boundaries include Brussels Activa ending for new hires on 15 July 2026, Wallonia’s Job Plus replacing older incentives from 1 July 2026, and VDAB ending new employer-started job/language coaching from 1 January 2026. (S26–S39)

**Practical routing for accommodation/address uncertainty:** public vacancy portals can be consulted before registration. For formal registration, contact the employment service responsible for the person’s actual place of stay and ask which proof of residence/status it accepts; explain if living in collective, emergency or private-hosted accommodation, or awaiting a municipal address check. These sources do not support either a categorical denial or a guaranteed workaround for a person without a registered address. OCMW appears as a Flemish information/referral route in the TP context, not as the employment-registration authority. (S01, S03–S04, S10, S19–S21, S33–S35)

## 6. Employer support and unemployment boundary

Employer incentives are paid to or claimed by employers, not a general cash grant to a jobseeker. Wallonia’s Job Plus replaced prior measures from 1 July 2026; eligibility depends on the employer and candidate criteria, including registration duration/profile, and is not automatic because a person has TP. Brussels Activa measures ended for new hires from 15 July 2026, with a limited transition for existing contracts. The Brussels replacement position was not established in sources reviewed. (S49–S51)

Formal jobseeker registration does not itself establish unemployment-benefit entitlement or payment. Regional services register jobseekers and have regional availability/control roles; federal ONEM/RVA governs unemployment rules, with CAPAC/HVW or a union payment body handling payment-file functions. TP status alone is not evidence that ordinary unemployment benefits are payable. Individual benefit eligibility is outside scope. (S33, S38–S40)

## 10. Blocking-gap format

- Availability/eligibility and benefit implications for any individual course, coach, internship or workplace programme.

## 11. Article drafting boundary

**Decision: Draftable with constrained scope.**

### Safe to state

- Belgium has four regionally responsible employment services; identify each by the reader’s place of residence.
- Public vacancy portals can be searched; EURES supports cross-border search and aggregates public-service listings with limited/non-exhaustive coverage.
- Accounts, vacancy profiles and formal jobseeker registration are separate actions.
- Regional services offer adviser support, language/vocational pathways and workplace programmes, but eligibility/admission varies.
- Forem currently lists A cards and specific Annex 15 situations; ADG’s Ukraine registration page says voluntary; Flanders directs TP beneficiaries to VDAB.
- Registering as a jobseeker does not itself establish unemployment benefit entitlement.

### Must qualify

- Residence/domicile/address rules, which exact TP document to present, local appointment route, National Register/BIS number, languages/interpreter, course costs/availability, and cross-region secondary registration.
- Actiris and ADG TP-document acceptance and any federal attestation before municipal card; no universal countrywide TP document rule is supported.
- English-language vacancy search and EURES coverage; apply according to each listing, and vacancy text may remain in its source language.
- Employer incentives are employer-side, targeted and time-sensitive; reference only the current regional status checked on the article date.

### Do not assert

- Every TP holder or pending applicant is formally registrable in every region, or that work permission alone proves jobseeker-registration eligibility.
- A federal TP attestation or generic Annex 15 is accepted everywhere; no e-ID or permanent address categorically prevents registration; or a specific workaround is guaranteed.
- A Ukrainian/Russian-speaking adviser/interpreter, automatic free training/place, all-job vacancy coverage, guaranteed recognition, or any unemployment benefit based solely on TP or registration.

## 12. Scope limits

This ledger does not determine exact work-right start, salary, contract terms, working time, dismissal, employer notification duties, profession-specific licensing, social-assistance amounts, or individual unemployment-benefit entitlement. Those boundaries should remain separate topics.
