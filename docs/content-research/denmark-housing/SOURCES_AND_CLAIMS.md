## Important limitations and unresolved questions

- S02, the Immigration Service page for people granted permits, was last updated on 18 August 2022. The 2026 municipal quota regulation supports continued allocation but does not refresh every accommodation and move detail. Recheck these passages with current Immigration Service guidance before publication.
- S04 is a useful official FAQ and directly addresses Ukrainians, but no single update date is shown. The same page contains historical amounts. No such figures are carried into the drafts. Recheck current lodging conditions, housing benefit treatment and the terminology for applicants before publication.
- A housing-benefit application and a deposit loan are separate routes. The drafts do not promise that a person will qualify for either. Udbetaling Danmark decides housing benefit; the municipality processes relevant deposit-loan applications.
- The sources do not establish a universal type, standard, cost or duration of municipal accommodation. Local service availability and individual arrangements must be confirmed with the assigned/current municipality.
- The address-change deadline is supported, but the reviewed sources do not comprehensively explain how changing municipality affects every Special Act integration arrangement. Contact the current municipality before moving if it assigned your residence or manages an integration programme.
- The national complaints-board guidance is in Danish. The Copenhagen English page is city-specific and should not be generalized nationally. Local municipality pages should be checked for current forms, fees, language support and which board handles a particular tenancy.
- We did not establish that any specific landlord practice is unlawful in a reader’s case. The drafts point to dispute bodies rather than make case-specific legal assessments.

## Questions for human review before publication

1. Does the wording on municipal lodging for applicants reflect current practice and law across municipalities, given that SIRI describes it as a local option and its FAQ has no page-level update date?
2. Should the article retain the cautious explanation that Special Act permit holders may apply for housing benefit if they meet the usual conditions, based on SIRI’s FAQ and the housing-support statute, or should Udbetaling Danmark’s individual assessment be emphasized more prominently?
3. Which current municipal deposit-loan rules and property types should be linked for a reader with a Special Act permit? The statutory scheme is explicit, but application details vary by the housing and case.
4. Has the Immigration Service updated its 2022 guidance on assigned municipalities, placement requests and later moves? If not, is a direct confirmation needed before those details are published?
5. Which national or municipality-level English-language tenant advice resource should be recommended beyond the formal complaints boards?

## Publication readiness

The three language drafts are suitable for **human content review**, not publication. They separate applicants from permit holders, distinguish housing benefit from municipal accommodation and case-specific municipal help, and omit unsupported guarantees and legal conclusions. Before catalog integration, recheck the dated/undated sources identified above, review all three translations, verify local help links, and obtain human editorial acceptance.
