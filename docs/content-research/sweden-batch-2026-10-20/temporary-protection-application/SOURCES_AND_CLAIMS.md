## Conflicts / stale-source notes

- EU scheme end date and Swedish permit validity concern separate scopes; Sweden distinguishes decision cohorts.
- The older extension page is for the 2026 cycle. September 2026 guidance anticipated an early-2027 opportunity, not yet open in reviewed material.
- EU military rules and Swedish administrative instructions differ in scope. Swedish guidance does not settle repeat/lapsed cases or all age/document situations.
- The Tax Agency page retains a cutoff tied to the previous EU endpoint; current law has a 12-month plus six-more-month test.
- Swedish treatment of every EU third-country/stateless cohort remains unclear.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

Recheck the 2027 extension operation, military edge cases, registration guidance and Sweden-specific third-country/stateless cohorts. Do not equate EU duration with personal permit/card validity, registration with status, or a pending application with a granted permit/work authorization. Do not import Czech evidence rules.
