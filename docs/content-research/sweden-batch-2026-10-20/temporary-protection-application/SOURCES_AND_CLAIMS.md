## Conflicts / stale-source notes

- EU scheme end date and Swedish permit validity concern separate scopes; Sweden distinguishes decision cohorts.
- The older extension page is for the 2026 cycle. The 10 September 2026 Agency notice still anticipates an early-2027 opportunity; the current application page and reviewed official guidance do not provide exact opening dates or booking instructions. The next-cycle operational window therefore remains pending.
- EU military rules and Swedish administrative instructions differ in scope. Swedish guidance does not settle repeat/lapsed cases or all age/document situations.
- Current Tax Agency guidance retains the 12-month plus further-six-month test and now says first permits granted on or after 5 September 2026 will not normally satisfy the expected-stay test before 4 March 2028; it also gives a four-week filing window and a child exception. The statutory test remains the controlling wording.
- The 22 December 2023 rule is about lawful presence in Sweden for people in the specified cohorts, not lawful presence in Ukraine. The article and `protection-scheme` comparison text were corrected to match the Agency's current wording and the current Aliens Ordinance.
- Swedish treatment of every EU third-country/stateless cohort remains unclear.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

Focused source refresh completed 27 October 2026. The existing 22 December 2023 location error was corrected in EN/UK/RU; the 2027 operational window remains unannounced. Do not equate EU duration with personal permit/card validity, registration with status, or a pending application with a granted permit/work authorization. Do not import Czech evidence rules.
