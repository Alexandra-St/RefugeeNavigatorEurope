Working title: **Residence Options Beyond Temporary Protection in Sweden**. Category: temporary-protection. “Beyond” means alternative or parallel routes, not only after temporary protection ends.

| SE-TP02-04 | A person cannot hold two permits simultaneously; after a qualifying permit is granted, the TPD permit may be revoked at the person's request (RES-S01, RES-S02, §§3–4). | Supported | Do not advise relinquishing TPD before grant. |
| SE-TP02-05 | A narrow exemption may allow work without a separate work permit while certain covered work applications are pending (RES-S03, 6 kap. 2 c §). | Conditional | Does not itself grant residence; not universal—confirm application and conditions. |
| SE-TP02-06 | Grant of an alternative permit may change accommodation, allowances and support; some unregistered applicants lose LMA support and must arrange housing/finances (RES-S01). | Conditional | Effects depend on circumstances; no individual benefit result. |
| SE-TP02-07 | Swedish permanent residence is connected to specified permit routes and requirements, not automatic after TPD time alone (RES-S04). | Supported | Do not promise PR after a fixed time under TPD. |

## Conflicts / stale-source notes

Focused official-source refresh completed 27 October 2026. The 2026 special law and current Agency guidance provide a narrow in-country pathway, qualifying older general “apply before entry” wording only for listed TPD-holder cases. The work exemption while an application is pending is limited to applications listed in the special law and does not itself confer residence. The abolished spårbyte route concerned asylum, not the TPD-specific law. July 2026 guidance removes PR for asylum-related permits but does not explicitly settle every TPD transition category. The current EU Directive excludes current TPD beneficiaries/applicants from its scope; neither that exclusion nor Swedish Agency guidance directly resolves how prior TPD time is counted after transition. Higher-education and derivative-family routes in the special law do not establish general routes for all holders.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

Recheck the statute and current permit requirements. Do not imply ordinary criteria are waived or filing guarantees approval. Explain possible support/housing effects. Do not promise PR, resolve EU long-term-residence time-counting by inference, or advise relinquishing TPD before another permit is granted.
