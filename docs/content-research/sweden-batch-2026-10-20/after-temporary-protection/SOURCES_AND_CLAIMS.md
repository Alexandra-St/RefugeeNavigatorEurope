## Atomic claims

| Claim ID | Proposition and source IDs | Status | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-TP02-01 | Since 11 June 2026, current Swedish TPD holders may apply in Sweden for listed work, self-employment, research, higher-education or EU Blue Card permits if ordinary criteria are met (RES-S01, RES-S02; RES-S10). | Supported | Specific exception, not a general conversion right. |
| SE-TP02-02 | The statute enumerates covered Aliens Act permits and qualifying derivative family permits (RES-S02, §§3–5; RES-S10). | Conditional | Not a general in-country family/study route for every TPD holder. |
| SE-TP02-03 | If the current TPD permit expires before decision, the covered application must have been made before expiry to benefit from the in-country grant exception (RES-S02, §5). | Conditional | Filing does not guarantee grant or continued residence. |
| SE-TP02-04 | A person cannot hold two permits simultaneously; after a qualifying permit is granted, the TPD permit may be revoked at the person's request (RES-S01, RES-S02, §§3–4). | Supported | Do not advise relinquishing TPD before grant. |
| SE-TP02-05 | A narrow exemption may allow work without a separate work permit while certain covered work applications are pending (RES-S03, 6 kap. 2 c §). | Conditional | Does not itself grant residence; not universal—confirm application and conditions. |
| SE-TP02-06 | Grant of an alternative permit may change accommodation, allowances and support; some unregistered applicants lose LMA support and must arrange housing/finances (RES-S01). | Conditional | Effects depend on circumstances; no individual benefit result. |
| SE-TP02-07 | Swedish permanent residence is connected to specified permit routes and requirements, not automatic after TPD time alone (RES-S04). | Supported | Do not promise PR after a fixed time under TPD. |
| SE-TP02-08 | A work-based PR route is an example requiring four years of qualifying work permits/work within seven years plus other conditions; July 2026 guidance restricts protection-ground PR but does not clearly settle every TPD category (RES-S05, RES-S09). | Conditional | Route-specific; do not make a categorical claim for all TP holders. |
| SE-TP02-09 | Swedish long-term resident status is distinct and has a five-year continuous qualifying-residence and support framework (RES-S06). | Conditional | Do not assume every stay type counts or equate it to Swedish PR. |
| SE-TP02-10 | A person granted Swedish long-term resident status receives a five-year residence permit; Agency guidance says this status does not later confer PR on that basis (RES-S06). | Supported | Distinguish status from permit document. |
| SE-TP02-11 | EU Directive 2003/109 excludes current TP beneficiaries/applicants; reviewed sources do not say whether earlier TP time counts after transition (RES-S07, Art. 3(2)(b); RES-S06). | Unresolved | Do not claim full, partial or zero credit for prior TP time. |
| SE-TP02-12 | Asylum-related spårbyte was abolished from 1 April 2025 and is not the 2026 TPD transition route (RES-S08; RES-S01, RES-S02). | Supported | Do not present it as available to a TP holder. |

## Conflicts / stale-source notes

The 2026 special law and current Agency guidance provide a narrow in-country pathway, qualifying older general “apply before entry” wording only for listed TPD-holder cases. The abolished spårbyte route concerned asylum, not the TPD-specific law. July 2026 PR guidance does not clearly settle every TPD category. Prior TPD-time counting toward EU long-term residence remains unstated. Higher-education and derivative-family routes in the special law do not establish general routes for all holders.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

Recheck the statute and current permit requirements. Do not imply ordinary criteria are waived or filing guarantees approval. Explain possible support/housing effects. Do not promise PR, resolve EU long-term-residence time-counting by inference, or advise relinquishing TPD before another permit is granted.
