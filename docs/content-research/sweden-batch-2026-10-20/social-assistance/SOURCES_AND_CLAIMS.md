# SE-SOC-01 — Sources and Claims

Research date: **28 October 2026 (UTC)**. Research-only evidence matrix; not article copy, individual benefits advice, legal certification, or publication approval. All nine listed owned official sources were reopened and reviewed on this date. Social-assistance, reception support and social-insurance rules are separate systems.

## Scope

Working title: **Financial and Social Assistance in Sweden**. Category: social-assistance. Focus: Migration Agency financial aid during temporary protection; municipal economic assistance; Arbetsförmedlingen establishment support and related compensation; social-insurance boundaries; child-related benefits. No individual entitlement is determined. Amounts are omitted because they are time-sensitive and depend on household/administrative conditions.

### SOC-S05 — Social Services Act (2025:400)
- Authority/title: Swedish Parliament, Socialtjänstlag (2025:400), current consolidated text.
- URL: https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/socialtjanstlag-2025400_sfs-2025-400/
- Current text reopened **28 October 2026**; Act effective from **1 July 2025**. Section 10:2 currently excludes people covered by the Reception Act from Chapter 12 §1 economic assistance through **1 January 2027**. From **2 January 2027**, SFS 2026:1552 adds a narrow exception where the prerequisites in Chapter 12 §2a, second paragraph, are met. - Relevant sections: Chapter 10 §2; Chapter 12 §§1–3; enacted transition/amendment provisions.
- Role: current exclusion for people covered by Reception Act from statutory Chapter 12 §1 assistance; individual economic-needs test and the narrow enacted dental-care exception effective 2 January 2027; claims SE-SOC01-08–09.

## Conflicts / stale-source notes

- The Migration Agency’s aid page uses broad wording that registered people may be entitled to the same welfare-system support as other residents. This is not a benefit-by-benefit decision and must not override the accepted, current Chapter 5 §3 Social Insurance Code distinction. More specific law and agency rules control each programme.
- Socialstyrelsen’s Ukraine-specific page gives a date-based child/housing allowance boundary: registration from 1 November 2024 does not confer those benefits; earlier registration may permit eligibility. This is consistent with the accepted statutory exclusion framework, not a general benefit entitlement.
- The 2026 Reception Act entered into force **1 October 2026**. Its new address/territorial and attendance rules must not be collapsed: the Migration Agency expressly says TPD holders from Ukraine are exempt from geographical restrictions and attendance checks, while the Act still requires presence in Sweden and address notification for daily allowance/special grant.
- The Social Services Act currently bars Chapter 12 §1 economic assistance for people covered by the Reception Act. SFS 2026:1552 takes effect **2 January 2027** and adds an exception by reference to Chapter 12 §2a, second paragraph, concerning dental care that cannot be postponed for people covered by §5 of Act 2013:407. This is not a general benefit entitlement; its application to an individual must not be inferred. Recheck the exact law and operational guidance before publication after that date; do not present the future exception as current.
- Migration Agency instructions issued before 1 October 2026 about reapplying by 30 September are expired. Do not carry that deadline into current reader-facing content.
- No material change to the accepted Phase 17.1B Chapter 5 §3 distinction was identified in the current consolidated Social Insurance Code; the new work concerns how not to conflate this with reception aid, municipal assistance or work-based insurance.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

- Do not describe TPD as an automatic cash allowance. Separate Migration Agency aid, municipality assistance, establishment compensation and Försäkringskassan benefits.
- Do not imply the municipal-assistance route is open to a person still covered by the Reception Act; current Social Services Act Chapter 10 §2 bars a right under Chapter 12 §1. Recheck the 2 January 2027 amendment before publication.
- Do not say population registration grants “normal” social-insurance benefits. Preserve the SFB Chapter 5 §3 exclusion and its express exception; describe work-based benefits separately.
- Do not state a universal entitlement to barnbidrag or bostadsbidrag. Keep the 1 November 2024 registration cohort and individual assessment explicit.
- Do not generalize asylum-only residence, attendance or work restrictions to TPD holders. Apply the specific TPD exceptions in the new Reception Act.
- The reviewed sources did not establish a current nationwide additional Ukraine-specific payment beyond the identified statutory support systems. Do not claim none exists, and do not list historical support as current without separate evidence.

Keep Migration Agency reception aid distinct from municipal assistance; municipal economic assistance distinct from residence-based social insurance; establishment programme distinct from ordinary jobseeker registration; establishment compensation dependent on programme participation; and work-based benefits distinct from residence-based benefits. Folkbokföring ends Reception Act financial aid but does not automatically establish rights in every other system.
