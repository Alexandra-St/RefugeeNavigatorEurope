## Focused refresh — Housing

**READY FOR HUMAN ACCEPTANCE** — reopened the article’s six existing owned official sources covering Migration Agency accommodation and Reception Act guidance, tenant rights, second-hand apartment rental, private-person rental, and Swedish Courts dispute navigation. The current Reception Act (2026:999), in force from 1 October 2026, resolves an important gap: Chapter 3 §§1 and 4 provide a right to a reception-accommodation place for a person covered by the Act and require the Migration Agency to offer a place to a person with temporary protection or assign a municipality to arrange accommodation. Chapter 1 §§2–3 and 8–10 set the coverage boundary; this does not apply after the relevant statutory endpoint, including population registration for a permit holder. The Migration Agency’s page confirms operational constraints: no choice of location, possible placement in another municipality, and an offer may take up to a month; it does not promise a fixed timeline. The article and `housing-initial` Draft fact were corrected and now cite the Act directly; private-rental claims remained supported by the current official guidance and were not changed. Translations remain `NeedsReview`, all three linked facts remain `Draft`, and Sweden remains hidden in Production.
