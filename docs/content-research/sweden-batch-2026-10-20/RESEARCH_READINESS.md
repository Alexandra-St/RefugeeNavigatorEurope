## Focused refresh — Healthcare

**READY FOR HUMAN ACCEPTANCE** — reopened the article’s six owned official sources: Socialstyrelsen and the Migration Agency confirm the registered/unregistered healthcare distinction, child entitlement, adult access to care that cannot be postponed and specified additional services, and regional fee/clinical-assessment caveats; 1177 and SOS Alarm confirm health advice/navigation versus emergency use of 112. The current Act 2024:691 and Skatteverket guidance still establish the first 12 months’ bar followed by the further-six-month expected-stay test, Skatteverket’s individual decision, the four-week notification deadline, and the general identity-check appointment. Skatteverket’s current page says first permits granted on/after 5 September 2026 are not normally registrable under the current 4 March 2028 protection endpoint, subject to the stated child exception. The general processing page gives about six weeks to assignment for a move-to-Sweden case, not a decision deadline or TPD-specific guarantee. The English Försäkringskassan summary still says “less than two years” in an ambiguous social-insurance context; it is not used as the population-registration rule, and this article does not make a benefit-eligibility claim. Article, translations and three linked facts were not changed; translations remain `NeedsReview`, facts remain `Draft`, and Sweden remains hidden in Production. Recheck the cohort-specific cutoff if the EU endpoint changes before publication. Three other topics still require focused source refresh.
