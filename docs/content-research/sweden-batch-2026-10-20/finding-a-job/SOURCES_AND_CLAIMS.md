## Scope

Working title: **Finding a Job in Sweden**. Category: employment. Focus: official vacancy search, access to Arbetsförmedlingen, conditional employment support, counselling, language/retraining navigation, and qualification recognition. No promise of placement, benefits, free courses, or automatic recognition.

## Atomic claims

| Claim ID | Proposition and sources | Status | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-EMP01-01 | Platsbanken is Arbetsförmedlingen’s vacancy-search service; its public job-search pages also link to skills profiles and job-search guidance (JOB-S01–03). | Supported | Vacancy listings and language vary; a listing is not a job offer or placement. |
| SE-EMP01-02 | A person can register for help through a National Government Service Centre; online registration described by Arbetsförmedlingen requires a personal identity number and BankID. Its Ukraine guidance says an interpreter is available when interacting with the service (JOB-S01, JOB-S07). | Conditional | Follow current instructions for the person’s identification and situation; do not imply every digital service is accessible without BankID. |
| SE-EMP01-03 | After registration, Arbetsförmedlingen may offer a planning meeting to review skills, job goals and possible support; certificates and transcripts may be useful (JOB-S01, JOB-S04, JOB-S07). | Conditional | The meeting is a service step, not a promise of a job or programme place. |
| SE-EMP01-04 | For people covered by its Ukraine guidance, available support depends in part on time in Sweden, population-registration circumstances and the requirements of each support form; compensation described on that page requires programme participation and activity (JOB-S01). | Conditional | Do not infer benefit or support eligibility from temporary protection alone. |
| SE-EMP01-05 | The introduction programme may include Swedish study, adult education, skills validation, civic orientation, work experience and job-search support, subject to programme requirements (JOB-S01). | Conditional | No universal entitlement to admission, a free course, compensation or a particular activity. |
| SE-EMP01-06 | UHR can evaluate certain completed foreign qualifications; regulated professions may require a separate decision from the competent professional authority (JOB-S02, JOB-S04–06). | Conditional | Evaluation is not automatic recognition, does not itself authorize a regulated profession, and is not required for every job. |
| SE-EMP01-07 | A person can ask Arbetsförmedlingen about suitable support, but the agency’s guidance makes support conditional and does not promise placement (JOB-S01, JOB-S03). | Supported | Do not promise employment, unemployment benefit, training or a particular result. |

## Conflicts / source limitations

Arbetsförmedlingen’s Ukraine page is the relevant agency route for this audience. General job-search pages include tools whose content or language can change. UHR qualification evaluation and authorization for a regulated profession are separate processes. No individual eligibility or support result is determined here.
