# SE-HOUSING-01 — Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Research only; not article copy, individualized housing or tenancy advice, legal certification, or publication approval. Sources were opened and relevant passages reviewed on the research date; exact check times were not recorded. Accommodation options depend on registration and individual circumstances.

## Scope

Working title: **Finding Accommodation in Sweden**. Category: housing. Focus: accommodation navigation for temporary-protection permit holders, own accommodation versus a possible Swedish Migration Agency offer, address notification, basic private-rental distinctions and official routes for disputes. No entitlement to municipal housing, indefinite state accommodation or rent reimbursement is asserted.

## Atomic claims

| SE-HOUSING01-01 | A person covered by the Migration Agency’s temporary-protection accommodation guidance may arrange accommodation independently or ask about an offered Agency accommodation; if population-registered, the person is responsible for arranging their own accommodation (HOUSE-S01). | Conditional | An offer is not guaranteed housing or a right to a chosen location. |
| SE-HOUSING01-02 | A person arranging their own accommodation pays its costs and must notify the Migration Agency of their address using its current procedure (HOUSE-S01). | Supported | Do not infer a rent subsidy or reimbursement. |

| SE-HOUSING01-05 | Private renting requires identifying the type of arrangement: renting an apartment second-hand and renting a home from a private individual are treated under different rules and guidance (HOUSE-S03–05). | Supported | Do not apply one tenancy model’s rent or permission rules to every rental. |

## Conflicts / source limitations

The Migration Agency page describes a possible accommodation offer, not a universal entitlement or a guaranteed timeline. Rental law differs by tenancy type; Consumer Agency pages are navigation information and do not provide individual legal advice. The October 2026 Reception Act guidance concerns its specified reception-system rules and expressly distinguishes people with TPD.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

Recheck current Agency accommodation procedures and the applicable private-rental rules before publication. Do not promise municipality housing, a specific region/facility, indefinite state accommodation, a fixed waiting time beyond the Agency’s qualified estimate, or rent reimbursement. Do not generalize tenancy rules across rental types.
