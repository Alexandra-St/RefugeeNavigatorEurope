# SE-HOUSING-01 — Sources and Claims

Focused refresh: **28 October 2026 (UTC)**. Research only; not article copy, individualized housing or tenancy advice, legal certification, or publication approval. The official accommodation guidance, new Reception Act, and owned tenancy/dispute sources below were reopened. Accommodation options depend on whether a person remains covered by the Act and on individual circumstances.

## Scope

Working title: **Finding Accommodation in Sweden**. Category: housing. Focus: accommodation navigation for people covered by the Reception Act, own accommodation versus reception accommodation, address notification, basic private-rental distinctions and official routes for disputes. No entitlement to ordinary municipal housing, indefinite state accommodation or rent reimbursement is asserted.

## Conflicts / source limitations

The Migration Agency’s operational page describes an offer dependent on available places and does not state the statutory entitlement. The current Reception Act separately establishes a right to a place for people covered by the Act and requires the Agency to offer a place to a person with temporary protection or assign a municipality. This does not guarantee a particular location, type of accommodation, or waiting time. Rental law differs by tenancy type; Consumer Agency pages are navigation information and do not provide individual legal advice. The Migration Agency confirms that TPD holders from Ukraine are not subject to the specified geographical restriction or attendance checks.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

The new Act’s conditional right and agency duty must be stated alongside the statutory coverage boundary. Do not promise a chosen municipality, a specific region/facility or type of housing, a fixed waiting time, indefinite state accommodation, or rent reimbursement. Do not generalize tenancy rules across rental types.

## Article-safety boundary

Do not generalize the Act’s accommodation right to people outside its coverage, promise a chosen place or municipality, a particular accommodation type or waiting time, indefinite state accommodation, municipal housing outside the Act, or rent support; do not apply one private-rental regime to every tenancy.
