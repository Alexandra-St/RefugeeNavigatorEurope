# SE-EDU-01 — Sources and Claims

Research date: **20 October 2026 (UTC)**. Research-only evidence matrix; not article copy, individual education advice, legal certification, or publication approval. Official pages were opened and reviewed on the research date; exact check times were not recorded. Where a page does not display an update date, that is stated below.

## Scope

Working title: **Education and Swedish-Language Learning in Sweden**. Category: education. Scope: children’s school access and enrolment, language support, adult Swedish/adult education, higher-education admission and foreign-qualification evaluation. It is not a complete guide to the Swedish education system, student finance, local school capacity, individual admission, or professional licensing.

## Atomic claims

| Claim ID | Proposition and sources | Status | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-EDU01-01 | A TPD child who is or should be population-registered has the same education rights and compulsory-school rules as other children in that position; actual registration or receipt of a personal identity number is not required first (EDU-S01, EDU-S05). | Supported | Eligibility to be registered is the relevant distinction; do not equate it with a completed registration record. |
| SE-EDU01-02 | A TPD child who is not and should not be population-registered has education rights comparable to asylum-seeking children but is not subject to compulsory-school duty; access to upper-secondary education is limited by the rule that studies must begin before age 18 (EDU-S01, EDU-S05). | Conditional | Keep the distinction between right and duty adjacent. Preserve the age-at-start condition; do not imply ordinary national-program eligibility without its entry requirements. |
| SE-EDU01-03 | Preschool-class/compulsory-school choices are subject to the statutory free-school-choice framework, but this does not guarantee a place at a particular school or programme (EDU-S01). | Conditional | Availability, placement and applicable admission rules remain relevant. |
| SE-EDU01-04 | The home municipality is responsible for arranging education for children and young people who have an education right and want it; a personal identity number or coordination number is not a prerequisite to a school placement (EDU-S01, EDU-S02). | Supported | Local contact route and practical intake requirements must be checked with the responsible municipality. |
| SE-EDU01-05 | For an unregistered/not-to-be-registered TPD child, school reception should occur as soon as suitable in light of personal circumstances and should occur no later than one month after arrival under the guidance cited by Skolverket (EDU-S01). | Conditional | This is not a guarantee of a chosen school, immediate first day, or particular class. Do not generalize the one-month statement beyond the described group. |
| SE-EDU01-06 | A newly arrived pupil’s prior knowledge is normally assessed unless assessment is clearly unnecessary; the rector is responsible and the results inform grade and teaching-group placement (EDU-S02). | Supported | Assessment is for educational planning, not automatic grade equivalency or a guaranteed placement. |
| SE-EDU01-07 | In compulsory school, the placement decision is to be made within two months of reception; for language-introduction programme, an individual study plan is prepared within two months, subject to the distinct statutory triggers described by Skolverket (EDU-S02). | Conditional | State the applicable start event and school form. Do not present a single universal two-month deadline for every education service. |
| SE-EDU01-08 | Preparatory classes or adjusted teaching may be considered as part of an individual educational assessment; a preparatory class is not a universal required route for every newly arrived pupil (EDU-S02). | Conditional | School-head/rector decisions and individual needs govern; avoid promising a particular support model. |
| SE-EDU01-09 | A pupil may receive study guidance in their mother tongue when needed; ordinary mother-tongue teaching has additional conditions, and the school head decides. In compulsory school these include daily home-language use and basic language knowledge for a non-minority language; provision is also constrained by organizer rules (EDU-S02, EDU-S03). | Conditional | Do not promise identical language hours or automatic Ukrainian/Russian classes. Do not conflate study guidance with mother-tongue subject teaching. |
| SE-EDU01-10 | SFI is a municipal adult-education route for eligible people who meet the Swedish-language and residence conditions. TPD alone does not establish an SFI right: a TPD holder who is not/should not be registered lacks that entitlement, although a municipality may allow participation under conditions from the second half-year of the year they turn 18; eligible registered-cohort cases remain subject to ordinary criteria (EDU-S01, EDU-S04, EDU-S05). | Conditional | Do not infer eligibility from the permit alone or actual folkbokföring alone. Municipality must offer SFI to those legally entitled. Check 2027 course changes before publication. |
| SE-EDU01-11 | TPD holders who are not/should not be population-registered do not have a right to other komvux education under the described rule, though a municipality may permit certain participation; registration-related access remains subject to the specific adult-education eligibility and admission criteria (EDU-S01, EDU-S04, EDU-S05). | Conditional | Do not promise a course place or treat the school-law concept “bosatt” as the social-insurance test. |
| SE-EDU01-12 | UniversityAdmissions/Antagning provide the national application routes; applicants must document completed qualifications and meet general and programme-specific entry and language requirements. Assessment of eligibility occurs through the application review, not as a guaranteed pre-approval (EDU-S06, EDU-S07). | Conditional | Programme language determines route; each course and institution can have specific requirements and deadlines. |
| SE-EDU01-13 | Under the fee rules effective 1 October 2026, a temporary Swedish residence permit for a reason other than studies generally exempts its holder from application and tuition fees, subject to statutory exceptions; the applicant may need to document the permit with University Admissions (EDU-S08, EDU-S09). | Conditional | Apply the current permit category and exception precisely; do not describe this as a Ukraine-specific or automatic concession. Recheck law and proof requirements at application time. |
| SE-EDU01-14 | UHR can assess completed foreign qualifications and issue a statement describing their comparison with Swedish education; the statement is not a Swedish qualification and does not itself grant professional authorization or university admission (EDU-S10). | Supported | Regulated-profession licensing, course-credit transfer and admissions decisions are separate processes. |

## Conflicts / stale-source notes

- Skolverket’s current Ukraine-specific guidance is more precise than a generic assumption that school rights and compulsory duty are identical: registration eligibility controls that distinction. Use its dated guidance and the cited Education Act provisions.
- The SFI page says the information reflects rules effective **1 January 2026** and separately flags new course structure effective **1 January 2027**. Any future article release must recheck the operational SFI rules and transition provisions.
- The fee rule was materially amended effective **1 October 2026**. Do not reuse older emergency-period Ukraine statements; use the current ordinance and verify permit proof/exception handling before publication.
- No material contradiction with the accepted Phase 17.1A or 17.1B claims was identified. Education rights, residence registration, healthcare, and social-insurance status remain separate legal domains.

## Publication blockers / boundaries

- Do not state that every child with TPD has compulsory-school duty; state the registered/should-be-registered distinction.
- Do not promise a specific school, programme, immediate placement, language-support hours, or a particular preparatory class.
- Do not say a TPD permit alone creates an SFI or komvux entitlement. Distinguish SFI from employment-programme language activities.
- Do not promise upper-secondary admission, university admission, or fee exemption without the applicable permit/entry/documentation conditions.
- Do not present UHR evaluation as a Swedish diploma, professional licence, or admissions decision.
- Student finance (CSN), specific university-level admissions decisions, and individual municipal capacity are outside this matrix.

## Article-safety boundary

Keep “education right” distinct from “compulsory-school duty”; school reception distinct from a chosen-school guarantee; language support distinct from universal hours; SFI distinct from non-formal or employment-programme Swedish; folkbokföring distinct from immigration status and from social-insurance “bosatt”; UHR qualification evaluation distinct from licensing/admission; and fee exemption distinct from admission. Recheck SFI changes, fee law, admission instructions and source dates before drafting or publication.
