# Austria — Education

**Research date / LastCheckedAt:** 2026-11-07
**Scope:** `education-school-enrolment`, `education-pupil-language`, `education-higher-admission`. Official-source research only; no article drafting, catalog integration, application or test changes.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Current primary sources establish the main national boundaries. Children who reside in Austria on a lasting basis are subject to the nine-year compulsory-school rule regardless of citizenship or residence status; a Ukrainian online school is not an automatic substitute for physical Austrian attendance. Enrollment and allocation are handled locally, and a requested campus is not guaranteed. German support was materially changed for 2026/27: schools can use the standard `Deutschförderklassen` / `Deutschförderkurse` model or an autonomous site plan. Higher-education admission remains institution- and programme-specific. Current law grants a limited tuition-fee waiver to Ukrainian citizens at public universities and teacher-education universities for winter semester 2026/27; temporary-protection status alone is not the test, and the rule does not cover every higher-education provider.

The Austrian temporary-protection residence right is established in the separate TP package through 2028-03-04. Ordinary residence registration (`Meldezettel`) and school admission are distinct procedures; the housing package establishes the ordinary registration deadline and signature rules. Neither should be presented as a substitute for contacting the competent school authority.

## Compulsory school, enrolment and placement boundaries

The `Schulpflicht` rule applies to children staying permanently in Austria irrespective of nationality or residence status. It begins on the 1 September following completion of the sixth year of life and lasts nine school years. The 2026 TP baseline does not create an education exemption. The separate `AusBildung bis 18` duty applies after compulsory school to qualifying young people; it is not the same rule.

For a school-age arrival, use the relevant Land Bildungsdirektion contact listed by BMB. The local Bildungsdirektion allocates a compulsory-school place, and there is no right to the specific school selected by a family. Exact steps, capacity response and requested documents are local. A family should submit whatever identity, address and school evidence is available and ask that authority for the applicable next step; do not turn this practical suggestion into a purported universal document checklist. The reviewed sources do not establish an automatic grade conversion from a Ukrainian report card or a universal rule that missing reports block enrollment.

Ordinary kindergarten is distinct from school admission. A free compulsory half-day kindergarten year applies in the final year before starting school; normal kindergarten minimum ages, procedures, capacity, hours and wider fee arrangements vary by Land/municipality. For public compulsory schooling, attendance is tuition-free. Meals, extended care and other incidental services may cost extra; separate schoolbook or transport supports have their own coverage rules and do not form a universal TP package.

The foreign-school rule is narrow: under §13, a non-Austrian child may fulfil schoolpflicht through actual attendance at a school abroad after notifying the Bildungsdirektion before each school year, with annual evidence rules. In VwGH Ra 2021/10/0123, distance learning / homeschooling from a foreign school did not count as attending that foreign school. Separately, the Vienna Bildungsdirektion says a Ukrainian upper-secondary online school leading to Matura can fulfil the post-school `AusBildung bis 18` obligation. This latter rule must never be described as an exception to compulsory attendance in grades 1–9.

## German support and Ukrainian online-study boundaries

The current 2026/27 system has two school-level options: the established standard model or autonomous, school-specific German support. In the standard model, MIKA-D and the applicable rules guide whether a pupil needs a `Deutschförderklasse`, a `Deutschförderkurs` or can follow ordinary instruction. A standard class is semester-based and may be repeated up to four times; the published class/course hour values are model specifications, not a guarantee that an individual receives that exact personal timetable. From 2026/27, many schools use their own site concept, so a single schedule should not be claimed nationwide.

Orientation classes are available for the narrower group with no or very limited prior schooling; entry follows an orientation conversation and decision, and is capped at one semester. Ukrainian learning materials and online courses may supplement Austrian schooling. They do not create automatic dispensation from Schulpflicht. The specifically recognized Ukrainian online-Matura route concerns the distinct post-school training obligation to age 18.

## Recognition and higher-education boundaries

Admission is handled by the chosen university, FH or other provider. TP does not itself confer admission. The 2026 Ukraine credential recommendation is unusually specific: a full secondary certificate with DPA is recommended as meeting the formal school-leaving qualification, with two supplementary examinations; where DPA was waived for wartime graduating cohorts, a positive NMT of at least 100 points is additionally recommended, alongside two supplementary exams. This is an ENIC NARIC / BMFWF recommendation, not a single automatic national admission decision. The target institution still decides the file, programme conditions, language evidence and any entrance examination.

If required school records are unavailable, the 2026 recommendation allows the receiving institution to waive individual documents only when the applicant credibly shows they cannot be obtained or would be disproportionately difficult and the available evidence suffices. This is institution-specific discretion, not a blanket displaced-person exception. Credit transfer for previous university study is a separate recognition request decided by the receiving institution based on course equivalence.

The current tuition exemption is limited by its text: Ukrainian nationality, specified public university or Pädagogische Hochschule, and winter semester 2026/27. TP is not itself the eligibility criterion. FH and private-provider tuition remains provider-specific. The Ernst Mach – Ukraine special scholarship expired on 2026-06-30; the old €715/month award must not be presented as open to new applicants in November 2026. No guaranteed scholarship or dormitory place was found.

## Evidence boundaries

- No single nationwide new-arrival school-enrollment checklist or automatic missing-document substitute was established.

## Critical questions — direct answers

1. **Are TP children subject to Austrian compulsory schooling?** Yes, where they reside in Austria on a lasting basis: regardless of nationality/status, from the September following age six for nine school years (S01–S03).
2. **Can Ukrainian online school replace compulsory attendance?** No, not automatically. Distance learning from a foreign school does not count as foreign-school attendance under §13; a distinct Ukrainian online-Matura rule relates to `AusBildung bis 18`, not Schulpflicht (S01, S05, S22).
3. **How does enrollment generally work?** Contact the Land Bildungsdirektion; BMB lists the regional Ukraine contact points, and the local Bildungsdirektion allocates compulsory-school places (S03–S04).
4. **Is a chosen school guaranteed?** No. BMB expressly says there is no right to the family’s requested school (S04).
5. **What if Ukrainian records are missing?** No uniform school-record waiver/checklist was established; disclose what is missing and ask the local Bildungsdirektion for case-specific placement/document steps (S03–S04).
6. **Is grade placement automatic?** No automatic Ukrainian-grade conversion was established; request an individual decision from the competent school authority (S03–S04).
7. **Is public compulsory schooling tuition-free?** Yes, public compulsory-school attendance is free; that does not make every optional/ancillary service free (S07–S09).
8. **Are meals/transport universally free for TP pupils?** No universal TP entitlement established. Travel assistance is conditional and all-day meal/care charges vary locally (S09–S10).
9. **What German support applies in 2026?** Standard `Deutschförderklassen` / `Deutschförderkurse` or school-autonomous support, with assessment-informed placement (S11–S13).
10. **Is a fixed course or number of hours guaranteed?** No. Standard-model hours and class durations do not create a personal guarantee; autonomous models differ (S11–S13).
11. **Can Ukrainian online/language study be combined with Austrian school?** It can be supplementary; it does not automatically replace schoolpflicht. A child attending Austrian school remains subject to attendance duties (S01, S03, S05).
12. **Does TP guarantee university/FH admission?** No; ordinary provider and programme requirements apply (S15–S18).
13. **Who assesses a Ukrainian school qualification?** The receiving university/provider decides admission; ENIC NARIC’s April 2026 recommendation informs its assessment but is not a separate universal admission guarantee (S15–S17).
14. **Is recognition the same as admission?** No. Qualification equivalence is one part; the provider separately decides programme admission and selection (S15–S17).
15. **Who decides transfer of previous university credits?** The receiving university/college decides whether and how much prior study is equivalent (S18).
16. **Is there a current nationwide tuition waiver for TP holders?** There is a specific waiver for Ukrainian citizens at public universities and Pädagogische Hochschulen for winter semester 2026/27. It is not a TP-status-wide or all-provider waiver (S19–S20).
17. **Are scholarships/dormitories guaranteed?** No. The special Ernst Mach Ukraine scholarship ended 2026-06-30; a student-hall place is not guaranteed (S18, S21).
18. **Are Ukraine-specific higher-education measures active?** The 2026/27 fee waiver is active within its limited statutory scope. Ernst Mach – Ukraine is closed. The 2026 ENIC NARIC qualification recommendation is current guidance, not a financial support scheme (S16, S19, S21).
