## 5. Aggregated drafting caveats

### Temporary Protection

- Use Austrian `vorübergehendes Aufenthaltsrecht` for the legal right and `Ausweis für Vertriebene` for the physical card. The right and card are distinct; the current scheme runs through **4 March 2028**.
- Do not resolve the military-obligation timeline interaction among 30 July 2026, 4 August 2026 and the EU 5 August 2026 threshold. The package leaves the conflict-period application unresolved.
- No universal complete initial application checklist, appointment route or fee was established.

### Residence Options

- The special §41a(7b) RWR+ route and other §4a NAG routes are distinct. The special RWR+ route does not require the §4a waiver; other §4a titles require a conditional waiver effective at grant.
- Special RWR+ filing itself does not establish an independent post-TP stay bridge. A §4a route can bridge lawful stay at grant but does not automatically authorize work.
- EU Blue Card transition is supported with qualification, not by a located Blue-Card-specific Austrian §4a instruction. Do not claim automatic post-TP conversion.

### Work Authorization and Employee Rights

- Current exemption is tied to covered displaced persons holding `Ausweis für Vertriebene`; police registration alone before card issuance was not established as sufficient.
- Self-employment has separate business/professional requirements. No general post-TP pending-work bridge was established.
- Austria has no one universal statutory minimum wage. Applicable `Kollektivvertrag` or other instrument and classification matter.

### Finding a Job

- AMS vacancy browsing is not AMS registration; AMS registration is not work authorization.
- Training, funding and placement depend on assessment and availability and are not guaranteed.
- `Arbeitslosengeld` is separate and insurance-based. Ukrainian insurance periods were not established as automatically credited.

### Healthcare

- Automatic Ukraine-specific compulsory insurance ended **31 May 2025**. TP/card alone no longer establishes active insurance.
- Employment, eligible family co-insurance, self-insurance, unemployment-benefit coverage or Grundversorgung may create coverage depending on circumstances.
- An e-card is not proof of active entitlement or universally free care. Emergency access does not mean all later treatment is free.

### Housing

- Grundversorgung is needs-based and provincial; no organized accommodation bed is guaranteed.
- Current first-arrival information is capacity-dependent and time-sensitive. Federal cost ceilings are not guaranteed beneficiary payments.
- There is no single nationwide Wohnbeihilfe. No universal statutory deposit cap was established; MRG/ABGB scope must be respected.

### Education

- Schulpflicht applies regardless of citizenship/status when the statutory residence conditions are met. Ukrainian online schooling does not automatically replace Austrian compulsory attendance.
- A chosen-school placement is not guaranteed. German support depends on assessment and the school model.
- Admission, qualification recognition and credit transfer are separate. Current tuition exemption is limited by nationality, institution and semester, not a universal TP exemption. `Ernst Mach – Ukraine` ended 30 June 2026.

### Financial and Social Assistance

- Grundversorgung is needs-based and provincially administered. Federal maxima are not guaranteed cash awards; ordinary Sozialhilfe/Mindestsicherung is separate and status/residence-limited.
- From 1 July 2026 Familienbeihilfe rules for displaced Ukrainians changed: retain the current general conditions, Grundversorgung interaction and narrow significant-disability exception. The former employment/AMS-registration extra condition no longer applies.
- Kinderbetreuungsgeld and Wochengeld are separate schemes with their own requirements; neither is an automatic TP payment.

## 6. Cross-package consistency check

The overlapping package statements are compatible for drafting; no contradiction requiring a topic to be marked `Needs source refresh` or `Blocked` was found.

- **TP duration/status:** packages consistently distinguish the temporary-protection right from the physical `Ausweis für Vertriebene` and use 4 March 2028 as the current endpoint. The military transition conflict is explicitly unresolved in TP research and remains unresolved here.
- **Grundversorgung:** Housing, Healthcare and Social Assistance describe it compatibly as needs-based, provincial and not automatic. Health insurance is a possible component for eligible recipients, not proof of automatic accommodation or cash support. Cost ceilings are not individual awards.
- **Employment:** work access is tied to the relevant covered status/card; AMS browsing, registration, work authorization and insurance-based Arbeitslosengeld remain separate. Social Assistance’s income-reporting rule for Grundversorgung does not conflict with those distinctions.
- **Residence pending rights:** Residence research distinguishes lawful stay from work authorization; Employment Rights establishes no general post-TP pending-work bridge. These statements align.
- **Family benefits:** Social Assistance’s 2026 Familienbeihilfe conditions do not conflict with education or healthcare findings. Kinderbetreuungsgeld’s family-benefit prerequisite is kept separate from health-insurance coverage and school access.

Wording can be normalized in future content, but no source package was changed.
