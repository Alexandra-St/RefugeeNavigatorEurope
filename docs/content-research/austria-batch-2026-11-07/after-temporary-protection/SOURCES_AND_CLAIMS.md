# Austria — Residence Options During and After Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-07

**Scope:** exactly `residence-routes`, `residence-conditions`, and `residence-filing`. Research only; individual eligibility and official decisions remain case-specific.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** Austria has a special application-based `Rot-Weiß-Rot – Karte plus` for displaced persons and, since 2026-10-16, a separate conditional-waiver procedure for other NAG titles. The two procedures have different waiver, status and pending-stay consequences. The EU Blue Card transition is supported by a nonbinding Council recommendation and the general Austrian §4a mechanism, not by a Blue-Card-specific Austrian §4a instruction located in this review. No general post-TP pending-work authorization was established. [S01–S08, S13–S16]

## Route and filing boundaries

**Option A — remain under TP:** existing temporary residence and associated work access continue only while the relevant entitlement remains valid. An `Ausweis für Vertriebene` documents that right; it is not treated as an ordinary NAG residence title. [S06–S08, S10–S11]

**Option B — special RWR+:** §41a(7b) and §20e(1)(4) establish the special route. Twelve qualifying months within 24 can be aggregated, including qualifying ASVG employment or GSVG self-employment. An applicant with TP can file in Austria; AMS confirms the insurance history. The Vienna authority says the special route requires no TP waiver and the card is retained. BMI’s older FAQ says the rights coexist; classify that conclusion with qualification because the FAQ predates §4a. While TP remains valid, its work access continues; after TP expiry no separate pending-work bridge is established. Section 21(6) includes the special inland-filing category and filing itself does not extend the permitted stay. [S02–S03, S07–S10]

**Option C — other NAG titles:** §4a permits qualifying TP holders to file in Austria with an express written irrevocable waiver conditional on grant; TP remains until grant or its separate lawful expiry. At grant the waiver takes effect. A timely §4a application gives lawful stay until final decision, subject to the specified exclusions; it is not itself a title or a work authorization. [S06–S07, S20]

**Blue Card:** §42 NAG, §12c and §20d AuslBG remain applicable. EU Directive 2021/1883 excludes TP beneficiaries from its scope; the 2025 Council recommendation expressly contemplates an application during TP for an EU Blue Card without concurrent TP after grant. Reading this with the general Austrian §4a conditional waiver supports in-country transition filing, subject to Blue Card requirements and authority review. This is an inference from official general provisions, not a located Blue-Card-specific Austrian §4a rule. A timely §4a application has its stay bridge; pending work after TP expiry is not established. The Austrian portal describes employer or applicant filing routes and AMS involvement; do not assume a specific §4a filing method without checking the competent authority. [S04, S06, S13–S15, S21–S22]

**Student and business:** a qualifying student may seek `Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Student` through the general §4a mechanism, with conditional waiver, inland filing and the timely-filing stay bridge. The §4a(3) exception means the prior TP period is not deemed settlement by that provision when an `Aufenthaltsbewilligung` is granted. A self-employed key-worker title is a separate route with its own conditions; neither an active business nor TP guarantees it. [S06, S17, S19]

**Family:** TP family eligibility remains separate from ordinary NAG reunification. MA35 identifies special RWR+ and Blue Card holders as possible sponsors for family RWR+, subject to the applicable NAG definition and conditions. A family member holding TP and applying independently through §4a has the waiver and bridge rules of that procedure; do not assume all relatives qualify or share one definition. [S06–S08, S18]

**Daueraufenthalt-EU:** after special RWR+ grant, §41a(7b) deems immediately preceding lawful TP residence settlement and MA35 says that time may count toward five years, subject to other requirements including integration module 2. For other §4a transitions, §4a(3) separately deems immediately preceding lawful TP residence settlement unless an `Aufenthaltsbewilligung` is granted. Do not conflate the two statutory rules or claim a direct automatic TP-to-long-term title. [S03, S05–S06, S12]

## Drafting boundaries for Phase 22 content

- Separate the special RWR+ exception from other §4a applications: no waiver for the former under current MA35 guidance; conditional waiver at grant for the latter. [S06–S08]
- Special RWR+ inland filing does not independently extend the permitted stay; timely §4a applications have their own limited lawful-stay bridge. Neither filing nor the bridge automatically grants a work right after TP expiry. [S02, S06, S10]
- Special RWR+ and TP may coexist while TP is valid, qualified by the age of BMI’s explicit coexistence statement. §4a titles end TP through the conditional waiver at grant. [S06–S08]
- Blue Card transition is supported with qualification by the EU recommendation and general Austrian mechanism; no Blue-Card-specific §4a administrative instruction was identified. Do not claim concurrent Blue Card and TP after grant. [S04, S06, S13–S15]
- A student `Aufenthaltsbewilligung` is expressly outside §4a(3)’s settlement deeming; TP time credit is route-specific. [S03, S06, S12, S17]
- Requirements, family relationships, documents, fees, appointments, and employer procedures depend on the route. Vienna instructions are Vienna-specific. No automatic post-TP conversion is established. [S07–S08, S15, S17–S19]
