# Austria — Employment Rights During Temporary Protection

**Research checked:** 2026-11-07

**Scope:** exactly `work-permission`, `work-employer-duties`, and `work-minimum-pay`. Research only; not an individual legal decision or a comprehensive employment guide. Read with Austria Phase 22.1 and 22.2 research packages.

## Research verdict

**READY FOR CONTENT DRAFTING WITH QUALIFICATIONS.** AuslBG §1(2)(k) excludes displaced persons under AsylG §62 who hold an `Ausweis für Vertriebene` from that Act’s scope. Current AMS employer guidance says no AMS employment permit is required for those cardholders. This is distinct from ordinary employer obligations, including social-insurance registration before work starts. Austrian Integration Fund guidance also describes self-employment after receipt of the card, with separate trade and professional requirements. Registration alone before card issuance is not established as sufficient for the statutory exemption. There is no uniform statutory national minimum wage: applicable collective agreements normally fix binding wage floors, with other mechanisms in relevant cases. No general work bridge after TP ends has been established. [S01–S05, S08–S12]

## Application and evidence boundaries

The Austrian temporary right and the physical card are related but distinct. The right of residence is documented by `Ausweis für Vertriebene`; the current AuslBG exemption specifically refers to displaced persons **who hold the card** (S01–S02). The source set does not establish a general right to start dependent employment on police registration alone while awaiting issuance. It also does not establish a fixed employer copy-retention rule or a separate TP-specific AMS start/end notice; use the actual current status and competent-authority guidance for an individual case. This uncertainty is not a claim that every other reporting duty is absent (S01–S02, S15).

Austria’s Integration Fund states that displaced-person cardholders may become self-employed, subject to trade or professional requirements (S03–S04). These requirements are separate from the dependent-employment exemption. Regulated occupations can require recognition, licences or entry in a professional register. This package does not prescribe the recognition procedure.

An employer ordinarily registers an insurable employee with the health insurer before commencement, normally through ELDA (S05). A written contract may satisfy the employer’s duty to record essential terms; otherwise a Dienstzettel must be provided as required by current law (S06–S07). Social insurance, payroll, labour protections and applicable collective agreements are separate from status verification.

The minimum is not a single Austrian figure. Identify the applicable collective agreement and the employee’s activity, classification, progression and working-time basis; check a relevant tariff or extended agreement where applicable (S08–S13). Actual contractual remuneration may exceed the binding floor. No example amount is published in this research.

## Critical questions — direct answers

1. **Work permit?** No separate AuslBG employment permit for a §62 displaced person holding the card (S01–S02).
2. **Prior AMS approval?** No AMS permit for that exempt cardholder (S02).
3. **Change employer?** No new job-specific immigration permit under this exemption; ordinary professional rules remain (S01–S02).
4. **Self-employment?** ÖIF says possible after receipt of the card; trade and professional requirements remain (S03–S04).
5. **Regulated professions/business?** Recognition, registration, trade authorization, tax and insurance may apply separately (S03–S04).
6. **Employer status checks/notices?** Verify the relevant status/card before relying on the exemption; TP-specific card-copy retention and a separate AMS start/end notice are not established in reviewed official sources (S01–S02, S15).
7. **ÖGK before work?** Yes, the employer generally registers an insurable employee before commencement, normally using ELDA (S05).
8. **Universal statutory national minimum?** No uniform Austrian minimum wage (S08).
9. **How is pay determined?** Normally the applicable collective agreement and actual classification; relevant tariffs/extended agreements can also apply (S08–S13).
10. **Collective wage floors binding?** Yes where applicable, subject to statutory/collective scope and the rules for more favourable terms (S10).
11. **Lower pay solely because of TP?** No TP-specific lower collective scale was established; applicable binding floors remain (S01–S02, S10).
12. **When TP ends?** The status-based exemption cannot simply be assumed to continue; check an independent valid authorization (S01, S16).
13. **Pending residence application after TP ends?** A general work bridge is not established; §4a lawful stay alone does not establish work permission (S16).

## Drafting boundaries for Phase 22 content

- Separate TP work access from ordinary employment law, employer reporting and social insurance.
- Do not assert that registration before card issuance is sufficient for the AuslBG exemption or invent a TP-specific AMS notification or copy-retention period.
- No universal Austrian minimum-wage amount should be published; identify the applicable `Kollektivvertrag` and classification for the actual employee.
- Self-employment and regulated professions have separate trade, tax, insurance, licence or recognition requirements; do not derive them from AuslBG’s dependent-employment exemption.
- While TP remains active, its work access may continue during the special RWR+, §4a or Blue Card application. The §4a lawful-stay bridge is not automatically work authorization; post-TP pending-work rights remain unsupported without an explicit separate basis.
- Do not promise a universal 13th/14th payment, instant work access before card issuance, or a specific gross/net amount without a dated applicable instrument.
