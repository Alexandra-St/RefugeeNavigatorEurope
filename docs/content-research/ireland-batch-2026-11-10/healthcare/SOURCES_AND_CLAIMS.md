# Ireland — Healthcare access, proof, and urgent care

Research checked: 2026-11-10

Baseline: `ecd7933563bbe3642f314732b726131905a9e119`

Scope: exactly `healthcare-access`, `healthcare-proof`, `healthcare-urgent`.

## Research verdict

The legal entitlement boundary, current Ukraine-specific medical-card application route, PPSN's administrative role, and national urgent-care signposting are established. The evidence does **not** establish a uniform, complete document checklist for ordinary HSE/GP registration or for the UKR card's “supporting documents”; nor does it establish that a TP letter or physical IRP is the required proof at every care point. The national HSE emergency guidance does not publish a single consolidated ED charge/exemption page, so this package uses current HSE operational listings for charges and keeps exemptions narrow.

## Required distinctions

- **Immigration permission ≠ healthcare registration.** Valid TP permission creates the statutory entitlement on Irish terms; it does not itself prove an HSE card was issued, a Medical/GP Visit Card application was assessed, or that a named GP is available.
- **PPSN ≠ healthcare entitlement.** It is an HSE identity/record-linking tool and is required for the UKR card application; HSE says care continues without a PPSN using another identification method.
- **Medical Card / GP Visit Card ≠ TP permission.** These are separate HSE schemes/records. The UKR card is an application route for the defined recent-arrival cohort; standard cards use HSE eligibility assessment.
- **Emergency access ≠ all care free of charge.** 112/999 calls are free; ED, Injury Unit and out-of-hours GP charges and exemptions are separate. Public inpatient charges ended for public patients, which does not make every service free.
- **National entitlement ≠ local operational practice.** HSE service listings give specific ED and Injury Unit details, and HSE directs users to local out-of-hours contacts. Do not generalize a named hospital's charge page or local provider availability beyond its scope.
- **TP permission document ≠ a universal proof checklist.** The current HSE UKR application page asks for supporting documents without listing them all; the linked form is dated 2022. Do not make a universal IRP/letter/address-document checklist from this evidence.

## Unresolved / conflicting points

- The current HSE UKR-card page requires PPSN and says to include supporting documents but does not state a complete, current checklist. Its linked application form is marked September 2022. Exact acceptable proof of TP permission, IRP/card requirements, address evidence and missing-document handling are not established.
- No reviewed national HSE page says whether every ordinary GP practice must accept a TP permission document as a registration artifact, or establishes GP capacity/assignment outside the UKR card process.
- HSE public charge guidance is not consolidated in a single current national ED fee/exemption page in the sources reviewed. A current named-hospital listing supports the €100 amount and its listed exemptions, but a full exhaustive national exception list was not independently established here.
- The HSE Injury Unit page's next review date was 29 November 2026, one day after this research date. The HSE ED page's review was overdue. Recheck both if this package is used after their review dates.
