# Ireland — School enrolment, pupil language support and higher education admission

Research checked: 2026-11-10

Baseline: `5e3b07ab06561b34bdcfa66b1463d90d0dc87400`

Scope: exactly `education-school-enrolment`, `education-pupil-language`, `education-higher-admission`.

## Research verdict

The State describes access to primary and post-primary education for children arriving from Ukraine, with a school application and help from Regional Education and Language Teams (REALT). A particular school or a universal documentary checklist is not guaranteed. National EAL staffing guidance explicitly applies to 2026/27, but allocated school teaching hours are not fixed individual pupil hours. For higher education, admissions decisions belong to institutions; NARIC provides comparability advice. The 2026/27 temporary tuition scheme is confirmed for a defined continuing undergraduate cohort and closed to new entrants; the separate maintenance position needs careful cohort-specific verification.

## Required distinctions

- Access to education is distinct from a place in a preferred school; school admission, administrative records and compulsory education have different rules.
- PPSN and immigration documents do not themselves constitute school admission; do not assert a universal missing-document bar.
- Compulsory appropriate education and Ukrainian online schooling are not interchangeable without confirmation of the applicable legal route.
- EAL school allocations and available staffing do not establish fixed individual pupil hours or a dedicated teacher.
- University admission is distinct from qualification recognition; a NARIC comparability statement is advice, not an admissions decision.
- Admission, fee classification, temporary tuition support, SUSI and an institution-specific scholarship are separate decisions; TP itself does not grant each automatically.
- National rules and institution- or school-specific implementation must remain distinct; academic-year limits matter.

## Unresolved / conflicting points

- The national sources reviewed do not establish a uniform current school enrolment checklist, physical IRP rule, the handling of missing Ukrainian records, or a universal year-placement method. PPSN's placeholder in the EAL staffing application is not a blanket waiver of school administration.
- The NTO 2026/27 funding page repeats the same heading for 2025/26 stipend recipients while stating both continuation and cessation. DCU's institution-specific notice distinguishes cohorts by the time since initial TP, but the national current technical notice and exact boundary should be verified with the department/institution; do not extrapolate the 2025/26 notice.
- The 2026/27 tuition document and SUSI evidence guide refer to legacy §60 permission. How new §237 grants are documented or assessed for these education schemes is not resolved by the reviewed material.
- No universal institution-specific fee classification, scholarship, advanced credit recognition, interpreter guarantee or current extension of every 2022 Ukraine-specific measure was established. The Irish TP documentation issue after March 2027 is carried forward without re-evaluation.
