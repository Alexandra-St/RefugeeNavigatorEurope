## 5. Cross-topic consistency checks

The packages are compatible when their stated scope and qualifications are carried through. No cross-topic finding is used to close a package's own evidence gap.

- **TP status, dates and documents:** the packages distinguish EU temporary-protection duration, Irish permission entitlement, physical permission evidence and IRP registration. New permissions under §237 (commenced 12 June 2026) and relevant legacy §60 cases must not be merged. The EU endpoint and the Irish Minister's statement confirm underlying TP through March 2028 for non-TPTS beneficiaries; they still do not resolve Irish permission, yellow-certificate/letter or IRP validity and holder action after 4 March 2027. Healthcare, work and education rights also attach to the relevant valid permission or scheme criteria, not automatically to a physical card or PPSN.
- **TP and work:** a valid TP permission provides its own work/business right. This is separate from JobsIreland/Intreo services, jobseeker-payment eligibility, ordinary Employment Permits and residence routes. A TP work right is not evidence that a person may file an ordinary permit/status change in-country. Section 237(7) bars simultaneous registered permissions but does not establish filing or transition procedure; generic in-country rules and omission from a route list establish neither eligibility nor prohibition. TPTS remains a distinct in-country route. An employment service, job offer or pending ordinary application does not itself grant work permission.
- **Accommodation and welfare:** designated accommodation centres, other State/commercial accommodation, Rapid Build, private rental and host accommodation are distinct. The BOTP Weekly Payment is described for designated-centre residents, is income-tested and does not follow from every State accommodation arrangement. Rent Supplement is an assessed tenant payment route; HAP depends on social-housing eligibility; ARP is a qualifying host/provider payment. None is interchangeable or automatic from TP. The housing package now confirms that the Citywest-specific continued-accommodation intake ran and closed, but this does not establish a general current UCTAT route. The UCTAT email is a contact for accommodation queries, not proof of an open application channel. Voluntary absence/re-entry under the absence protocol differs from refusal of an offer under the refusals policy. The package's €400/€600 ARP material conflicts, so the current rate remains unresolved.
- **Healthcare:** statutory TP medical entitlement is on the same conditions as Irish citizens; this does not issue a UKR Medical Card or standard Medical/GP Visit Card automatically. PPSN is needed for the UKR card application but not for all healthcare. Emergency access and free 112/999 calls do not remove ED, Injury Unit, GP or medicine charges; keep the specific card-based exemptions narrow.
- **Education:** school access, PPSN administration, EAL staffing, higher-education admission, NARIC advice, tuition support and SUSI are separate. EAL allocations are school-level, not guaranteed personal hours. 2026/27 tuition support is limited to a specified continuing legacy §60 cohort; it does not imply automatic admission, fee status, SUSI or maintenance support for every TP holder.
- **Social welfare and HRC:** right to reside and the Habitual Residence Condition are not interchangeable. Current guidance expressly addresses legacy §60 permission; the packages do not establish a universal HRC waiver for every payment or how the historic Ukraine deeming practice applies to new §237 permissions. Payment-specific conditions, accommodation designation and §60/§237 wording must remain visible. No contradiction is resolved by borrowing another package's broader language.

## 6. Drafting constraints

### Safe to state

- A valid Irish TP permission provides the statutory work and medical-care entitlements described in the research, subject to the applicable legal conditions; scheme permission is distinct from an IRP or proof document.
- The EU temporary-protection extension and a later Irish ministerial statement confirm underlying TP through March 2028 for non-TPTS beneficiaries. That does not establish validity or renewal dates for individual Irish permissions, certificates/letters or IRPs after 4 March 2027.
- Current published guidance describes up to 30 days in designated accommodation for new arrivals registering for TP who need State accommodation; the guidance names Punchestown and Wallslough. This is a qualified, availability-dependent offer, not a complete public intake procedure or guaranteed placement.
- TPTS is a distinct route for eligible existing TP beneficiaries. It is not automatic conversion or proof that ordinary residence applications can be made in-country. Section 237(7)'s bar on holding another registered permission simultaneously does not itself specify how to file or switch.
- Public employment-service access, private rental, designated-centre accommodation, host payments and social-welfare claims are different processes with their own eligibility and capacity limits.
- HSE describes a specific UKR Medical Card application route for qualifying recent arrivals from Ukraine; a PPSN is needed for that application but is not a prerequisite to all healthcare.
- Irish school and higher-education systems describe access and support routes, while admission, placement, recognition and funding decisions remain subject to the stated school, institution, cohort and scheme rules.

### Require qualification

- Keep §237 new permissions, transitional §60 legacy permissions and physical permission/IRP documents separate. Current Irish document validity after 4 March 2027 remains unresolved.
- TP initial-arrival information is limited to a border-level path; no complete current application checklist or new-route proof format/timing is established.
- TPTS public criteria and ordinary routes are not a full legal checklist; ordinary in-country filing and pending-status/work effects for TP holders remain unresolved.
- The housing-initial UCTAT process is incomplete in the reviewed public guidance. A closed Citywest-specific continued-accommodation process is not a general current national intake; the UCTAT query contact is not an established application channel. Separate the 30-day new-arrival offer, voluntary travel absence/re-entry and refusal consequences. Commercial phase-out, Rapid Build arrangements and any continued stay are cohort- and programme-specific.
- Accommodation designation controls the BOTP Weekly Payment boundary; do not infer designation from State funding, commercial operation or Rapid Build. Keep HAP, Rent Supplement and ARP scopes separate.
- Job-search services, payment claims and training programmes have different entry conditions; vacancy coverage, programme capacity and open Ukraine-specific employment intakes are not guaranteed.
- HSE card eligibility, local GP registration, service charges and emergency access have separate rules; the package does not establish a universal document checklist or nationwide exhaustive emergency-fee list.
- School admission, EAL staffing, higher-education selection, NARIC comparisons, 2026/27 tuition support, SUSI and maintenance support have separate cohort and institution limits.
- Welfare and HRC treatment varies by payment. The current scope of historic Ukraine-specific HRC treatment and its application to new §237 permissions is unresolved.
- Use the 2026 minimum-wage rates as of the research date. The announced 2027 adult rate was not found enacted in the source package.

### Do not assert

- That all Ukrainians automatically have Irish TP or work rights; that work begins before a valid TP grant; or that a PPSN or IRP itself creates work, healthcare, housing or welfare rights.
- That the EU 4 March 2028 date automatically extends every Irish certificate, permission or IRP; that the Irish document date is settled; or that no renewal/action is needed after 4 March 2027.
- That border interaction itself is a complete current §237 application procedure, or that a complete current TP application checklist, appointment/biometric requirement, processing time or guaranteed new §237 proof document is established.
- That all ordinary residence routes can be filed in-country by TP holders, that none can be filed in-country, that pending filing automatically bridges permission/work, or that all applicants must leave Ireland. Apply route-specific evidence; generic-list omission is not prohibition.
- That the closed Citywest continued-accommodation process is the current general UCTAT procedure, that the UCTAT email proves an open application route, or that voluntary absence/re-entry is governed like refusal/re-entry.
- Automatic State accommodation, automatic TP HAP/social housing, or that every State accommodation site uses the designated-centre BOTP reduced payment. Do not present Rapid Build or commercial accommodation as synonymous with a designated centre.
- That private tenancy is created by TP, ARP is rent paid to the beneficiary, HAP and Rent Supplement are the same, or a current ARP rate is confirmed at €400 or €600.
- That PPSN alone creates a job, healthcare access, payment or housing entitlement; that Medical Card/GP Visit Card is automatic; or that all care is free.
- Automatic university admission, qualification recognition, credit transfer, tuition waiver, SUSI grant or BOTP stipend for every TP holder; or a guaranteed school place, fixed EAL hours or Ukrainian online schooling as automatic fulfilment of Irish compulsory education.
- That the HRC is identical across all payments, that TP universally waives HRC, or that a rule stated for legacy §60 automatically applies to every new §237 case.
- That a job, work-permission right or Intreo service automatically qualifies someone for unemployment welfare; that Jobseeker's Allowance, Basic SWA and BOTP Weekly Payment are interchangeable; or that Ukrainian insurance periods aggregate for Irish unemployment insurance.
- That the announced €14.94 adult minimum wage is already in force on 10 November 2026, or that one national rate overrides age rates, statutory exceptions and applicable sectoral rates.

## 7. Blocking gaps and refresh list

### Blocks drafting now

- **`protection-application`** — Targeted official registration and TP-document guidance adds context, but no complete current §237 initial workflow was established: post-border steps, grant/proof format and timing, and new-applicant evidence remain unclear. This blocks a practical application guide. **Evidence needed:** current official procedure stating the steps after border interaction, proof issued and essential evidence for new §237 grants.
- **`protection-dates`** — An Irish Minister confirmed underlying TP through March 2028 for non-TPTS beneficiaries, but ISD's legacy guidance retains 4 March 2027 for certain permissions/certificates and IRP renewal. Individual permission, certificate/letter and IRP mechanics after that date and required holder action remain unresolved. **Evidence needed:** a current official Irish update reconciling underlying protection with each document's validity and renewal instructions.
- **`residence-filing`** — A targeted ISD/DETE refresh still did not establish TP-specific in-country filing for ordinary employment-permit/Stamp 1, Stamp 2, family or STEP routes, or pending/bridging stay and work rights. Section 237(7)'s simultaneous-permission restriction does not provide a filing procedure. **Evidence needed:** current official route-specific rules for TP holders and any interim-status/work consequences.
- **`housing-initial`** — The high-level new-arrival offer of up to 30 days, named centres and query contact are clearer, and voluntary travel absence/re-entry is partially clarified. The Citywest-specific self-declaration intake opened and closed in summer 2026, with cohort-specific decisions/appeals; it is not evidence of a current general UCTAT process. The complete initial intake and current continued-accommodation route for other cohorts or people already living independently remain unresolved. **Evidence needed:** current official national intake instructions, eligibility/assessment criteria and applicable continued-accommodation route beyond the closed Citywest process.

### Does not block drafting, but requires qualification

- **`protection-scheme`:** preserve the precise Irish cohort, family, military-evidence and §60/§237 boundaries; do not add optional EU cohorts by inference.
- **`residence-routes` / `residence-conditions`:** TPTS' public core criteria are not its full checklist; ordinary route conditions do not establish TP-holder in-country eligibility. The ordinary family-policy exclusion uses §60 wording that has not been reconciled with §237.

- **`employment-assistance-conditions`:** no universal Intreo service checklist or current open Ukraine-specific job/training intake was established; individual welfare rules and HRC must be checked separately.
- **Healthcare aspects:** HSE does not provide a complete UKR supporting-document checklist or one consolidated exhaustive ED exemption page; TP permission does not guarantee no-cost service or local GP capacity.
- **`housing-private-rental` / `housing-financial-support`:** tenancy and immigration documents are distinct; ARP rate pages conflict; HAP and Rent Supplement differ; proposed social-housing changes must not be treated as enacted based on this package.
- **Education aspects:** school documentation/placement and EAL delivery are local; higher-education tuition support is limited to a specified continuing cohort, while maintenance/stipend wording and new §237 documentation require confirmation.
- **Social-assistance aspects:** maintain payment-specific HRC and accommodation rules; the historic Ukraine deeming practice is not reconciled with current general guidance or new §237 permissions.

Obtain current official TP-specific ordinary filing guidance before advising a TP-to-employment, study, family or STEP switch.
