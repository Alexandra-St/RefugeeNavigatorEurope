## Research verdict

**NOT READY — MATERIAL EVIDENCE GAP**

The broad Irish eligibility cohorts, the current statutory permission mechanism, the border-level initial path, and the EU extension are established. Drafting an Irish end-date claim is blocked by contradictory official Irish operational guidance: the EU legal extension runs to 4 March 2028, while ISD's current beneficiary-renewal page still says Irish permissions and certificates are extended only to 4 March 2027. The current public guidance does not resolve how existing Irish permissions/certificates and IRP cards are to be treated after that date. The exact current initial registration steps and current proof document under the new statutory route also need confirmation before an unqualified application article is drafted.

## Cross-source conflicts and drafting boundaries

1. **EU duration versus Irish documents — material conflict.** The binding EU Decision extends the scheme to 4 March 2028. The ISD renewal page continues to state that a defined group of Irish permissions/yellow certificates are extended to 4 March 2027 and IRP cards need renewal. The latest general ISD page does not explain document validity through 2028. Treat EU duration as established, Irish existing-document end/renewal treatment as unresolved. This blocks drafting an Irish document-validity date.
2. **Current Irish statute versus older page citations.** ISD's Ukraine pages continue to describe the grant under §60 of the 2015 Act. The 2026 Act commenced on 12 June and provides §237 for new permissions, while retaining §60 for relevant existing cases. Cite §237 for the current legal route and qualify older process descriptions as legacy where the page itself does so.
3. **EU test versus Irish administrative wording.** The 2026 Decision says satisfy military obligations in Ukraine, where applicable, and present proof where applicable. ISD says applicants must show authorization under Ukrainian law to leave Ukraine and provide acceptable evidence. Preserve both formulations and do not invent the documents ISD accepts.
4. **FAQ freshness.** The FAQ includes a recent eligibility update but also legacy locations, PPSN arrangements, §60 permission-letter language, and a 2027 end date. Use only the claims specifically supported and disclose its limitations; do not treat its old operational details as a current complete application path.
