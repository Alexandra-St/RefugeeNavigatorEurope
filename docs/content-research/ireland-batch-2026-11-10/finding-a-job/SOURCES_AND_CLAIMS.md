## Claim matrix

### `employment-vacancies`

| Material claim | Assessment | Evidence, safe wording and prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| JobsIreland is the public employment vacancy service associated with DSP/Intreo. | Supported | DSP describes the website as connecting jobseekers and employers (S01, S03). Safe: “JobsIreland is the State's public job vacancy and matching service.” Do not imply every employer or vacancy is represented. |
| A person can browse JobsIreland search results without creating a jobseeker profile. | Supported | The live FAQ provides search box and Top Jobs routes; profile sign-in is an additional route (S02). |
| Account/profile login is needed for additional self-service functions. | Supported | Register/sign in to apply online, follow applications, receive alerts, generate profile CV and message employers (S02). Do not imply login needed merely to view postings. |
| JobsIreland provides filters. | Supported with qualification | FAQ confirms search fields; available fields depend on the live interface. Do not promise specific exhaustive filters without checking the current search page. |
| JobsIreland is a complete list of all Irish jobs. | Official guidance says no | No reviewed official source claims completeness. State only it is a public vacancy channel; combine with employer career pages and other channels. |
| JobsIreland has Ukrainian language support. | Supported with qualification | DSP TP service page says JobsIreland information is in Ukrainian and Russian (S06); it does not say every page or every vacancy is translated. The current interface's exact language coverage can change. |
| JobsIreland vacancy descriptions are translated into Ukrainian. | Not established | S06 says information in Ukrainian/Russian, not that vacancy text is translated. Do not claim translated listings. |
| EURES is an official public route to search jobs in Ireland and across participating European labour markets. | Supported | EURES covers participating countries including Ireland; search by country/region/occupation/contract (S04). Vacancies come from EURES Members/Partners and national public employment services, so it is not “all jobs”. |
| EURES search requires registration. | Official guidance says no | Several online services and Find a Job browsing are available without registration; registration adds features such as CV/profile, saved searches and matching (S04). |
| EURES has Ukrainian-language interface/search support. | Not established | The service states that Find a Job is available in 26 EU/EEA languages but does not identify Ukrainian among them (S04). Search facets are multilingual, but vacancy titles/descriptions generally remain in source language; keyword search in one language can miss results. Do not claim a Ukrainian interface or translated vacancy text without direct confirmation.
| EURES advisers provide job-search/mobility assistance; EURES is an immigration authority. | Supported / Official guidance says no | Adviser contacts and tailored cross-border job-search advice are part of EURES; EURES explicitly says it does not change national work/residence permission requirements (S04, S05). |
| Other useful official routes include employer career pages and public-sector recruitment. | Supported with qualification | Employer pages are a direct employer channel; EURES points to EU institutional vacancies at EPSO, but that is not a general Irish vacancy list (S04). Do not expand into commercial board recommendations. |

## Required distinctions

- Vacancy browsing ≠ jobseeker registration.
- Jobseeker registration ≠ being treated as unemployed.
- Unemployed status ≠ unemployment-payment entitlement.
- Employment assistance ≠ immigration permission.
- Employment Permit ≠ immigration residence permission.
- NARIC academic comparison ≠ regulated-profession recognition/licence.
- Programme eligibility ≠ guaranteed place, funding or payment.

## Unresolved / conflicting points

- The Ukraine-specific Intreo guidance page was last updated 2024-03-14. Its service descriptions are useful, but its narrower DAC payment and mandatory attendance conditions must not be generalized to every TP beneficiary in November 2026.
- Published government pages differ over accommodation/payment timelines as policy evolved. This phase does not use those dates to make job-search claims; confirm current DSP/DAC rules separately if needed.
- No universal document list for registering solely for employment services was found. PPSN may be requested for Intreo services; payment claims have distinct identity/address requirements.
- No official evidence located for Ukrainian insurance-period aggregation into Irish unemployment insurance.
- No currently open, dedicated Ukraine employment programme or 2026/27 bursary intake verified. The 2025/26 PLC bursary is historical; do not present it as open.
- JobsIreland's FAQ establishes search and account functions, but official evidence does not assert vacancy completeness or translation of vacancy bodies into Ukrainian.
