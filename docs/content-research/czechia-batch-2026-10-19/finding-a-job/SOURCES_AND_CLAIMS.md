## Article

- Working English title: **Finding a Job in Czechia**
- Category: `employment`
- User question: Where can a TP holder find official job-search help and language/qualification support?
- Scope boundary: Service navigation, not guaranteed placement, benefits or universal funding; no unverified commercial employer recommendations.

## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Limits

No claim that every English-language job needs only English; no universal language requirement inferred. No unemployment-benefit entitlement inferred from jobseeker registration. Recognition source EDU-S02 is owned by Education and reused here deliberately. Vacancy availability and a reader’s registration status are operational/individual checks, not unresolved legal contradictions.
