## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Exclusions and stale material

The historic 150-day accommodation rule and open-ended vulnerability exception are not the 19 October 2026 rule. HOUSE-S01 expressly applies the 90-day rule from 1 September 2024. No fixed address for KACPU is copied because centres move. No universal rent-deposit refund date is asserted. Housing amounts are left to the live humanitarian-benefit assessment; the article must not build a calculator or substitute a general Czech housing benefit for TP-specific support.
