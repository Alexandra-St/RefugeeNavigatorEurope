## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Current-versus-old guidance

WORK-S04 is a 2023 leaflet, not a current minimum-wage or termination reference. WORK-S03 supplies 2026 pay and WORK-S06 the post-1 June 2025 notice rules. Their dated differences are explained by subsequent changes, not silently harmonized. Probation, detailed severance, DPP/DPČ limits and litigation deadlines are outside this overview and require separate current evidence if later added. No unresolved material contradiction remains within the proposed claims.
