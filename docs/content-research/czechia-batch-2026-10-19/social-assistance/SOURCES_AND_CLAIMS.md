## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### 2026 reform and evidence boundary

Act 66 contains its own HuD base figures and formula. Do not automatically import the ordinary Czech subsistence-minimum increase or the new general dávka státní sociální pomoci into a TP claim. The MPSV announcement of 4 March 2026 (Senate approval/deferral of general benefit and minimum changes) was read; it expressly distinguishes HuD, but a Senate announcement is not used as proof of final enactment. The actual consolidated Act 66 was read for this matrix.

SOC-S02 contains material from different periods, including old flat disability amounts. Current degree-based Act 66 provisions control; older guidance is labelled historical, not silently copied. Current Act 111 numbering differs from 2024/2022 MOP leaflets, so current §§2 and 5 govern the conditional navigation claim. Ordinary parental/child benefits, detailed DSSP eligibility, housing-cost numerical ceilings and marginal disability cases are excluded unless separately rechecked. A narrow HuD child component is sufficient for comparison, not a claim that no other benefit can ever apply. No unresolved material contradiction remains in the supported overview.
