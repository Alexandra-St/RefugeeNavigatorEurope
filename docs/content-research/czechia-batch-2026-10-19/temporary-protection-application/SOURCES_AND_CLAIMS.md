## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Resolved source conflict: military evidence

TP-S01 general eligibility summary uses full-removal wording for the whole 18–60 group. Its detailed documents section and TP-S02 (explicitly updated 4 August 2026) distinguish 18–22 from 23–60. Under the project owner’s 19 October 2026 research ruling, use the specific, dated Czech administrative rule and document the overbroad summary as an internal inconsistency. Claims 07–08 are Confirmed with that limitation. Do not present the two Czech statements as two alternative applicant requirements. TP-S03/04 establish the EU rule, effective date and existing-holder exception, not the Czech evidentiary age split.

### Extension publication gate

EU 4 March 2028, Czech 31 March 2027 and the next Czech 31 March 2028 cycle are different dates. Act 65 supplies the annual mechanism; the dedicated announcement supplies the next-cycle intention. No claim that the next online registration is already open. Re-open the portal before drafting operational instructions. No remaining unresolved material contradiction after the explicit research ruling.
