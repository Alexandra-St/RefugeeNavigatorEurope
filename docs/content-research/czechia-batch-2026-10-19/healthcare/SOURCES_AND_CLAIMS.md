## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### High-risk insurance review

Do not use the Ministry’s old Ukrainian overview (created 28 February 2022, updated 5 March 2024) as current payer eligibility: [historical page](https://mzd.gov.cz/zakladni-informace-pro-obcany-ukrajiny-v-oblasti-poskytovani-zdravotnich-sluzeb-uk/). It was opened and read on 19 October 2026, but its broad early-war statements do not capture the current initial-payment cohorts. It is excluded from planned production-source counts. Act 65 and current insurer guidance provide the controlling temporal distinction.

Pregnancy alone is not represented here as a universal independent state-payer category. The draft must refer category-specific eligibility to the insurer. Medicine reimbursement, detailed pregnancy care and cross-border coverage are omitted unless separately evidenced; this is a scope limit, not a claim of no coverage. No unresolved material ambiguity found in the proposed TP/ZDP insurance transition claims.
