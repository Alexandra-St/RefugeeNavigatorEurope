## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Stage and legal hierarchy check

RES-S07, in its 15 March 2026 version, resolves dates against earlier rounds: April interest, October–December registration, then personal biometrics/card collection. These are not conflicting deadlines. The annex controls reference dates, including the 2025 income year and 2026 insurance/continuity tests. A tax return required for the income assessment had to be filed by 4 May 2026; this is not a new October filing window. School attendance for the relevant 6–14 cohort is assessed at 31 March 2026. Exact exceptional household/tax scenarios need authority-level individual assessment, not a product calculator.

No unresolved material stage conflict found. Publish only with the round/year explicit and a fresh deadline check. Source reuse: TP-S05/TP-S01 primary owners remain TP-01; SOC-S01 primary owner remains Social Assistance.
