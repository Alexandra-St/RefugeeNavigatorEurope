## Contradictions, limits and publication checks

### Annual-measure boundary

The PDF itself, not its search-result heading, was read. Its §2b interpretation distinguishes filing an admission request from the first physical attendance day. The older portal shorthand “start school within 90 days” should not obscure that qualification.

MŠMT file 65634_1_1 returned no readable text in the research tool; the attempted older secondary-language file 63306_1_1/download returned “Stránka nenalezena”. Neither is claimed as verified evidence or included in planned source counts. Detailed 2026/2027 exam waivers, deadlines and secondary language-preparation entitlements are omitted until an accessible exact annual measure is read. This does not block the supported school-navigation overview. Ordinary school rules and Ukraine-specific temporary adaptations must remain separate in drafting.
