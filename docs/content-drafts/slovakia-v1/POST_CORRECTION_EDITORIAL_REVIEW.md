### Source-association findings by provenance and severity

**Introduced by the latest corrections — 4 blockers, 2 minors:**

- Blocker: `residence-filing` adds an operational §131o reservation instruction without associating the current transition notice.
- Blocker: `work-minimum-pay` now states specific hourly/monthly wage amounts without associated wage/grade authority.
- Blocker: `healthcare-urgent` adds emergency number roles and copayment detail without their supporting records.
- Blocker: `social-child-benefits` expands the RU-specific factual detail and the all-language fact retains substantive benefit claims, but its associated sources are about material-need assistance.
- Minor: `healthcare-proof` adds legacy A4/provider guidance without the operational provider instructions.
- Minor: `social-child-benefits` fact detail is materially uneven between RU and EN/UK.

**Pre-existing source gaps identified by this audit — 5 blockers, 3 minors:**

- Blockers: `protection-scheme` (EU military-rule authority missing); `protection-application` (current TP booking authority missing); `housing-private-rental` (lease/deposit authorities missing); `education-higher-admission` (higher-education/recognition/tuition authorities missing); `social-application` (municipal and humanitarian aid route authorities missing). The unsupported ordinary child/parent/childcare benefit association in `social-child-benefits` predates the latest RU expansion, while the expanded scope makes it a new correction blocker counted above.
- Minors: `protection-dates` (individual validation-service record missing); `employment-institutions` (EURES source missing); `employment-assistance-conditions` (specific eligibility guidance missing).
- No source record was added, removed, or made cross-article by the corrections.
