# Finding Employment in Norway

You can look for vacancies independently and ask NAV, Norway’s Labour and Welfare Administration, about employment guidance. NAV does not guarantee a job or a particular support measure; it assesses what follow-up may fit your situation.

## Searching and NAV support

If you reside legally in Norway, you have the right to register as a jobseeker with NAV. After registration, NAV assesses your need for work-related guidance and may share information about relevant vacancies. You can register online with an approved electronic ID or contact your local NAV office if you cannot use electronic ID. Registered jobseekers must confirm every 14 days that they still want to remain registered.

Registration with NAV is not an application for a residence permit. NAV does not decide immigration status. The services or measures available to you depend on NAV’s assessment and the conditions for each programme.

## Introduction programme

The municipality provides the introduction programme. The rules changed on 1 January 2026. People receiving their first collective-protection permit from that date are generally placed under the ordinary rules, which include both a right and an obligation to participate. People whose first collective-protection permit was granted before 2026 retain the right-only category, including when that permit is later renewed, according to IMDi’s transition guidance.

Your date of first permit, age, settlement circumstances and the applicable programme rules matter. Ask your municipality to assess your eligibility and what it offers. Introduction allowance is linked to participation and applicable programme conditions; it is not an automatic payment to everyone with collective protection.
