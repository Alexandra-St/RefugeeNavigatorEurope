# Finding Accommodation in Norway

- **Article ID:** NO-HOUSING-01
- **Research date:** 14 November 2026
- **Editorial status:** DRAFT
- **Compare aspects:** `housing-initial`, `housing-private-rental`, `housing-financial-support`
- **Last verified:** 14 November 2026

Accommodation may be arranged through the protection and settlement process, or you may look for a private rental. A place to live, a particular municipality, or financial support is not automatic.

## Initial accommodation and settlement

If you seek protection, follow UDI’s instructions for registering at the National Arrival Centre and ask UDI about the accommodation options available at that stage. For refugees settled with public assistance, IMDi selects a municipality based on the person’s circumstances, local services and capacity. Municipal participation in settlement is voluntary, and you are not guaranteed your preferred municipality.

Ask UDI or IMDi what applies before arranging private accommodation if you expect to use public settlement support. Your choice may affect access to publicly supported settlement and related integration services.

## Renting privately

You may search for a home on the private rental market. Read the tenancy agreement before signing. If the landlord asks for a deposit, Norwegian law limits it to six months’ rent and requires it to be placed in a separate bank account in the tenant’s name. The landlord pays the cost of setting up that account.

If you need NAV help with rent or a deposit, contact the NAV office before signing the rental agreement. NAV assesses applications individually and is not a party to the tenancy contract.

## Housing support

You may check whether you qualify for housing allowance (bostøtte) from Husbanken. Eligibility depends on household income, housing costs and whether the home meets the housing requirements. Renting alone does not guarantee eligibility. You may also apply to NAV for help with necessary housing costs if you cannot cover them; NAV will make an individual assessment.
