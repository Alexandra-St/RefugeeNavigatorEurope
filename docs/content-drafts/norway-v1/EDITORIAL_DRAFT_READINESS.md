## Editorial questions and limits

- Before publication, recheck date-sensitive UDI eligibility, application instructions, and the stated one-year permit / five-year individual maximum. Do not state a seven-year rule, automatic permanent residence, or a special work-based permit.
- Residence-route steps and filing location depend on the specific permit. These drafts direct readers to UDI rather than assert a universal in-country process.
- Introductory programme eligibility and allowance depend on first-permit date, age, settlement and programme conditions; readers are directed to IMDi and their municipality.
- Healthcare costs, exact service coverage and proof requirements can vary by status and service. The draft does not say all care is free.
- NAV benefits, housing support, settlement and municipal services are conditional and may require individual assessment. No automatic benefit or accommodation is promised.
- Lånekassen eligibility for collective-protection holders was not established in Source Gate 02; no funding entitlement is claimed.
- The two UDI pages cited for waiting-for-decision and D-number information expose a non-English (`lang=uk`) route. Replace with a verified English UDI equivalent if one is available during final copy-editing; the legal statement itself is constrained to the Source Gate wording.
- No Hungary sample draft was present under `docs/content-drafts`; the supplied Norway gate structure was followed instead.

All drafts are English, labelled DRAFT, use direct HTTPS links to official sources and preserve the qualifications and exclusions in the two research gates. No translations, production facts or application changes were made.
