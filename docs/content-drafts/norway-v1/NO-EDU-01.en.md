# Education and Norwegian-Language Learning

- **Article ID:** NO-EDU-01
- **Research date:** 14 November 2026
- **Editorial status:** DRAFT
- **Compare aspects:** `education-school-enrolment`, `education-pupil-language`, `education-higher-admission`
- **Last verified:** 14 November 2026

Children, pupils and adults entering education in Norway follow different rules. Contact the municipality or institution responsible for the programme you want; eligibility and admission depend on age, previous education and individual requirements.

## School-age children

For children of compulsory school age, Udir says education should begin as soon as possible and no later than one month after arrival in Norway. Contact the municipality where the child is staying to ask how to enrol. The school and municipality assess the child’s school background and appropriate placement; this does not guarantee a particular school or class.

## Language support at school

Pupils whose first language is not Norwegian or Sami have a right to special language instruction until their Norwegian is good enough to follow ordinary teaching. Support includes strengthened Norwegian instruction. Mother-tongue teaching or bilingual subject teaching may also be provided when needed, based on an assessment; these formats are not automatic for every pupil. The municipality reviews language progress.

## Higher education

Applicants with foreign education generally need to meet the Higher Education Entrance Qualification (GSU), including education and language requirements. HK-dir’s Ukraine guidance says you usually do not need to apply for recognition of your education with HK-dir just to apply for admission. For bachelor’s and other first-cycle programmes, check the relevant Samordna opptak process; for master’s and doctoral programmes, contact the university or university college directly. Individual programmes may set additional requirements.

Admission requirements do not establish eligibility for Lånekassen financial support. Check those rules separately; this draft does not confirm that collective protection alone qualifies a student for funding.
