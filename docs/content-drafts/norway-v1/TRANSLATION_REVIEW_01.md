## Terminology and completeness

- **Collective protection** remains distinct from an individual asylum/protection assessment; Norway’s national scheme is not described as EU temporary protection.
- **Residence permit** and **permanent residence** are distinguished. No translation promises automatic permanent residence or another permit.
- **Introduction Programme** and **introduction allowance** are distinct. Participation and allowance remain conditional.
- NAV registration and follow-up are not presented as guaranteed employment or benefits. Housing, social support, child benefits and Lånekassen funding are not promised automatically.
- Healthcare wording preserves the distinction between asylum-applicant guidance while a case is pending and UDI’s separate confirmation after collective protection is granted.
- Each UK/RU file retains its EN Article ID, all three assigned Compare aspect IDs, the same section count and the same HTTPS URLs in the same order. The digits and dates match the corresponding EN article.
- No contradiction in the EN originals was identified during translation review; no English file was changed.
