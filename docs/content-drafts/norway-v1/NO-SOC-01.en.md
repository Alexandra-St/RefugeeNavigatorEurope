# Financial and Social Assistance

- **Article ID:** NO-SOC-01
- **Research date:** 14 November 2026
- **Editorial status:** DRAFT
- **Compare aspects:** `social-subsistence`, `social-application`, `social-child-benefits`
- **Last verified:** 14 November 2026

Norwegian benefits have separate eligibility rules. Collective protection does not automatically qualify you for NAV payments. NAV and other agencies assess applications using your residence status, household, income, expenses and the rules for each benefit.

## Financial social assistance

Anyone may apply for financial social assistance and have their need individually assessed. Normally, you must reside legally in Norway. NAV may grant temporary help when other income and resources are not enough to cover necessary living expenses. The amount depends on your situation; national guideline rates are a starting point, and municipalities may use their own rates.

Apply to the NAV office in the municipality where you are staying. Provide information about your income, expenses, housing and residence status, and any documents NAV requests. If you have an urgent need, say so in your application or contact the office. If you have nowhere to sleep for the next 24 hours, NAV says you can also apply for temporary emergency accommodation.

## Child-related benefits

Child benefit (barnetrygd) may be available when the child lives in Norway and the relevant residence conditions are met. For many foreign nationals, the whole family must live in Norway and plan to remain for more than 12 months, with lawful residence and registration. Separate EEA and social-security coordination rules can affect the result. Some situations require an application and supporting documents; do not assume a payment will start automatically.

Cash-for-care benefit (kontantstøtte) is a separate benefit for children aged 13–19 months. The amount depends in part on the childcare place the child has been offered. The applicant generally needs at least five years of membership in Norway’s National Insurance Scheme; if the child lives with both parents, both generally need the required membership, subject to applicable coordination rules.
