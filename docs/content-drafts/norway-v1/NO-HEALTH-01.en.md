# Accessing Healthcare in Norway

- **Article ID:** NO-HEALTH-01
- **Research date:** 14 November 2026
- **Editorial status:** DRAFT
- **Compare aspects:** `healthcare-access`, `healthcare-proof`, `healthcare-urgent`
- **Last verified:** 14 November 2026

Healthcare rights depend on your status and where you are in the protection process. Medical staff assess what care is needed. Having a right to healthcare does not mean that every service is free or that you can choose any treatment.

## While an asylum application is pending

UDI’s guidance for asylum applicants says the right to healthcare starts when they arrive in Norway and ask for protection. After registration, keep your asylum seeker card and any D-number you receive. Helsenorge provides information about registering with a general practitioner (GP) and healthcare for asylum seekers and refugees. Show the documents you have and ask the provider or Helsenorge what applies to your situation. This general asylum-applicant guidance does not decide every service or documentation question for an individual case.

If collective protection is granted, UDI separately confirms a right to healthcare through Norway’s public health services. Some services have user fees; exemptions and coverage depend on the service and your circumstances. Do not assume that all treatment, medicines or dental care are free.

## Urgent help

- Call **116 117** for the out-of-hours medical service when you need urgent help and your GP is unavailable.
- Call **113** for a serious or life-threatening emergency.
