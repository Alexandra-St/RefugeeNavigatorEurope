## Required corrections

### 1. IE-EMP-02 — Ukrainian PPSN wording

The UK version says **“PPSN потрібен для адміністративних і податкових процедур”** (“PPSN is needed for administrative and tax procedures”), while EN and RU say it is used for those purposes. This reads as a general requirement and is broader than the source-supported statement; the same article notes that Revenue accepts specified alternative employee details where a PPSN is not yet available.

**Correction:** change “потрібен” to “використовується” and retain the nearby qualification that alternative details may be used for payroll when the PPSN is not yet available. Keep the separate point that PPSN does not create work authorization.

### 2. IE-EDU-01 — QQI/NARIC advice is non-binding

EN says comparability advice is **non-binding**. UK says the opinion “не є обов’язковим”, and RU says it “не обязательно для вуза”. These formulations can be read as saying the advice is optional or unnecessary, rather than that it does not bind the institution.

**Correction:** in both translations state that the QQI/NARIC comparability advice is advisory/non-binding and does not oblige the institution to admit the applicant or accept credit transfer. Keep admission and credit transfer as separate institutional decisions.

### 3. IE-SOC-01 — Russian WFP eligibility limits

EN and UK state that WFP normally requires 38 hours of insurable employee work per fortnight, that eligible partner hours may count, and that self-employed hours do not count. RU only describes general conditions on hours, children and income. This makes the Russian version materially less precise.

**Correction:** add the same 38-hour-per-fortnight rule, partner-hours qualification, and exclusion of self-employed hours to RU. Preserve the separate qualifying-child and income conditions and the statement that TP status or having a job alone does not establish entitlement.
