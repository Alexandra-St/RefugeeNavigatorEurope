# Ireland Compare Review 01

**Review date:** 15 November 2026
**Scope:** Editorial, source-ownership, evidence-limit and EN/UK/RU review of the 24 Ireland Compare facts. No new research was conducted, and Production data was not changed.

## Aspect review

### protection-scheme

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The fact keeps eligibility category-specific and distinguishes the EU Temporary Protection framework from an individual asylum application and the Irish permission that evidences protection. Its ISD and section 237 sources belong to `IE-TP-01`.

**Changes:** None.

### protection-application

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Border contact and the applicable Ukraine-law evidence requirement are bounded to the relevant applicants. The in-country first-permission route is explicitly described as not fully set out in public guidance; no universal form, office, appointment, checklist or grant is implied. Sources are owner-bound to `IE-TP-01`.

**Changes:** None.

### protection-dates

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The EU scheme date of 4 March 2028 is kept separate from individual Irish permission and document validity. The 4 March 2027 ISD instructions remain limited to their specified certificate-holder group.

**Changes:** None.

### residence-routes

**Result:** REVISED

**Reason:** The original wording could be read as confirming that employment, study and family routes are open to every TP holder applying from inside Ireland. The Core review says that this is not established for every route; it treats TPTS as a separate route for eligible existing beneficiaries.

**Changes:** EN / UK / RU now state that public guidance does not confirm every ordinary route is open to TP holders applying from inside Ireland. TPTS remains a separate, specific route and is not described as automatic conversion.

### residence-conditions

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The TPTS one-year residence, six-month employment, €29,432 earnings, continued-employment and independent-accommodation conditions match the reviewed Core summary. They are identified as core published conditions, not a complete checklist or guarantee of approval.

**Changes:** None.

### residence-filing

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Ordinary-route filing is kept route-specific, while the TPTS portal is identified as a separate channel. No universal in-country filing route or pending stay/work right is asserted, and filing is not described as extending current permission.

**Changes:** None.

### employment-vacancies

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** JobsIreland sign-in functions and EURES participating-market search are accurately distinguished; neither is presented as exhaustive or as a job guarantee.

**Changes:** None.

### employment-institutions

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Intreo, EURES and QQI/NARIC roles are separate, and hiring, admission and licensing decisions remain with the relevant employers, providers and regulators.

**Changes:** None.

### employment-assistance-conditions

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** TP-related Intreo support and training are conditional. Service access is not equated with payment or programme eligibility.

**Changes:** None.

### work-permission

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Work is tied to valid Irish TP permission and its conditions. Arrival or a pending initial request is not presented as permission, and TP work is distinguished from an ordinary Employment Permit.

**Changes:** None.

### work-employer-duties

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Written terms, payslips and applicable employer duties are summarized consistently with Core. Safety instruction remains qualified by a form and language reasonably likely to be understood; no general translated-contract right or TP-specific employer procedure is added.

**Changes:** None.

### work-minimum-pay

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The dated €14.15 gross adult hourly rate for workers aged 20 and over matches Core. Age rates, statutory exceptions, reckonable-pay rules and higher sectoral rates remain qualified; the 2027 announcement is not presented as enacted.

**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-access

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Entitlement for a valid TP permission holder is distinct from automatic GP registration, a Medical Card decision and free treatment.

**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-proof

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The UKR Medical Card route is limited to the described applicant group and its PPSN/form requirements. Eligibility to apply is not described as guaranteed approval, and PPSN is not made a universal care prerequisite.

**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-urgent

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Free calls to 112/999 are distinguished from potentially chargeable treatment. Emergency Departments, GP out-of-hours and Injury Units retain their service-specific limits.

**Changes:** None.

### housing-initial

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The up-to-30-day offer is limited to eligible new arrivals who need State accommodation and is subject to current policy and availability. Neither placement, duration nor a fallback bed is guaranteed.

**Changes:** None.

### housing-private-rental

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Private renting is distinct from State accommodation and social housing; TP is not a tenancy and landlord acceptance is not guaranteed. The one-month deposit/advance-rent summary is tied to covered ordinary tenancies and their exceptions. The RTB law-change source directly supports the limit.

**Changes:** None.

### housing-financial-support

**Result:** REVISED

**Reason:** The original HAP summary omitted landlord participation, which the reviewed Core lists among the scheme conditions.

**Changes:** Added landlord participation as a HAP condition in EN / UK / RU. The separation between HAP, Rent Supplement, Additional Needs Payment and private rent, and the no-guarantee wording, remain.

### education-school-enrolment

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** The fact describes access and application to local schools without promising a preferred or immediate place. REALT/TESS support is described as assistance, not a placement guarantee.

**Changes:** None.

### education-pupil-language

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** EAL is limited to the 2026/27 school-level allocation framework; individual hours or a dedicated teacher are not guaranteed.

**Changes:** None.

### education-higher-admission

**Result:** REVISED

**Reason:** The prior wording categorically excluded new section 237 permissions from the tuition-support scheme. Core says the reviewed scheme does not establish their coverage; that is an evidence limit, not a confirmed exclusion.

**Changes:** EN / UK / RU now say coverage for new section 237 permissions is not established. The defined continuing section 60 cohort progressing from an approved 2025/26 course and exclusion of new entrants are retained.

### social-subsistence

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** BOTP is tied to income assessment and designated accommodation; other named welfare schemes retain separate eligibility. TP permission is not presented as an automatic award.

**Changes:** None.

### social-application

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Application channels are scheme-dependent, and neither PPSN nor an application is described as an award.

**Changes:** None.

### social-child-benefits

**Result:** PASS

**Reason:** Child Benefit and Working Family Payment remain separate schemes with distinct conditions. TP or employment alone is not presented as entitlement.

**Changes:** None.

## Evidence ceilings

### §237 inside-Ireland application

**PRESERVED.** No universal first-application procedure, mandatory online form, office, appointment or exhaustive checklist is asserted. The fact directs readers to current ISD instructions and the published query channel without presenting that contact as filing or grant.

### Post-4-March-2027 permission/document

**PRESERVED.** The EU Temporary Protection date of 4 March 2028 is not treated as the validity date of an individual Irish permission or document. The specified 4 March 2027 renewal instruction is not generalized.

### Ordinary residence pending rights

**PRESERVED.** No universal pending stay/work right, automatic TP continuation, conversion or residence credit is claimed. Ordinary routes and TPTS remain separate.

### Initial accommodation no-place fallback

**PRESERVED.** The conditional offer of up to 30 days is not a guarantee of a place, a minimum stay or a fallback when capacity is unavailable.
