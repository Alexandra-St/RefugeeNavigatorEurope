## Article-by-article review

### ES-TP-01 — Temporary Protection in Spain: Applying and Extending Your Status

- Verdict: **OK**
- Claim coverage: 19 of 20 matrix claims appear in Content in EN/UK/RU. ES-TP01-02 is explicitly excluded because its 4 March 2027 EU endpoint is stale; it is not presented as current.
- Referenced claims: EN / UK / RU each map ES-TP01-01 and ES-TP01-03 through ES-TP01-20; mappings correspond to the sections where the claims are expressed.
- Source set: TP-S01, TP-S03, TP-S04, TP-S05, TP-S06, TP-S07, TP-S08; all are present in the matrix and relevant to the draft.
- Cross-language review: EU temporary protection through 4 March 2028 remains distinct from Spanish TIE validity through 4 March 2027. The draft does not infer an individual's permit validity. The 5 August 2026 military-obligation rule retains its scope and same-Member-State continuous-status exception; no unsupported Spanish evidence requirements or age bands are added. Application, receipt/NIE, grant, TIE, and service conditions remain distinct.
- Findings: none.

### ES-TP-02 — Residence Options Beyond Temporary Protection in Spain

- Verdict: **CHANGES REQUIRED**
- Claim coverage: 14 of 14 matrix claims appear in Content in EN/UK/RU; all three mappings cover ES-TP02-01 through ES-TP02-14.
- Referenced claims: EN / UK / RU mappings are equivalent and correspond to the sections where expressed.
- Source set: RES-S01, RES-S02, RES-S03, RES-S05 are the sources used for substantive claims. RES-S04 is an instruction index that lists SEM 2/2026; the draft does not rely on it as substantive support.

National long-term residence is distinguished from EU long-term residence; no unsupported full, partial, or zero credit conclusion is made for the EU route. The renunciation sequence is conditional on a new authorisation being granted.
- Findings:

| Severity | Finding | Recommended correction |

| CORRECTION | The file contains two `Editorial source set` blocks, one between the EN and UK sections and another after RU. The lists differ, and RES-S04 appears only in the earlier block despite not supporting a substantive draft claim. | Keep one source set at the end of the file, after all language sections, listing RES-S01, RES-S02, RES-S03, and RES-S05. |

## Cross-article consistency

- Temporary-protection dates and the distinction between EU protection and Spanish permits are preserved; the dated military-obligation rule is not broadened beyond its documented scope.
- Employment and healthcare drafts do not treat application receipts or NIE as equivalent to a granted status or TIE.
- Housing, healthcare, education, and social-assistance service access remains conditional where the matrices require it.
- The reviewed drafts do not imply that an individual reader qualifies for a status, benefit, place, course, or service.
- EN/UK/RU claim mappings generally cover the same claims. The four corrections above concern the TP-02 source-set structure and narrow wording precision; no material cross-language date, amount, cohort, exception, or legal-scope divergence was identified.
