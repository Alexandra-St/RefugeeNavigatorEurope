## Residence topic

### `residence-routes`

**Status: READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Ordinary permit routes exist and some prefectures publish TP-holder procedures from France. The supported statement is that named departments document local procedures, not that every applicant or prefecture has the same route. Ordinary routes have separate eligibility conditions. The source inventory contains 26 official URLs; **none is currently present in Production `official-sources.json`**.

The strongest reusable route examples are the [Seine-Maritime TP change-of-status guide](https://www.seine-maritime.gouv.fr/index.php/layout/set/print/Demarches/Etrangers-en-Seine-Maritime/Je-demande-un-titre-de-sejour-ou-un-document-de-voyage-en-Seine-Maritime/Ukraine-Protection-temporaire-Ukrayina-timchasovu-ohoronu-Ukraine-Temporary-protection/Je-souhaite-basculer-vers-le-droit-commun/Je-souhaite-demander-un-titre-de-sejour-de-droit-commun) and [Essonne’s TP change-of-status guide](https://www.essonne.gouv.fr/Demarches/Accueil-des-etrangers-dans-l-Essonne/Ressortissants-ukrainiens-protection-temporaire/Changement-de-statut-Protection-temporaire-Ukraine/Changement-de-statut-Protection-temporaire-Ukraine); both are research-only for Production. DGEF’s [2026 residence-permit statistics](https://www.immigration.interieur.gouv.fr/documentation/etudes-et-statistiques/presence-des-ukrainiens-adultes-ayant-autorisation-de-sejour-en-france.html) are descriptive context, not proof of eligibility or a filing right.

### `residence-conditions`

**Status: READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** State only that the relevant route has its own conditions. CESEDA visa rules and route-specific exceptions, work/student/family/business criteria, and long-term residence criteria are documented; APS-BPT is not an ordinary residence card. The sources do not establish a general APS-to-ordinary-permit eligibility rule or that APS time counts toward the five-year long-term EU residence period. Relevant research-only sources include [CESEDA L. 412-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042776891), [L. 412-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000049052720/2026-09-16), [L. 412-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042776882), [L. 426-17](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042776450), and [Service-Public’s five-year long-term residence guidance](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F17359). All are research-only in the current Production catalog.

**Status: READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** The existing research supports a high-level overview of leases, deposits, permitted rental-dossier documents, anti-discrimination protections and locally varying rent controls. It does not resolve APS-BPT acceptance as a private rental identity document or Visale eligibility. Suitable research-only URLs include Service-Public [permitted rental documents](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1169), [lease form](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F920), [entry condition report](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31270), [deposit](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31269), [rental discrimination](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14750), and [rent control](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1314). None is currently present in Production `official-sources.json`.

**Status: READY WITH QUALIFICATION.** Suitable evidence distinguishes HLM applications from allocations, housing benefit from social housing, local FSL and conditional Visale. Sources include Service-Public [HLM applications](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10007), [APL conditions](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F12006), [housing-benefit applications](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R1292), [FSL](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1334), Action Logement’s [Visale scheme](https://www.actionlogement.fr/la-garantie-visale), and the current general [CAF account guidance](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F32823). CAF des Yvelines’ [Ukraine guidance](https://www.caf.fr/allocataires/caf-des-yvelines/offre-de-service/je-suis-de-nationalite-etrangere/je-suis-deplacee-ukrainienne) is already in Production, owned by `FR-SOC-01`; if cited by `FR-HOUSING-01`, it still needs a separate source record owned by that new article. Do not rely on the unavailable CAF 2026 guide PDF or treat the dated national 2022 CAF page as current proof.

**Topic verdict: READY FOR ARTICLE DRAFTING** — for the qualified general emergency/local example, ordinary rental rules, and conditional financial-support routes above. A nationwide TP-specific housing intake or placement claim remains outside this readiness decision.
