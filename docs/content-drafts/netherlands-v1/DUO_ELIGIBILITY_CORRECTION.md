# Netherlands DUO Eligibility Correction

**Phase baseline:** `7e86c5e316e406c7fde5eae6c87ff979fda6f9fd`
**Correction date:** 21 October 2026
**Editorial status:** NeedsReview / Draft; no publication approval.

## Original omission

The Netherlands Education article and the `education-higher-admission` comparison answer stated that an applicant must lack eligibility for student finance or a grant, but omitted DUO's reason: the applicant does not meet the applicable nationality criteria. This is one of the cumulative DUO conditions, not a general nationality exclusion that replaces the other criteria.

## Corrected meaning and affected fields

The EN, UK and RU Education article passage and Netherlands comparison answer now say that the applicant must not be eligible for student finance or the relevant secondary-education student grant because the nationality criteria are not met. Localized wording preserves that cause in each language.

- Core article: NL-EDU-01, DUO course-fee-remission section, EN / UK / RU `Content`.
- Comparison V2: Netherlands `education-higher-admission`, EN / UK / RU `Values`.

## Evidence

The evidence is claim **NL-EDU01-05**, source **EDU-S04**, in the [Netherlands Education source-and-claims matrix](../../content-research/netherlands-batch-2026-10-20/education/SOURCES_AND_CLAIMS.md). The current [DUO course-fee page](https://duo.nl/particulier/course-fees/asylum-seeker-or-ukrainian.jsp), “Conditions” item 1, states that the applicant must not qualify for student finance or a secondary-education student grant because the nationality criteria are not met. It requires all four criteria. The page's current 2026–27 form section gives 31 July 2027 as the application deadline. DUO was directly checked on 21 October 2026; exact time was not recorded.

## Conditions preserved

The correction retains the conditional scope and existing limits:

- specified applicants under temporary protection applying for MBO course-fee remission;
- all listed criteria must be met, including the applicable status condition;
- age 18 to under 31 on 1 August, with the existing continuation rule after age 30 kept separate;
- inability to pay and no organization/sponsor paying the fees;
- school year 2026–2027 and deadline 31 July 2027;
- no promise of admission, remission, funding or free education generally.

The existing EDU-S04 source relationship is retained. No source record, source ID, source check date, research matrix, status or unrelated content was changed.
