# Netherlands Targeted Freshness Resolution

**Verification date:** 21 October 2026. Official pages were reopened and read on that date; exact check times were not recorded. This is a targeted evidence review, not legal advice, publication approval, or deployment authorization.

## 1. Executive summary

The checks confirmed several bounded, date-specific statements and one specific correction that is required before publication: the DUO MBO course-fee-remission condition must explain that the applicant is ineligible for student finance or a secondary-education student grant **because the nationality criteria are not met**. That reason is omitted from the Netherlands Education article in EN/UK/RU and from the Netherlands `education-higher-admission` comparison answer in EN/UK/RU. The existing DUO source is sufficient; no new source record is needed. No content was edited in this phase.

The current official TPD scheme horizon remains 4 March 2028, but individual IND documents have separate proof and extension rules. The IND's public military evidence is limited to the described liable/reservist 23–60 cohort; this check found no accessible authoritative text that establishes additional age bands or the full scope. The planned transition document remains policy planning, with no verified enacted measure or operational filing route. The general in-country `residence-filing` comparison remains unavailable. UWV's two-working-day notice rule is directly confirmed for covered Ukrainian employees. The healthcare worker-insurance conflict remains unresolved. Local reception, education and service availability cannot be guaranteed nationally.

**Recommendation:** retain the existing bounded wording and statuses; make the DUO correction before publication; keep the TP/transition, healthcare and residence-filing gates open. Other independently supported content may proceed to a separate editorial publication decision with its date, cohort and local-availability qualifications intact. The Netherlands package as a whole is not publication-ready.
