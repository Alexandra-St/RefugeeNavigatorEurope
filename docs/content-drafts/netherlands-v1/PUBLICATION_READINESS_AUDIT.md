## 7. Legal conflicts and excluded conclusions

- **EU scheme date vs personal IND document:** 4 March 2028 is a scheme horizon, not a conclusion about the date on a specific sticker, O-document or extension letter. Government.nl's English 2027 statement remains an attributed stale-source discrepancy; it is not treated as the governing current date.
- **EU Decision vs IND military wording:** the corrected Decision's prior-holder exception refers to continuous protection in the same Member State; IND's public wording refers more broadly to prior TPD in the EU and separately to joining a protected family member. The reconciliation remains unresolved. Do not claim “all Ukrainians” or “all Ukrainian men,” invent an 18–22 evidence rule, or infer an individual outcome.
- **Transition measure:** proposal/planning is not enactment, commencement or an operating route. In-country filing, duration/conditions and permanent-residence counting remain unestablished. These are **UNRESOLVED BUT EXCLUDED** conclusions, not reader-facing choices.
- **Healthcare:** the official explanation distinguishes formal Zvw liability from a policy not to enforce policy-taking while the person remains under RMO, but current public UWV and TPD/RMO guidance still conflict and individual triggers are not settled. The comparison's `healthcare-access` fact is marked conflicting in the research and remains Draft. The bounded reader-facing content does not tell a person to obtain, retain, cancel or change insurance. Do not present the conflict as resolved or describe all healthcare as free.
- **Housing/social assistance:** municipal reception, private rent, huurtoeslag and personal reception contributions are different systems. No chosen placement, rent reimbursement, universal bijstand, or general MOP eligibility is established. The 2027 hosted-household statement is excluded from production content.
- **Education/employment:** no guaranteed school place, adult course, job placement, course funding or benefit follows from the navigation information. Employer notification and local service access need the specific conditions stated above.
