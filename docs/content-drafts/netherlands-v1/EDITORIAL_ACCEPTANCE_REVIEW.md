## Preserved legal and editorial caveats

1. Temporary protection, a Dutch residence permit, and a possible future transition arrangement are distinct. EU protection through 4 March 2028 does not itself establish a person's individual permit validity or conversion route.
2. Government.nl's older 4 March 2027 / transition-document wording remains attributed as older guidance. The materials reviewed do not reconcile that page with later IND guidance; the article must not treat it as current filing instructions.
3. Whether time under temporary protection counts toward permanent residence remains unestablished in the reviewed IND material.
4. UWV's municipality-first and in-person local-office process is a described route, not a universal entitlement to job-search support. Workcentrum provision is local and conditional.
5. School placements, exceptions, language support and adult courses require local/institutional confirmation. The 2022 exemptions page is not an exhaustive statement of current individual cases.
6. DUO MBO course-fee relief remains conditional for the stated 2026–27 cohort and deadline; it is not a general education grant or an admission decision.
