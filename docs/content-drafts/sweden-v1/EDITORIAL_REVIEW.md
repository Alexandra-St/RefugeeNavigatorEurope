## Findings

### SW-REV-01 — SE-EMP-01 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: “Search for vacancies”, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: The drafts say that not every job is necessarily listed on Platsbanken.
- Matrix boundary: SE-EMP01-01 establishes Platsbanken as the Public Employment Service vacancy-search service and says listings and language vary; it does not establish that the service is incomplete.
- Required correction: Remove the unsupported completeness statement or narrow it to what the matrix supports. Keep the point that a listing is not a job offer or placement.

### SW-REV-02 — SE-EMP-01 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: “Register and contact Arbetsförmedlingen”, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: EN says an interpreter “is available”; UK and RU say one “may be available,” weakening the statement in the accepted matrix.
- Matrix boundary: SE-EMP01-02 says the Agency’s Ukraine guidance states an interpreter is available when interacting with the service.
- Required correction: Align the three versions to the same source-supported scope and certainty, without implying interpreter availability for unrelated services or circumstances.

### SW-REV-03 — SE-EMP-02 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: Editorial source set; pay and collective-agreement section, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: The article states that Sweden has no general statutory national minimum-wage amount and that wages are commonly set through collective bargaining. Its source set includes WR-S07 but omits WR-S09.
- Matrix boundary: SE-EMP02-06 cites WR-S07 and WR-S09; WR-S09 is specifically identified as an accessible wage-setting overview supporting that claim.
- Required correction: Include WR-S09 in the article source set, or establish from the accepted matrix that WR-S07 alone directly supports both statements. No wage figure should be added.

### SW-REV-04 — SE-EDU-01 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: “Assessment and placement”, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: The two-month limit for the language-introduction individual study plan is described only as applying “under its separate trigger”; the trigger itself is not identified. The translations preserve the same ambiguity.
- Matrix boundary: SE-EDU01-07 requires the two-month timing to retain the distinct school form and statutory trigger. The matrix points to Skolverket’s description but does not record the specific start event.
- Required correction: Before integration, either make the trigger explicit from the already accepted research record or omit/narrow this timing statement until its trigger is documented. Do not generalize the two-month deadline.

### SW-REV-05 — SE-SOC-01 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: “How to apply to the Migration Agency”, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: The draft says applicants “may need” to disclose income/assets and report changes.
- Matrix boundary: SE-SOC01-04 says current operational instructions require disclosure of income/assets and changes such as address or work.
- Required correction: Preserve the required nature of the disclosure/reporting duties while directing readers to current forms and instructions. The current wording makes a required step sound optional.

### SW-REV-06 — SE-SOC-01 — CORRECTION

- Location/language: “Municipal economic assistance”, EN / UK / RU.
- Current wording/problem: After saying that a person no longer covered by the Reception Act may apply, the draft omits the matrix’s qualification that the described applicant is a registered temporary-protection holder.
- Matrix boundary: SE-SOC01-08 supports that a registered TPD holder who is no longer covered may apply under ordinary needs-based rules; it does not state that every person no longer covered follows this route.
- Required correction: Restore the registered-TPD-holder scope in all three languages. Retain the individual municipal assessment and the 2 January 2027 legal-change warning.
