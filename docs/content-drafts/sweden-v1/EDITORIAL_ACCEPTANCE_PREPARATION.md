## Unresolved boundaries for the owner

These are existing, documented limits. The readiness report still marks every topic **Ready for human acceptance**; the boundaries below do not independently make an item unready.

- **Temporary Protection:** exact date and booking process for the next extension window; repeat/interrupted/out-of-band military-document cases; some third-country and stateless eligibility.
- **Residence Options:** how every TPD transition cohort is treated under the 2026 permanent-residence change; whether prior TPD time counts toward EU long-term resident status.
- **Employment:** permit-specific scope and case-specific terms; variable collective-agreement coverage; no guaranteed job, placement, programme, support or payment.
- **Healthcare:** registration cutoff depends on the current EU protection endpoint and stated exceptions; regional fees and clinical decisions vary.
- **Housing:** individual reception placement, location and timing; distinct private-tenancy rules; no general rent reimbursement established by the reviewed housing route.
- **Education:** municipal delivery and course availability; individual admission and placement; future SFI curriculum transition and permit-specific fee evidence.
- **Social Assistance:** benefit-by-benefit eligibility; narrow Social Services Act change effective 2 January 2027; additional-payment boundary unresolved. The unavailable `social-child-benefits` fact remains an evidence gap.
