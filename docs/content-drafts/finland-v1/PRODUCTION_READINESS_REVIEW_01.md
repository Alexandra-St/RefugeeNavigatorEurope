## FI-TP-01 protection-dates resolution

- **EU scheme:** Runs through **4 March 2028**. This is not an individual Finnish permit or physical-card expiry date.
- **Current Finnish permit:** Migri states that Finnish temporary-protection permits are currently valid until **4 March 2027**. Separate expiry situations may apply, including where another Finnish residence permit has been issued.
- **Extension:** Migri says a separate extension application is currently not required and that it extends the permit automatically. Migri will publish further instructions when the 2028 extension rollout begins. Holders should follow those instructions and check their own permit record.
- **Physical card:** Cards printed with expiry dates 4 March 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 remain valid **in Finland** until 4 March 2027 if the underlying permit remains valid. A new card is not needed solely because Migri extends the permit if card details are current.
- **Limits:** The reviewed sources do not establish travel use of an expired-looking card or the validity of an older physical card after 4 March 2027. A lost card or changed personal details may require a separate card application.
