# Finland Compare Review 01

**Review date:** 15 November 2026
**Scope:** Final editorial, source-binding and language review of the 24 facts in `COMPARE_DRAFT_01.md`. No new research or Production changes were made.

## Aspect review

### protection-scheme

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The fact describes defined Finnish eligibility groups and says that nationality, residence or family connection alone does not guarantee eligibility. It remains distinct from ordinary asylum and residence permits. EN/UK/RU preserve the same conditional meaning.
**Changes:** None.

### protection-application

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The in-person police/Border Guard registration route and Migri's decision role match Core. Local intake variation and the absence of an established initial online route are qualified; no appointment or exhaustive document requirement is invented.
**Changes:** None.

### protection-dates

**Result:** REVISED
**Reason:** The revised values preserve the separate EU scheme, Finnish permit and physical-card dates. They limit the card rule to the specified 4 March 2023–2026 printed dates and Finnish use through 4 March 2027, keep permit validity and current card details as conditions, and make the no-new-card statement conditional on current details. Travel use and post-2027 card validity remain unclaimed.
**Changes:** Specified the exact printed card dates and clarified that Migri's no-replacement-card guidance applies when card details are current. Mirrored the clarification in UK/RU.

### residence-routes

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Work, study and family are examples of separate qualifying ordinary routes; the fact does not imply automatic conversion or that a job, study place or relationship grants a permit.
**Changes:** None.

### residence-conditions

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Route-specific legal and evidence requirements are retained, including that temporary-protection time does not count where a route requires qualifying continuous A-permit residence.
**Changes:** None.

### residence-filing

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** First-permit and extended-permit filing rules are distinguished. The fact avoids universal in-country filing, universal pending rights and automatic continuation after temporary protection.
**Changes:** None.

### employment-vacancies

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Job Market Finland, Work in Finland and EURES are accurately presented as official vacancy channels; the fact does not promise exhaustive listings, a job or language sufficiency.
**Changes:** None.

### employment-institutions

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The 1 January 2025 TE Office closure, current municipal employment-area role and separate Job Market Finland/KEHA roles match Core. Responsibility without `kotikunta` remains address- and situation-dependent.
**Changes:** None.

### employment-assistance-conditions

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The responsible authority assesses services and support; the fact separates job-search access and temporary-protection work rights from programme, jobseeker and benefit eligibility.
**Changes:** None.

### work-permission

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The temporary-protection applicant work right begins on police/Border Guard registration; the fact distinguishes the pending certificate from a residence permit and does not extend the rule to ordinary permits.
**Changes:** None.

### work-employer-duties

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Employer verification, record-retention and applicable notification duties are separated from the worker's authorization. The uncertain early-ending notice rule is not generalized.
**Changes:** None.

### work-minimum-pay

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The draft does not invent a national statutory minimum-wage amount; it retains the collective-agreement and usual/reasonable-remuneration qualifications.
**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-access

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Pending/granted status, age and reception-service status remain distinct. Section 26 is bounded to a granted holder covered by active reception services; no identical access is promised at every stage.
**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-proof

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Proof is tied to the applicable route. A personal identity code or Kela card is not represented as universal healthcare entitlement.
**Changes:** None.

### healthcare-urgent

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Emergency and urgent advice numbers are stated with the location qualification; possible billing is explicit, so urgent care is not described as universally free.
**Changes:** None.

### housing-initial

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Reception accommodation depends on capacity. The three-month transition rule is dated, begins at eligibility to apply for `kotikunta`, and retains the unaccompanied-minor exception and non-guaranteed discretionary extension.
**Changes:** None.

### housing-private-rental

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Private rental is presented as an option under the lease and lawful landlord process, not guaranteed accommodation. Acceptance and document requirements remain qualified.
**Changes:** None.

### housing-financial-support

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Kela housing or deposit support remains conditional and separately assessed; application is not entitlement, and reception allowance is not described as private-rent reimbursement.
**Changes:** None.

### education-school-enrolment

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Access to pre-primary/basic education without `kotikunta` is limited to a child living in a municipality and is distinguished from compulsory-education duties. No immediate or preferred placement is promised.
**Changes:** None.

### education-pupil-language

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Preparatory and S2/R2 support is described as dependent on pupil needs and provider/local decisions, not as a guaranteed uniform programme.
**Changes:** None.

### education-higher-admission

**Result:** REVISED
**Reason:** The original wording could imply a general tuition exemption for every granted temporary-protection holder. Core limits the exemption to a qualifying granted Finnish permit and covered degree-leading Bachelor's/Master's programmes taught in a language other than Finnish or Swedish.
**Changes:** Limited the exemption to the qualifying permit and covered programmes in EN/UK/RU; clarified that application-fee, scholarship and Kela student-aid rules are separate.

### social-subsistence

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Reception allowance and Kela basic social assistance remain distinct, eligibility-based systems. No automatic payment, rent reimbursement or regular post-reception support without `kotikunta` is promised.
**Changes:** None.

### social-application

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** The reception-centre and Kela routes are distinguished, non-online Kela application channels are retained, and jobseeker registration is not treated as a benefit decision.
**Changes:** None.

### social-child-benefits

**Result:** PASS
**Reason:** Child and family benefits are not described as automatic. The one-year temporary-protection route is qualified by separate Kela assessment, the dated form and individual family circumstances, including a child abroad.
**Changes:** None.

## protection-dates review

- EU temporary-protection scheme: through **4 March 2028**.
- Current Finnish temporary-protection permit validity: through **4 March 2027**.
- Migri currently extends automatically; a separate extension application is not required, and further 2028 instructions are expected.
- Cards printed with expiry dates **4 March 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026** are covered only for use **in Finland** through 4 March 2027 while the permit is valid. No replacement card is needed solely because of the extension if card details are current.
- No travel-validity claim or old-card-validity claim after 4 March 2027 is made.
- The EU scheme date, Finnish permit validity and printed card date remain distinct.
