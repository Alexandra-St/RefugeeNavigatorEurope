### Language, date, and wording check

- All eight catalog entries have EN, UK, and RU translations. No raw URLs or editorial notes appear in catalog article bodies; research notes remain outside the user-facing text.
- The principal dates in the three languages match: the 5 August 2026 military rule, the 4 August 2026 exception date, and the 4 March 2028 EU endpoint. The extension’s start date, 5 March 2027, is absent from all three TP-01 versions. This is a minor date-context correction, not a translation disagreement.
- The wage amounts are absent from all three Employee Rights versions; this is a shared omission, not a language mismatch.
- The most material language mismatch is in TP-01: EN and UK do not explicitly name the permanent-residence-in-Ukraine cohort, while RU uses the loose phrase “людей с защитой или постоянным проживанием в Украине”. That phrase does not express the researched permanent-residence permit and safe-and-durable-return condition precisely.
- TP-02 RU says that having an ordinary permit does not mean automatic transition. The intended proposition is that the existence of an ordinary route does not establish a direct transition from temporary protection. This changes the subject of the caveat and should be corrected.
- The remaining article translations preserve the same core qualifications and dates in substance. No other cross-language date or numeric conflict was identified.
