### Focused blocker verification

- **RO-TP-01:** EU cohorts are distinguished from Romania’s additional categories; the permanent-residence-in-Ukraine cohort is conditioned on inability to return safely and durably. The military condition and same-Member-State exception (continuous protection in the same Member State by/on 4 August 2026) point to Decision (EU) 2026/1912. The extension is given as 5 March 2027 through 4 March 2028; the Romanian dynamic permit rule is separately cited, without a universal replacement claim.
- **RO-TP-02:** the TP-linked work route is bounded to its statutory TP period and separated from ordinary routes. Ordinary study/family procedures have OUG 194, eVisa and IGI sources. The text distinguishes an ordinary route from a direct TP switch, preserves “not established” rather than “prohibited,” and leaves direct Blue Card transition, pending work rights and TP-time counting unresolved.
- **RO-EMP-02:** EN/UK/RU state 4,325 lei gross/month from 1 July 2026 and 4,582 lei gross/month for construction; gross is not take-home, and sector/date sensitivity is explicit. Law 319/2006 and Law 367/2022 are separately associated with safety and collective-agreement claims.
- **RO-HOUSING-01:** EN/UK/RU state one three-consecutive-month period, 750 lei/month for a single person and 2,000 lei/month for a family, with statutory cohort, local application and later-month conditions. The payment is not actual-rent reimbursement or an ongoing general subsidy. The 30-day rental registration duty is on the qualifying lessor and cites ANAF.

### Other article corrections

- **RO-EMP-01:** ANOFM and EURES claims have separate owned source tokens.
- **RO-HEALTH-01:** CASS may apply to income subject to it, including applicable employment income; this is separate from the special OUG 96 package and does not itself establish ordinary insured classification.
- **RO-EDU-01:** the annual state-funded-place measure has its plan-level source; it is not phrased as a guaranteed admission or funded place.
- **RO-SOC-01:** VMI assessment cites Law 196/2016 and HG 1154/2022; the opening benefit list links each named route to its respective owned source.
