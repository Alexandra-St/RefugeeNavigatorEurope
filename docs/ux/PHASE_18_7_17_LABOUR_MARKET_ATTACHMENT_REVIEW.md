# Phase 18.7.17 — Labour Market Attachment Review

Baseline: `cf394cd5a9767b07d8ac7d62e155f13f384dd311`

## Verdict

`Current wording supported`

The current Residence Options article wording is supported by the existing, article-owned SIRI source. The page contains two distinct pathways: one after a permit extension is refused or a permit is revoked, and another for people who still hold a valid residence and work permit under the Ukraine Special Act. The earlier Phase 18.7.16 note was based on checking only the first pathway and should not be read as a finding that the current-permit route is unsupported.

## Current article wording

The three language versions express the same points:

- EN: “The Labour Market Attachment Scheme describes a separate route for a person with a valid residence and work permit under the Special Act. The application must be submitted before that permit expires.”
- UK: “Схема Labour Market Attachment Scheme передбачає окремий маршрут для людини з чинним дозволом на проживання та роботу за Спеціальним законом. Подати заяву потрібно до завершення цього дозволу.”
- RU: “Labour Market Attachment Scheme описывает отдельный маршрут для человека с действующим разрешением на проживание и работу по Специальному закону. Заявление нужно подать до окончания этого разрешения.”

The following conditions are also stated equivalently in EN/UK/RU: at least two years of legal, continuous employment with the same employer; terms and pay corresponding to Danish standards; full-time work as the general rule, with some industries allowing fewer than 37 hours to count as full time; no state or municipal wage subsidy to the employer; self-employment is not a basis for the scheme; and further requirements apply.

## Official source checked

- SIRI / New to Denmark, “Labour Market Attachment”: [https://nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work/Labout-Market-Attachment](https://nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work/Labout-Market-Attachment)
- Existing owned `OfficialSource` GUID: `4b278bd5-dcc7-4a9d-96eb-ecf20719d9d3`.
- The page was opened directly. In its scheme overview it first describes applications after refusal of an extension or revocation, then explicitly says that a person with a still-valid residence and work permit under the Special Act for displaced persons from Ukraine may also apply and must do so before the permit expires.
- The conditions section confirms at least two years of legal, continuous, unsupported work with the same employer, Danish-standard pay and terms, full-time work as a rule, and industry-specific cases where fewer than 37 hours count as full time. It also says the employer must not have received state or municipal wage subsidies. The page separately excludes self-employment as a basis.

## Source-to-claim assessment

The article’s current-permit route is not conflated with the separate refusal/revocation route: the SIRI page explicitly presents both. The two-year employment and listed work-condition summaries also match the page. The existing owned source is therefore sufficient; no additional source record is needed.

The source URL’s path contains the spelling `Labout-Market-Attachment`. That is the existing catalog URL and it resolves to the official SIRI page; the spelling does not indicate a different route.
