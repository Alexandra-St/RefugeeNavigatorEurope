## Temporary-protection research findings

- EU Decision 2026/1912 extends the scheme for covered cohorts through **4 March 2028**. Swedish guidance differentiates permit cohorts: decisions before **5 August 2026** are stated valid through **4 March 2027**, while decisions on/after that date are stated valid through **4 March 2028**. The EU endpoint is not automatically the date printed on every Swedish permit or card.
- Swedish sources identify conditional applicant cohorts; nationality alone does not establish eligibility. First application uses the Migration Agency route, with an in-person alternative if the e-service cannot be used. Evidence and biometrics are procedural; a receipt is not described as a grant. A granted TPD permit includes work authorization during its validity.
- EU military rule applies from **5 August 2026**, subject to the corrected continuous same-state holder exception. Separate Swedish guidance dated **10 September 2026** gives first-time-applicant instructions and a male 23–65 example with illustrative evidence. Repeat, interrupted and all out-of-band cases remain unresolved; do not import Czech rules.
- A next extension opportunity was expected in early 2027 but was not shown open at the research date. The former 4 March 2026 deadline relates to the previous cycle.
- Population registration is distinct from immigration status. Swedish law sets a first 12-month period and expected additional six-month stay test. An older Tax Agency date tied to the former EU endpoint must not be reused.
- Travel, moving abroad, support consequences and formal transfer are distinct events; ordinary movement is not automatic transfer.

## Phase 17.1B research findings

- **Job search (SE-EMP-01):** Arbetsförmedlingen provides Platsbanken, registration and planning-meeting routes. Support type depends on time in Sweden, population-registration circumstances and programme-specific criteria. UHR qualification evaluation and authorization for regulated professions are separate.
- **Work rights (SE-EMP-02):** a granted Swedish temporary-protection permit carries work authorization during its validity. Employer tax notifications are separate from authorization. Swedish law requires written information on material employment terms; it does not support a blanket claim that every employment agreement must be signed in writing. Ordinary hours and leave have statutory baselines and scope/collective-agreement qualifications. Sweden has no general statutory national minimum-wage amount; wage-setting commonly uses collective agreements.
- **Healthcare (SE-HEALTH-01):** healthcare is distinct from residence-based social insurance. Population-registered TPD holders receive healthcare and dental care on the same terms as other registered residents; unregistered children under 18 receive the same terms as other children in the region; unregistered adults have specified TPD healthcare rights, including care that cannot be postponed, with urgent needs assessed individually. Regional fees and service routes must not be generalized.
- **Housing (SE-HOUSING-01):** a person may arrange accommodation independently or ask about a possible Migration Agency offer. Own housing costs are the person’s responsibility; an offer depends on availability, location cannot be chosen, and the Agency’s page estimates that an offer may take up to a month. Private tenancy rules differ by arrangement. The 1 October 2026 Reception Act notice says its new geographical restrictions and attendance checks do not apply to TPD holders from Ukraine.

### Resolved population-registration / social-insurance source conflict

The current Act (2024:691) §5 and Skatteverket’s operational guidance control the folkbokföring threshold: no population registration during the first 12 months of combined covered permit validity; afterwards registration applies if regular overnight stay is expected for at least a further six months and other conditions are met. Skatteverket decides each case. Försäkringskassan’s English “less than two years” summary is preserved as stale/misleading conflicting wording, not used as the registration rule. Folkbokföring is legally distinct from being “bosatt” under the Social Insurance Code: Chapter 5 §3 generally excludes covered TPD permit holders from that residence-based benefits test, subject to its stated exception. Healthcare is governed separately. Detailed benefit and social-assistance eligibility remains deferred to Phase 17.1C.

## Residence research findings

- Since **11 June 2026**, Act 2026:456 provides a limited in-country application path for specified work, self-employment, research, higher-education or EU Blue Card permits, subject to each route's normal requirements. Certain derivative family applications are included.
- If the current permit expires before decision, filing must have been made before expiry for the statutory exception. Filing does not guarantee a permit; a grant may affect support and housing.
- Asylum-related spårbyte ended on **1 April 2025** and is not the 2026 TPD-holder path.
- Swedish permanent residence is route-specific, not automatic after TPD time. The reviewed July 2026 guidance does not clearly settle every TPD category.
- EU long-term resident status is distinct from Swedish PR. Current TP beneficiaries/applicants are excluded from the Directive; prior TP-time counting after transition is not explicit in reviewed sources.

## Phase 17.1C research findings

- **Education (SE-EDU-01):** Skolverket distinguishes school rights from compulsory-school duty by whether a child is or should be population-registered. Municipal responsibility and national reception timelines do not guarantee a chosen school or immediate placement. Language support is individually/locally arranged; mother-tongue teaching has separate criteria. TPD alone does not establish SFI or komvux entitlement. Higher-education admission depends on documented qualifications and programme requirements. From **1 October 2026**, a temporary Swedish residence permit for a reason other than studies generally exempts its holder from application and tuition fees, subject to exceptions and proof requirements. UHR qualification evaluation is not a Swedish qualification, licence or admission decision.
- **Social assistance (SE-SOC-01):** Migration Agency reception support, municipal economic assistance, establishment-programme compensation, residence-based social insurance and work-based benefits are separate systems. Covered TPD holders may apply for Migration Agency daily allowance/special grant subject to resource and statutory conditions; the daily-allowance calculation depends in part on own or Agency accommodation and whether food is provided. Support changes when population registration takes effect, but no other benefit follows automatically. The Social Services Act currently bars Chapter 12 §1 assistance while covered by the Reception Act; its amended rule takes effect **2 January 2027** and must be rechecked before publication. Establishment support requires programme-specific assessment and participation; compensation is separately applied for. Folkbokföring does not itself satisfy the Social Insurance Code’s “bosatt” test for residence-based benefits. Socialstyrelsen gives a registration-date boundary for housing/child allowance, not an automatic award. The reviewed sources did not establish an additional current nationwide cash payment solely for TPD holders; this is a research limit, not proof that no other support exists.

## Sweden terminology boundaries

- **tillfälligt skydd / uppehållstillstånd:** temporary protection is the protection framework; a residence permit is an individual Swedish status document/decision under that framework.
- **uppehållstillståndskort:** residence-permit card; its validity dates are not automatically identical to the EU scheme endpoint.
- **folkbokföring / personnummer / samordningsnummer:** population registration, personal identity number and coordination number are distinct administrative concepts; none alone determines every immigration, education or benefit right.
- **bosatt under Socialförsäkringsbalken:** a statutory residence concept for social-insurance rules, not a synonym for folkbokföring.
- **Arbetsförmedlingen:** Swedish Public Employment Service; establishment-programme participation is distinct from ordinary jobseeker registration.
- **SFI / komvux:** Swedish for Immigrants and municipal adult education; eligibility differs and must not be inferred from TPD alone.
- **UHR:** Swedish Council for Higher Education; qualification evaluation is distinct from licensing and admissions.
- **dagersättning / särskilt bidrag:** Migration Agency daily allowance and special grant under reception-support rules; neither is an unconditional general benefit.
- **etableringsprogrammet / etableringsersättning:** employment-service programme and related compensation; participation and payment require their own conditions and decisions.
- **ekonomiskt bistånd / försörjningsstöd:** municipal economic assistance / income support, subject to the applicable law and individual assessment.
- **1177 / 112:** healthcare information and advice / emergency number; use only in the healthcare context and do not confuse general advice with emergency response.

## Conflicts and publication boundaries

Permit dates refer to distinct scopes/cohorts. The old extension page describes a previous cycle. Swedish military guidance does not settle all repeat/interrupted/out-of-band cases. Treatment of all EU third-country/stateless cohorts remains unclear in Swedish public guidance. The Tax Agency page retains an older endpoint-linked cutoff. The 2026 transition is limited and does not waive normal requirements. Whether prior TP time counts toward EU long-term resident status after transition is unresolved. Do not make individual eligibility conclusions.

For drafting and any later release, retain these additional boundaries explicitly:

- EU temporary protection runs through **4 March 2028**, while Swedish permit information distinguishes cohorts through **4 March 2027** and **4 March 2028**. Do not treat those dates as interchangeable or infer an individual card’s validity.
- The next Swedish extension operation expected in 2027 was not open or operationally settled on the research date. Recheck the official portal; do not imply registration or booking is available.
- Swedish military-obligation guidance is specific to a dated first-time-applicant cohort and does not resolve repeat, interrupted or out-of-band cases. Do not extend the Czech age-specific rule to Sweden.
- Swedish guidance remains incomplete for some third-country-national/stateless cohorts under the EU scheme.
- Folkbokföring is distinct from a residence permit, the first-12-month rule and later stay test; registration is a separate Tax Agency decision. It is also distinct from “bosatt” under the Social Insurance Code.
- The in-country residence-transition route effective **11 June 2026** is limited to enumerated permit categories and ordinary route requirements; it is not a general status switch. Prior temporary-protection time toward EU long-term resident status remains unresolved in the reviewed sources.
- Sweden has no statutory nationwide minimum-wage amount. Do not supply a legal national rate; collective-agreement coverage and terms vary.
- Healthcare access and charges depend on age, population-registration status, clinical circumstances and region; do not describe all healthcare as free or identical.
- Housing support is conditional, and private tenancy rules differ by tenancy form. Do not promise accommodation, location choice or rent reimbursement.
- Social support is system-specific, means-tested or programme-dependent. TPD alone does not guarantee cash support; registration does not automatically create ordinary social-insurance entitlement. Keep the current Social Services Act rule separate from the amendment effective **2 January 2027**.
- School right, compulsory duty, language support, adult education, admission and fee exemption each have separate conditions. Recheck SFI course changes effective **1 January 2027**, the current fee rules, school-year guidance and admission instructions.
- The child/housing allowance registration-date boundary is a cohort rule subject to individual statutory conditions; it is not an award guarantee. The reviewed sources did not establish an additional current nationwide TPD-only payment, which must remain a bounded research statement.
