# Structured comparison — claim-level evidence audit

All 72 country answers remain Draft. This is an internal editorial mapping of approved project evidence, not a new source-verification event or legal approval. Existing source LastCheckedAt values are preserved. No page is represented as reopened during this refinement.

## Method and limits

Each populated answer was compared with a specific passage of the approved English article and the corresponding source-and-claim matrix where available. Only the sources supporting that particular wording were selected; article-wide and first-three-source assignment rules have been removed. The UK and RU answers were reviewed against the same EN claim, including dates, conditions and exceptions. Automated tests check relationships and rendering, not legal truth.

If the approved material does not establish the question’s answer, the fact is unavailable, has no source IDs and uses the existing localized fallback. A missing answer is not evidence that a requirement does not exist.

The Denmark job-search pilot has no dedicated matrix in the current research tree; its narrow portal/service facts are mapped to the explicit approved passage and matching source page title/function. No assistance-eligibility claim was extrapolated from portal access.
