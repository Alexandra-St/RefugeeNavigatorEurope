# Country Comparison V2 — approved

V2 presents three concise, side-by-side questions for each of the eight existing comparison topics. It retains the verified V1 article comparison and makes the full article comparison a keyboard-accessible disclosure below the structured facts.

## Structured facts

`comparison-facts.json` contains 24 aspects across eight topics and three countries. It has 72 country answers: 70 populated, source-linked facts and two unavailable answers. Each populated fact is linked to the mapped article and only the existing official-source records selected for that claim. The catalog contains no source titles, URLs, or verification dates; the page resolves those from the existing official-source records, preserving their recorded `LastCheckedAt` values.

All 72 answers have the `Approved` editorial status, recorded by the project owner on 17 October 2026. Questions and answers are localized in Ukrainian, English, and Russian. The 70 populated answers link to existing official-source records; two answers remain unavailable, are approved as accurate statements of evidence gaps, and have no source IDs. This editorial approval is not independent legal certification.

The internal [claim-level evidence audit](COUNTRY_COMPARISON_FACT_AUDIT.md) maps every country answer to the exact supporting passage in the approved article, relevant claim IDs where a research matrix exists, selected source records and their historical check dates, and the applicable limits. Source records’ historical check dates were not reset. The §4a(5) text was checked through the official page’s indexed content after a direct page open timed out; see the audit’s source-access note. The Denmark employment pilot has no dedicated matrix; its bounded portal/service statements are mapped to the approved article passage and the matching official page, without inferring access to employment assistance.

## Display and release boundary

With the current fully approved catalog, Development and Production show structured V2 first without Draft preview notices. The V1 comparison appears beneath it under the localized “Read full country articles” disclosure, collapsed by default. It retains existing article summaries, status and update dates, full formatted content, source links, and links to country/category pages. Development continues to support future Draft facts with the existing preview notice and labels.

The existing production gate suppresses structured V2 while any fact is Draft and exposes it only when every fact is Approved. Under the approved catalog, Production shows V2 and keeps full V1 articles accessible in the collapsed disclosure. Startup validation checks structure, relationships, language coverage and valid statuses, not legal accuracy. No deployment was performed as part of this approval.

## Editorial review

The project owner approved the 24 aspects and all 72 localized answers on 17 October 2026. Facts are not rankings and do not decide personal eligibility. The two unavailable items remain approved statements of evidence gaps:

- Denmark: conditions for access to public employment assistance.
- Germany: school-pupil-specific language support.

Questions, qualifications, source relationships and equivalent UK/EN/RU meaning were included in the owner’s editorial approval. Approval does not independently certify legal accuracy; the documented evidence limitations and publication-time freshness checks remain applicable.
