## Implementation

The guide reuses the existing step-by-step guide model and generic country/slug route. It contains four situations and sixteen ordered recommendation steps. Steps A–C link to the approved Poland temporary-protection article; situation D links to the approved residence-options article. Each step retains the proposal's ClaimIds and only source IDs belonging to its linked article.

The Poland Temporary Protection category callout is generated by the existing guide discovery logic. It appears in Development while the guide is Draft and remains absent in Production. The route uses the same gate: Draft content is available for local Development preview only. The guide has not been approved or published, and no deployment was performed.

No application code or guide schema changes were needed. The generic route already resolves the country and guide slugs; regression tests cover Poland alongside the existing Denmark and Germany guides.

## Editorial and legal boundaries

The guide is a non-personalized navigation aid, not an eligibility assessment, status checker, or recommendation about which application to file. It does not collect answers, passport information, or documents, and does not store progress.

The content is based on the existing approved Poland articles and the Phase 12.1 proposal's recorded research. No official source was reopened for this implementation, so the existing research check date is not a freshness guarantee. The guide remains Draft pending visual and editorial acceptance and the proposal's publication-time rechecks.

The content keeps separate:

- EU temporary protection through 4 March 2028, applying from 5 March 2027;
- the Article 106 rule for its defined statutory group, including exclusions and ending events;
- PESEL and a PESEL UKR record;
- the cohort-specific identity-confirmation deadline of 31 August 2026 and reported effect from 1 September 2026;
- the older UdSC paper-certificate transition through 4 March 2027, subject to earlier-ending events;
- CUKR as a separate three-year temporary residence permit, its cumulative and child-specific conditions, and the recorded MOS filing deadline of 4 March 2027;
- filing a residence application and the grant of a permit.

The KSI page's recorded 30/31 July dates were stale relative to the corrected EU instrument's 4/5 August corrigendum dates. The guide does not rely on the stale dates to determine eligibility or an individual case. The publication-time review must recheck the corrigendum and whether KSI has corrected its page.
