Sources were directly reopened on 18 October 2026 and their relevant content reviewed:

- [Act of 23 January 2026, Dz.U. 2026 item 203](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/203/text/I/D20260203.pdf): Article 110a's any-gmina route and 30-day deadline; Article 109b ending events; and the Act's transition rules. The official consolidated text of the Ukraine assistance Act, [Dz.U. 2026 item 1293](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/1293/text/I/D20261293.pdf), records the legal state through 8 September 2026 and confirms the 31 August identity-confirmation deadline and 1 September status consequence for the defined declaration-based cohort.
- [Gov.pl PESEL service](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/uzyskaj-numer-pesel-oraz-profil-zaufany--usluga-dla-obywateli-ukrainy): current in-person procedure and local-instruction caveats for children and missing-document situations.
- [UdSC identity-confirmation notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/wazna-informacja-dla-obywateli-ukrainy-posiadajacych-numer-pesel-ze-statusem-ukr): the defined declaration-only cohort, 31 August 2026 deadline, and stated consequence from 1 September 2026. No individual record or status was inferred.
- [Act on granting protection to foreigners, consolidated text Dz.U. 2026 item 862](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/862/text/I/D20260862.pdf): Article 106's defined statutory scope and Article 109a exclusions/Article 109b ending events; no material change to the guide's treatment was identified.
- [Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2026/1912/oj/eng/pdf), read with the [5 August 2026 corrigendum](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2026/1912/corrigendum/2026-08-05/oj/eng): EU temporary protection through 4 March 2028, applying from 5 March 2027; corrected military-rule dates of 4 August and 5 August 2026.
- [UdSC extension notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/przedluzenie-ochrony-czasowej-do-4-marca-2028-r) and the [KSI page](https://www.gov.pl/web/krajowa-siec-integracji/przedluzenie-ochrony-czasowej-do-4-marca-2028-r): UdSC presents the corrected dates; KSI still displays the superseded 30/31 July dates. The guide continues to identify KSI as stale and does not treat those dates as governing law.
- [UdSC 2026 changes notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/zmiany-w-szczegolnych-zasadach-dotyczacych-legalnosci-pobytu-obywateli-ukrainy-na-terytorium-rzeczypospolitej-polskiej) and [MRPiPS 2026 assistance notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/co-sie-zmienia-w-pomocy-dla-obywateli-ukrainy-w-2026-r): current transition context and the Ministry page's 4 March 2027 statement. The guide attributes that older page and distinguishes EU protection, Article 106's scope, PESEL UKR records, and individual document validity rather than treating one date as automatically deciding an individual case.
- [UdSC CUKR procedure](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/CUKR-procedura) and [UdSC MOS information](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/info-mos): CUKR remains a separate three-year temporary residence permit; the listed cumulative conditions and separate child-specific rules remain; filing is through MOS; the recorded filing deadline remains 4 March 2027.

The direct UdSC page for [discontinuation of former protection certificates](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/zaprzestanie-wydawania-zaswiadczen-potwierdzajacych-korzystanie-z-ochrony-czasowej) timed out during this recheck, so its `LastCheckedAt` was not refreshed. The transition through 4 March 2027, subject to earlier exceptions including assignment of PESEL UKR, was independently confirmed in Article 20 of Act 203 and the current UdSC news listing.

No source was added and no source URL or ownership relationship was changed. `LastCheckedAt` was refreshed only for records whose relevant source content was successfully reopened and read; the two former-certificate source records retain their previous check timestamp.

## Scope and safeguards

The guide remains a non-personalized navigation aid, not an eligibility assessment or status checker. It does not collect answers, passport information, or documents, and does not store progress. It preserves the separation between EU temporary-protection duration, Polish statutory stay under Article 106, PESEL UKR records, paper certificates, and individual permits. It does not infer an individual's eligibility, status, or document validity.

Publication approval does not authorize deployment. No Render configuration was changed and no deployment was performed.
