### HU-TP-01

**COMPLETE** — bounded eligibility and in-person process have evidence; individual no-document and military-obligation determinations remain qualified.

- Eligibility covers the national-law cohorts: qualifying Ukrainian citizens displaced on/after 24 February 2022 who resided in Ukraine before that date; non-Ukrainian/stateless people with qualifying protection in Ukraine; and qualifying family members. A Ukrainian residence permit alone and the optional EU categories do not establish eligibility in Hungary.
- Hungarian citizens (including dual nationals) are not recognized as foreign TP beneficiaries under OIF guidance. Special pre-24-February 2022 Ukrainian residents already in Hungary are directed to ordinary immigration routes.
- Applicant must establish the relevant cohort and family link. OIF lists passport or other available documents; no complete no-document fallback or exhaustive checklist is established.
- Apply personally to OIF/NDGAP. After optional online data entry, attend a Government Office; otherwise attend the competent OIF regional directorate. Online entry is preparation, not filing. Identity checks, photo and fingerprints (age 14+) occur in person. Do not promise a guaranteed walk-in or appointment-free slot.
- Keep pending humanitarian proof, granted biometric beneficiary document, and address card distinct. No `lakcímkártya` is issued per OIF; report accommodation changes. Statutory decision period is 45 days; no same-day card timing is established.
- Another Member State’s active TP is incompatible with a new Hungarian grant under the reviewed OIF/law materials; transfer mechanics are not established.

### HU-TP-02

**COMPLETE** — options are mapped with route-specific eligibility and filing caveats; no universal in-country switch or pending-right bridge is asserted.

- No automatic conversion to an ordinary permit and no enacted Hungarian post-4 March 2028 transition instrument were identified. Council Recommendation C/2025/5129 is nonbinding.
- National Card: Ukraine remains on the official nationality list. A qualifying employment/business basis is also required. Nationality eligibility does not establish that an active `menedékes` can file domestically or switch during TP.
- Hungarian Card: eligible only for listed professions/activities with required qualifications and contract. General domestic filing instructions do not establish an active-TP switch.
- Employment-purpose permit: OIF’s current list (Georgia, Armenia, Philippines) excludes Ukraine. New guest-worker applications have not been accepted since 6 June 2026; transitional legacy cases do not create a new Ukrainian route.
- EU Blue Card and study permits cannot be granted while the person is a Hungarian TP beneficiary. Ordinary family reunification is separate from TP family unity; `menedékes` is not established as an ordinary sponsor. Guest self-employment is a distinct route with its own conditions and no established TP switch.
- National Residence Card and EU Residence Card are distinct. TP-time credit is explicit for the EU card but TP beneficiaries cannot receive it while protected; the National Residence Card’s TP-time credit is not established.

## Military-obligation rule

**FINAL RESULT:** Corrected Decision (EU) 2026/1912, Article 2 applies from **5 August 2026**. Its exception covers a person who enjoyed TP in a given Member State before or on **4 August 2026** and continuously retains it in that same state thereafter. EU law is the binding condition for new grants, including proof where applicable. OIF’s current public notice says Hungary’s national eligibility terms are unchanged and says military-liable Ukrainians may receive TP under the old rules; Hungarian Decree 145/2026 likewise states that Hungary maintains its national personal scope. That public implementation position conflicts with the EU decision. Do not present OIF/UNHCR older/general wording as overriding the corrected EU rule or promise an individual outcome. Direct affected applicants to OIF for the evidence procedure and flag the unresolved implementation conflict.

## Temporary-protection dates

**FINAL RESULT:** EU temporary-protection period and the Hungarian TP document/status framework run through **4 March 2028** under the corrected EU decision and Hungarian Decree 145/2026 amending Decree 196/2025. OIF says existing documents remain valid by law irrespective of earlier printed dates and no replacement is needed solely for this extension. An old OIF validity page still contains a contradictory 2027 sentence; use the newer OIF notice and current law. Keep the EU period, Hungarian legal extension and individual physical card expiry distinct; do not infer status/document validity solely from the EU horizon.

## Residence filing

**FINAL RESULT:** No universal active-TP switch/filer rule is established. Route eligibility, in-country submission, pending lawful stay/work, and effect on TP are separate. §1(3) Asylum Act says an existing title under that Act is withdrawn simultaneously with grant of a new residence permit; it does not prove that every ordinary route accepts an active-TP application, nor a stay/work bridge after TP expiry. The prior HU-TP-01 blanket statement that TP simply continues pending and ends on any permit grant is **INCORRECT** as an unqualified universal claim. For a specific case, ask OIF about that exact route, filing location, pending rights, and status consequences.

## Post-TP transition

**FINAL RESULT:** No enacted Hungarian post-4 March 2028 transition permit or automatic grace mechanism was identified in the reviewed sources. EU Recommendation C/2025/5129 and Commission/UNHCR transition materials are recommendations/guidance, not enacted Hungarian law. Recheck before publication.

### HU-HEALTH-01 — COMPLETE

- **TP package:** Act L of 2025 §19 and Decree 196/2025 §3 provide defined healthcare bases for recognized beneficiaries and formally filed pending applicants. Online preparation is not filing. Applicant coverage follows the pending legal status; rejection/withdrawal ends it when that status ends. Ordinary insurance, TP entitlement and TAJ registration are distinct. Work is not required and no automatic cancellation by employment was identified.
- **Coverage/cost:** primary care; urgent specialist/hospital care; necessary urgent follow-up to recovery/stabilization; prenatal/obstetric care; mandatory age-based vaccinations; limited dental care; specified medicines; and defined oncology/chronic care. Not every service or prescription is free.
- **Proof:** bring beneficiary/applicant status evidence and official accommodation evidence; show TAJ if held. A TAJ card is not stated as a prerequisite. The current TAJ-less provider workflow remains unclear because Decree 171/2022 was repealed.
- **Urgent:** 112 for life-threatening emergencies. 1830 is nationwide OMSZ urgent out-of-hours primary care, not the ambulance emergency number; weekdays 16:00–08:00, weekends/public holidays 24 hours. Rechecked against current OMSZ source.
- **Employment:** work may create ordinary insured status, separate from TP entitlement; individual insurance transition is a NEAK/NAV record question.

### HU-HOUSING-01 — COMPLETE

- **Initial housing:** `housing-initial` is **UNAVAILABLE** as a verified nationwide TP first-night route. Current official sources do not establish a single intake contact, hours, referral/self-referral procedure, same-day placement or capacity fallback. BMSZKI Budapest night shelters are general homelessness services with variable admission/capacity, not TP reception.
- **Public accommodation/support:** Decree 196/2025 recognizes conditional designated accommodation and local/provider funding with eligibility/time limits. It does not guarantee a bed or universal direct cash entitlement. Recognized beneficiaries are excluded from ordinary reception-centre accommodation under the current rule.
- **Financial support:** employer-side housing scheme through 4 March 2028 for qualifying post-recognition Hungary employment of at least 20 hours weekly; employer applies/receives, stated cap HUF 120,000 in designated preferred small settlements / HUF 80,000 elsewhere. Municipal aid depends on local rules. No nationwide direct TP rent/deposit/utility benefit found.
- **Private rental:** written lease required. Three months' rent is a court-review threshold for grossly excessive security, not a hard deposit cap. Landlord acceptance is not guaranteed.
- **Address:** OIF accommodation reporting is distinct from a lease and ordinary `lakcímkártya`; applicant reporting is required, but reviewed guidance does not establish a universal deadline for this situation.
