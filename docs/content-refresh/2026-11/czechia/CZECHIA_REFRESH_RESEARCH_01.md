## H–J. Temporary protection, extension and military condition

EU temporary protection is the EU framework, currently extended to **4 March 2028** by Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912. Czech dočasná ochrana and the individual Czech visa/document date are separate. Czech guidance says successful completion of its next extension’s online registration and in-person visa-sticker stages will extend Czech TP to **31 March 2028**. The operational next-cycle dates had not been announced publicly by the as-of date. The 2026 cycle is closed: registration deadline **15 March 2026**, personal sticker visit by **30 September 2026**, successful extension to **31 March 2027**.

Rules effective **5 August 2026** govern affected Ukrainian citizens aged **18–59** subject to military obligations filing initial, repeat, or family-cohabitation TP requests. Ages 18–22: Reserv+ registration and printed eVOD. Ages 23–59: printed eVOD showing removal from military obligation. OAMP checks the current profile directly in Reserv+; a screenshot is not enough. Alternatives: consular-certified paper eVOD issued no more than **3 working days** before filing, or a valid Ukrainian passport with a qualifying post-decision exit stamp plus eVOD. The rule does not apply to ordinary extensions. A person holding valid TP in the same Member State on **4 August 2026** and maintaining it continuously there is within the stated exemption. This date is based on the corrigendum to Decision (EU) 2026/1912, which changes the originally published 30 July cutoff. Do not generalize the exception across states.

Czech first/repeat/family filing routes and eligibility depend on the circumstances and applicable current instructions. Applications are personal through the competent KACPU or OAMP route; evidence depends on route. Other-state protection/residence history is assessed under Czech and EU rules; a prior foreign document alone does not establish a universal answer. Proof of filing does not itself establish broad work, healthcare, housing, benefits, education, or travel rights. A repeat grant is re-assessed under current eligibility and does not automatically restore residence continuity or reset time-limited benefits.

## K. Special long-term residence (ZDP)

ZDP is a distinct optional five-year residence status, not an automatic extension of TP and not permanent residence. The 2026 cycle required expression of interest **1–30 April 2026**, cumulative eligibility assessment, then registration **1 October–31 December 2026** for the cohort that passed; biometrics/card collection complete the process, and the status is acquired on card collection. Conditions include two years’ TP as of **1 April 2026** (under-18 exemption applies only to that duration test), continuous TP for all relevant household members through card collection, 2025 gross household income of **CZK 440,000 + CZK 110,000** per additional jointly assessed person, the HuD exclusion/reference period, insurance and no arrears, residence/housing, valid travel document and school conditions for children. An earlier expression of interest is not approval. ZDP brings a different five-year residence and labour-access framework; insurance, benefits, and family effects differ from TP, so the content avoids suggesting unchanged TP rights.

## L–N. Employment and wages

Valid Czech TP gives free labour-market access without a separate work permit. This does not itself authorize every regulated profession or equalize employee work with self-employment: business activity requires the relevant trade/professional registration and separate tax/insurance compliance. From **1 April 2026**, employers must report the foreign worker before work begins through the applicable JMHZ/REGZEC system, report changes and end of employment within **8 calendar days**, and retain the prescribed records for **3 years** after employment ends.

2026 statutory minimum wage for the standard 40-hour week: **CZK 22,400/month; CZK 134.40/hour**. It is distinct from guaranteed salary. Guaranteed salary applies to public services/administration salary employees, not private-sector wages generally. 2026 groups 1–4: **22,400/134.40; 26,880/161.30; 31,360/188.20; 35,840/215.10** (monthly/hourly CZK). Announced from **1 January 2027**: minimum **24,900/148.30**; groups 1–4 **24,900/148.30; 29,880/178.00; 34,860/207.70; 39,840/237.30**. The 2027 figures remain future values as of 17 November 2026.

## O–R. Healthcare, housing and social support

TP grant gives participation in Czech public health insurance; this is separate from who pays the premium. For a first TP grant through **10 February 2025**, the initial state-paid premium period is **150 days**; from **11 February 2025**, **90 days**. A repeat grant does not restart the exhausted initial period. Afterward payer responsibility depends on employment, self-employment, age, vulnerable/state-insured status, or other statutory category. “Insured” does not mean every service is free; scope, medicines, dental care and charges depend on applicable insurance rules.

State humanitarian accommodation is free for at most **90 days**, counted from the day after the first TP grant; repeat grants do not restart it. Private rent is governed by ordinary tenancy law. HuD is means-tested and household-based; the housing-cost component is a calculation input subject to accommodation/evidence and vulnerability rules, not a guaranteed rent refund. Other benefits have their own status, insurance, residence and means tests; no universal separate child benefit is inferred.

## Q. Education

School access and compulsory education are covered with current-year and capacity qualifications. For 2026/27, MŠMT’s measure effective **20 October 2026** gives eligible Ukraine-law applicants **25% extra written-test time** and the right to a translation dictionary for secondary-school, conservatory and higher-professional-school admission tests, subject to application documentation and exceptions where a different statutory adjustment applies. This is not a universal adjustment for all education or university admissions. Higher education institution/programme admission, language and tuition conditions are not generalized from one institution.
