## High-risk verification notes

- **EU Decision (EU) 2026/1912:** Current consolidated text/corrigendum makes military-obligation compliance, with proof where applicable, a condition for grants from 5 August 2026. The exception requires protection in the same Member State by 4 August and continuous retention there. Production states those conditions and separates them from the German 90-day title exemption.
- **Germany4Ukraine discrepancy:** Its current 23 September 2026 notice still says the exception applies to people already living in the EU, including if new military obligations later arose. The corrected decision is narrower. Production describes the discrepancy accurately and defers individual cross-state cases to the competent immigration authority.
- **Entry and §24 procedure:** UkraineAufenthÜV's 90-day rule, 4 December 2027 entry cutoff, other-EU-protection exclusion and German military-obligation condition from 9 October 2026 remain distinct from an issued §24 permit. The article does not present the Brandenburg online application route as nationwide and states that local appointment/online routes vary.
- **§81/Fiktionswirkung:** Production distinguishes lawful-presence cases, timely renewal of an existing title, the certificate's legal basis, and work conditions. It does not imply that any pending application or Fiktionsbescheinigung automatically grants identical stay, travel, or work rights.
- **Alternative residence:** Current §20a restriction and §39 procedure remain correctly qualified; §39 is not itself permission, and a §24 title is not described as automatically convertible to a Chancenkarte or another route.
- **Minimum wage:** Current general statutory floor is **€13.90 gross per hour** from 1 January 2026. The adopted general rate is **€14.60 gross per hour** from 1 January 2027. Both dates and the gross/hour unit are correct; no sectoral floor is presented as the universal rate.
- **Benefits and Kindergeld:** The current 2026 SGB II/SGB XII figures (€563/€506/€451/€471/€390/€357), AsylbLG totals (€455/€409/€365/€405/€337/€309), and Kindergeld (€259 per eligible child monthly) match current official schedules. The article preserves eligibility conditions and does not promise these standard rates as a guaranteed total award.
- **Current law vs proposal:** The 13th SGB II Amendment Act is enacted, with main provisions effective 1 July 2026; its housing changes are presented as current. The Ukraine-specific Leistungsrechtsanpassungsgesetz remains unpublished as law and is still a government bill in current BMAS/Bundestag materials; production calls it a bill/proposal, not an operative rule.
- **Healthcare, housing, education:** The routes under SGB V/SGB II/SGB XII/§264/AsylbLG remain distinguished; local card and treatment procedures are qualified. Housing assignment, local cost limits, heating, prior assurances and the 1 July 2026 cap remain conditional. School administration and compulsory-school processes remain Land/local; federal childcare statutory entitlements are separated from a guaranteed local place.
