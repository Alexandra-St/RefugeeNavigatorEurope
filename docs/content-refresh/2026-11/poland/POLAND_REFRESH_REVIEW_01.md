## D. Numeric and date review

**Result: ACCEPT — 51/51.** The high-risk dates and amounts were checked against primary or competent official evidence. In particular:

The staged wording keeps the EU horizon distinct from national lawful-stay implementation and older certificates. [Decision 2026/1912](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026D1912), [corrigendum](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202690680).
- Poland's current consolidated Foreigners Protection Act is Dz.U. 2026 item 862, in force; Article 106 is the Polish statutory bridge, subject to its defined scope and exceptions. The staged source title was corrected to identify this Act accurately. [ELI item 862](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/862/ogl).
- The 2026 rates are PLN 4,806/month and PLN 31.40/hour from 1 January 2026. The enacted 2027 rates are PLN 4,950/month and PLN 32.30/hour from 1 January 2027. [2026 regulation, item 1242](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1242/ogl), [2027 regulation, item 1213](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/1213/ogl).
- NFZ confirms the narrower post-5 March 2026 healthcare framework and the covered UKR categories; its published guidance distinguishes those entitlements from full insurance coverage. [NFZ guidance](https://www.nfz.gov.pl/aktualnosci/aktualnosci-centrali/pacjent-z-ukrainy-co-sie-zmienilo-od-5-marca-2026-r-%2C8907.html).
- The PESEL UKR identity deadline/consequence, 30-day application rule, CUKR dates and duration, housing transition date, and education/school-year dates remain supported by their named official service, act, or agency records. No numeric/date edit was required.

## E. Topic review and material evidence sufficiency

**TP / PESEL UKR — ACCEPT.** The Council decision and corrigendum support the EU period and start date. The current consolidated Act supports the Article 106 role. UdSC's certificate notice supports the separate historical-document period. The PESEL service and UdSC identity notice support the application and identity steps. The Ministry page showing the earlier 4 March 2027 horizon is explicitly treated as superseded for the EU extension; it is not used as current operative horizon evidence.

**CUKR — ACCEPT.** UdSC's current procedure and FAQ identify the eligible cohort, three-year duration, MOS-only filing, dates, uninterrupted-status condition, collection effects, and pending-stage limits. The FAQ is not used to establish the EU protection horizon. [UdSC FAQ](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/cukr-QA), [procedure](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/CUKR-procedura).

**Employment and wages — ACCEPT.** PIP supports the Ukraine temporary-protection work route, notification within seven days through praca.gov.pl, and written-contract requirements. The two wage regulations support the distinct current/future rates; future rates are explicitly labeled FUTURE. The text does not equate gross statutory rates with take-home pay or immigration salary thresholds.

**Healthcare — ACCEPT.** NFZ's March 2026 guidance supports limited public-service coverage, POZ and reimbursed medicines for the named categories, with insurance as a separate route. The article preserves exclusions and asks users to verify specific service and coverage; urgent-care guidance does not make entitlement promises.

**Housing — ACCEPT.** Official government material supports the 30 June transition end, current vulnerable-group collective accommodation and local implementation. Private rental claims are general precautions; the Tenant Rights Protection Act is used only as general statutory background. [Official accommodation guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-mazowiecki/pomoc-obywatelom-ukrainy).

**Education — ACCEPT.** The Education Law consolidated text and Ministry/NAWA guidance support the bounded claims. Discretionary support, local arrangements, and institution-specific admissions are not stated universally. [Education Law item 820](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/820/ogl), [NAWA secondary certificate guidance](https://www.nawa.gov.pl/uznawalnosc/wydawanie-informacji-o-zagranicznych-dyplomach/swiadectwo-szkoly-sredniej).

**Social benefits — ACCEPT.** Current ZUS/MRPiPS sources distinguish 800+ conditions and the Dobry Start rules from general social assistance. The existing ZUS 800+ evidence supports amount, monthly nature, and application/decision role; MRPiPS supports the current foreign-national/UKR rules. Dobry Start's PLN 300 annual amount is supported by ZUS, while the current 2026/27 activity and Polish-school requirements are supported by MRPiPS. [MRPiPS 800+ rules](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/nowe-zasady-przyznawania-swiadczenia-wychowawczego-cudzoziemcom), [Dobry Start 2026 changes](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/zmiany-w-programie-dobry-start-obowiazujace-od-1-lipca-2026-r), [ZUS programme guidance](https://psz.zus.pl/pl/web/guest/swiadczenia/swiadczenia-dla-rodzin/program-dobry-start).
