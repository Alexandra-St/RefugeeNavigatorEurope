## Resolved legal findings and candidate changes

- **Temporary protection / Article 106:** Article 106(1) of the 2026 consolidated Protection Act makes covered beneficiaries’ Polish stay lawful from entry through the period in which the Council decision remains in force, unless the statute provides otherwise. Decision (EU) 2026/1912 extends EU protection through 4 March 2028, effective 5 March 2027. Accordingly, Article 106 dynamically links that defined group’s lawful stay to the EU decision; the Ministry’s 26 March page stating 4 March 2027 predates the later Council decision. This does not extend a particular certificate or other residence document automatically. UdSC says the older paper certificates are valid through 4 March 2027 subject to early-ending events. Evidence: [consolidated Protection Act, Dz.U. 2026 item 862](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/862/ogl), [Decision (EU) 2026/1912 as corrected](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02026D1912-20260804), and [UdSC certificate notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/wazna-informacja-dla-cudzoziemcow-korzystajacych-z-ochrony-czasowej-na-podstawie-zaświadczen-wydanych-przez-szefa-urzedu-do-spraw-cudzoziemcow).
- **PESEL UKR:** PESEL UKR is the official register confirmation of Polish temporary protection/legal-stay rights for the statutory group, not a general residence permit. Current gov.pl service lists in-person gmina filing, a 30-day period after entry, a 35 × 45 mm photo, identity data and fingerprints for applicants aged 12 or older; it allows a declaration where identity documents are absent. The specific identity-confirmation deadline was 31 August 2026; for covered non-confirmers, the register changes UKR to NUE and Article 106 rights end from 1 September 2026. Absence from Poland over 30 days is a separate loss event. The MOS/official guidance describes case-specific restoration through register updates where the person proves absence did not exceed 30 days and no other loss ground applies. Review copy now tells users to have the gmina verify records and does not treat the PESEL number as proof by itself. Evidence: [gov.pl PESEL service](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/uzyskaj-numer-pesel-oraz-profil-zaufany--usluga-dla-obywateli-ukrainy), [2026 Act, transitional articles 25–26](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl/pol), and [MOS detailed explanation](https://mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl/kategorie-informacji/mozliwosci-legalizacji/rozwiazania-ob-ukrainy/ustawa-o-ochronie/szczegolowe-objasnienie/).
- **CUKR:** Applications are open from 4 May 2026 and are electronic-only through MOS. It is a three-year temporary permit for defined UKR-status cohorts; requirements include UKR on 4 June 2025, at application and permit grant, and at least 365 continuous days, with separate rules for children born in Poland. Applications may be filed through 4 March 2027. A timely application provides a statutory lawful-stay bridge until collection, invalidation for not collecting within 60 days, or final refusal/discontinuance; application alone is not the permit. At collection UKR ends and certain UKR-specific assistance ends. Evidence: [UdSC CUKR procedure](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/CUKR-procedura), [MOS CUKR requirements](https://www.mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl/kategorie-informacji/mozliwosci-legalizacji/rozwiazania-ob-ukrainy/karta-pobytu-cukr/wymogi/), [UdSC CUKR FAQ](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/cukr-QA), and Article 42x in the consolidated Ukraine Act.
- **Employment / wages:** 2026 statutory rates are PLN 4,806 monthly and PLN 31.40 hourly for covered contracts. Regulation Dz.U. 2026 item 1213 has already enacted FUTURE rates of PLN 4,950 monthly and PLN 32.30 hourly effective 1 January 2027. Employer notification for temporary-protection workers is through praca.gov.pl within 7 days of start; written contract, copy, and understandable written terms before signature remain required. Evidence: [2026 wage regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1242/ogl/pol/pdf), [2027 wage regulation, Dz.U. 2026 item 1213](https://www.dziennikustaw.gov.pl/DU/2026/1213), [PIP lawful-employment guidance](https://cudzoziemcywpracy.pip.gov.pl/legalne-zatrudnienie).
- **Self-employment:** The eight articles have no standalone self-employment article. Healthcare references business only as an insurance route, and the 800+ section refers to business activity as one form of activity. Official Biznes.gov.pl says a Ukrainian citizen lawfully staying in Poland with PESEL UKR may conduct business on Polish-citizen terms and requires PESEL for CEIDG; this is conditional on lawful stay and sector licensing. ZUS and tax duties depend on the actual form/activity. CUKR also grants business rights on Polish-citizen terms once the card is issued. Evidence: [Biznes.gov.pl rules for foreign entrepreneurs](https://biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/00806), [ZUS business registration](https://www.zus.pl/en/firmy/zgloszenie-platnika/firmy/zgloszenie-przedsiebiorcy-ceidg-).
- **Healthcare:** NFZ confirms the defined UKR groups (under 18; pregnancy, childbirth and postnatal period; torture/rape survivors; certified collective-accommodation residents) receive specified free care including POZ and reimbursed medicines, but not all publicly funded care. Listed exclusions include spa treatment/rehabilitation, infertility treatment, ordinary endoprosthesis/cataract procedures, specified ministerial drug programmes and preventive programmes; emergency life-saving services remain for all UKR beneficiaries. Full public coverage follows qualifying compulsory insurance, including qualifying employment, registered unemployment or lawful business; voluntary insurance is another possible route subject to conditions. NFZ checks rights in eWUŚ and providers may request status evidence. Candidate copy retains a qualified scope statement; it does not claim PESEL UKR alone means universal free care. Evidence: [NFZ 5 March 2026 guidance](https://www.nfz.gov.pl/aktualnosci/aktualnosci-centrali/pacjent-z-ukrainy-co-sie-zmienilo-od-5-marca-2026-r-,8907.html), [Act 2026 item 203](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl/pol), [patient emergency routes](https://pacjent.gov.pl/na-ratunek).
- **Housing:** Transitional continuation of the former Ukraine-specific collective-accommodation scheme ended 30 June 2026. For the 2022 displacement event, the new interventional stay-and-meal support is no longer available because its 12-month statutory limit from the Council mass-influx event has expired. Collective accommodation with meals may be funded for UKR holders in statutory vulnerable groups who cannot arrange accommodation independently. This is not universal and depends on statutory eligibility, available funds and administration. Private rental and housing allowance are separate. Evidence: [Mazowieckie Voivodeship Office, current accommodation summary](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-mazowiecki/pomoc-obywatelom-ukrainy), Act 2026 item 203, and [housing-allowance service](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/uzyskaj-dodatek-mieszkaniowy).
- **Education:** Ukrainian children have been subject to Polish schooling since 1 September 2024; Ukrainian remote classes alone do not satisfy the Polish duty. The only narrow last-year/2024–25 remote-learning exception is historical and expired. Additional Polish lessons can last up to 24 months; a teacher’s assistant up to 12 months; a preparatory class may be arranged and an intercultural assistant may be hired subject to law and resources. The 2026 consolidated Education Law is current through its stated legal status.
- **800+ and Dobry Start:** 800+ is PLN 800 monthly per eligible child. Ukrainian UKR applicants have had the work-activity condition since 1 February 2026, with statutory exceptions; a registered unemployed parent with health insurance through registration may qualify for 3 months, or 6 months when raising at least three children. School-age child must attend Polish education. Dobry Start is a separate PLN 300 annual benefit; from school year 2026/27 the covered foreign-national/UKR groups also face an activity condition, and UKR beneficiaries must satisfy Polish-school attendance. Do not infer eligibility across these schemes. Evidence: [MRPiPS 800+ rules](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/nowe-zasady-przyznawania-swiadczenia-wychowawczego-cudzoziemcom), [ZUS 800+ foreign-national conditions](https://www.zus.pl/en/-/%C5%9Awiadczenie-wychowawcze-800-warunki-dla-cudzoziemc%C3%B3w-i-mo%C5%BCliwe-przyczyny-wstrzymania-wyp%C5%82at?redirect=%2Fo-zus%2Faktualnosci), [Dobry Start changes from 1 July 2026](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/zmiany-w-programie-dobry-start-obowiazujace-od-1-lipca-2026-r).
