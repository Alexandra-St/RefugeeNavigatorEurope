## Article results

### NL-TP-01 — Applying for Temporary Protection in the Netherlands

**Decision: REVISED.** The EN/UK/RU draft now states IND's current proof cohorts, including the 4 March 2027 O-document cohort and January 2027 instruction letter. The EU-wide scheme end date (4 March 2028), individual entitlement, proof document validity, and reissue procedure remain separate. The IND page says a sticker without an expiry date needs no extension; specified Ukrainian sticker/card cohorts use a personal extension letter; covered non-Ukrainian card holders have an O-document through 4 March 2027 and will receive a January 2027 letter. The page also directs a person with a card dated 4 March 2026 and no letter to contact IND.

The baseline's other qualifications remain: the public IND and corrected EU Decision use different prior-holder exception wording; the readable public evidence does not establish the full military screening scope; the described municipal triage pilot's continuation is not confirmed; and the individual negative-triage challenge route remains decision-specific. Do not infer a right to stay, work, or receive reception while a challenge is pending.

### NL-EMP-01 — Finding a Job in the Netherlands

**Decision: UNCHANGED.** Existing bounded UWV and local employment-support language remains appropriate. It does not promise placement, funded training, or universal programme access. The current official work-and-income page and UWV Ukraine-worker guidance were re-opened; no material change to the existing article claims was identified.

### NL-EMP-02 — Work Authorization and Employee Rights

**Decision: UNCHANGED.** The official UWV page confirms the covered employee route, employer notification, and that work may begin two or more working days after the notification. Self-employment remains a separate route. The official 2026 minimum-wage table still shows the rates effective 1 July 2026: €14.99/hour age 21+, €11.99 (20), €8.99 (19), €7.50 (18), €5.92 (17), €5.17 (16), and €4.50 (15). The checked official table publishes no later effective 2026 rate; do not substitute an announced or future rate before its effective date.

### NL-HEALTH-01 — Healthcare in the Netherlands

**Decision: UNCHANGED, WITH QUALIFICATION.** Current Rijksoverheid guidance confirms RMO coverage for the defined TPD cohort with BSN title code 46, and states that a covered person does not need to take out ordinary Dutch health insurance to receive RMO-covered care. Workers may choose ordinary insurance and then may owe premiums, deductible, and contributions; general UWV insurance guidance and TPD-specific public guidance have previously conflicted on formal Zvw liability. The article must continue to avoid individual advice to take out, cancel, or change insurance. RMO is not the same scheme as standard Zorgverzekeringswet insurance. Urgent-care and local GP/HAP directions remain qualified by local route and covered care.

### NL-HOUSING-01 — Accommodation in the Netherlands

**Decision: UNCHANGED, WITH QUALIFICATION.** Current municipal reception continues to depend on eligibility, referral path, and capacity. The current Rijksoverheid personal-contribution page lists amounts effective 1 August 2026 (up to €244.22 per adult for operating costs and €260.97 per adult for meals where meals are provided), with a household maximum and the 115% income rule; it also allows municipal proportionality discretion. Keep the article's household, date, municipal-assessment, and income qualifications. The official current rent-benefit page requires general eligibility conditions and an application; no universal entitlement is implied. The national funding change effective 1 July 2026 concerns municipal financing and does not itself establish an individual reception entitlement or place.

### NL-EDU-01 — Education and Dutch-language Learning

**Decision: UNCHANGED.** The draft already includes the DUO correction: the 2026–27 remission criterion requires ineligibility for student finance or a secondary-education grant because nationality criteria are not met, alongside the other cumulative conditions; the deadline is 31 July 2027. DUO's current page confirms this wording and the deadline. Current government guidance confirms compulsory education applies to Ukrainian children and youth; individual placement and local course offers remain local. No new national education entitlement was established in this check.

### NL-SOC-01 — Financial and Social Assistance

**Decision: UNCHANGED, WITH QUALIFICATION.** The current official work-and-income and personal-contribution pages show the living-allowance figures effective 1 August 2026 and the current contribution framework. Preserve household, age, income, reception-setting, and municipality conditions. Do not extend this evidence to ordinary bijstand or universal MOP eligibility. Current Dienst Toeslagen guidance confirms housing benefit is conditional on general requirements and that healthcare allowance requires Dutch insurance; it does not make either benefit automatic for TPD holders.
