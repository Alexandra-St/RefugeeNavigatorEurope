## Temporary protection and residence

The audit confirmed the relevant new-grant military-obligation rule from 5 August 2026, the corrected EU continuity exception for protection held in the same Member State on or before 4 August and continuously retained, and Lithuania's separate national transition treatment. The EU and Lithuanian protection horizon is 4 March 2028, while individual digital permit/document validity and replacement remain separate matters. Replacement instructions are not a universal first-application checklist. The 1 November 2026 ordinary filing change is limited to listed lawful-presence categories and work/first-cycle study routes. No automatic TP conversion or universal pending right is asserted.

## Employment

TP work-right trigger and employer duties were checked separately. UŽT registration/services do not guarantee benefit eligibility or payment; Migration Department and Sodra roles remain distinct. Written contract, right-to-work verification, 1-SD start notice, monthly SAM, 2-SD end notice, and other reporting duties are not conflated. The statutory minimums are €1,153/month and €7.05/hour in 2026, and enacted rates of €1,245/month and €7.61/hour from 1 January 2027. The statutory minimum wage's legal scope is qualified, and it is distinguished from immigration salary thresholds.

## Healthcare

Pending applicants, granted TP holders, children and state-insured categories are distinguished. Full PSD/CHI is not promised solely from Ukrainian nationality or a TP permit. Employment/contribution insurance, active-status verification, identity/status evidence, and care entitlement are separate. Emergency number 112 is distinguished from the non-emergency medical advice line 113; calling either does not make every subsequent service free.

## Housing

Eligible TP applicants may use the limited initial-accommodation route for up to six months from application registration, subject to availability; no bed or immediate placement is guaranteed. Private rental, advance rent, deposit, residence declaration, and actual accommodation are distinguished. Housing and social-support routes have separate eligibility and award decisions; TP alone does not guarantee support.

## Education

School access and status boundaries, local placement, and qualified age rules were reviewed. Language/adaptation support depends on pupil needs and local provision. The eight Ukrainian-curriculum schools figure is labelled as a June 2026 snapshot. Higher-education admission, recognition, language, tuition and funding remain institution/programme-specific. A closed 2026 scholarship call is not represented as currently available.

## Social assistance

TP eligibility to apply is not an automatic award. Municipal, SPIS and Sodra roles remain distinct. The 2026 €256.30 figure is an assessment threshold, not the monthly payment. Child benefit is €129.50/month per eligible child; the €76.22 supplement is conditional. No universal Ukraine-only monthly subsistence payment is asserted.
