## H–J. Temporary protection, 2026 cards, and 2027 renewal
EU Temporary Protection for covered categories is extended to 4 March 2028 under the Council’s 2026 extension decision. Separately, Bulgaria’s Council of Ministers approved an amendment on 4 November 2026 extending the national temporary-protection period through 4 March 2028 and announced re-registration from 5 March to 30 April 2027. This is enacted government action, not merely the EU endpoint. The decision summary does not provide a complete practical procedure or conclusively resolve validity of each existing registration card through the next cycle. AREF operational guidance must be checked before 5 March 2027.

The 2026 card cycle is historical: previously issued cards were temporarily valid through 30 April 2026; 2026 renewal required personal appearance with different locations before/after that cutoff, according to AREF notice. Do not tell readers to take that closed route now. Earlier national protection end date 4 March 2027 is superseded as the current horizon but remains a historical date. First registration is a distinct process; AREF/MOI information points to in-person processing and identity/family details, but the currently reviewed sources do not make a complete document checklist or proxy rule for all card replacement scenarios.

Temporary protection is a group-based EU mechanism and Bulgarian status/document recording, distinct from an individual asylum/international-protection decision and ordinary residence permits. EU 2026 rules address military obligations where applicable and contain a narrow same-Member-State continuity exception. Bulgarian local proof instructions were not found. Do not infer all rights during a pending application or from another Member State’s card; obtain AREF confirmation for a move, prior status, return, or cessation event. The reviewed eight production articles do not establish a general pending-stage entitlement bundle.

## K. Employment
People currently enjoying Bulgarian TP can work as employees without a separate work permit, subject to status continuing and ordinary employment law. Existing employer notification and NRA registration details need authority-level revalidation before final source binding; retain the instruction to follow current contract/notification rules and do not rely on obsolete emergency rules. Job-seeker registration, mediation, unemployment insurance and regulated-profession recognition are separate procedures. Self-employment is not automatically established by employee work permission; company/sole-trader registration, profession licensing, tax, social and health insurance require separate checks.

## L–N. Currency and wages
Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026 at the fixed rate €1 = BGN 1.95583. Dual cash circulation ended 31 January 2026; the euro became sole legal tender 1 February 2026. Dual price display ended 8 August 2026. As of this review, current amounts should be stated in euro; BGN figures are historical or conversion context only.

The 2026 gross minimum wage is €620.20 per month or €3.74 per hour, effective 1 January 2026 for the normal full-time basis. Decree No. 235 of 29 October 2026 sets future rates from 1 January 2027 at €660 monthly / €3.98 hourly; this is enacted, but not yet current as of 17 November 2026. The 2027 rate should not be called a proposal or used as the current 2026 wage.

## O. Tax and social insurance
The reviewed pages do not support generic claims that all workers pay one simple flat rate or that employment guarantees unemployment/maternity benefits. NSSI cash benefits depend on insured risk and contribution periods; unemployment entitlement includes a contribution-history test. Tax residence, withholding, personal returns and self-employed contribution values need individualized competent-authority assessment and are not comprehensively evidenced by the eight articles. Avoid adding a generic tax percentage or a contribution amount.

## P. Healthcare and health insurance
TP holders are included in the statutory compulsory-insurance framework subject to the applicable payer rules. Under Decree 69/2022, state payment applies throughout TP for children below 18 and women 63+/men 65+, absent another insurance basis. For adults 18 to below the applicable age threshold, state payment runs for 90 days from grant; after that, contribution responsibility follows the general rules unless another basis applies. This is a contribution-payer rule, not 90 days of healthcare eligibility. The 2026 payment amount for self-payers was not safely established from a current primary source in this review; omit historic BGN 28.40 or unverified converted amounts. Insurance status, covered package, provider and co-pay rules remain separate from the protection card.

## Q. Housing
Program 3 reimbursement was extended through 4 March 2027 under the September 2026 government decision. It reimburses eligible accommodation providers; it is not a guaranteed hotel place or direct rent payment to a displaced person. Eligibility, provider documentation and amounts must be checked against current tourism-program rules. The right to suitable accommodation/means is conditional on need and does not create a universal placement guarantee. Private tenancy is separate ordinary contract law.

## R. Social assistance and family benefits
ASP states a 2026 poverty line of €390.63 and monthly base for general assistance of €117.19; actual aid is means-tested and assessed by household circumstances. The 2026/27 heating allowance is €64.39 per month for five months (total €321.95), with applications due by 31 October 2026; by the as-of date the application window has closed, though the season runs through 31 March 2027. Family benefits are a separate statutory scheme with scheme-specific status/residence and income rules; ASP guidance does not support a blanket TP entitlement. NSSI maternity and unemployment payments are insurance-based and distinct from means-tested social assistance.

## S. Education
MОН/RDE procedure provides the route for children seeking/holding protection to apply for state/municipal kindergarten and school. Compulsory school starts in the school year the child turns seven (possible at six with parental choice and certified readiness) and continues through age 16; preschool has its own compulsory age rule. Application is distinct from guaranteed seat/class placement; RDE assesses schooling records and missing-document routes. Additional Bulgarian-language support depends on need and applicable provisions; maximum-course limits do not guarantee a fixed number of hours. Higher education remains institution/programme-specific; limited state-funded route, recognition, admission, tuition and language requirements are distinct decisions.
