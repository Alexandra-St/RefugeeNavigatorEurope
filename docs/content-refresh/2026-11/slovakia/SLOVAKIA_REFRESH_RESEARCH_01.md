## G. Article-by-article findings

### SK-TP-01
Existing eligibility, EU 2028 horizon, national extension mechanism and individual document validity are distinct. EU Decision 2026/1912 extends the scheme until 4 March 2028 and applies from 5 March 2027; the military-obligation condition separately applies from 5 August 2026 with the narrow same-Member-State continuous-status exception. Current Ministry booking is a separate operational step; no universal evidence checklist for the military condition was found. Candidate adds statutory first-application handling, 24-hour receipt/reception boundary, decision periods, termination boundaries, and avoids generic pending-stage rights. Travel, individual extension of printed cards and any restoration/re-activation route remain for confirmation.

### SK-TP-02
Purpose-specific residence, Blue Card and §131o remain separate; no automatic conversion. Pre-end and post-end six-month tests differ. The statutory cross-reference creates a genuine interpretive ambiguity for importing the continuity condition into the pre-end route; candidate preserves that qualification. Complete ordinary filing under §31(4), §131o pending treatment and work permission are not conflated. Long-term residence counting of earlier refuge time after status change remains unresolved.

### SK-EMP-01
Public vacancy search, UoZ and ZoZ registers, ÚPSVaR services and EURES remain separate. Current reviewed sources do not settle UoZ access for every temporary-refuge holder; confirm with ÚPSVaR. No placement, training or benefit is guaranteed. Recognition is required in regulated-profession contexts, not universally.

### SK-EMP-02
Temporary-refuge employee exemption is not generalized to self-employment or pending residence. Employer electronic notice duties and seven-working-day deadlines apply to both commencement and termination in covered cases. The 2026 six-grade monthly/hour amounts remain current. New Slov-Lex Notice 233/2026 makes 2027 grade floors enacted FUTURE values from 1 January 2027; exact six monthly and six hourly figures are added to all candidate locales.

### SK-HEALTH-01
Temporary refuge alone is not Slovak public insurance membership. Necessary-care reimbursement under §9h is a separate limited route, including exclusions for Slovak or other-EU public coverage and adult/child scope distinctions. Provider proof is not an insurance card; urgent care, planned care, reimbursement and full insured coverage remain separate. Identity-only pre-document handling and transitions after employment ends require case-specific confirmation.

### SK-HOUSING-01
§68 facility accommodation and §69 host contributions are distinct. Candidate preserves up to 60 days, exact vulnerable categories, written extensions up to six months, repeat requests subject to capacity, and 15-day advance application. Private-lease deposit cap applies only to statutory short-term leases. §69 amounts are host/municipality-side; beneficiary and host reporting deadlines and rates are dated/conditional, not refugee cash support.

### SK-EDU-01
Compulsory attendance is limited to the specified Ukrainian status/proceeding cohorts and age bands. Three transition deadlines are distinct and not universal new-enrolment deadlines. Capacity, placement, language-support, remote schooling and higher-education/recognition/tuition limits are qualified; public/state tuition exemption does not cover private universities or every fee.

### SK-SOC-01
Material-need assistance is household/income/assets assessed; municipal one-off and MPSVR humanitarian routes are separate discretionary schemes. Temporary refuge alone does not establish ordinary child, parental or disability cash benefits. Childcare allowance has a distinct special-protection path with work/study, age, provider and duplicate-payment limits; cross-border coordination is case-specific. Candidate aligns EN/UK detail with RU.

## I. Temporary refuge, application and status

- **EU duration:** Decision (EU) 2026/1912 sets the horizon to 4 March 2028; its extension applies from 5 March 2027.
- **National implementation:** Government Resolution 185 as amended provides the Slovak automatic-extension mechanism; no separate Slovak 2026 instrument naming the 2028 date was identified in the earlier official-source audit. This is not a guarantee that every card’s printed date was changed.
- **Application:** Act 69/2026 §§59–60: declaration at designated police unit, minor representative/presence rules, document handling and biometrics, 24-hour reception/receipt details, 30-day statutory decision period with possible repeated 30-day reasoned extensions. Current Ministry reservation guidance is an operational prerequisite and is separate from the legal act of filing.
- **Pending stage:** application confirmation is short-lived evidence for the statutory application/reception process; it is not general proof of every work, healthcare, housing or benefit right. A separate applicant card after reception is distinct from the grant document.
- **Cessation:** Act §64 has multiple status-ending events; departure during the pending proceeding may stop the case under §62. No automatic restoration claim is made. The current Ministry process for travel, return, absence, document replacement and reactivation needs current source confirmation before broader advice.
- **Article wording:** avoid equating EU protection horizon, Slovak status, pending applicant documents, and an individual paper/card validity date.
