### BE-TP-01: COMPLETE

#### Military-obligation rule — FINAL RESULT

The corrected consolidated Council Decision 2026/1912 applies the military-obligation condition to relevant new grants from **5 August 2026**. It excludes a person who already enjoyed temporary protection in a given Member State on or before **4 August 2026** and continuously retains that protection in that same state afterward. The correction controls over the pre-corrigendum dates on the Flanders news page. Existing beneficiaries continuously protected in Belgium across that cutoff are within the exception; a prior grant in another Member State does not establish the same-state exception. The Commission FAQ describes general evidence routes, including an official exit record or verifiable proof of compliance/exemption, but Belgian public guidance does not yet set out the corrected dates, the continuity exception, or a complete Belgium-specific checklist. Individual evidence acceptance and any special-case interpretation remain for the Belgian Immigration Office.

#### Application procedure — FINAL RESULT

The federal Immigration Office handles the centralized, in-person temporary-protection registration and decision. Its live page directs applicants to **Rue Belliard 68, 1000 Brussels**, on weekdays at **08:30**. Applicants should bring available identity and eligibility evidence, including proof of qualifying residence/status in Ukraine where relevant; the Office records identity data, takes fingerprints and copies documents as required. The federal page does not settle appointment vs walk-in, give a full no-document process, or guarantee same-day processing. If eligible, the federal Office issues an Attestation of Temporary Protection. Current eligibility evidence and intake details should be checked before travel.

#### Municipality/document chain — FINAL RESULT

The sequence is federal registration/decision → federal Attestation of Temporary Protection → registration with the municipality of actual residence → Belgian residence document, generally the A card. The municipality does not make the federal temporary-protection decision. Local residence verification may apply. The attestation, any pending-document proof, and the A card are distinct; do not treat one as the other.

#### TP/document validity — FINAL RESULT

The EU scheme runs through **4 March 2028**. This date does not itself extend every Belgian card. Immigration Office guidance says holders may request A-card renewal from their municipality starting **4 January 2027**, with a renewed card valid through **4 March 2028**. If a replacement cannot be issued before the existing card expires on **4 March 2027**, the Office says it will issue an attestation of registration in the foreigners or population register. A person granted a TP attestation after 4 January 2027 is to receive an A card through 4 March 2028. A card or Annex 15 pending issuance is the document identified by the Office for work authorization. The page contains older inconsistent extension text; the current dated instructions above are the operative guidance identified in this review. Check the printed expiry and municipality instructions; no automatic physical-card renewal is established.

#### Pending rights — FINAL RESULT

Do not infer a universal right to work or a specific residence-document status from a pending federal TP registration or a pending card. For recognized TP holders, the Immigration Office identifies the A card or Annex 15 while the card is pending as work proof. The sources reviewed do not establish a blanket work entitlement before the TP decision, or a universal lawful-stay document during every municipal processing delay. Confirm the exact document with the municipality/Immigration Office.

### BE-TP-02: COMPLETE

#### Residence filing — FINAL RESULT

Temporary protection does not automatically convert to an ordinary Belgian permit. Federal Ukraine guidance allows Ukrainian citizens already in Belgium to apply through the municipality for specified long-stay routes (work, study or family), subject to each route’s conditions. This does not prove that every TP holder can use every in-country procedure. For a single permit, the employer generally files through the competent region; the region assesses work authorization and the federal Immigration Office decides residence. Student in-country filing has a specific statutory route for a person already admitted/authorized to stay, enrolled full-time, and filing before their permission expires. Family applications normally proceed from abroad subject to statutory in-country exceptions and sponsor/relationship rules. A pending application is not a general extension of stay or work rights; route-specific pending rights need confirmation.

#### Single permit — FINAL RESULT

Employer-led, regional work authorization combined with federal residence authorization. The EU Single Permit Directive and regional guidance do not by themselves establish that every Belgian TP A card satisfies each in-country filing condition or creates pending work rights. No generalized right to keep working during a change-of-status application is asserted. TP status/document and any permit application should be checked with the responsible region, Immigration Office and municipality.

#### Professional card — FINAL RESULT

Professional-card competence and filing procedures are regional. Official Brussels, Flanders and Wallonia materials support an exemption for TP beneficiaries while the qualifying TP exemption applies. Do not assume it persists after TP ends. The sources do not create a general transition guarantee for a self-employed person after the exemption ends.

#### Other residence routes — FINAL RESULT

Study, family reunification, employment/single permit, professional card and Blue Card are separate routes with their own requirements. A Blue Card route may exist under regional law, but the reviewed material does not establish the sequence for a person with active TP; the EU transition recommendation is nonbinding. TP time is excluded from EU long-term-resident qualifying residence. Whether TP time counts toward Belgium’s separate national establishment status was not established. No automatic permanence follows from time under TP.

#### Post-TP transition — FINAL RESULT

**NO NATIONAL MECHANISM FOUND** for after 4 March 2028 as of the review date. No enacted Belgian automatic conversion or general transitional filing scheme was found. Council Recommendation (EU) 2025/5129 is a nonbinding EU recommendation, not Belgian legislation. The EMN national report is historical context for possible replacement/reactivation before TP ends, not evidence of a current universal path or pending-status entitlement.

### Competence split

Federal competence covers immigration/work status, employment standards, employer social-security declarations and employee protections. Regions govern public employment services and employment authorization/single permits; professional-card procedures also vary by region. The named routes in these drafts are: VDAB in Flanders, Actiris in Brussels-Capital, Le Forem in French-speaking Wallonia, and Arbeitsamt (ADG) in the German-speaking Community. Registration rules in one service do not apply to another region. Qualification recognition and some vocational/community supports are also community-specific.

### BE-EMP-01: COMPLETE

#### Employment services — FINAL RESULT

- **Flanders:** VDAB provides job-search help, vacancies, guidance and vocational training. People registered as temporarily displaced are required to register as jobseekers with VDAB under the Flemish decree in force since 16 May 2023; the current government page says to attend a VDAB office with residence documents received on arrival or from the municipality. This jobseeker registration is separate from the right to work.
- **Brussels-Capital:** Actiris registration requires a valid residence title and, in some cases, work authorization. First registration is through My Actiris or an appointment at an Actiris branch. Current general material does not enumerate every TP paper; verify the exact document with Actiris.
- **French-speaking Wallonia:** Forem requires residence in the French-speaking region and a valid Belgian residence document from its listed categories. It accepts an A card (except study stay) and only specified Annex 15 situations. It offers job-search support and may refer to language, vocational, validation or equivalence services subject to eligibility and availability.
- **German-speaking Community:** ADG says Ukraine registration is voluntary and can be made online (German, French or Ukrainian form) or in person. Do not carry Flanders’ mandatory VDAB rule across to ADG.
- Vacancy browsing/account creation does not necessarily complete formal jobseeker registration. Registration is not a work permit or an unemployment-benefit award. Coaching, interpreter/language service, training admission, funding and benefits are conditional, not guaranteed.

A person with recognized TP, a TP applicant before decision, and someone holding an A card/Annex 15 can face different service document tests. The review does not establish a single national employment-service document checklist.

### BE-EMP-02: COMPLETE

#### TP/A-card/Annex-15 work permission — FINAL RESULT

A person must first be granted Belgian TP and hold the residence document that evidences permission to stay/work: normally the A card, or the qualifying Annex 15 issued while waiting for that A card. The federal TP attestation is the status decision/certificate used for the next municipal step; it is not itself established as standalone work proof. A pending TP application or pre-decision Annex 3 does not authorize starting employee work under the reviewed official guide. The covered TP employee route is exempt from ordinary regional work/single-permit authorization while the exemption applies. Do not generalize this to all Ukrainians, all Annex 15 documents, or a route-change application. Batch 1's federal decision → attestation → municipality → A-card chain remains consistent.

#### Minimum pay — FINAL RESULT

The National Labour Council currently states that the **RMMMG/GGMMI is €2,189.81 gross per month from 1 April 2026** for workers aged 18+ covered by CCT 43. It is an average monthly minimum-income guarantee, not a universal hourly wage or necessarily ordinary base pay in each month; qualifying annual remuneration components may count. CCT 43 does not apply to 18–20-year-olds under student contracts, to 16–17-year-olds, or to specified alternating-training cases; the applicable reduced youth/student framework must be checked. Sectoral collective-agreement scales may be higher and depend on the employer's main activity/joint committee and job classification. Do not convert the monthly RMMMG to an hourly rate without the correct work schedule/sector basis.

#### Wage indexation — FINAL RESULT

Most wages follow automatic indexation mechanisms established by sectoral collective agreements. A law effective **1 June 2026** limits indexation of the portion of monthly fixed basic reference salary above €4,000 by a 2% wage moderation; monthly reference salaries up to €4,000 are fully indexed under that rule. This does not change the stated €2,189.81 RMMMG. A second application is currently scheduled for 1 January 2028 and is expressly subject to possible postponement, so it is future-only. No wage-indexation change after the 11 November draft/check date was found as of 16 November 2026. [FPS Employment — index cents](https://emploi.belgique.be/fr/themes/remuneration/indexation-automatique-des-salaires/index-centimes); [National Labour Council — minimum wage](https://cnt-nar.be/fr/dossiers-thematiques/salaire-minimum).

#### Employer/Dimona — FINAL RESULT

Before hiring a third-country national, the employer must verify a valid residence authorization/work document and retain a copy or the relevant data for inspection at least during the employment period. The employer submits DIMONA IN before work starts and DIMONA OUT no later than the next working day after the employment ends; the worker does not submit these declarations. Quarterly DmfA is a separate payroll/social-contribution declaration. NISS can be a National Register number or, when applicable, a BIS number; employer declaration and social-security identification are ordinary payroll duties, separate from the immigration work document. Sector pay classification, contract terms, payslips and occupational-safety duties still apply.

#### Contract, time and leave cross-check

The article's written-form statement for fixed-term, part-time and student contracts remains correct, but does not say every contract must be written. General probation clauses remain abolished subject to student and temporary/interim exceptions. Ordinary federal working-time baseline remains framed around an 8-hour day and 40-hour weekly ceiling, with a 38-hour effective/averaged schedule and exceptions. Daily/weekly rest and break rules have exceptions and collective arrangements. Since **1 June 2026**, night work is no longer generally prohibited, but work-time/rest/safety rules and employer introduction procedures remain; distribution-sector premiums for new starters have a special rule. A separate June 2026 reform reduced the general minimum weekly duration for part-time contracts from one-third to one-tenth of the full-time schedule, subject to exceptions. These enactments predate 11 November 2026; no later relevant change was found through 16 November. Ordinary annual leave generally accrues from work in the previous calendar year; eligible new workers may request European leave under separate conditions and payment rules.

#### Professional card — FINAL RESULT

TP beneficiaries authorized to stay as such are exempt from the professional-card requirement for self-employment in the current official Flanders, Brussels and Wallonia materials. This concerns the regional professional-card requirement while the exemption applies. It does not remove business-registration, social-insurance, sector or regulated-profession requirements, and does not establish a post-TP continuation or pending-application right.

#### Qualification recognition — FINAL RESULT

Work permission does not grant recognition or a professional license. Recognition for regulated professions is organized by Community and profession; the research package identifies NARIC Vlaanderen and Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles equivalence channels, and the EU regulated-professions database for finding the competent authority. Do not describe either national portal as the single Belgium-wide recognition authority. Detailed licensing advice for a particular profession is outside these two article scopes.

**Health-insurance access — FINAL RESULT.** TP does not automatically enrol a person. A recognized beneficiary can affiliate under the resident route without employment; an actual mutuality or CAAMI/HZIV affiliation is required for ordinary compulsory-insurance reimbursement. Retroactive quarter-start effect follows only after accepted proof and completed affiliation. The specific rule does not establish ordinary insured coverage for every pending applicant. Dependants may be included subject to the applicable rules.

**Proof — FINAL RESULT.** INAMI accepts the federal TP attestation or electronic status verification for the resident route before an A card; the TP-specific MI-IS FAQ also names a TP-related Annex 15. The historical registration proof is not a new-document route. A card is not proof of fund membership. CAAMI’s public checklist still lists the A card, so have the selected fund confirm documents and effective date. An eID/ISI+ or provider electronic check can support identification/status after affiliation, but no single paper proves every person’s entitlement.

**CPAS healthcare — FINAL RESULT.** The Ukraine-specific pending-TP medical-aid route is an individually assessed CPAS pathway, distinct from ordinary insurance and from emergency-department care. General AMU/MediPrima has its own eligibility, social inquiry and territorial-competence rules. No universal no-address/document workaround was established.

**Emergency/urgent — FINAL RESULT.** Call 112 for life-threatening emergencies. Call 1733 for urgent non-life-threatening out-of-hours GP help only where active; coverage varies, and the call/consultation may incur charges. AMU/DMH is a CPAS medical-cost aid term, not an emergency service. Ordinary healthcare can include patient shares, supplements or non-covered costs.

**Initial housing — FINAL RESULT.** A current national first contact is verifiable but placement is not guaranteed: at the Immigration Office TP service point, state that you have nowhere to stay and ask Fedasil about assessment/referral. Temporary Brussels shelter depends on need and available capacity. This is not a nationwide bed guarantee or a substitute for local homelessness services.

**Regional housing — FINAL RESULT.** Flanders: current Ukraine-specific collective route is narrowly described for Ghent referrals from Ariane; general municipal reception funding ended 1 April 2026. **Rent/deposit support — FINAL RESULT.** CPAS/OCMW/ÖSHZ may assess rent, deposit, installation or emergency aid individually. **School access — FINAL RESULT.** Compulsory/public-school access is available without waiting for a final TP decision or A card; school residence registration is not a general precondition. **Compulsory education — FINAL RESULT.** The general framework runs from age five through 18, but attendance modality, full/part-time obligation after age 15/16, end dates, foreign-minor registration timing and home-education requirements are specific statutory details. Earlier school access must not be confused with the timing of a foreign child’s compulsory-duty registration rule.

**Language/newcomer support — FINAL RESULT.** Flanders uses taalheldklassen for relevant primary newcomers from the second primary year under rules effective 1 September 2026, and OKAN for eligible secondary-age newcomers. FWB uses DASPA and FLA support. The German-speaking Community uses Sprachlernklassen/-kurse and school-level support councils. Eligibility, duration and actual places depend on the relevant Community and school; no nationwide Ukrainian-language or interpreter service is guaranteed.

**Higher education — FINAL RESULT.** Apply to the selected institution and follow its Community’s admission and credential-recognition rules. Academic equivalence/recognition is separate from professional recognition; language, programme entry and credit-transfer decisions remain institution-specific. TP does not guarantee admission, diploma equivalence, credit transfer, or a scholarship. ### BE-SOC-01: COMPLETE

**Social assistance — FINAL RESULT.** After TP is granted and the applicant has presented to the municipality, they may ask the competent CPAS/OCMW/ÖSHZ to assess equivalent financial social assistance. This is distinct from statutory RI/leefloon and is not automatic. A newly arrived person with TP pending does not have the same general cash route; the federal guidance identifies medical aid and the TP registration step at that stage. Means, household situation, actual available resources, work income and circumstances are assessed individually. CPAS financial aid does not automatically enrol a person in health insurance.

**Application and competence — FINAL RESULT.** The competent CPAS depends on register status and actual habitual residence, with specific homeless/institution rules. Applications can be oral, written or electronic; retain a dated receipt. A decision is generally due within 30 days, which does not guarantee payment by then. The TP attestation plus proof of municipal presentation can support a request before the A card; Annex 15 is one possible proof, and federal guidance allows other proof if it is not yet issued.

No later indexation effective by the as-of date was identified.

**Family benefits — FINAL RESULT.** Separate child-benefit systems apply in Flanders (Groeipakket), Brussels, Wallonia and the German-speaking Community. TP eligibility is not interchangeable: child residence, valid status, registration/National Register or domicile requirements, application route and supplements vary. Pending TP, school attendance, or a child remaining abroad does not automatically establish entitlement. Contact the payer serving the child’s residence region.
