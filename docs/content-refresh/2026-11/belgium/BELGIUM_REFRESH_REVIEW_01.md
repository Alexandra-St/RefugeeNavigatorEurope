## Critical temporary-protection checks

- **Military rule:** EU corrected Decision 2026/1912 applies the relevant new-grant military-obligation condition from 5 August 2026; uninterrupted TP in the same Member State as of 4 August 2026 is excepted. Belgian public material does not provide a complete implementation/evidence checklist. Draft and Compare must keep EU legal rule, Belgian published procedure and individual Immigration Office assessment distinct.
- **Registration centre:** DOFI pages rechecked on 16 November 2026 confirm Rue Belliard 68, Brussels, weekdays at 08:30. Capacity deferrals may occur. The reviewed pages do not establish appointment or walk-in rules or a complete no-document route; the copy qualifies both.
- **Municipality/document chain:** Federal Immigration Office decides TP and issues the federal TP attestation; the person then registers at their actual-residence municipality and requests the Belgian residence document. Attestation is not the A card. Annex 15 is a separate temporary document and only a qualifying one supports the stated work route.
- **A-card validity:** EU protection runs to 4 March 2028. DOFI says renewal requests may be made to municipalities from 4 January 2027, with renewed cards to 4 March 2028; it also gives a registration certificate if a new card is not issued before 4 March 2027. The DOFI page retains an inconsistent legacy 2025 passage. No automatic extension is inferred; check printed expiry and municipality.
- **Residence filing:** No automatic conversion or enacted Belgian post-2028 scheme is evidenced. Single permit, study, family, Blue Card, professional card and long-term residence each have separate conditions; a route in law does not establish an active-TP holder's in-country filing or pending work/stay rights.

## Employment review

Work permission after TP grant is supported for an employee with an A card or qualifying Annex 15 while the card is pending; federal TP attestation alone and pending/pre-decision Annex 3 are not treated as work authorization. Single permit is employer-led, with regional work assessment and federal residence decision; no blanket pending bridge is introduced. VDAB, Actiris, Forem and ADG are not collapsed into one national service rule. Professional-card exemptions remain region-specific and do not waive business, social-security or licensing duties. The RMMMG is €2,189.81 gross/month from 1 April 2026 under CCT 43 for covered workers aged 18+; student-contract workers aged 18–20 are excluded from CCT 43 and have a reduced CCT 50 guarantee. Sector minima can be higher.
