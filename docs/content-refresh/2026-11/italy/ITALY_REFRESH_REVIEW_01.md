## Critical temporary-protection review

- **Military rule:** Pass. Corrected EU Decision 2026/1912 applies the military-obligation condition to relevant new grants from 5 Aug 2026; exception is continuous protection in the same Member State by 4 Aug 2026. No Italy-wide evidence checklist is asserted.
- **Questura:** Pass. Filing is with the competent Questura for domicile and direct/in-person as described by the reviewed DPCM guidance; no nationwide appointment portal or universal checklist is invented. 2022 receipt/codice-fiscale evidence is limited to the specific point it supports.
- **Permit horizon:** Pass. EU scheme through 4 Mar 2028 is distinct from covered Italian permits renewed on request through 4 Mar 2027. No claim of physical Italian permits nationally valid through 2028.
- **Receipts:** Pass. TP application receipt supports TP-specific work/SSN rights where stated. Ordinary Article 5(9-bis) applies only after a qualifying ordinary process receipt and other legal requirements; it does not create route eligibility.
- **Conversion:** Pass. Law 213/2023 ¶396 is a scoped, non-automatic work conversion for covered permits and qualifying actual work. No unsupported Decreto Flussi dependency or general study/family/Blue Card conversion is claimed. TP itself is not a basis for EU long-term residence; later TP-time credit remains case-specific.

## Employment review

Pass. CPI, DID, SIISL, EURES and regional portals are distinguished; job-search registration does not create work authorization or benefit entitlement. Local service variation is explicit. TP self-employment access is separate from tax, VAT, business, INPS and professional licensing steps. No universal statutory minimum wage is stated; pay is tied to the applicable representative CCNL, sector, category/level and contract. Employer hiring notification and vacancy-publication obligations are not conflated; relevant payroll, INPS, INAIL, safety and written-information duties are preserved.

## Healthcare review

Pass with near-term date sensitivity recorded. TP applicant/holder SSN enrollment is supported through 31 Dec 2026 only. A qualifying TP receipt and codice fiscale can support enrollment before the physical Tessera Sanitaria is issued; the card is not portrayed as the source of entitlement. X22 is limited to eligible non-working TP holders after SSN enrollment/declaration, not universal free care or cash. 112/118 and Pronto Soccorso are emergency routes; 116117 is non-urgent and locally activated. No 2027 extension is asserted.

## Housing review

Pass. Specified existing CAS/SAI arrangements may continue through 4 Mar 2027 subject to scope, referral, eligibility and capacity; no universal intake or guaranteed bed. Private rental and municipal residence registration are distinct. The former national Ukraine subsistence contribution is closed; its final request deadline was 20 Apr 2025.

## Education review

Pass. School access is independent of residence status; TP card is not a prerequisite. Compulsory education is ten years, generally ages 6–16, and the education/training completion duty extends through 18 as supported. Class placement, school capacity and language support vary; higher education admission, recognition, language, tuition and aid are not automatic from TP.

## Social-assistance review

Pass. No current national TP-specific cash subsistence scheme was found. ADI/SFL require their own residence/status, household, ISEE and activation conditions; TP or an application receipt alone is insufficient. INPS recognizes a TP permit for AUU subject to ordinary conditions, but the pending TP receipt alone is not established as sufficient. 2026 AUU varies by ISEE/family circumstances (1.4% adjustment); Bonus nido may reach €3,600 only under its eligibility, birth-date/ISEE and documented-cost rules. No fixed universal payment is implied.
