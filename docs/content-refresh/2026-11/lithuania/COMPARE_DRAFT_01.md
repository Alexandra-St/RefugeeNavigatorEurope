## residence-filing — REVISE

**EN:** From 1 November 2026, a legally present applicant may file in Lithuania for an ordinary employment or first-cycle/bachelor study residence permit if they hold a valid Lithuanian temporary residence permit, a Lithuanian national visa, a residence permit issued by another Schengen/EU Member State, or visa-free stay. A short-stay Schengen visa or another country’s national visa does not qualify under this rule. Highly qualified employment follows a separate in-country process while the applicant is legally present. Filing does not guarantee approval or establish a universal pending right.
