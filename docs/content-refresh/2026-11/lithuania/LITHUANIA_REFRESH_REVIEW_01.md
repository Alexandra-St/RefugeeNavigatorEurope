- **TP document:** PASS. Call the Lithuanian status document a digital temporary residence permit; do not imply a physical card.
- **2028 horizon:** PASS. EU and Lithuanian protection horizon through 4 March 2028 is distinct from each person’s document expiry and does not establish automatic replacement or a universal renewal procedure.

## Employment review

PASS. The TP-entitled group may work after applying within the statutory TP scope without a separate work permit on that basis. This is not a right based on any receipt or any pending residence application. Self-employment remains within the qualified TP scope; VMI/tax registration is separate and unsettled pending-applicant details remain an evidence ceiling. Keep TP work rights separate from ordinary quota and immigration salary thresholds. 2026 floors: €1,153 gross/month for unqualified work and €7.05/hour; enacted 2027 floors €1,245/month and €7.61/hour from 1 January 2027. Employer duties are separately stated: written contract, 1-SD generally at least one working day before start, monthly SAM by the 15th, 2-SD within one working day after end, foreign-worker reporting (with TP category qualification), safety, payroll and social insurance.

## Healthcare review

PASS. Do not describe all adult TP holders as automatically having full PSD/CHI. State-insured TP categories include children under 18 and specified pension/disability groups; other adults need another qualifying basis, including eligible employment/contributions. Active insurance is verified with VLK/provider. Persons legally entitled to TP may receive state-funded emergency and qualifying necessary care while awaiting decision; a generic receipt alone does not prove that entitlement or full insurance. Identity/TP evidence and active CHI record are distinct. 112 is emergency; 113 is non-emergency medical advice; neither means all care is free.

## Education review

PASS. Relevant right-to-reside holders, including TP-protected minors, can access education; a generic pending receipt is not universal proof. Pre-primary generally begins in the calendar year a child turns six; primary normally starts at seven; compulsory education through 16. Do not convert “generally/normally” to universal cutoffs. Enrolment and placement are local; missing Ukrainian records are not established as an initial prerequisite. Language/adaptation support depends on need and capacity; eight Ukrainian-curriculum schools is a June 2026 snapshot only. HEI/programme controls admission, evidence, language, tuition and funding; TP alone grants none. Closed 2026 scholarship call remains historical.

## Social-assistance review

PASS. No universal Ukraine-only monthly subsistence payment. Granted TP may qualify a person to apply but does not guarantee award. Municipality decides relevant assistance; SPIS is a channel, not the grant authority. Pre-decision municipal aid is discretionary. €256.30/person/month for 2026 is an assessment threshold, not a payment. Child money is €129.50/month per eligible child; €76.22 is conditional. Sodra insurance benefits are separate and depend on insured status/contributions.
