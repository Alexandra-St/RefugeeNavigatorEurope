### LT-TP-01 — COMPLETE

**Eligibility.** Lithuania’s adopted national rules include Ukrainian citizens displaced from Ukraine from 24 February 2022, defined family members, stateless people and third-country nationals who had international or equivalent national protection in Ukraine, and qualifying people with Ukrainian permanent residence who cannot safely and durably return. Eligibility is category-specific; nationality or previous residence by itself is insufficient. National implementation and an individual Migration Department decision must not be collapsed into the EU framework.

**Military rule — FINAL RESULT:** From 5 August 2026 the condition applies to relevant new grants. The EU continuity exception concerns continuous temporary protection in the same Member State across 4 August 2026. Lithuania’s Resolution 742 contains a distinct national transition exception for a person who had TP in any EU Member State on/through the cutoff and retained it continuously, and separately for a Lithuanian TP application pending by 4 August. Do not substitute the broader Lithuanian wording for the EU rule. Migration Department examples of evidence include a Ukrainian passport exit stamp, or an official document confirming fulfilment/exemption such as Reserv+ when the stamp is absent. These are examples, not a closed checklist; individual evidence is for the Department to assess.

**Application/MIGRIS — FINAL RESULT:** Current 5 October 2026 instructions describe replacement of an existing permit, not the entire first-time application process. For replacement, applicant selects the relevant Ukraine-displacement service and permit-change option in MIGRIS, completes the form and books an in-person visit; applicant attends the selected Migration Department client-service office with originals. Any office can be selected, there is no replacement fee, and the new digital permit is delivered to the same MIGRIS account. Children over two attend with a parent/guardian; a parent/guardian files for a child under 16. First-application document list, biometrics, no-document procedure, complete child/family process, and exact confirmation/pending-rights triggers were not fully established. Do not imply that first application is fully online or that an appointment confirmation itself grants TP rights.

**Authority — FINAL RESULT:** Migration Department handles the national TP application/registration and permit. MIGRIS is its online service channel; client-service offices handle the required in-person replacement visit. Municipality is not the TP adjudicator. Border authorities’ entry functions do not replace the Department’s status decision. No current police adjudication role was established in the reviewed owner sources.

**Pending proof/rights — FINAL RESULT:** The reviewed primary sources do not establish one generic pending receipt or online status as proof of lawful stay, work, healthcare, accommodation, social assistance or education. The digital foreigner-registration certificate guidance concerns specified ordinary residence-permit cases and must not be generalized to TP. Treat each right and triggering stage as unresolved absent a case-specific legal basis.

**TP document — FINAL RESULT:** Current document terminology is a digital temporary residence permit (digital residence permit), confirming granted TP; it is not a plastic card or the EU scheme itself. A valid document can be checked through the official e-permit service. In the replacement process, the new digital document is sent to the applicant’s MIGRIS account.

**TP/document validity — FINAL RESULT:** EU scheme runs through 4 March 2028; Lithuania adopted a corresponding national period. The national scheme horizon does not mean every currently held document has that individual expiry or automatically changes. The 5 October guidance says existing digital TP permits are replaced, free of charge, with documents valid to 4 March 2028; those whose permit already states 4 March 2028 do not need replacement under the August guidance. Applicants with earlier expiry dates should follow the current MIGRIS replacement process, not assume automatic reissue.

### LT-TP-02 — COMPLETE

Ordinary employment, highly qualified employment/Blue Card, study, family, business/self-employment, international protection and permanent/long-term residence are separate legal routes. Temporary protection is not an automatic conversion or general eligibility waiver. Family-member eligibility for TP is distinct from ordinary family reunification. TP work permission is distinct from an ordinary employment residence permit. No enacted, generally available Lithuanian post-TP special transition pathway or automatic conversion was found; Council Recommendation C/2025/5129 is non-binding. Result: **NO NATIONAL MECHANISM FOUND** (as of 17 Nov 2026), not a prediction that one cannot later be adopted.

**Residence filing — FINAL RESULT:** From 1 November 2026 the in-country filing rule for ordinary employment or first-cycle/bachelor study covers legally present applicants holding a valid Lithuanian TRP, Lithuanian national visa, residence permit from another Schengen/EU state, or visa-free stay. A short-stay Schengen visa and a national visa issued by another country do not qualify under that rule. Highly qualified employment has its separate ordinary in-country process while legally present. This does not establish in-country filing for family, business or every other route. Filing does not guarantee approval or itself establish pending work/stay rights; check the chosen route’s rules. The TP-related work-permit exemption is a separate employment rule.

**Permanent/P-EU credit — FINAL RESULT:** The reviewed Lithuanian sources do not establish that TP-only time counts toward the five-year long-term residence calculation or national permanent residence. Directive 2003/109 is not by itself proof that TP time qualifies. The Department’s rule counting specified periods on a qualifying digital registration certificate during some pending ordinary permit applications is distinct; it does not prove TP-time credit. Verify national law and the selected residence category before calculating.

### LT-EMP-01 — COMPLETE

**Employment Service — FINAL RESULT:** UŽT provides vacancy access, job-search information, career counselling, formal registration and (for eligible registered clients) active labour-market measures. It does not determine TP/residence status. UŽT’s own foreign-worker page says work permits are now issued only in specified cases, including seasonal employment and certain posted workers; that administrative permit is distinct from TP work authorization. UŽT, Migration Department, Sodra and VDI roles must stay separate.

**Jobseeker registration — FINAL RESULT:** Public vacancy browsing does not require registration. Formal registration can be started online through UŽT e-services or in person at a territorial customer-service office for the person’s place of residence; telephone registration is unavailable. Online registration requires identity confirmation through the e-government portal and choosing a consultation appointment. In-person identity documents include a valid Lithuanian residence permit for third-country nationals and, for Ukrainian war refugees, a travel document or Migration Department registration-confirmation document. UŽT also requests education/competence/qualification records in Lithuanian. The page does not state a personal code or declared residence as universal prerequisites. It does not settle all pending TP cases; do not infer registration rights from a generic pending receipt. Jobseeker registration, “unemployed” status and service eligibility are distinct.

**Vacancies/assistance — FINAL RESULT:** UŽT public vacancies can be viewed before registration; its Ukrainian locale exists, but not every listing is translated. EURES is for cross-border job search/mobility and is not an immigration decision maker. Counselling may be offered without registration; training/active measures require applicable status and conditions, assessment/plan and available funding/places. No blanket course, subsidy or placement is guaranteed. JOINT-UA-EMPLOY Lithuanian-course registration is closed; selection is underway. Sodra unemployment benefit is separate: current 2026 rules generally require UŽT unemployed status plus at least 12 months of unemployment insurance in the preceding 24 months and an application, subject to exceptions. The Ukraine bilateral information page does not establish Ukrainian unemployment contribution aggregation.

### LT-EMP-02 — COMPLETE

**TP work right — FINAL RESULT:** A person entitled to TP may start work after applying for temporary protection in Lithuania; the current Migration Department page explicitly confirms this. Foreigners Law Art. 94 provides covered beneficiaries with work and self-employment rights and the corresponding work-permit exemption while the statutory TP entitlement/protection conditions apply. The digital permit need not first be issued. This does not extend to all Ukrainian nationals, arrival alone, any pending ordinary residence application, or a generic receipt. The permit confirms granted TP; a generic pending proof’s full rights are not established. The employer should verify a case-specific entitlement with the Migration Department when the application/status proof is unclear.

**Work-right proof — FINAL RESULT:** The official start point is filing a Lithuanian TP application by a person entitled to TP. No universal pending certificate or receipt is established as proof of every right. Once granted, the digital temporary residence permit confirms TP status. Do not use a UŽT registration document or personal code by itself as proof of work authorization.

**1 Nov in-country filing — FINAL RESULT:** Batch 1’s 16 Nov Migration Department update remains applicable: specified legally present applicants may file in country for ordinary work or first-cycle study based on a valid Lithuanian TRP, Lithuanian national visa, a residence permit from another Schengen/EU state, or visa-free stay. A TP holder whose digital TP residence permit is valid falls within the listed Lithuanian-TRP document basis, subject to lawful presence and the route conditions. A short-stay Schengen visa or another country’s national visa is excluded. Highly qualified employment has its own in-country process while legally present. This is filing permission, not itself a TP-work right or a universal pending-application work right. TP work authorization and ordinary TRP routes remain separate.

**Minimum wage — FINAL RESULT:** From 1 Jan 2026, the general gross floor is €1,153 monthly and €7.05 hourly. Labour Code Art. 141 restricts monthly minimum wage to unqualified work; these amounts are gross, not take-home pay. Government Resolution 509 already enacted 2027 rates of €1,245 monthly and €7.61 hourly from 1 Jan 2027. Do not substitute 2027 rates for the current 2026 rate or treat the monthly floor as a skilled-worker immigration salary threshold.

**Other immigration pay thresholds:** As of 17 Nov 2026 the 2026 work-based TRP quota had been exhausted. Migration Department’s current notice says applications under the relevant quota route may proceed only if the promised salary meets 1.2 BDU (€2,893.68 at the published 2026 BDU) or, for the specified high-added-value shortage occupations, 1 BDU (€2,411.40), unless a supplemental quota applies. This is a conditional ordinary immigration-route threshold, distinct from the statutory national minimum wage and the TP work exemption. EU Blue Card/high-skilled thresholds are also route-specific; no general salary figure is stated in the article copy.

**Employer duties — FINAL RESULT:** A written employment contract and ordinary payroll, social-insurance, occupational-safety and labour-law requirements continue to apply. Sodra 1-SD is generally due at least one working day before work begins; Sodra describes monthly SAM reporting/payment and 2-SD termination reporting. Foreigners Law Art. 62(8) separately provides foreign-worker information reporting through Sodra to VDI and UŽT. Its exact application/details for every TP document/cohort are not explicit in the reviewed public guidance; do not treat it as a work-permit decision or infer an exemption. The employer must ensure the worker has a lawful work basis.

**Self-employment — FINAL RESULT:** Foreigners Law TP provision includes self-employment for the qualifying protected group, subject to statutory TP scope. VMI registration, taxes, social contributions and any sector licence remain separate. VMI’s general third-country national registration page asks for a Lithuanian residence permit, and public pages do not reconcile every pending TP applicant/document case. Do not assert pending-application individual-activity registration as settled; advise confirmation with VMI before starting.
