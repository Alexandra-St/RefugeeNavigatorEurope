## Controlling DUO evidence and interpretation

The controlling administrative instrument is the Minister of Education, Culture and Science's policy under Article 9b of the Les- en cursusgeldwet, as amended by [Staatscourant 2021, 33878 — Beleidsregel wijziging kwijtschelding lesgeld](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-33878.html). Its Article 2 requires both that the applicant is not eligible for WTOS/WSF 2000 course-fee assistance and that the applicant cannot pay; the latter includes confirmation that the responsible benefit/recognized support organisation cannot pay either. The related WSF 2000 age rule appears in Article 2.3(3)–(4) of [Staatsblad 2000, 286 — Wet studiefinanciering 2000](https://zoek.officielebekendmaking.nl/stb-2000-286.html). DUO's current Dutch operational page supplies the exact 2026–27 application of the age rule and the separate remission-continuation conditions: [DUO, Asielzoeker of Oekraïner — Lesgeld](https://duo.nl/particulier/lesgeld/asielzoeker-en-lesgeld.jsp).

The exact DUO wording is **18 or older and younger than 30 on 1 August** for initial 2026–27 remission. In date terms, an applicant must not yet have reached their 30th birthday on 1 August. An applicant who is already 30 on that date does not meet the initial age condition. This is not the English page's “younger than 31” interpretation, which includes age 30. The uninterrupted-study provision is a separate continuation rule: after 30, remission may continue only for an MBO programme when the first request was before the 30th birthday, enrolment continued without interruption, and remission was granted every year without interruption.

Other checked conditions are preserved: MBO-BOL or full-time VAVO scope; 18+ starting point; qualifying asylum/Temporary Protection Directive status; nationality/student-finance condition; inability to pay with no organisation able to pay; applicant must apply; 31 July 2027 deadline; and fees already paid are not refundable. Continuation after 30 is limited to MBO. The wording remains conditional and does not promise entitlement.
